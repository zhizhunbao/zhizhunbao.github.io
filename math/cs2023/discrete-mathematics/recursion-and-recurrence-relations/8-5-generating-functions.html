              Operations on Generating Functions
              生成函数的运算              		 "If \(G(z)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} a_k z^k\) and \(H(z) =\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} b_k z^k\) are generating functions and \(c\) is a real number, then the sum \(G + H\text{,}\) scalar product \(c G\text{,}\) product \(G H\text{,}\) and monomial product \(z^p G\text{,}\) \(p \geq 1\) are generating functions, where" 若 \(G(z)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} a_k z^k\) 与 \(H(z) =\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} b_k z^k\) 是生成函数，\(c\) 为实数，则和 \(G + H\)、数乘 \(c G\)、积 \(G H\)， 以及单项式积 \(z^p G\)，（\(p \geq 1\)）都是生成函数，其中 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Definition 8.5.7
              Generating functions related to Pop and Push
              与 Pop 和 Push 相关的生成函数              		 "If \(p \gt 1\), \(\displaystyle G(S\uparrow p;z) = \left(G(S;z) -\left.\sum_{k=0}^{p-1} S(k) z^k\right)/z^k\right.\) \(G(S\downarrow p;z) = z^p G(S;z)\)." 若 \(p \gt 1\)，\(\displaystyle G(S\uparrow p;z) = \left(G(S;z) -\left.\sum_{k=0}^{p-1} S(k) z^k\right)/z^k\right.\) \(G(S\downarrow p;z) = z^p G(S;z)\)。 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Theorem 8.5.9
