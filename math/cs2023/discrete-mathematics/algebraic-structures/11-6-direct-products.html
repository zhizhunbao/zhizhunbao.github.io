              11.6 Direct Products
              直积              		 (No definition) （无定义） Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., section 11.6
              Direct Product
              直积              		 "If \(\left[V_i;*_i, \diamond_i, \ldots \right]\text{,}\) \(i = 1, 2, \ldots, n\) are algebraic systems of the same kind, then the direct product of these systems is \(V =V_1\times V_2\times \cdots \times V_n\), with operations defined below. The elements of \(V\) are \(n\)-tuples of the form \(\left(a_1, a_2, \dots, a _n \right)\text{,}\) where \(a_k \in V_k\text{,}\) \(k = 1, \dots, n\text{.}\) The systems \(V_1\text{,}\) \(V_2, \dots, V_n\) are called the factors of \(V\text{.}\) There are as many operations on \(V\) as there are in the factors. Each of these operations is defined componentwise:" 若 \(\left[V_i;*_i, \diamond_i, \ldots \right]\)， \(i = 1, 2, \ldots, n\) 是同类的代数系统，则这些系统的直积是 \(V =V_1\times V_2\times \cdots \times V_n\)，其运算定义如下。\(V\) 的元素是形如 \(\left(a_1, a_2, \dots, a _n \right)\)， 的 \(n\) 元组，其中 \(a_k \in V_k\)， \(k = 1, \dots, n\)。 系统 \(V_1\)， \(V_2, \dots, V_n\) 称为 \(V\) 的因子。\(V\) 上的运算与各因子中的运算一样多。其中每个运算都按分量定义： Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Definition 11.6.1
              Direct Product of Two Groups
              两个群的直积              		 "Let \([G_1;*_1]\) and \([G_2;*_2]\) be two groups. Their direct product is the system \([G_1 \times G_2; *]\) with domain equal to the Cartesian product of the domains of the two groups and with the coordinatewise operation \(*\) defined by" 设 \([G_1;*_1]\) 和 \([G_2;*_2]\) 为两个群。它们的直积是系统 \([G_1 \times G_2; *]\)，其定义域等于这两个群的定义域的笛卡尔积，其按坐标运算 \(*\) 定义为 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Definition 11.6.3
              The Direct Product of Groups is a Group
              群的直积是群              		 "The direct product of two or more groups is a group; that is, the algebraic properties of a system obtained by taking the direct product of two or more groups includes the group axioms." 两个或多个群的直积是群；即，取两个或多个群的直积所得到的系统，其代数性质包含群公理。 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Theorem 11.6.6
              Properties of Direct Products of Groups
              群直积的性质              		 "If \(G = G_1\times G_2 \times \cdots \times G_n\) is a direct product of \(n\) groups and \(\left(a_1, a_2 , \dots , a_n\right) \in G\), then: The identity of \(G\) is \(\left(e_1, e_2 , . . . , e_n\right)\), where \(e_k\) is the identity of \(G_k\). \(\left(a_1, a_2 , . . . , a_n\right) {}^{-1}= \left(a_1{}^{-1}, a_2{}^{-1} , . . . , a_n{}^{-1}\right)\). \(\left(a_1, a_2 , . . . , a_n\right) {}^m= \left(a_1{}^m, a_2{}^m , . . . , a_n{}^m\right)\) for all \(m\in \mathbb{Z}\). \(G\) is abelian if and only if each of the factors \(G_1, G_2, \ldots , G_n\) is abelian. lf \(H_1, H_2, \ldots , H_n\) are subgroups of the corresponding factors, then \(H_1\times H_2 \times \cdots \times H_n\) is a subgroup of \(G\)." 若 \(G = G_1\times G_2 \times \cdots \times G_n\) 是 \(n\) 个群的直积，且 \(\left(a_1, a_2 , \dots , a_n\right) \in G\)，则：\(G\) 的单位元是 \(\left(e_1, e_2 , . . . , e_n\right)\)，其中 \(e_k\) 是 \(G_k\) 的单位元。\(\left(a_1, a_2 , . . . , a_n\right) {}^{-1}= \left(a_1{}^{-1}, a_2{}^{-1} , . . . , a_n{}^{-1}\right)\)。对所有 \(m\in \mathbb{Z}\)，\(\left(a_1, a_2 , . . . , a_n\right) {}^m= \left(a_1{}^m, a_2{}^m , . . . , a_n{}^m\right)\)。\(G\) 是阿贝尔群，当且仅当每个因子 \(G_1, G_2, \ldots , G_n\) 都是阿贝尔群。若 \(H_1, H_2, \ldots , H_n\) 是相应因子的子群，则 \(H_1\times H_2 \times \cdots \times H_n\) 是 \(G\) 的子群。 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Theorem 11.6.9
