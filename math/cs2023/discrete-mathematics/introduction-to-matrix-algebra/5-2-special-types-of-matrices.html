              5.2 Special Types of Matrices
              特殊类型的矩阵              		 (No definition) （无定义） Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., section 5.2
              Diagonal Matrix
              对角矩阵              		 "A square matrix D is called a diagonal matrix if \(d_{i j}\) = 0 whenever \(i \neq j\text{.}\)" 方阵 D 称为对角矩阵，若每当 \(i \neq j\)。 时 \(d_{i j}\) = 0。 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Definition 5.2.1
              Identity Matrix
              单位矩阵              		 "The \(n\times n\) diagonal matrix \(I_n\) whose diagonal components are all 1’s is called the identity matrix. If the context is clear, we simply use \(I\text{.}\)" 对角线元素全为 1 的 \(n\times n\) 对角矩阵 \(I_n\) 称为单位矩阵。若上下文清楚，我们简记为 \(I\)。 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Definition 5.2.4
              Matrix Inverse
              逆矩阵              		 "Let \(A\) be an \(n\times n\) matrix. If there exists an \(n\times n\) matrix \(B\) such that \(A B = B A =I\text{,}\) then \(B\) is a multiplicative inverse of \(A\) (called simply an inverse of \(A\)) and is denoted by \(A^{-1}\)" 设 \(A\) 是 \(n\times n\) 矩阵。若存在 \(n\times n\) 矩阵 \(B\) 使得 \(A B = B A =I\)，则 \(B\) 是 \(A\) 的乘法逆元（简称 \(A\) 的逆矩阵）记作 \(A^{-1}\) Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Definition 5.2.5
              Inverses are unique
              逆矩阵是唯一的              		 "The inverse of an \(n\times n\) matrix A, when it exists, is unique." \(n\times n\) 矩阵 A 的逆矩阵（若存在）是唯一的。 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Theorem 5.2.6
              Determinant of a 2 by 2 matrix
              2 阶矩阵的行列式              		 "Let \(A =\left(\begin{array}{cc} a & b \\ c & d \\ \end{array} \right)\text{.}\) The determinant of \(A\) is the number \(\det A = a d - b c\text{.}\)" 设 \(A =\left(\begin{array}{cc} a & b \\ c & d \\ \end{array} \right)\)。\(A\) 的行列式是数 \(\det A = a d - b c\)。 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Definition 5.2.7
              Inverse of 2 by 2 matrix
              2 阶矩阵的逆矩阵              		 "Let \(A =\left( \begin{array}{cc} a & b \\ c & d \\ \end{array} \right)\). If \(\det A\neq 0\), then \(A^{-1} =\frac{1}{\det A}\left( \begin{array}{cc} d & -b \\ -c & a \\ \end{array} \right)\)." 设 \(A =\left( \begin{array}{cc} a & b \\ c & d \\ \end{array} \right)\)。若 \(\det A\neq 0\)，则 \(A^{-1} =\frac{1}{\det A}\left( \begin{array}{cc} d & -b \\ -c & a \\ \end{array} \right)\)。 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Theorem 5.2.9
