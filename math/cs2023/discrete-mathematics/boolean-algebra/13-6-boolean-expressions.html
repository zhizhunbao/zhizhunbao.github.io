              13.6 Boolean Expressions
              布尔表达式              		 (No definition) （无定义） Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., section 13.6
              Boolean Expression
              布尔表达式              		 "Let \([B; \lor, \land, - ]\) be any Boolean algebra, and let \(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_k\) be variables in \(B\text{;}\) that is, variables that can assume values from \(B\text{.}\) A Boolean expression generated by \(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_k\) is any valid combination of the \(x_i\) and the elements of \(B\) with the operations of meet, join, and complementation." 设 \([B; \lor, \land, - ]\) 为任意布尔代数，\(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_k\) 为 \(B\) 中的变量，即可取 \(B\) 中值的变量。由 \(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_k\) 生成的布尔表达式是由 \(x_i\) 与 \(B\) 的元素通过交、并和求补运算构成的任意有效组合。 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Definition 13.6.1

              Uniqueness of Minterm Normal Form
              极小项范式的唯一性              		 "Let \(e\left(x_1,\ldots , x_k\right)\) be a Boolean expression over B. There exists a unique minterm normal form \(M\left(x_1, \ldots , x_k\right)\) that is equivalent to \(e\left(x_1, \ldots , x_k\right)\) in the sense that e and M define the same function from \(B^k\) into \(B\)." 设 \(e\left(x_1,\ldots , x_k\right)\) 是 B 上的布尔表达式。存在唯一的极小项范式 \(M\left(x_1, \ldots , x_k\right)\) 与 \(e\left(x_1, \ldots , x_k\right)\) 等价，意思是 e 和 M 定义了从 \(B^k\) 到 \(B\) 的同一个函数。 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Theorem 13.6.6
