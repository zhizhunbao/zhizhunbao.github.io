              15.2 Cosets and Factor Groups
              陪集与商群              		 (No definition) （无定义） Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., section 15.2
              Coset
              陪集              		 "If \([G;*]\) is a group, \(H \leq G\) and \(a \in G\text{,}\) the left coset of \(H\) generated by \(a\) is" 若 \([G;*]\) 是群，\(H \leq G\) 且 \(a \in G\)， 则 \(H\) 的由 \(a\) 生成的左陪集为 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Definition 15.2.2
              Coset Representative
              陪集代表元              		 "Any element of a coset is called a representative of that coset." 陪集的任一元素称为该陪集的代表元。 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Definition 15.2.4
              Cosets Partition a Group
              陪集划分群              		 "If \([G; *]\) is a group and \(H\leq G\), the set of left cosets of \(H\) is a partition of \(G\). In addition, all of the left cosets of \(H\) have the same cardinality. The same is true for right cosets." 若 \([G; *]\) 是群且 \(H\leq G\)，则 \(H\) 的左陪集的集合是 \(G\) 的一个划分。此外，\(H\) 的所有左陪集具有相同的基数。右陪集也是如此。 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Theorem 15.2.8
              A Coset Counting Formula
              陪集计数公式              		 "If \(\lvert G\rvert \lt \infty\) and \(H\leq G\), the number of distinct left cosets of \(H\) equals \(\frac{\lvert G\rvert }{\lvert H\rvert }\). For this reason we use \(G/H\) to denote the set of left cosets of \(H\) in \(G\)" 若 \(\lvert G\rvert \lt \infty\) 且 \(H\leq G\)，则 \(H\) 的不同左陪集的个数等于 \(\frac{\lvert G\rvert }{\lvert H\rvert }\)。因此，我们用 \(G/H\) 表示 \(H\) 在 \(G\) 中的左陪集的集合 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Corollary 15.2.9
              Lagrange’s Theorem
              拉格朗日定理              		 "The order of a subgroup of a finite group must divide the order of the group." 有限群的子群的阶必整除该群的阶。 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Theorem 15.2.12
              Operation on Cosets
              陪集上的运算              		 "Let \(C\) and \(D\) be left cosets of \(H\text{,}\) a subgroup of \(G\) with representatives \(c\) and \(d\text{,}\) respectively. Then" 设 \(C\) 与 \(D\) 为 \(H\)， 的左陪集，\(H\)， 是 \(G\) 的子群，其代表元分别为 \(c\) 和 \(d\)， 则 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Definition 15.2.13
              Coset operation is well-defined (Abelian Case)
              陪集运算是良定义的（阿贝尔群情形）              		 "If \(G\) is an abelian group, and \(H \leq G\), the operation induced on cosets of \(H\) by the operation of \(G\) is well defined." 若 \(G\) 是阿贝尔群且 \(H \leq G\)，则由 \(G\) 的运算在 \(H\) 的陪集上诱导的运算是良定义的。 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Theorem 15.2.18
              Factor Group
              商群              		 "Let \(G\) be a group and \(H \leq G\text{.}\) If the set of left cosets of \(H\) forms a group, then that group is called the factor group of “\(G\) modulo \(H\text{.}\)” It is denoted \(G/H\text{.}\)" 设 \(G\) 为群且 \(H \leq G\)。 若 \(H\) 的左陪集的集合构成群，则该群称为“\(G\) 模 \(H\)”的商群。记作 \(G/H\)。 Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Definition 15.2.20
