              14.7 Introduction to Multiple Regression
              多元回归介绍              		 (No definition) （无定义） Online Statistics Education, n.d., section 14.7
              Beta Weight
              贝塔权重              		 "A standardized regression coefficient." 标准化回归系数。 Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary
              Criterion Variable
              效标变量              		 "In regression analysis (such as linear regression) the criterion variable is the variable being predicted. In general, the criterion variable is the dependent variable." 在回归分析（如线性回归）中，效标变量是被预测的变量。一般而言，效标变量就是因变量。 Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary
              Multiple Regression
              多元回归              		 "Multiple regression is linear regression in which two or more predictor variables are used to predict the criterion." 多元回归是使用两个或多个预测变量来预测效标的线性回归。 Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary
              Partial Slope
              偏斜率              		 "The partial slope in multiple regression is the slope of the relationship between a predictor variable that is independent of the other predictor variables and the criterion. It is also the regression coefficient for the predictor variable in question." 多元回归中的偏斜率是某个与其他预测变量无关的预测变量同效标之间关系的斜率。它也是所讨论预测变量的回归系数。 Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary
              Predictor
              预测变量              		 "A predictor variable is a variable used in regression to predict another variable. It is sometimes referred to as an independent variable if it is manipulated rather than just measured." 预测变量是回归中用来预测另一个变量的变量。如果它是被操纵的而不仅仅是被测量的，有时也称其为自变量。 Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary
              Regression Coefficient
              回归系数              		 "A regression coefficient is the slope of the regression line in simple regression or the partial slope in multiple regression." 回归系数是简单回归中回归线的斜率或多元回归中的偏斜率。 Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary
              Simple Regression
              简单回归              		 "Simple regression is linear regression in which one more predictor variable is used to predict the criterion." 简单回归是使用一个以上预测变量来预测效标的线性回归。 Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary
              Slope
              斜率              		 "The slope of a line is the change in \(Y\) for each change of one unit of \(X\). It is sometimes defined as "rise over run" which is the same thing. The slope of the black line in the graph is 0.425 because the line increases by 0.425 each time \(X\) increases by 1.0." 直线的斜率是 \(X\) 每变化一个单位时 \(Y\) 的变化量。它有时被定义为“上升量除以水平移动量”，这是同一回事。图中黑线的斜率为 0.425，因为 \(X\) 每增加 1.0，该线就上升 0.425。 Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary
