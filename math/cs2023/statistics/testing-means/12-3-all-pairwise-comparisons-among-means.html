              12.3 All Pairwise Comparisons Among Means
              均值间的所有两两比较              		 (No definition) （无定义） Online Statistics Education, n.d., section 12.3
              Comparisons Among Means
              均值间比较              		 "A method for testing differences among means for significance. For example, one might test whether the difference betwen Mean 1 and the average of Means 2 and 3 is significantly different. See also: Planned Comparions and Orthogonal Comparisons" 一种检验均值之间差异是否显著的方法。例如，可以检验均值 1 与均值 2 和均值 3 的平均值之差是否显著不同。另见：计划比较与正交比较 Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary
              Homogeneity of Variance
              方差齐性              		 "The assumption that the variances of all the populations are equal." 假定所有总体的方差相等。 Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary
              Pairwise Comparisons
              两两比较              		 "Pairwise comparisons are comparisons between pairs of means." 两两比较是成对均值之间的比较。 Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary
              Robust
              稳健              		 "Something is robust if it holds up well in the face of adversity. A measure of central tendency or variability is considered robust if it is not greatly affected by a few extreme scores. A statistical test is considered robust if it works well in spite of moderate violations of the assumptions on which it is based." 某事物如果在逆境中表现良好，则是稳健的。如果一种集中趋势或变异性的度量不受少数极端分数的很大影响，则认为它是稳健的。如果一种统计检验在其所依据的假设受到适度违背时仍能良好运作，则认为它是稳健的。 Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary
              Studentized Range Distribution
              学生化极差分布              		 "The studentized range distribution is used to test the difference between the largest and smallest means. It is similar to the \(t\) distribution which is used when there are only two means." 学生化极差分布用于检验最大均值与最小均值之间的差异。它类似于仅有两个均值时使用的 \(t\) 分布。 Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary
              Tukey HSD Test
              图基 HSD 检验              		 "The "Honestly Significantly Different" (HSD) test developed by the statistician John Tukey to test all pairwise comparisons among means. The test is based on the " studentized range distribution."" 由统计学家 John Tukey 提出的“真实显著差异”（HSD）检验，用于检验均值之间所有的两两比较。该检验基于“ 学生化极差分布”。 Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary
