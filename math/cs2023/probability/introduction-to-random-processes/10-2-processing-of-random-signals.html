              Transfer Function
              传递函数              		 "Let \(H(f)\) be the Fourier transform of \(h(t)\), \(\begin{align*} H(f)=\mathcal{F} \{h(t) \}= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} h(t) e^{-2 j \pi f t} \; dt. \end{align*}\) \(H(f)\) is called the transfer function of the system." 设 \(H(f)\) 为 \(h(t)\) 的傅里叶变换，\(\begin{align*} H(f)=\mathcal{F} \{h(t) \}= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} h(t) e^{-2 j \pi f t} \; dt. \end{align*}\) \(H(f)\) 称为该系统的传递函数。 Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 10.2.2
