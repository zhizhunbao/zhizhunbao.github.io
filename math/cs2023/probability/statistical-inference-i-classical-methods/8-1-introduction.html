              Statistical Inference
              统计推断              		 "Statistical inference is a collection of methods that deal with drawing conclusions from data that are prone to random variation." 统计推断是一组方法，用于从易受随机变异影响的数据中得出结论。 Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 8.1.0
              Frequentist Inference
              频率学派推断              		 "In this approach, the unknown quantity \(\theta\) is assumed to be a fixed quantity. That is, \(\theta\) is a deterministic (non-random) quantity that is to be estimated by the observed data." 在这种方法中，未知量 \(\theta\) 被假定为固定量。也就是说，\(\theta\) 是一个确定性的（非随机的）量，需要由观测数据来估计。 Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 8.1.0
              Bayesian Inference
              贝叶斯推断              		 "In the Bayesian approach the unknown quantity \(\Theta\) is assumed to be a random variable, and we assume that we have some initial guess about the distribution of \(\Theta\). After observing the data, we update the distribution of \(\Theta\) using Bayes' Rule." 在贝叶斯方法中，未知量 \(\Theta\) 被假定为随机变量，并假定我们对 \(\Theta\) 的分布有某种初始猜测。观测到数据后，我们利用贝叶斯法则更新 \(\Theta\) 的分布。 Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 8.1.0
              Statistics (as a subject)
              统计（作为学科）              		 "The mathematical study of the likelihood and probability of events occurring based on known information and inferred by taking a limited number of samples." 对事件发生的可能性和概率所作的数学研究，依据已知信息，并通过抽取有限数量的样本来推断。 Weisstein, n.d., "Statistics" entry, para. 1
              8.1.1 Random Sampling
              随机抽样              		 (No definition) （无定义） Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 8.1.1
              Random Sample
              随机样本              		 "The collection of random variables \(X_1\), \(X_2\), \(X_3\), \(...\), \(X_n\) is said to be a random sample of size \(n\) if they are independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.)," 若随机变量集合 \(X_1\), \(X_2\), \(X_3\), \(...\), \(X_n\) 独立同分布（i.i.d.），则称其为容量为 \(n\) 的随机样本， Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 8.1.1
              Point Estimator
              点估计量              		 "In general, a point estimator is a function of the random sample \(\hat{\Theta}=h(X_1,X_2,\cdots,X_n)\) that is used to estimate an unknown quantity." 一般地，点估计量是随机样本的函数 \(\hat{\Theta}=h(X_1,X_2,\cdots,X_n)\)，用来估计未知量。 Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 8.1.1
              Estimate
              估计值              		 "Here, \(\hat{\theta}\) is called an estimate of the average height in the population." 这里 \(\hat{\theta}\) 称为总体平均身高的估计值。 Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 8.1.1
              Order Statistics
              顺序统计量              		 "Thus, we often order the observed data from the smallest to the largest. We call the resulting ordered random variables order statistics." 因此，我们常把观测数据从小到大排序。我们把得到的有序随机变量称为顺序统计量。 Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 8.1.1
