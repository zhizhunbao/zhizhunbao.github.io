              1.4.0 Conditional Probability
              条件概率              		 (No definition) （无定义） Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 1.4.0
              Prior Probability
              先验概率              		 "It is reasonable to assume that in this example, \(P(R \vert C)\) should be larger than the original \(P(R)\), which is called the prior probability of \(R\)." 在这个例子中，可以合理地假定 \(P(R \vert C)\) 应当大于原来的 \(P(R)\)，后者称为 \(R\) 的先验概率。 Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 1.4.0
              Conditional Probability
              条件概率              		 "If \(A\) and \(B\) are two events in a sample space \(S\), then the conditional probability of \(A\) given \(B\) is defined as" 若 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 是样本空间 \(S\) 中的两个事件，则给定 \(B\) 时 \(A\) 的条件概率定义为 Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 1.4.0
              Chain Rule for Conditional Probability
              乘法公式（链式法则）              		 "Chain rule for conditional probability: Let us write the formula for conditional probability in the following format \(\hspace{100pt} P(A \cap B)=P(A)P(B\vert A)=P(B)P(A\vert B) \hspace{100pt} (1.5)\)" 条件概率的链式法则：把条件概率公式写成如下形式 \(\hspace{100pt} P(A \cap B)=P(A)P(B\vert A)=P(B)P(A\vert B) \hspace{100pt} (1.5)\) Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 1.4.0
              Conditional probability
              条件概率              		 "The conditional probability of an event A assuming that B has occurred with P(B)>0, denoted P(A|B), equals P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B)/P(B)." 事件 A 在已知 B 已经发生（P(B)>0）时的条件概率记为 P(A|B)，等于 P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B)/P(B)。 Weisstein, n.d.-b, "Conditional probability" entry, para. 1
              1.4.1 Independence
              独立性              		 (No definition) （无定义） Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 1.4.1
              Independent Events
              独立事件              		 "Two events are independent if one does not convey any information about the other." 若一个事件不提供关于另一个事件的任何信息，则称这两个事件相互独立。 Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 1.4.1
              1.4.2 Law of Total Probability
              全概率公式              		 (No definition) （无定义） Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 1.4.2
              1.4.3 Bayes' Rule
              贝叶斯法则              		 (No definition) （无定义） Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 1.4.3
              Bayes' rule
              贝叶斯法则              		 "Bayes' theorem, also called Bayes' rule, expresses a conditional probability in terms of its reverse and the corresponding marginal probabilities." 贝叶斯定理也称为贝叶斯法则，它用反向的条件概率来表示一个条件概率，并用到相应的边缘概率。 Weisstein, n.d.-a, "Bayes' theorem" entry, para. 1
              1.4.4 Conditional Independence
              条件独立性              		 (No definition) （无定义） Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 1.4.4
              Conditionally Independent Events
              条件独立事件              		 "Two events \(A\) and \(B\) are conditionally independent given an event \(C\) with \(P(C)>0\) if \(\hspace{100pt}P(A \cap B\vert C)=P(A\vert C)P(B\vert C) \hspace{100pt} (1.8)\)" 对于满足 \(P(C)>0\) 的事件 \(C\)，若 \(\hspace{100pt}P(A \cap B\vert C)=P(A\vert C)P(B\vert C) \hspace{100pt} (1.8)\)，则称两个事件 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 在给定 \(C\) 的条件下条件独立。 Pishro-Nik, 2014, section 1.4.4
