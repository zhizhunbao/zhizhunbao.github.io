              7.2 Quadratic Forms
              二次型              		 (No definition) （无定义） Austin, 2023, section 7.2
              Quadratic Form
              二次型              		 "If \(A\) is a symmetric \(m\times m\) matrix, the quadratic form defined by \(A\) is the function \(q_A(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{x}\cdot(A\mathbf{x})\)." 若 \(A\) 是 \(m\times m\) 对称矩阵，则由 \(A\) 定义的二次型是函数 \(q_A(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{x}\cdot(A\mathbf{x})\)。 Austin, 2023, Definition 7.2.4
              Principal Axes Theorem
              主轴定理              		 "If \(A\) is a symmetric \(m\times m\) matrix with eigenvalues \(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\ldots,\lambda_m\), then the quadratic form \(q_A\) can be written, after an orthogonal change of coordinates \(\mathbf{y}=Q^T\mathbf{x}\), as \(q_A(\mathbf{x}) = \lambda_1y_1^2 + \lambda_2y_2^2 + \ldots + \lambda_my_m^2\)." 若 \(A\) 是 \(m\times m\) 对称矩阵，其特征值为 \(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\ldots,\lambda_m\)，则经过正交坐标变换 \(\mathbf{y}=Q^T\mathbf{x}\) 后，二次型 \(q_A\) 可写成 \(q_A(\mathbf{x}) = \lambda_1y_1^2 + \lambda_2y_2^2 + \ldots + \lambda_my_m^2\)。 Austin, 2023, Theorem 7.2.13
              Positive Definite
              正定              		 "A symmetric matrix \(A\) is called positive definite if its associated quadratic form satisfies \(q_A(\mathbf{x}) \gt 0\) for any nonzero vector \(\mathbf{x}\). If \(q_A(\mathbf{x}) \geq 0\) for all nonzero vectors \(\mathbf{x}\), we say that \(A\) is positive semidefinite. Likewise, we say that \(A\) is negative definite if \(q_A(\mathbf{x}) \lt 0\) for all nonzero vectors \(\mathbf{x}\). Finally, \(A\) is called indefinite if \(q_A(\mathbf{x}) \gt 0\) for some \(\mathbf{x}\) and \(q_A(\mathbf{x}) \lt 0\) for others." 如果对称矩阵 \(A\) 的相应二次型满足对任意非零向量 \(\mathbf{x}\) 都有 \(q_A(\mathbf{x}) \gt 0\)，则称 \(A\) 为正定的。如果对所有非零向量 \(\mathbf{x}\) 都有 \(q_A(\mathbf{x}) \geq 0\)，则称 \(A\) 为半正定的。类似地，如果对所有非零向量 \(\mathbf{x}\) 都有 \(q_A(\mathbf{x}) \lt 0\)，则称 \(A\) 为负定的。最后，如果对某些 \(\mathbf{x}\) 有 \(q_A(\mathbf{x}) \gt 0\)，而对另一些 \(\mathbf{x}\) 有 \(q_A(\mathbf{x}) \lt 0\)，则称 \(A\) 为不定的。 Austin, 2023, Definition 7.2.14
              Second Derivative Test
              二阶导数判别法              		 "The nature of a critical point of a multivariable function is determined by the Hessian \(H\) of the function at the critical point. If \(H\) has all positive eigenvalues, the critical point is a local minimum. \(H\) has all negative eigenvalues, the critical point is a local maximum. \(H\) has both positive and negative eigenvalues, the critical point is neither a local maximum nor minimum." 多元函数临界点的性质由该函数在临界点处的黑塞矩阵 \(H\) 决定。如果 \(H\) 的特征值全为正，则临界点是局部极小值点。如果 \(H\) 的特征值全为负，则临界点是局部极大值点。如果 \(H\) 既有正特征值又有负特征值，则临界点既不是局部极大值点也不是局部极小值点。 Austin, 2023, Proposition 7.2.18
              Hessian
              黑塞矩阵              		 "The matrix \(H = \begin{bmatrix} f_{xx}(x_0,y_0) & f_{xy}(x_0,y_0) \\ f_{yx}(x_0,y_0) & f_{yy}(x_0,y_0) \end{bmatrix}\) is called the Hessian of \(f\), and we see now that the eigenvalues of this symmetric matrix determine the nature of the critical point \((x_0,y_0)\)." 矩阵 \(H = \begin{bmatrix} f_{xx}(x_0,y_0) & f_{xy}(x_0,y_0) \\ f_{yx}(x_0,y_0) & f_{yy}(x_0,y_0) \end{bmatrix}\) 称为 \(f\) 的黑塞矩阵，我们现在看到，这个对称矩阵的特征值决定了临界点 \((x_0,y_0)\) 的性质。 Austin, 2023, section 7.2
