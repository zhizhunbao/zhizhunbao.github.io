              6.1 The Dot Product
              点积              		 (No definition) （无定义） Austin, 2023, section 6.1
              Dot Product
              点积              		 "The dot product is defined, more generally, for any two \(m\)-dimensional vectors: \(\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{w} = \left[ \begin{array}{c} v_1 \\ v_2 \\ \vdots \\ v_m \\ \end{array} \right] \cdot \left[ \begin{array}{c} w_1 \\ w_2 \\ \vdots \\ w_m \\ \end{array} \right] = v_1w_1 + v_2w_2 + \ldots + v_mw_m\). The important thing to remember is that the dot product will produce a scalar." 更一般地，点积对任意两个 \(m\) 维向量都有定义：\(\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{w} = \left[ \begin{array}{c} v_1 \\ v_2 \\ \vdots \\ v_m \\ \end{array} \right] \cdot \left[ \begin{array}{c} w_1 \\ w_2 \\ \vdots \\ w_m \\ \end{array} \right] = v_1w_1 + v_2w_2 + \ldots + v_mw_m\)。需要记住的重要一点是，点积产生一个标量。 Austin, 2023, section 6.1
              Orthogonal
              正交              		 "We say that vectors \(\mathbf{v}\) and \(\mathbf{w}\) are orthogonal if \(\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{w}=0\)." 如果 \(\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{w}=0\)，则称向量 \(\mathbf{v}\) 与 \(\mathbf{w}\) 正交。 Austin, 2023, Definition 6.1.9
              Time Series
              时间序列              		 "When interpreted in this way, we call the vector a time series." 按这种方式解释时，我们称该向量为时间序列。 Austin, 2023, section 6.1
              Correlated
              相关              		 "We often say that they are correlated, and we would like to measure the degree to which they are correlated." 我们常说它们是相关的，并且我们希望度量它们相关的程度。 Austin, 2023, section 6.1
              Demean
              去均值              		 "In order to compare the ways in which they rise and fall, we will first demean the time series; that is, for each time series, we will subtract its average value to obtain a new time series." 为了比较它们升降的方式，我们首先对时间序列去均值；也就是说，对每个时间序列，减去其平均值，得到一个新的时间序列。 Austin, 2023, section 6.1
              Clustering
              聚类              		 "We call the result a clustering." 我们称这个结果为一个聚类。 Austin, 2023, section 6.1
              Centroid
              质心              		 "To begin, we identify the centroid, or the average, of a set of vectors \(\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2, \ldots,\mathbf{v}_n\) as \(\frac1n\left(\mathbf{v}_1+\mathbf{v}_2+\ldots+\mathbf{v}_n\right)\)." 首先，我们将一组向量 \(\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2, \ldots,\mathbf{v}_n\) 的质心（即平均值）定义为 \(\frac1n\left(\mathbf{v}_1+\mathbf{v}_2+\ldots+\mathbf{v}_n\right)\)。 Austin, 2023, section 6.1
              Objective
              目标函数              		 "If we have a clustering, we therefore define a function, called the objective, which measures the average of the square of the distance from each point to the centroid of the cluster to which that point belongs." 因此，如果有一个聚类，我们就定义一个称为目标函数的函数，它度量每个点到其所属簇的质心的距离平方的平均值。 Austin, 2023, section 6.1
              Elbow Plot
              肘部图              		 "This plot is called an elbow plot due to its shape." 这种图因其形状而称为肘部图。 Austin, 2023, section 6.1
