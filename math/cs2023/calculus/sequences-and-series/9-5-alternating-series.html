              Absolute Convergence
              绝对收敛              		 "if the series \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty }\vert a_{n}\vert\) converges, the series \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty }a_{n}\) is said to converge absolutely" 若级数 \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty }\vert a_{n}\vert\) 收敛，就说级数 \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty }a_{n}\) 绝对收敛 Strang & Herman, 2016b, chapter 5 Key Terms
              Alternating Series
              交错级数              		 "a series of the form \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty }(-1)^{n+1}b_{n}\) or \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty }(-1)^{n}b_{n},\) where \(b_{n}\ge 0,\) is called an alternating series" 形如 \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty }(-1)^{n+1}b_{n}\) 或 \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty }(-1)^{n}b_{n},\) 的级数，其中 \(b_{n}\ge 0,\) 称为交错级数 Strang & Herman, 2016b, chapter 5 Key Terms
              Alternating Series Test
              交错级数检验              		 "for an alternating series of either form, if \(b_{n+1}\le b_{n}\) for all integers \(n\ge 1\) and \(b_{n}\to 0,\) then an alternating series converges" 对两种形式之一的交错级数，若对所有整数 \(n\ge 1\) 有 \(b_{n+1}\le b_{n}\) 且 \(b_{n}\to 0,\) 则交错级数收敛 Strang & Herman, 2016b, chapter 5 Key Terms
              Conditional Convergence
              条件收敛              		 "if the series \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty }a_{n}\) converges, but the series \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty }\vert a_{n}\vert\) diverges, the series \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty }a_{n}\) is said to converge conditionally" 若级数 \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty }a_{n}\) 收敛，但级数 \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty }\vert a_{n}\vert\) 发散，就说级数 \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty }a_{n}\) 条件收敛 Strang & Herman, 2016b, chapter 5 Key Terms
