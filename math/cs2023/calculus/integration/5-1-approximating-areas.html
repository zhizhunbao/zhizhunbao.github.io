              5.1 Approximating Areas
              面积的近似              		 (No definition) （无定义） Strang & Herman, 2016a, section 5.1
              Left-endpoint Approximation
              左端点近似              		 "an approximation of the area under a curve computed by using the left endpoint of each subinterval to calculate the height of the vertical sides of each rectangle" 曲线下面积的一种近似，用每个子区间的左端点计算每个矩形竖边的高 Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 5 Key Terms
              Lower Sum
              下和              		 "a sum obtained by using the minimum value of \(f(x)\) on each subinterval" 在每个子区间上用 \(f(x)\) 的最小值得到的和 Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 5 Key Terms
              Partition
              分划              		 "a set of points that divides an interval into subintervals" 把区间分成子区间的一组点 Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 5 Key Terms
              Regular Partition
              等距分划              		 "a partition in which the subintervals all have the same width" 各子区间都有相同宽度的分划 Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 5 Key Terms
              Riemann Sum
              黎曼和              		 "an estimate of the area under the curve of the form \(A\approx \sum_{i=1}^{n}f(x_{i}^{*})\Delta x\)" 曲线下面积的一种估计，形如 \(A\approx \sum_{i=1}^{n}f(x_{i}^{*})\Delta x\) Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 5 Key Terms
              Right-endpoint Approximation
              右端点近似              		 "the right-endpoint approximation is an approximation of the area of the rectangles under a curve using the right endpoint of each subinterval to construct the vertical sides of each rectangle" 右端点近似是曲线下矩形面积的一种近似，用每个子区间的右端点构造每个矩形的竖边 Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 5 Key Terms
              Sigma Notation
              Σ 记号              		 "(also, summation notation) the Greek letter sigma (Σ) indicates addition of the values; the values of the index above and below the sigma indicate where to begin the summation and where to end it" （也称求和记号）希腊字母 sigma（Σ）表示把各值相加；sigma 上下方的指标值表示从哪里开始求和、到哪里结束 Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 5 Key Terms
              Upper Sum
              上和              		 "a sum obtained by using the maximum value of \(f(x)\) on each subinterval" 在每个子区间上用 \(f(x)\) 的最大值得到的和 Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 5 Key Terms
              Method of Exhaustion
              穷竭法              		 "He used a process that has come to be known as the method of exhaustion, which used smaller and smaller shapes, the areas of which could be calculated exactly, to fill an irregular region and thereby obtain closer and closer approximations to the total area." 他使用了一种后来被称为穷竭法的方法，用越来越小、面积可以精确计算的图形填满一个不规则区域，从而得到越来越接近总面积的近似。 Strang & Herman, 2016a, section 5.1
              Summation Notation
              求和记号              		 "To make it easier to write down these lengthy sums, we look at some new notation here, called sigma notation (also known as summation notation)." 为了更容易写出这些冗长的和，我们在这里看一种新记号，称为 sigma 记号（也称求和记号）。 Strang & Herman, 2016a, section 5.1
              Dummy Variable
              哑变量              		 "The index is therefore called a dummy variable." 因此这个指标称为哑变量。 Strang & Herman, 2016a, section 5.1
