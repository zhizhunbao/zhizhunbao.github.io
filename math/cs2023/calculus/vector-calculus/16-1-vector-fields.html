              16.1 Vector Fields
              向量场              		 (No definition) （无定义） Strang & Herman, 2016c, section 6.1
              Conservative Field
              保守场              		 "a vector field for which there exists a scalar function \(f\) such that \(\nabla f=\text{F}\)" 存在标量函数 \(f\) 使得 \(\nabla f=\text{F}\) 的向量场 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Gradient Field
              梯度场              		 "a vector field \(\text{F}\) for which there exists a scalar function \(f\) such that \(\nabla f=\text{F};\) in other words, a vector field that is the gradient of a function; such vector fields are also called conservative" 存在标量函数 \(f\) 使得 \(\nabla f=\text{F};\) 的向量场 \(\text{F}\)；换句话说，是某个函数的梯度的向量场；这样的向量场也称为保守场 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Potential Function
              势函数              		 "a scalar function \(f\) such that \(\nabla f=\text{F}\)" 使得 \(\nabla f=\text{F}\) 的标量函数 \(f\) Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Radial Field
              径向场              		 "a vector field in which all vectors either point directly toward or directly away from the origin; the magnitude of any vector depends only on its distance from the origin" 所有向量都直接指向原点或直接背离原点的向量场；任一向量的大小只取决于它到原点的距离 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Rotational Field
              旋转场              		 "a vector field in which the vector at point \((x,y)\) is tangent to a circle with radius \(r=\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}};\) in a rotational field, all vectors flow either clockwise or counterclockwise, and the magnitude of a vector depends only on its distance from the origin" 点 \((x,y)\) 处的向量与半径为 \(r=\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}};\) 的圆相切的向量场；在旋转场中，所有向量都沿顺时针或逆时针流动，向量的大小只取决于它到原点的距离 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Unit Vector Field
              单位向量场              		 "a vector field in which the magnitude of every vector is 1" 每个向量的大小都是 1 的向量场 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Vector Field
              向量场              		 "measured in \(\mathbb{R}^{2},\) an assignment of a vector \(\text{F}(x,y)\) to each point \((x,y)\) of a subset \(D\) of \(\mathbb{R}^{2};\) in \(\mathbb{R}^{3},\) an assignment of a vector \(\text{F}(x,y,z)\) to each point \((x,y,z)\) of a subset \(D\) of \(\mathbb{R}^{3}\)" 在 \(\mathbb{R}^{2},\) 中，对 \(\mathbb{R}^{2};\) 的子集 \(D\) 中每一点 \((x,y)\) 指定一个向量 \(\text{F}(x,y)\)；在 \(\mathbb{R}^{3},\) 中，对 \(\mathbb{R}^{3}\) 的子集 \(D\) 中每一点 \((x,y,z)\) 指定一个向量 \(\text{F}(x,y,z)\) Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Cross-partial Property
              交叉偏导性质              		 "Conservative vector fields also have a special property called the cross-partial property." 保守向量场还有一个特殊性质，称为交叉偏导性质。 Strang & Herman, 2016c, section 6.1
              Clairaut’s Theorem
              克莱罗定理              		 "Clairaut’s theorem gives a fast proof of the cross-partial property of conservative vector fields in \(\mathbb{R}^{3},\) just as it did for vector fields in \(\mathbb{R}^{2}.\)" 克莱罗定理对 \(\mathbb{R}^{3},\) 中的保守向量场给出了交叉偏导性质的快速证明，正如它对 \(\mathbb{R}^{2}.\) 中的向量场所做的那样 Strang & Herman, 2016c, section 6.1
