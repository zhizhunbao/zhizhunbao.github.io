              16.2 Line Integrals
              线积分              		 (No definition) （无定义） Strang & Herman, 2016c, section 6.2
              Circulation
              环量              		 "the tendency of a fluid to move in the direction of curve C. If C is a closed curve, then the circulation of F along C is line integral \({\int }_{C}\text{F}\cdot \text{T}ds,\) which we also denote \({\int }_{C}\text{F}\cdot \text{T}ds\)" 流体沿曲线 C 方向运动的趋势。若 C 是闭曲线，则 F 沿 C 的环量是线积分 \({\int }_{C}\text{F}\cdot \text{T}ds,\) 我们也记作 \({\int }_{C}\text{F}\cdot \text{T}ds\) Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Closed Curve
              闭曲线              		 "a curve for which there exists a parameterization \(\text{r}(t),\) \(a\le t\le b,\) such that \(\text{r}(a)=\text{r}(b),\) and the curve is traversed exactly once" 存在参数化 \(\text{r}(t),\) \(a\le t\le b,\) 使得 \(\text{r}(a)=\text{r}(b),\) 并且曲线恰好被走过一次的曲线 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Flux
              通量              		 "the rate of a fluid flowing across a curve in a vector field; the flux of vector field F across plane curve C is line integral \({\int }_{C}\text{F}\cdot \frac{\text{n}(t)}{\Vert \text{n}(t)\Vert }\,ds\)" 向量场中流体穿过曲线的流率；向量场 F 穿过平面曲线 C 的通量是线积分 \({\int }_{C}\text{F}\cdot \frac{\text{n}(t)}{\Vert \text{n}(t)\Vert }\,ds\) Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Line Integral
              线积分              		 "the integral of a function along a curve in a plane or in space" 函数沿平面或空间中曲线的积分 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Orientation of a Curve
              曲线的方向              		 "the orientation of a curve C is a specified direction of C" 曲线 C 的方向是 C 的一个指定方向 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Piecewise Smooth Curve
              分段光滑曲线              		 "an oriented curve that is not smooth, but can be written as the union of finitely many smooth curves" 不光滑、但可以写成有限条光滑曲线之并的有向曲线 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Scalar Line Integral
              标量线积分              		 "the scalar line integral of a function \(f\) along a curve C with respect to arc length is the integral \({\int }_{C}fds,\) it is the integral of a scalar function \(f\) along a curve in a plane or in space; such an integral is defined in terms of a Riemann sum, as is a single-variable integral" 函数 \(f\) 沿曲线 C 对弧长的标量线积分是积分 \({\int }_{C}fds,\) 它是标量函数 \(f\) 沿平面或空间中曲线的积分；这样的积分与一元积分一样，用黎曼和定义 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Vector Line Integral
              向量线积分              		 "the vector line integral of vector field F along curve C is the integral of the dot product of F with unit tangent vector T of C with respect to arc length, \({\int }_{C}\text{F}\cdot \text{T}ds;\) such an integral is defined in terms of a Riemann sum, similar to a single-variable integral" 向量场 F 沿曲线 C 的向量线积分是 F 与 C 的单位切向量 T 的点积对弧长的积分，\({\int }_{C}\text{F}\cdot \text{T}ds;\) 这样的积分与一元积分类似，用黎曼和定义 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Mass of a Wire
              导线的质量              		 "They can be used to calculate the length or mass of a wire, the surface area of a sheet of a given height, or the electric potential of a charged wire given a linear charge density." 它们可以用来计算导线的长度或质量、给定高度的薄片的表面积，或给定线电荷密度时带电导线的电势。 Strang & Herman, 2016c, section 6.2
              Work Done by a Vector Field
              向量场所做的功              		 "Therefore, it is not surprising that calculating the work done by a vector field representing a force is a standard use of vector line integrals." 因此，计算表示力的向量场所做的功是向量线积分的标准用法，这并不奇怪。 Strang & Herman, 2016c, section 6.2
