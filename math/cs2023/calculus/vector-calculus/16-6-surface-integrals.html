              16.6 Surface Integrals
              曲面积分              		 (No definition) （无定义） Strang & Herman, 2016c, section 6.6
              Flux Integral
              通量积分              		 "another name for a surface integral of a vector field; the preferred term in physics and engineering" 向量场曲面积分的另一个名称；物理和工程中的首选术语 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Grid Curves
              网格曲线              		 "curves on a surface that are parallel to grid lines in a coordinate plane" 曲面上平行于坐标平面中网格线的曲线 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Heat Flow
              热流              		 "a vector field proportional to the negative temperature gradient in an object" 物体中与负温度梯度成正比的向量场 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Mass Flux
              质量通量              		 "the rate of mass flow of a fluid per unit area, measured in mass per unit time per unit area" 单位面积上流体的质量流率，以单位时间单位面积的质量度量 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Orientation of a Surface
              曲面的方向              		 "if a surface has an “inner” side and an “outer” side, then an orientation is a choice of the inner or the outer side; the surface could also have “upward” and “downward” orientations" 若曲面有“内”侧和“外”侧，则方向是对内侧或外侧的一种选择；曲面也可以有“向上”和“向下”的方向 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Parameter Domain (Parameter Space)
              参数域（参数空间）              		 "the region of the uv plane over which the parameters u and v vary for parameterization \(\text{r}(u,v)=\langle x(u,v),y(u,v),z(u,v)\rangle\)" 参数化 \(\text{r}(u,v)=\langle x(u,v),y(u,v),z(u,v)\rangle\) 中参数 u 和 v 在其上变化的 uv 平面区域 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Parameterized Surface (Parametric Surface)
              参数化曲面（参数曲面）              		 "a surface given by a description of the form \(\text{r}(u,v)=\langle x(u,v),y(u,v),z(u,v)\rangle ,\) where the parameters u and v vary over a parameter domain in the uv-plane" 由形如 \(\text{r}(u,v)=\langle x(u,v),y(u,v),z(u,v)\rangle ,\) 的描述给出的曲面，其中参数 u 和 v 在 uv 平面的参数域上变化 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Regular Parameterization
              正则参数化              		 "parameterization \(\text{r}(u,v)=\langle x(u,v),y(u,v),z(u,v)\rangle\) such that \({\text{r}}_{u}\,\times \,{\text{r}}_{v}\) is not zero for any point \((u,v)\) in the parameter domain" 参数化 \(\text{r}(u,v)=\langle x(u,v),y(u,v),z(u,v)\rangle\)，使得 \({\text{r}}_{u}\,\times \,{\text{r}}_{v}\) 在参数域中任一点 \((u,v)\) 处都不为零 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Surface Area
              曲面面积              		 "the area of surface S given by the surface integral \(\int {\int }_{S}dS\)" 曲面 S 的面积，由曲面积分 \(\int {\int }_{S}dS\) 给出 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Surface Integral
              曲面积分              		 "an integral of a function over a surface" 函数在曲面上的积分 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Surface Integral of a Scalar-valued Function
              标量值函数的曲面积分              		 "a surface integral in which the integrand is a scalar function" 被积函数是标量函数的曲面积分 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
              Surface Integral of a Vector Field
              向量场的曲面积分              		 "a surface integral in which the integrand is a vector field" 被积函数是向量场的曲面积分 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 6 Key Terms
