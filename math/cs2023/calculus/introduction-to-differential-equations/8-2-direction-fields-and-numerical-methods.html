              Asymptotically Semi-stable Solution
              渐近半稳定解              		 "\(y=k\) if it is neither asymptotically stable nor asymptotically unstable" \(y=k\)，如果它既不是渐近稳定的，也不是渐近不稳定的 Strang & Herman, 2016b, chapter 4 Key Terms
              Asymptotically Stable Solution
              渐近稳定解              		 "\(y=k\) if there exists \(\varepsilon >0\) such that for any value \(c\in (k-\varepsilon ,k+\varepsilon )\) the solution to the initial-value problem \(y^{'}=f(x,y),\,y(x_{0})=c\) approaches \(k\) as \(x\) approaches infinity" \(y=k\)，如果存在 \(\varepsilon >0\)，使得对任意值 \(c\in (k-\varepsilon ,k+\varepsilon )\)，初值问题 \(y^{'}=f(x,y),\,y(x_{0})=c\) 的解在 \(x\) 趋于无穷时趋近 \(k\) Strang & Herman, 2016b, chapter 4 Key Terms
              Asymptotically Unstable Solution
              渐近不稳定解              		 "\(y=k\) if there exists \(\varepsilon >0\) such that for any value \(c\in (k-\varepsilon ,k+\varepsilon )\) the solution to the initial-value problem \(y^{'}=f(x,y),\,y(x_{0})=c\) never approaches \(k\) as \(x\) approaches infinity" \(y=k\)，如果存在 \(\varepsilon >0\)，使得对任意值 \(c\in (k-\varepsilon ,k+\varepsilon )\)，初值问题 \(y^{'}=f(x,y),\,y(x_{0})=c\) 的解在 \(x\) 趋于无穷时永远不趋近 \(k\) Strang & Herman, 2016b, chapter 4 Key Terms
              Direction Field (Slope Field)
              方向场（斜率场）              		 "a mathematical object used to graphically represent solutions to a first-order differential equation; at each point in a direction field, a line segment appears whose slope is equal to the slope of a solution to the differential equation passing through that point" 一种数学对象，用来以图形表示一阶微分方程的解；在方向场的每一点出现一条线段，其斜率等于经过该点的微分方程解的斜率 Strang & Herman, 2016b, chapter 4 Key Terms
              Equilibrium Solution
              平衡解              		 "any solution to the differential equation of the form \(y=c,\) where \(c\) is a constant" 微分方程形如 \(y=c,\) 的任一解，其中 \(c\) 是常数 Strang & Herman, 2016b, chapter 4 Key Terms
              Euler’s Method
              欧拉法              		 "a numerical technique used to approximate solutions to an initial-value problem" 一种数值方法，用来近似初值问题的解 Strang & Herman, 2016b, chapter 4 Key Terms
              Solution Curve
              解曲线              		 "a curve graphed in a direction field that corresponds to the solution to the initial-value problem passing through a given point in the direction field" 画在方向场中的一条曲线，对应于经过方向场中给定点的初值问题的解 Strang & Herman, 2016b, chapter 4 Key Terms
              Step Size
              步长              		 "the increment \(h\) that is added to the \(x\) value at each step in Euler’s Method" 欧拉法每一步中加到 \(x\) 值上的增量 \(h\) Strang & Herman, 2016b, chapter 4 Key Terms
