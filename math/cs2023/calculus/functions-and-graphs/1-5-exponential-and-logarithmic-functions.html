              1.5 Exponential and Logarithmic Functions
              指数函数与对数函数              		 (No definition) （无定义） Strang & Herman, 2016a, section 1.5
              Base
              底              		 "the number \(b\) in the exponential function \(f(x)=b^{x}\) and the logarithmic function \(f(x)=\log_{b}x\)" 指数函数 \(f(x)=b^{x}\) 和对数函数 \(f(x)=\log_{b}x\) 中的数 \(b\) Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 1 Key Terms
              Exponent
              指数              		 "the value \(x\) in the expression \(b^{x}\)" 表达式 \(b^{x}\) 中的值 \(x\) Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 1 Key Terms
              Hyperbolic Functions
              双曲函数              		 "the functions denoted \(\sinh ,\cosh ,\tanh ,\operatorname{csch},\operatorname{sech},\) and \(\coth ,\) which involve certain combinations of \(e^{x}\) and \(e^{-x}\)" 记作 \(\sinh ,\cosh ,\tanh ,\operatorname{csch},\operatorname{sech},\) 和 \(\coth ,\) 的函数，它们涉及 \(e^{x}\) 与 \(e^{-x}\) 的某些组合 Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 1 Key Terms
              Inverse Hyperbolic Functions
              反双曲函数              		 "the inverses of the hyperbolic functions where \(\cosh\) and \(\operatorname{sech}\) are restricted to the domain \([0,\infty );\) each of these functions can be expressed in terms of a composition of the natural logarithm function and an algebraic function" 双曲函数的反函数，其中 \(\cosh\) 和 \(\operatorname{sech}\) 限制在定义域 \([0,\infty );\) 上；每个这样的函数都可以表示为自然对数函数与一个代数函数的复合 Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 1 Key Terms
              Natural Exponential Function
              自然指数函数              		 "the function \(f(x)=e^{x}\)" 函数 \(f(x)=e^{x}\) Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 1 Key Terms
              Natural Logarithm
              自然对数              		 "the function \(\ln \,x=\log_{e}x\)" 函数 \(\ln \,x=\log_{e}x\) Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 1 Key Terms
              Number e
              数 e              		 "as \(m\) gets larger, the quantity \((1+(1/m))^{m}\) gets closer to some real number; we define that real number to be \(e;\) the value of \(e\) is approximately \(2.718282\)" 随着 \(m\) 变大，量 \((1+(1/m))^{m}\) 越来越接近某个实数；我们把这个实数定义为 \(e;\) \(e\) 的值约为 \(2.718282\) Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 1 Key Terms
              Common Logarithm
              常用对数              		 "When evaluating a logarithmic function with a calculator, you may have noticed that the only options are \(\log_{10}\) or log, called the common logarithm, or ln, which is the natural logarithm." 用计算器计算对数函数时，你可能注意到只有 \(\log_{10}\) 或 log（称为常用对数），或 ln（即自然对数）这几个选项。 Strang & Herman, 2016a, section 1.5

              References
