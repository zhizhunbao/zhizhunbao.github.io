              Differentiable
              可微              		 "a function \(f(x,y,z)\) is differentiable at \((x_{0},y_{0})\) if \(f(x,y)\) can be expressed in the form \(f(x,y)=f(x_{0},y_{0})+f_{x}(x_{0},y_{0})(x-x_{0})+f_{y}(x_{0},y_{0})(y-y_{0})+E(x,y),\) where the error term \(E(x,y)\) satisfies \(\lim_{(x,y)\to (x_{0},y_{0})}\frac{E(x,y)}{\sqrt{(x-x_{0})^{2}+(y-y_{0})^{2}}}=0\)" 函数 \(f(x,y,z)\) 在 \((x_{0},y_{0})\) 处可微，如果 \(f(x,y)\) 可以表示成形式 \(f(x,y)=f(x_{0},y_{0})+f_{x}(x_{0},y_{0})(x-x_{0})+f_{y}(x_{0},y_{0})(y-y_{0})+E(x,y),\) 其中误差项 \(E(x,y)\) 满足 \(\lim_{(x,y)\to (x_{0},y_{0})}\frac{E(x,y)}{\sqrt{(x-x_{0})^{2}+(y-y_{0})^{2}}}=0\) Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 4 Key Terms
              Linear Approximation
              线性近似              		 "given a function \(f(x,y)\) and a tangent plane to the function at a point \((x_{0},y_{0}),\) we can approximate \(f(x,y)\) for points near \((x_{0},y_{0})\) using the tangent plane formula" 给定函数 \(f(x,y)\) 及其在点 \((x_{0},y_{0}),\) 处的切平面，可以用切平面公式对 \((x_{0},y_{0})\) 附近的点近似 \(f(x,y)\) Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 4 Key Terms
              Tangent Plane
              切平面              		 "given a function \(f(x,y)\) that is differentiable at a point \((x_{0},y_{0}),\) the equation of the tangent plane to the surface \(z=f(x,y)\) is given by \(z=f(x_{0},y_{0})+f_{x}(x_{0},y_{0})(x-x_{0})+f_{y}(x_{0},y_{0})(y-y_{0})\)" 给定在点 \((x_{0},y_{0}),\) 处可微的函数 \(f(x,y)\)，曲面 \(z=f(x,y)\) 的切平面方程由 \(z=f(x_{0},y_{0})+f_{x}(x_{0},y_{0})(x-x_{0})+f_{y}(x_{0},y_{0})(y-y_{0})\) 给出 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 4 Key Terms
              Total Differential
              全微分              		 "the total differential of the function \(f(x,y)\) at \((x_{0},y_{0})\) is given by the formula \(dz=f_{x}(x_{0},y_{0})dx+f_{y}(x_{0},y_{0})dy\)" 函数 \(f(x,y)\) 在 \((x_{0},y_{0})\) 处的全微分由公式 \(dz=f_{x}(x_{0},y_{0})dx+f_{y}(x_{0},y_{0})dy\) 给出 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 4 Key Terms
              Error Term
              误差项              		 "The last term in Equation 4.26 is referred to as the error term and it represents how closely the tangent plane comes to the surface in a small neighborhood \((\delta\) disk) of point \(P.\)" 方程 4.26 的最后一项称为误差项，它表示切平面在点 \(P.\) 的小邻域 \((\delta\) 圆盘）内与曲面有多接近 Strang & Herman, 2016c, section 4.4
