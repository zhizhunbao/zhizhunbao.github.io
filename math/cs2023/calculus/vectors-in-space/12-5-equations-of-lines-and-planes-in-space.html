              12.5 Equations of Lines and Planes in Space
              空间直线与平面的方程              		 (No definition) （无定义） Strang & Herman, 2016c, section 2.5
              Direction Vector
              方向向量              		 "a vector parallel to a line that is used to describe the direction, or orientation, of the line in space" 平行于直线的向量，用来描述直线在空间中的方向或取向 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              General Form of the Equation of a Plane
              平面方程的一般式              		 "an equation in the form \(ax+by+cz+d=0,\) where \(\text{n}=\langle a,b,c\rangle\) is a normal vector of the plane, \(P=(x_{0},y_{0},z_{0})\) is a point on the plane, and \(d=-ax_{0}-by_{0}-cz_{0}\)" 形如 \(ax+by+cz+d=0,\) 的方程，其中 \(\text{n}=\langle a,b,c\rangle\) 是平面的法向量，\(P=(x_{0},y_{0},z_{0})\) 是平面上一点，且 \(d=-ax_{0}-by_{0}-cz_{0}\) Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Normal Vector
              法向量              		 "a vector perpendicular to a plane" 垂直于平面的向量 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Parametric Equations of a Line
              直线的参数方程              		 "the set of equations \(x=x_{0}+ta,\) \(y=y_{0}+tb,\) and \(z=z_{0}+tc\) describing the line with direction vector \(\text{v}=\langle a,b,c\rangle\) passing through point \((x_{0},y_{0},z_{0})\)" 方程组 \(x=x_{0}+ta,\) \(y=y_{0}+tb,\) 和 \(z=z_{0}+tc\)，描述方向向量为 \(\text{v}=\langle a,b,c\rangle\)、经过点 \((x_{0},y_{0},z_{0})\) 的直线 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Scalar Equation of a Plane
              平面的数量方程              		 "the equation \(a(x-x_{0})+b(y-y_{0})+c(z-z_{0})=0\) used to describe a plane containing point \(P=(x_{0},y_{0},z_{0})\) with normal vector \(\text{n}=\langle a,b,c\rangle\) or its alternate form \(ax+by+cz+d=0,\) where \(d=-ax_{0}-by_{0}-cz_{0}\)" 方程 \(a(x-x_{0})+b(y-y_{0})+c(z-z_{0})=0\)，用来描述含点 \(P=(x_{0},y_{0},z_{0})\)、法向量为 \(\text{n}=\langle a,b,c\rangle\) 的平面，或其另一种形式 \(ax+by+cz+d=0,\) 其中 \(d=-ax_{0}-by_{0}-cz_{0}\) Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Skew Lines
              异面直线              		 "two lines that are not parallel but do not intersect" 不平行但也不相交的两条直线 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Symmetric Equations of a Line
              直线的对称式方程              		 "the equations \(\frac{x-x_{0}}{a}=\frac{y-y_{0}}{b}=\frac{z-z_{0}}{c}\) describing the line with direction vector \(\text{v}=\langle a,b,c\rangle\) passing through point \((x_{0},y_{0},z_{0})\)" 方程 \(\frac{x-x_{0}}{a}=\frac{y-y_{0}}{b}=\frac{z-z_{0}}{c}\)，描述方向向量为 \(\text{v}=\langle a,b,c\rangle\)、经过点 \((x_{0},y_{0},z_{0})\) 的直线 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Vector Equation of a Line
              直线的向量方程              		 "the equation \(\text{r}={\text{r}}_{0}+t\text{v}\) used to describe a line with direction vector \(\text{v}=\langle a,b,c\rangle\) passing through point \(P=(x_{0},y_{0},z_{0}),\) where \({\text{r}}_{0}=\langle x_{0},y_{0},z_{0}\rangle ,\) is the position vector of point \(P\)" 方程 \(\text{r}={\text{r}}_{0}+t\text{v}\)，用来描述方向向量为 \(\text{v}=\langle a,b,c\rangle\)、经过点 \(P=(x_{0},y_{0},z_{0}),\) 的直线，其中 \({\text{r}}_{0}=\langle x_{0},y_{0},z_{0}\rangle ,\) 是点 \(P\) 的位置向量 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Vector Equation of a Plane
              平面的向量方程              		 "the equation \(\text{n}\cdot \vec{PQ}=0,\) where \(P\) is a given point in the plane, \(Q\) is any point in the plane, and \(\text{n}\) is a normal vector of the plane" 方程 \(\text{n}\cdot \vec{PQ}=0,\) 其中 \(P\) 是平面上给定的一点，\(Q\) 是平面上任一点，\(\text{n}\) 是平面的法向量 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms

              References
