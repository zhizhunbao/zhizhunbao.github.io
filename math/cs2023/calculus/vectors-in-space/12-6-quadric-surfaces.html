              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              12 Vectors in Space

              12.6 Quadric Surfaces
              二次曲面              		 (No definition) （无定义） Strang & Herman, 2016c, section 2.6
              Cylinder
              柱面              		 "a set of lines parallel to a given line passing through a given curve" 平行于给定直线并经过给定曲线的一族直线 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Ellipsoid
              椭球面              		 "a three-dimensional surface described by an equation of the form \(\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}+\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}=1;\) all traces of this surface are ellipses" 由形如 \(\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}+\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}=1;\) 的方程描述的三维曲面；这个曲面的所有截痕都是椭圆 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Elliptic Cone
              椭圆锥面              		 "a three-dimensional surface described by an equation of the form \(\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}-\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}=0;\) traces of this surface include ellipses and intersecting lines" 由形如 \(\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}-\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}=0;\) 的方程描述的三维曲面；这个曲面的截痕包括椭圆和相交直线 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Elliptic Paraboloid
              椭圆抛物面              		 "a three-dimensional surface described by an equation of the form \(z=\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}};\) traces of this surface include ellipses and parabolas" 由形如 \(z=\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}};\) 的方程描述的三维曲面；这个曲面的截痕包括椭圆和抛物线 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Hyperboloid of One Sheet
              单叶双曲面              		 "a three-dimensional surface described by an equation of the form \(\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}-\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}=1;\) traces of this surface include ellipses and hyperbolas" 由形如 \(\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}-\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}=1;\) 的方程描述的三维曲面；这个曲面的截痕包括椭圆和双曲线 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Hyperboloid of Two Sheets
              双叶双曲面              		 "a three-dimensional surface described by an equation of the form \(\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}-\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1;\) traces of this surface include ellipses and hyperbolas" 由形如 \(\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}-\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1;\) 的方程描述的三维曲面；这个曲面的截痕包括椭圆和双曲线 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Quadric Surfaces
              二次曲面              		 "surfaces in three dimensions having the property that the traces of the surface are conic sections (ellipses, hyperbolas, and parabolas)" 三维空间中的曲面，其性质是曲面的截痕都是圆锥曲线（椭圆、双曲线和抛物线） Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Rulings
              母线              		 "parallel lines that make up a cylindrical surface" 构成柱面的平行直线 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Trace
              截痕              		 "the intersection of a three-dimensional surface with a coordinate plane" 三维曲面与坐标平面的交 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
