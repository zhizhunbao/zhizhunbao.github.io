              Cross Product
              叉积              		 "\(\text{u}\,\times \,\text{v}=(u_{2}v_{3}-u_{3}v_{2})\text{i}-(u_{1}v_{3}-u_{3}v_{1})\text{j}+(u_{1}v_{2}-u_{2}v_{1})\text{k},\) where \(\text{u}=\langle u_{1},u_{2},u_{3}\rangle\) and \(\text{v}=\langle v_{1},v_{2},v_{3}\rangle\)" \(\text{u}\,\times \,\text{v}=(u_{2}v_{3}-u_{3}v_{2})\text{i}-(u_{1}v_{3}-u_{3}v_{1})\text{j}+(u_{1}v_{2}-u_{2}v_{1})\text{k},\) 其中 \(\text{u}=\langle u_{1},u_{2},u_{3}\rangle\) 且 \(\text{v}=\langle v_{1},v_{2},v_{3}\rangle\) Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Determinant
              行列式              		 "a real number associated with a square matrix" 与方阵相联系的实数 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Parallelepiped
              平行六面体              		 "a three-dimensional prism with six faces that are parallelograms" 六个面都是平行四边形的三维棱柱 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Torque
              力矩              		 "the effect of a force that causes an object to rotate" 力使物体转动的效应 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Triple Scalar Product
              三重数量积              		 "the dot product of a vector with the cross product of two other vectors: \(\text{u}\cdot (\text{v}\,\times \,\text{w})\)" 一个向量与另外两个向量的叉积的点积：\(\text{u}\cdot (\text{v}\,\times \,\text{w})\) Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
              Vector Product
              向量积              		 "the cross product of two vectors" 两个向量的叉积 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 2 Key Terms
