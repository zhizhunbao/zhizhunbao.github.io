              Calculus
              微积分              		 "'The' calculus, more properly called analysis (or real analysis or, in older literature, infinitesimal analysis), is the branch of mathematics studying the rate of change of quantities (which can be interpreted as slopes of curves) and the length, area, and volume of objects." 这门微积分更恰当的名称是分析（或实分析，旧文献中称无穷小分析），是研究量的变化率（可解释为曲线的斜率）以及物体的长度、面积和体积的数学分支。 Weisstein, n.d., "Calculus" entry, para. 2
