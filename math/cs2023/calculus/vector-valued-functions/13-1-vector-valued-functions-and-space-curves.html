              13.1 Vector-Valued Functions and Space Curves
              向量值函数与空间曲线              		 (No definition) （无定义） Strang & Herman, 2016c, section 3.1
              Component Functions
              分量函数              		 "the component functions of the vector-valued function \(\text{r}(t)=f(t)\,\text{i}+g(t)\,\text{j}\) are \(f(t)\) and \(g(t),\) and the component functions of the vector-valued function \(\text{r}(t)=f(t)\,\text{i}+g(t)\,\text{j}+h(t)\,\text{k}\) are \(f(t),\) \(g(t)\) and \(h(t)\)" 向量值函数 \(\text{r}(t)=f(t)\,\text{i}+g(t)\,\text{j}\) 的分量函数是 \(f(t)\) 和 \(g(t),\) 向量值函数 \(\text{r}(t)=f(t)\,\text{i}+g(t)\,\text{j}+h(t)\,\text{k}\) 的分量函数是 \(f(t),\) \(g(t)\) 和 \(h(t)\) Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Helix
              螺旋线              		 "a three-dimensional curve in the shape of a spiral" 螺旋形状的三维曲线 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Limit of a Vector-valued Function
              向量值函数的极限              		 "a vector-valued function \(\text{r}(t)\) has a limit L as t approaches a if \(\lim_{t\to a}\vert \,\text{r}(t)-\text{L}\vert =0\)" 向量值函数 \(\text{r}(t)\) 在 t 趋近 a 时有极限 L，如果 \(\lim_{t\to a}\vert \,\text{r}(t)-\text{L}\vert =0\) Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Plane Curve
              平面曲线              		 "the set of ordered pairs \((f(t),g(t))\) together with their defining parametric equations \(x=f(t)\) and \(y=g(t)\)" 有序对 \((f(t),g(t))\) 的集合，连同定义它们的参数方程 \(x=f(t)\) 和 \(y=g(t)\) Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Reparameterization
              重新参数化              		 "an alternative parameterization of a given vector-valued function" 给定向量值函数的另一种参数化 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Space Curve
              空间曲线              		 "the set of ordered triples \((f(t),g(t),h(t))\) together with their defining parametric equations \(x=f(t),\) \(y=g(t)\) and \(z=h(t)\)" 有序三元组 \((f(t),g(t),h(t))\) 的集合，连同定义它们的参数方程 \(x=f(t),\) \(y=g(t)\) 和 \(z=h(t)\) Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Vector Parameterization
              向量参数化              		 "any representation of a plane or space curve using a vector-valued function" 用向量值函数对平面曲线或空间曲线的任一表示 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Vector-valued Function
              向量值函数              		 "a function of the form \(\text{r}(t)=f(t)\,\text{i}+g(t)\,\text{j}\) or \(\text{r}(t)=f(t)\,\text{i}+g(t)\,\text{j}+h(t)\,\text{k},\) where the component functions f, g, and h are real-valued functions of the parameter t" 形如 \(\text{r}(t)=f(t)\,\text{i}+g(t)\,\text{j}\) 或 \(\text{r}(t)=f(t)\,\text{i}+g(t)\,\text{j}+h(t)\,\text{k},\) 的函数，其中分量函数 f、g 和 h 是参数 t 的实值函数 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Standard Position
              标准位置              		 "A vector is considered to be in standard position if the initial point is located at the origin." 若向量的起点位于原点，就认为它处于标准位置。 Strang & Herman, 2016c, section 3.1
