              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              13 Vector-Valued Functions
              向量值函数              		 (No definition) （无定义） Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3
              13.3 Arc Length and Curvature
              弧长与曲率              		 (No definition) （无定义） Strang & Herman, 2016c, section 3.3
              Arc-length Function
              弧长函数              		 "a function \(s(t)\) that describes the arc length of curve C as a function of t" 把曲线 C 的弧长描述为 t 的函数的函数 \(s(t)\) Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Arc-length Parameterization
              弧长参数化              		 "a reparameterization of a vector-valued function in which the parameter is equal to the arc length" 向量值函数的一种重新参数化，其中参数等于弧长 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Binormal Vector
              副法向量              		 "a unit vector orthogonal to the unit tangent vector and the unit normal vector" 与单位切向量和单位法向量都正交的单位向量 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Curvature
              曲率              		 "the derivative of the unit tangent vector with respect to the arc-length parameter" 单位切向量对弧长参数的导数 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Frenet Frame of Reference
              弗勒内标架              		 "(TNB frame) a frame of reference in three-dimensional space formed by the unit tangent vector, the unit normal vector, and the binormal vector" （TNB 标架）由单位切向量、单位法向量和副法向量构成的三维空间参考系 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Normal Plane
              法平面              		 "a plane that is perpendicular to a curve at any point on the curve" 在曲线上任一点处垂直于曲线的平面 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Osculating Circle
              密切圆              		 "a circle that is tangent to a curve C at a point P and that shares the same curvature" 在点 P 处与曲线 C 相切并具有相同曲率的圆 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Osculating Plane
              密切平面              		 "the plane determined by the unit tangent and the unit normal vector" 由单位切向量和单位法向量确定的平面 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Principal Unit Normal Vector
              主单位法向量              		 "a vector orthogonal to the unit tangent vector, given by the formula \(\frac{T^{'}(t)}{\Vert T^{'}(t)\Vert }\)" 与单位切向量正交的向量，由公式 \(\frac{T^{'}(t)}{\Vert T^{'}(t)\Vert }\) 给出 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Radius of Curvature
              曲率半径              		 "the reciprocal of the curvature" 曲率的倒数 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms
              Smooth
              光滑              		 "curves where the vector-valued function \(\text{r}(t)\) is differentiable with a non-zero derivative" 向量值函数 \(\text{r}(t)\) 可导且导数非零的曲线 Strang & Herman, 2016c, chapter 3 Key Terms

              References
