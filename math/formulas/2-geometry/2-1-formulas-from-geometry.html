              2.1 Formulas from Geometry
              几何公式              		 Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C
              \(A = bh\)
              平行四边形的面积              		 "Parallelograms which are on the same base and in the same parallels equal one another." 在同一底上且在相同两条平行线之间的平行四边形彼此相等。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Formulas from Geometry"
              Definition: Euclid, Book I, Proposition 35
              \(A = \frac{1}{2}bh\)
              三角形的面积              		 "If a parallelogram has the same base with a triangle and is in the same parallels, then the parallelogram is double the triangle." 如果一个平行四边形与一个三角形同底，且在相同两条平行线之间，那么这个平行四边形是这个三角形的两倍。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Formulas from Geometry"
              Definition: Euclid, Book I, Proposition 41
              \(A = \frac{1}{2}(a+b)h\)
              梯形的面积              		 (pending) 待补 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Formulas from Geometry"
              \(A = \pi r^2,\ C = 2\pi r\)
              圆的面积；圆的周长              		 Circumference: "The circumference-to-diameter ratio \(C/d\) for a circle is constant as the size of the circle is changed"
              Area: "As the number of concentric strips increases to infinity as illustrated above, they form a triangle"              		 周长：圆的周长与直径之比是常数，不随圆的大小而改变
              面积：当同心圆环的数量增加到无穷多时（如该词条的图所示），它们构成一个三角形              		 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Formulas from Geometry"
              Definition: Weisstein, n.d., "Circle" entry
              \(A = \frac{1}{2}r^2\theta,\ s = r\theta\ (\theta \text{ in radians})\)
              扇形的面积；弧长（θ 用弧度）              		 Area: "the area of a sector is the fraction \(\frac{\theta}{2\pi}\) multiplied by the entire area."
              Arc length: "The radian measure of an angle is the ratio of the length of the arc subtended by the angle to the radius of the circle."              		 面积：扇形的面积是圆的整个面积乘以分数 \(\frac{\theta}{2\pi}\)。
              弧长：角的弧度数，是该角所对弧的长度与圆的半径之比。              		 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Formulas from Geometry"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.1
              \(V = \pi r^2h,\ S = 2\pi rh\)
              圆柱的体积；侧面积              		 Volume: "To calculate the volume of a cylinder, then, we simply multiply the area of the cross-section by the height of the cylinder"
