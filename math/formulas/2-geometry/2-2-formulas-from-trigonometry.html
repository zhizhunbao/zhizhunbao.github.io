              \(\csc\,\theta = \frac{\text{hyp}}{\text{opp}}\)

              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.2
              \(\cos\,\theta = \frac{\text{adj}}{\text{hyp}}\)
              余弦 = 邻边 / 斜边              		 "cosine as the ratio of the adjacent side to the hypotenuse" 余弦是邻边与斜边之比 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Right-Angle Trigonometry"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.2
              \(\sec\,\theta = \frac{\text{hyp}}{\text{adj}}\)
              正割 = 斜边 / 邻边              		 "secant as the ratio of the hypotenuse to the adjacent side" 正割是斜边与邻边之比 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Right-Angle Trigonometry"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.2
              \(\tan\,\theta = \frac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}\)

              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.2

              Trigonometric Functions of Important Angles
              特殊角的三角函数值              		 Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Trigonometric Functions of Important Angles"
              \(\theta = 0^\circ = 0:\ \sin\theta = 0,\ \cos\theta = 1,\ \tan\theta = 0\)
              0° 的三角函数值              		 "For quadrantral angles, the corresponding point on the unit circle falls on the \(x\)- or \(y\)-axis." 对于终边在坐标轴上的角，单位圆上对应的点落在 \(x\) 轴或 \(y\) 轴上。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Trigonometric Functions of Important Angles"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.3
              \(\theta = 30^\circ = \pi/6:\ \sin\theta = 1/2,\ \cos\theta = \sqrt{3}/2,\ \tan\theta = \sqrt{3}/3\)
              30° 的三角函数值              		 "Now we have an equilateral triangle." "we know one side is the radius of the unit circle, all sides must be of length 1." 现在得到一个等边三角形。已知一条边是单位圆的半径，所以所有边长都是 1。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Trigonometric Functions of Important Angles"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.3
              \(\theta = 45^\circ = \pi/4:\ \sin\theta = \sqrt{2}/2,\ \cos\theta = \sqrt{2}/2,\ \tan\theta = 1\)
              45° 的三角函数值              		 "A \(45°–45°–90°\) triangle is an isosceles triangle, so the \(x\)- and \(y\)-coordinates of the corresponding point on the circle are the same." 45°–45°–90° 三角形是等腰三角形，所以圆上对应点的 \(x\) 坐标和 \(y\) 坐标相同。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Trigonometric Functions of Important Angles"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.3
              \(\theta = 60^\circ = \pi/3:\ \sin\theta = \sqrt{3}/2,\ \cos\theta = 1/2,\ \tan\theta = \sqrt{3}\)
              60° 的三角函数值              		 "Now we have an equilateral triangle." "we know one side is the radius of the unit circle, all sides must be of length 1." 现在得到一个等边三角形。已知一条边是单位圆的半径，所以所有边长都是 1。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Trigonometric Functions of Important Angles"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.3
              \(\theta = 90^\circ = \pi/2:\ \sin\theta = 1,\ \cos\theta = 0\)
              90° 的三角函数值              		 "For quadrantral angles, the corresponding point on the unit circle falls on the \(x\)- or \(y\)-axis." 对于终边在坐标轴上的角，单位圆上对应的点落在 \(x\) 轴或 \(y\) 轴上。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Trigonometric Functions of Important Angles"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.3
              Fundamental Identities
              基本恒等式              		 Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Fundamental Identities"
              \(\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta = 1\)
              平方关系（正弦与余弦）              		 "the Pythagorean Identity is simply one case of the Pythagorean Theorem" 平方关系只是勾股定理的一个特例 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Fundamental Identities"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.3
              \(\sin (-\,\theta) = -\sin\,\theta\)
              正弦是奇函数              		 "Sine, tangent, cosecant, and cotangent are odd" 正弦、正切、余割和余切是奇函数 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Fundamental Identities"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.4
              \(1+\tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta\)
              平方关系（正切与正割）              		 "One form is obtained by dividing both sides by \(\cos^2 t\)." 一种形式由两边同除以 \(\cos^2 t\) 得到。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Fundamental Identities"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.4
              \(\cos (-\,\theta) = \cos\,\theta\)
              余弦是偶函数              		 "Cosine and secant are even" 余弦和正割是偶函数 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Fundamental Identities"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.4
              \(1+\cot^2\theta = \csc^2\theta\)
              平方关系（余切与余割）              		 "The other form is obtained by dividing both sides by \(\sin^2 t\)." 另一种形式由两边同除以 \(\sin^2 t\) 得到。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Fundamental Identities"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.4
              \(\tan (-\,\theta) = -\tan\,\theta\)
              正切是奇函数              		 "Sine, tangent, cosecant, and cotangent are odd" 正弦、正切、余割和余切是奇函数 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Fundamental Identities"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.4
              \(\sin (\frac{\pi}{2}-\theta) = \cos\,\theta\)
              余角关系（正弦）              		 "From these relationships, the cofunction identities are formed." 由这些关系得到余角关系式。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Fundamental Identities"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 9.2
              \(\sin (\theta+2\pi) = \sin\,\theta\)
              正弦的周期为 2π              		 "The period of the cosine, sine, secant, and cosecant functions is \(2\pi\)." 余弦、正弦、正割和余割函数的周期是 \(2\pi\)。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Fundamental Identities"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.4
              \(\cos (\frac{\pi}{2}-\theta) = \sin\,\theta\)
              余角关系（余弦）              		 "From these relationships, the cofunction identities are formed." 由这些关系得到余角关系式。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Fundamental Identities"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 9.2
              \(\cos (\theta+2\pi) = \cos\,\theta\)
              余弦的周期为 2π              		 "The period of the cosine, sine, secant, and cosecant functions is \(2\pi\)." 余弦、正弦、正割和余割函数的周期是 \(2\pi\)。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Fundamental Identities"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.4
              \(\tan (\frac{\pi}{2}-\theta) = \cot\,\theta\)
              余角关系（正切）              		 "From these relationships, the cofunction identities are formed." 由这些关系得到余角关系式。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Fundamental Identities"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 9.2
              \(\tan (\theta+\pi) = \tan\,\theta\)
              正切的周期为 π              		 "The period of the tangent and cotangent functions is \(\pi\)." 正切和余切函数的周期是 \(\pi\)。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Fundamental Identities"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 7.4
              Law of Sines
              正弦定理              		 Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Law of Sines"
              \(\frac{\sin\,A}{a} = \frac{\sin\,B}{b} = \frac{\sin\,C}{c}\)
              正弦定理              		 "In any triangle, we can draw an altitude, a perpendicular line from one vertex to the opposite side, forming two right triangles." 在任意三角形中，都可以作一条高，即从一个顶点到对边的垂线，得到两个直角三角形。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Law of Sines"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 10.1
              Law of Cosines
              余弦定理              		 Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Law of Cosines"
              \(a^2 = b^2+c^2-2bc\,\cos\,A\)
              余弦定理（求 a）              		 "The derivation begins with the Generalized Pythagorean Theorem, which is an extension of the Pythagorean Theorem to non-right triangles." 推导从推广的勾股定理开始，它是勾股定理在非直角三角形上的推广。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Law of Cosines"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 10.2
              \(b^2 = a^2+c^2-2ac\,\cos\,B\)
              余弦定理（求 b）              		 "The derivation begins with the Generalized Pythagorean Theorem, which is an extension of the Pythagorean Theorem to non-right triangles." 推导从推广的勾股定理开始，它是勾股定理在非直角三角形上的推广。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Law of Cosines"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 10.2
              \(c^2 = a^2+b^2-2ab\,\cos\,C\)
              余弦定理（求 c）              		 "The derivation begins with the Generalized Pythagorean Theorem, which is an extension of the Pythagorean Theorem to non-right triangles." 推导从推广的勾股定理开始，它是勾股定理在非直角三角形上的推广。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Law of Cosines"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 10.2
              Addition and Subtraction Formulas
              和差公式              		 Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Addition and Subtraction Formulas"
              \(\sin\,(x+y) = \sin\,x\,\cos\,y+\cos\,x\,\sin\,y\)
              两角和的正弦              		 "The sum and difference formulas for sine can be derived in the same manner as those for cosine, and they resemble the cosine formulas." 正弦的和差公式可以用与余弦相同的方法推导，它们也和余弦公式相似。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Addition and Subtraction Formulas"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 9.2
              \(\sin\,(x-y) = \sin\,x\,\cos\,y-\cos\,x\,\sin\,y\)
              两角差的正弦              		 "The sum and difference formulas for sine can be derived in the same manner as those for cosine, and they resemble the cosine formulas." 正弦的和差公式可以用与余弦相同的方法推导，它们也和余弦公式相似。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Addition and Subtraction Formulas"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 9.2
              \(\cos\,(x+y) = \cos\,x\,\cos\,y-\sin\,x\,\sin\,y\)
              两角和的余弦              		 "We can use similar methods to derive the cosine of the sum of two angles." 我们可以用类似的方法推导两角和的余弦。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Addition and Subtraction Formulas"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 9.2
              \(\cos\,(x-y) = \cos\,x\,\cos\,y+\sin\,x\,\sin\,y\)
              两角差的余弦              		 "We can find the distance from \(P\) to \(Q\) using the distance formula." 我们可以用距离公式求出从 \(P\) 到 \(Q\) 的距离。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Addition and Subtraction Formulas"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 9.2
              \(\tan\,(x+y) = \frac{\tan\,x+\text{tan }y}{1-\tan\,x\,\text{tan }y}\)
              两角和的正切              		 "Finding the sum of two angles formula for tangent involves taking quotient of the sum formulas for sine and cosine and simplifying." 求两角和的正切公式，要把正弦和余弦的和角公式相除，再化简。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Addition and Subtraction Formulas"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 9.2
              \(\tan (x-y) = \frac{\tan\,x-\text{tan }y}{1+\tan\,x\,\text{tan }y}\)
              两角差的正切              		 "Finding the sum of two angles formula for tangent involves taking quotient of the sum formulas for sine and cosine and simplifying." 求两角和的正切公式，要把正弦和余弦的和角公式相除，再化简。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Addition and Subtraction Formulas"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 9.2
              Double-Angle Formulas
              倍角公式              		 Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Double-Angle Formulas"
              \(\sin\,2x = 2\,\sin\,x\,\cos\,x\)
              二倍角的正弦              		 "The double-angle formulas are a special case of the sum formulas, where \(\alpha=\beta\)." 倍角公式是和角公式的特例，此时 \(\alpha=\beta\)。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Double-Angle Formulas"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 9.3
              \(\cos\,2x = \cos^2x-\sin^2x = 2\,\cos^2x-1 = 1-2\,\sin^2x\)
              二倍角的余弦              		 "The double-angle formulas are a special case of the sum formulas, where \(\alpha=\beta\)." 倍角公式是和角公式的特例，此时 \(\alpha=\beta\)。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Double-Angle Formulas"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 9.3
              \(\tan\,2x = \frac{2\,\tan\,x}{1-\tan^2x}\)
              二倍角的正切              		 "The double-angle formulas are a special case of the sum formulas, where \(\alpha=\beta\)." 倍角公式是和角公式的特例，此时 \(\alpha=\beta\)。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Double-Angle Formulas"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 9.3
              Half-Angle Formulas
              半角公式              		 Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Half-Angle Formulas"
              \(\sin^2x = \frac{1-\cos\,2x}{2}\)
              降幂公式（正弦）              		 "We can use two of the three double-angle formulas for cosine to derive the reduction formulas for sine and cosine." 可以用余弦的三个倍角公式中的两个，推导正弦和余弦的降幂公式。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Half-Angle Formulas"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 9.3
              \(\cos^2x = \frac{1+\cos\,2x}{2}\)
              降幂公式（余弦）              		 "We can use two of the three double-angle formulas for cosine to derive the reduction formulas for sine and cosine." 可以用余弦的三个倍角公式中的两个，推导正弦和余弦的降幂公式。 Formula: Strang & Herman, 2016, appendix C, "Half-Angle Formulas"
              Definition: Abramson, 2021, section 9.3
