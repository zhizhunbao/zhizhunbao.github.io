              34 Ordinary Differential Equations
              常微分方程              		 "An ordinary differential equation (frequently called an 'ODE,' 'diff eq,' or 'diffy Q') is an equality involving a function and its derivatives." 常微分方程（常称为“ODE”“diff eq”或“diffy Q”）是含有一个函数及其导数的等式。 Weisstein, n.d., "Ordinary differential equation" entry, para. 1
