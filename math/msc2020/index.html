              一般与综合性主题；文集              		 (context) "Mathematics is a broad-ranging field of study in which the properties and interactions of idealized objects are examined." （语境）数学是一个范围广泛的研究领域，它考察理想化对象的性质和相互作用。 Weisstein, n.d.-ag, "Mathematics" entry, para. 1

              03 Mathematical Logic and Foundations
              数理逻辑与基础              		 "Logic is the formal mathematical study of the methods, structure, and validity of mathematical deduction and proof." 逻辑是对数学演绎与证明的方法、结构和有效性所作的形式化数学研究。 Weisstein, n.d.-ad, "Logic" entry, para. 1
              05 Combinatorics
              组合数学              		 "Combinatorics, also called combinatorial mathematics, is the branch of discrete mathematics concerned with enumeration problems, existence problems, constructions, and structural properties of discrete objects." 组合数学（也称组合学）是离散数学的一个分支，研究离散对象的计数问题、存在性问题、构造以及结构性质。 Weisstein, n.d.-i, "Combinatorics" entry, para. 1
              06 Order, Lattices, Ordered Algebraic Structures
              序、格、有序代数结构              		 (context) "A partially ordered set (or poset) is a set taken together with a partial order on it." （语境）偏序集（poset）是一个集合连同它上面的一个偏序。 Weisstein, n.d.-aq, "Partially ordered set" entry, para. 1
              08 General Algebraic Systems
              一般代数系统              		 (context) "Universal algebra studies common properties of all algebraic structures, including groups, rings, fields, lattices, etc." （语境）泛代数研究所有代数结构的共同性质，这些结构包括群、环、域、格等。 Weisstein, n.d.-bb, "Universal algebra" entry, para. 1
              11 Number Theory
              数论              		 "Number theory is a vast and fascinating field of mathematics, sometimes called 'higher arithmetic,' consisting of the study of the properties of whole numbers." 数论是一个广阔而迷人的数学领域，有时被称为“高等算术”，它由对整数性质的研究构成。 Weisstein, n.d.-aj, "Number theory" entry, para. 1
              12 Field Theory and Polynomials
              域论与多项式              		 "A field is any set of elements that satisfies the field axioms for both addition and multiplication and is a commutative division algebra." 域是满足加法和乘法两方面的域公理、并且是交换除代数的任意元素集合。 Weisstein, n.d.-p, "Field" entry, para. 1
              13 Commutative Algebra
              交换代数              		 "A commutative algebra over a field k is a vector space A over k equipped with bilinear multiplication satisfying ab=ba for all a,b in A." 域 k 上的交换代数是 k 上的向量空间 A，它配有双线性乘法，并对 A 中所有 a、b 满足 ab=ba。 Weisstein, n.d.-j, "Commutative algebra" entry, para. 1
              14 Algebraic Geometry
              代数几何              		 "Algebraic geometry is the study of geometries that come from algebra, in particular, from rings." 代数几何研究来自代数的几何，特别是来自环的几何。 Weisstein, n.d.-a, "Algebraic geometry" entry, para. 1
              15 Linear and Multilinear Algebra; Matrix Theory
              线性与多重线性代数；矩阵论              		 "Linear algebra is the study of sets of linear equations and their transformation properties." 线性代数研究线性方程组及其变换性质。 Weisstein, n.d.-ac, "Linear algebra" entry, para. 1
              16 Associative Rings and Algebras
              结合环与结合代数              		 "A ring in the mathematical sense is a set S together with two binary operators + and * (commonly interpreted as addition and multiplication, respectively) satisfying the following conditions ..." 数学意义上的环是一个集合 S 连同两个二元运算 + 和 *（通常分别解释为加法和乘法），并满足以下条件…… Weisstein, n.d.-av, "Ring" entry, para. 1
              17 Nonassociative Rings and Algebras
              非结合环与非结合代数              		 "A nonassociative algebra is an algebra which does not satisfy a(bc)=(ab)c." 非结合代数是不满足 a(bc)=(ab)c 的代数。 Weisstein, n.d.-ai, "Nonassociative algebra" entry, para. 1
              18 Category Theory; Homological Algebra
              范畴论；同调代数              		 "The branch of mathematics which formalizes a number of algebraic properties of collections of transformations between mathematical objects (such as binary relations, groups, sets, topological spaces, etc.) of the same type ..." 这个数学分支把同一类型的数学对象（如二元关系、群、集合、拓扑空间等）之间变换的汇集所具有的若干代数性质加以形式化…… Weisstein, n.d.-g, "Category theory" entry, para. 1
              19 K-Theory
              K-理论              		 "K-theory is a branch of mathematics which brings together ideas from algebraic geometry, linear algebra, and number theory." K-理论是一个数学分支，它把代数几何、线性代数和数论的思想结合在一起。 Weisstein, n.d.-ab, "K-theory" entry, para. 1
              20 Group Theory and Generalizations
              群论及其推广              		 "The study of groups." 对群的研究。 Weisstein, n.d.-v, "Group theory" entry, para. 1
              22 Topological Groups, Lie Groups
              拓扑群、李群              		 "A topological group is a continuous group G which has the topology of a T2-space." 拓扑群是具有 T2 空间拓扑的连续群 G。 Weisstein, n.d.-az, "Topological group" entry, para. 1
              26 Real Functions
              实函数              		 "A function whose range is in the real numbers is said to be a real function, also called a real-valued function." 值域在实数中的函数称为实函数，也称为实值函数。 Weisstein, n.d.-au, "Real function" entry, para. 1
              28 Measure and Integration
              测度与积分              		 "Measure theory is the study of measures. It generalizes the intuitive notions of length, area, and volume." 测度论是对测度的研究。它推广了长度、面积和体积这些直观概念。 Weisstein, n.d.-ah, "Measure theory" entry, para. 1
              30 Functions of a Complex Variable
              复变函数              		 (context) "Complex analysis is the study of complex numbers together with their derivatives, manipulation, and other properties." （语境）复分析研究复数及其导数、运算和其他性质。 Weisstein, n.d.-k, "Complex analysis" entry, para. 1
              31 Potential Theory
              位势论              		 "The study of harmonic functions (also called potential functions)." 对调和函数（也称位势函数）的研究。 Weisstein, n.d.-ar, "Potential theory" entry, para. 1
              32 Several Complex Variables and Analytic Spaces
              多复变与解析空间              		 (context) "Complex analysis is the study of complex numbers together with their derivatives, manipulation, and other properties." （语境）复分析研究复数及其导数、运算和其他性质。 Weisstein, n.d.-k, "Complex analysis" entry, para. 1
              33 Special Functions
              特殊函数              		 "A special function is a function (usually named after an early investigator of its properties) having a particular use in mathematical physics or some other branch of mathematics." 特殊函数是一种函数（通常以早期研究其性质的人命名），它有特定的用途，用在数学物理或其他数学分支中。 Weisstein, n.d.-ax, "Special function" entry, para. 1
              34 Ordinary Differential Equations
              常微分方程              		 "An ordinary differential equation (frequently called an 'ODE,' 'diff eq,' or 'diffy Q') is an equality involving a function and its derivatives." 常微分方程（常称为“ODE”“diff eq”或“diffy Q”）是含有一个函数及其导数的等式。 Weisstein, n.d.-ao, "Ordinary differential equation" entry, para. 1
              35 Partial Differential Equations
              偏微分方程              		 "A partial differential equation (PDE) is an equation involving functions and their partial derivatives ..." 偏微分方程（PDE）是含有函数及其偏导数的方程…… Weisstein, n.d.-ap, "Partial differential equation" entry, para. 1
              37 Dynamical Systems and Ergodic Theory
              动力系统与遍历理论              		 "A dynamical system describes how a state evolves with time." 动力系统描述状态如何随时间演化。 Weisstein, n.d.-o, "Dynamical system" entry, para. 1
              39 Difference and Functional Equations
              差分方程与函数方程              		 "A difference equation, also called a finite difference equations, is an equation that involves finite differences of a function." 差分方程（也称有限差分方程）是含有函数的有限差分的方程。 Weisstein, n.d.-m, "Difference equation" entry, para. 1
              40 Sequences, Series, Summability
              序列、级数、可和性              		 "A sequence is an ordered set of mathematical objects." 序列是数学对象的有序集合。 Weisstein, n.d.-aw, "Sequence" entry, para. 1
              41 Approximations and Expansions
              逼近与展开              		 "The mathematical study of how given quantities can be approximated by other (usually simpler) ones under appropriate conditions." 对给定的量如何在适当条件下用其他（通常更简单的）量来近似所作的数学研究。 Weisstein, n.d.-d, "Approximation theory" entry, para. 1
              42 Harmonic Analysis on Euclidean Spaces
              欧氏空间上的调和分析              		 "Harmonic analysis studies the representation and analysis of functions through oscillatory components." 调和分析研究通过振荡分量来表示和分析函数。 Weisstein, n.d.-w, "Harmonic analysis" entry, para. 1
              43 Abstract Harmonic Analysis
              抽象调和分析              		 (context) "The field includes such branches as Fourier series, isospectral manifolds (hearing the shape of a drum), and topological groups." （语境）这一领域包括傅里叶级数、等谱流形（听出鼓的形状）以及拓扑群等分支。 Weisstein, n.d.-w, "Harmonic analysis" entry, para. 2
              44 Integral Transforms, Operational Calculus
              积分变换、算子演算              		 "A general integral transform is defined by g(alpha)=int_a^bf(t)K(alpha,t)dt, where K(alpha,t) is called the integral kernel of the transform." 一般的积分变换定义为 g(alpha)=int_a^bf(t)K(alpha,t)dt，其中 K(alpha,t) 称为该变换的积分核。 Weisstein, n.d.-aa, "Integral transform" entry, para. 1
              45 Integral Equations
              积分方程              		 "An integral equation is an equation involving a function f(x) and integrals of that function to solved for f(x)." 积分方程是含有一个函数 f(x) 以及该函数的积分、要从中解出 f(x) 的方程。 Weisstein, n.d.-z, "Integral equation" entry, para. 1
              46 Functional Analysis
              泛函分析              		 "Functional analysis is a branch of mathematics concerned with infinite-dimensional vector spaces (mainly function spaces) and mappings between them." 泛函分析是一个数学分支，研究无穷维向量空间（主要是函数空间）以及它们之间的映射。 Weisstein, n.d.-r, "Functional analysis" entry, para. 1
              47 Operator Theory
              算子理论              		 "Operator theory is a broad area of mathematics connected with functional analysis, differential equations, index theory, representation theory, and mathematical physics." 算子理论是一个广阔的数学领域，与泛函分析、微分方程、指标理论、表示论和数学物理相关联。 Weisstein, n.d.-am, "Operator theory" entry, para. 1
              49 Calculus of Variations and Optimal Control; Optimization
              变分法与最优控制；最优化              		 "Calculus of variations seeks to find the path, curve, surface, etc., for which a given functional has a stationary value (which, in physical problems, is usually a minimum or maximum)." 变分法寻找使给定泛函取驻值的路径、曲线、曲面等（在物理问题中通常是极小值或极大值）。 Weisstein, n.d.-f, "Calculus of variations" entry, para. 1
              51 Geometry
              几何              		 "Geometry is the study of figures in a space of a given number of dimensions and of a given type." 几何研究给定维数、给定类型的空间中的图形。 Weisstein, n.d.-u, "Geometry" entry, para. 1
              52 Convex and Discrete Geometry
              凸几何与离散几何              		 (context) "Combinatorial geometry is a blending of principles from the areas of combinatorics and geometry. It deals with combinations and arrangements of geometric objects and with discrete properties of these objects." （语境）组合几何融合了组合学和几何学两方面的原理。它研究几何对象的组合与排列，以及这些对象的离散性质。 Weisstein, n.d.-h, "Combinatorial geometry" entry, para. 1
              53 Differential Geometry
              微分几何              		 "Differential geometry is the study of Riemannian manifolds." 微分几何是对黎曼流形的研究。 Weisstein, n.d.-n, "Differential geometry" entry, para. 1
              54 General Topology
              一般拓扑              		 (context) "Topology is the mathematical study of the properties that are preserved through deformations, twistings, and stretchings of objects." （语境）拓扑学是对物体经过变形、扭曲和拉伸后仍保持的性质所作的数学研究。 Weisstein, n.d.-ba, "Topology" entry, para. 1
              55 Algebraic Topology
              代数拓扑              		 "Algebraic topology is the study of intrinsic qualitative aspects of spatial objects (e.g., surfaces, spheres, tori, circles, knots, links, configuration spaces, etc.) that remain invariant under both-directions continuous one-to-one (homeomorphic) transformations." 代数拓扑研究空间对象的内在定性特征（如曲面、球面、环面、圆、纽结、链环、构形空间等），这些特征在双向连续的一一（同胚）变换下保持不变。 Weisstein, n.d.-b, "Algebraic topology" entry, para. 1
              57 Manifolds and Cell Complexes
              流形与胞腔复形              		 "More concisely, any object that can be 'charted' is a manifold." 更简洁地说，任何可以“绘制成图册”的对象都是流形。 Weisstein, n.d.-ae, "Manifold" entry, para. 2
              58 Global Analysis, Analysis on Manifolds
              整体分析、流形上的分析              		 (context) "A manifold without boundary is a T2-space with a second countable topology that is locally Euclidean (i.e., around every point, there is a neighborhood that is topologically the same as the open unit ball in R^n)." （语境）无边流形是具有第二可数拓扑、并且局部是欧几里得的 T2 空间（即每一点周围都有一个邻域，它在拓扑上与 R^n 中的开单位球相同）。 Weisstein, n.d.-ae, "Manifold" entry, para. 1
              60 Probability Theory and Stochastic Processes
              概率论与随机过程              		 "Probability is the branch of mathematics that studies the possible outcomes of given events together with the outcomes' relative likelihoods and distributions." 概率是数学的一个分支，研究给定事件的可能结果，以及这些结果的相对可能性和分布。 Weisstein, n.d.-as, "Probability" entry, para. 1
              62 Statistics
              统计              		 "The mathematical study of the likelihood and probability of events occurring based on known information and inferred by taking a limited number of samples." 对事件发生的可能性和概率所作的数学研究，依据已知信息，并通过抽取有限数量的样本来推断。 Weisstein, n.d.-ay, "Statistics" entry, para. 1
              65 Numerical Analysis
              数值分析              		 "Numerical analysis is the study of algorithms for obtaining numerical approximations to mathematical problems." 数值分析研究求数学问题数值近似解的算法。 Weisstein, n.d.-ak, "Numerical analysis" entry, para. 1
              68 Computer Science
              计算机科学              		 (context) "An algorithm is a specific set of instructions for carrying out a procedure or solving a problem, usually with the requirement that the procedure terminate at some point." （语境）算法是执行某个过程或解决某个问题的一组具体指令，通常要求该过程在某一时刻终止。 Weisstein, n.d.-c, "Algorithm" entry, para. 1

              78 Optics, Electromagnetic Theory
              光学、电磁理论              		 "Optics is the study of the propagation of electromagnetic radiation (most commonly, visible light)." 光学研究电磁辐射的传播（最常见的是可见光）。 Weisstein, n.d.-an, "Optics" entry, para. 1
              80 Classical Thermodynamics, Heat Transfer
              经典热力学、热传递              		 (context) "The heat conduction equation is a partial differential diffusion equation ..." （语境）热传导方程是一种偏微分扩散方程…… Weisstein, n.d.-x, "Heat conduction equation" entry, para. 1
              81 Quantum Theory
              量子理论              		 "Quantum mechanics is the description of motion and interaction of particles at the small scales where the discrete nature of the physical world becomes important." 量子力学描述在微小尺度上粒子的运动和相互作用，在这些尺度上物理世界的离散性变得重要。 Weisstein, n.d.-at, "Quantum mechanics" entry, para. 1

              运筹学、数学规划              		 "Operations research is a vast branch of mathematics which encompasses many diverse areas of minimization and optimization." 运筹学是一个广阔的数学分支，涵盖极小化与最优化的许多不同领域。 Weisstein, n.d.-al, "Operations research" entry, para. 1
              91 Game Theory, Economics, Finance, and Other Social and Behavioral Sciences
              博弈论、经济学、金融及其他社会与行为科学              		 "Game theory is a branch of mathematics that deals with the analysis of games (i.e., situations involving parties with conflicting interests)." 博弈论是一个数学分支，处理对博弈的分析（即涉及利益冲突各方的情形）。 Weisstein, n.d.-s, "Game theory" entry, para. 1
              92 Biology and Other Natural Sciences
              生物学及其他自然科学              		 "Mathematical biology is the use of mathematical methods to formulate and analyze models of biological systems." 数学生物学是运用数学方法来建立和分析生物系统的模型。 Weisstein, n.d.-af, "Mathematical biology" entry, para. 1
              93 Systems Theory; Control
              系统论；控制              		 "The mathematical study of how to manipulate the parameters affecting the behavior of a system to produce the desired or optimal outcome." 对如何调节影响系统行为的参数、以得到期望或最优结果所作的数学研究。 Weisstein, n.d.-l, "Control theory" entry, para. 1
              94 Information and Communication Theory, Circuits
