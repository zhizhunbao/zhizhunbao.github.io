              Multiplicative Function Preserved by Summation
              积性函数在求和下保持              		 "If \(g\) is multiplicative and \(f(n)\) is defined as \(f(n)=\sum_{d\mid n}g(d)\) then \(f\) is also multiplicative." 若 \(g\) 是积性的，且 \(f(n)\) 定义为 \(f(n)=\sum_{d\mid n}g(d)\)，则 \(f\) 也是积性的。 Crisman, 2024, Theorem 19.2.7
