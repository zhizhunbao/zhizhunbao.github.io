              1.2 Review of Previous Ideas
              已有知识回顾              		 (No definition) （无定义） Crisman, 2024, section 1.2
              Well-Ordering Principle
              良序原理              		 "Any nonempty set of positive integers has a least/smallest element." 任何非空的正整数集合都有最小元素。 Crisman, 2024, Axiom 1.2.1
              Proof by Contradiction
              反证法              		 "To review, proofs by contradiction and contrapositive both start by assuming the negation of the conclusion. A proof by contrapositive uses that assumption to prove the negation of the original assumption. A proof by contradiction, on the other hand, leads to some absurdity, but not necessarily just negating the original assumptions." 回顾一下，反证法和逆否证法都从假设结论的否定开始。逆否证法用这个假设去证明原来假设的否定。而反证法则导出某种荒谬，不一定只是否定原来的假设。 Crisman, 2024, Remark 1.2.3
              Proof by Contrapositive
              逆否证法              		 "To review, proofs by contradiction and contrapositive both start by assuming the negation of the conclusion. A proof by contrapositive uses that assumption to prove the negation of the original assumption." 回顾一下，反证法和逆否证法都从假设结论的否定开始。逆否证法用这个假设去证明原来假设的否定。 Crisman, 2024, Remark 1.2.3
              Proof by Induction
              归纳法证明              		 "Sometimes we need a way to prove a statement for all integers \(n\) after a certain point, for instance integers greater than or equal to \(n=1\). This is usually called proof by induction." 有时我们需要一种方法来对某一点之后的所有整数 \(n\) 证明一个命题，例如对所有大于或等于 \(n=1\) 的整数。这通常称为归纳法证明。 Crisman, 2024, subsection 1.2.2
              Divisibility
              整除              		 "If an integer \(n\) can be written as a product \(kd=n\) of two integers \(k\) and \(d\), then we say that \(d\) divides \(n\), or that \(n\) is divisible by \(d\), or that \(d\) is a divisor of \(n\). We write \(d\mid n\) to denote that \(d\) divides \(n\)." 若整数 \(n\) 能写成两个整数 \(k\) 与 \(d\) 的乘积 \(kd=n\)，则称 \(d\) 整除 \(n\)，或 \(n\) 能被 \(d\) 整除，或 \(d\) 是 \(n\) 的因数。我们用 \(d\mid n\) 表示 \(d\) 整除 \(n\)。 Crisman, 2024, Definition 1.2.5
              Divisor
              因数              		 "If an integer \(n\) can be written as a product \(kd=n\) of two integers \(k\) and \(d\), then we say that \(d\) divides \(n\), or that \(n\) is divisible by \(d\), or that \(d\) is a divisor of \(n\)." 若整数 \(n\) 能写成两个整数 \(k\) 与 \(d\) 的乘积 \(kd=n\)，则称 \(d\) 整除 \(n\)，或 \(n\) 能被 \(d\) 整除，或 \(d\) 是 \(n\) 的因数。 Crisman, 2024, Definition 1.2.5
              Proper Divisor
              真因数              		 "We occasionally use the term proper divisor to denote a positive divisor of \(n\) which is not \(n\)." 我们有时用真因数这个词来表示 \(n\) 的不等于 \(n\) 的正因数。 Crisman, 2024, Example 1.2.6
              Direct Proof
              直接证明              		 "These are not hard to prove (see Exercise 1.4.1) using direct proof, where no indirect or inductive steps are needed." 这些用直接证明不难证明（见习题 1.4.1），不需要任何间接或归纳的步骤。 Crisman, 2024, subsection 1.2.3
