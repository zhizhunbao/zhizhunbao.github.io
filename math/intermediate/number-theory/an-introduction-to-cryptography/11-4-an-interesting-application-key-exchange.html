              11.4 An Interesting Application: Key Exchange
              一个有趣的应用：密钥交换              		 (No definition) （无定义） Crisman, 2024, section 11.4
              Key Exchange
              密钥交换              		 "There is a quite useful application of Diffie-Hellman called key exchange." 迪菲-赫尔曼方法有一个很有用的应用，称为密钥交换。 Crisman, 2024, section 11.4
              Diffie-Hellman Key Exchange
              迪菲-赫尔曼密钥交换              		 "First, Alice and Bob jointly pick a big prime \(p\) and a base for exponentiation \(g\), presumably with \(1<g<p\). This doesn’t need to be secret. Now, they each secretly choose an exponent; maybe Alice chooses \(m\) and Bob chooses \(n\). The key step: Each of them exponentiates \(g\) to their secret power, modulo \(p\). Then they pass off these numbers to each other, and once again exponentiate the other person’s number to their own secret power, modulo \(p\). The resulting numbers are the same and give the secret key." 首先，Alice 和 Bob 共同选一个大素数 \(p\) 和一个求幂的底数 \(g\)，大概取 \(1<g<p\)。这些不需要保密。然后，他们各自秘密地选一个指数；比如 Alice 选 \(m\)，Bob 选 \(n\)。关键一步：每人把 \(g\) 求自己的秘密次幂，模 \(p\)。然后他们把这些数交给对方，再把对方的数求自己本人的秘密次幂，模 \(p\)。得到的两个数相同，就是秘密密钥。 Crisman, 2024, Algorithm 11.4.2
              Man in the Middle Attack
              中间人攻击              		 "Eve can in this situation add her own exponent, \(\ell\), to the game, so that she pretends to have secret key \(g^{m\ell}\) with Alice and secret key \(g^{n\ell}\) with Bob. Both of their keys’ security is now compromised. Such a situation is historically known as a “Man in the Middle” attack." 这时 Eve 可以把她自己的指数 \(\ell\) 加进来，从而假装与 Alice 共有秘密密钥 \(g^{m\ell}\)，与 Bob 共有秘密密钥 \(g^{n\ell}\)。两人密钥的安全都被破坏了。这种情形历来称为“中间人”攻击。 Crisman, 2024, subsection 11.4.2
