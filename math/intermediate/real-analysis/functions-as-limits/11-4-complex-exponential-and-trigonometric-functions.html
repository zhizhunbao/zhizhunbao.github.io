              11.4 Complex exponential and trigonometric functions
              复指数函数与三角函数              		 (No definition) （无定义） Lebl, 2026, section 11.4
              Law of Exponents
              指数律              		 "Let \(z,w \in \mathbb{C}\) be complex numbers. Then \(e^{z+w} = e^z e^w\)." 设 \(z,w \in \mathbb{C}\) 是复数，则 \(e^{z+w} = e^z e^w\)。 Lebl, 2026, Proposition 11.4.1
              Cosine
              余弦              		 "In fact, we define sine and cosine for all complex \(z\): \(\cos(z) := \frac{e^{iz} + e^{-iz}}{2}\) and \(\sin(z) := \frac{e^{iz} - e^{-iz}}{2i}\)." 实际上，我们对所有复数 \(z\) 定义正弦与余弦：\(\cos(z) := \frac{e^{iz} + e^{-iz}}{2}\)，\(\sin(z) := \frac{e^{iz} - e^{-iz}}{2i}\)。 Lebl, 2026, section 11.4
              Sine
              正弦              		 "In fact, we define sine and cosine for all complex \(z\): \(\cos(z) := \frac{e^{iz} + e^{-iz}}{2}\) and \(\sin(z) := \frac{e^{iz} - e^{-iz}}{2i}\)." 实际上，我们对所有复数 \(z\) 定义正弦与余弦：\(\cos(z) := \frac{e^{iz} + e^{-iz}}{2}\)，\(\sin(z) := \frac{e^{iz} - e^{-iz}}{2i}\)。 Lebl, 2026, section 11.4
              Euler’s Formula
              欧拉公式              		 "For all \(z \in \mathbb{C}\), \(e^{iz} = \cos(z) + i\sin(z)\) (Euler's formula)." 对所有 \(z \in \mathbb{C}\)，\(e^{iz} = \cos(z) + i\sin(z)\)（欧拉公式）。 Lebl, 2026, Proposition 11.4.2
              Unimodular
              单模              		 "In particular, \(e^{ix}\) is unimodular for real \(x\); the values lie on the unit circle." 特别地，对实数 \(x\)，\(e^{ix}\) 是单模的；它的值位于单位圆上。 Lebl, 2026, section 11.4
              Hyperbolic Sine
              双曲正弦              		 "Define the hyperbolic sine and hyperbolic cosine by \(\sinh(z) := \frac{e^z-e^{-z}}{2}\), \(\cosh(z) := \frac{e^z+e^{-z}}{2}\)." 定义双曲正弦和双曲余弦为 \(\sinh(z) := \frac{e^z-e^{-z}}{2}\)，\(\cosh(z) := \frac{e^z+e^{-z}}{2}\)。 Lebl, 2026, Exercises 11.4
              Hyperbolic Cosine
              双曲余弦              		 "Define the hyperbolic sine and hyperbolic cosine by \(\sinh(z) := \frac{e^z-e^{-z}}{2}\), \(\cosh(z) := \frac{e^z+e^{-z}}{2}\)." 定义双曲正弦和双曲余弦为 \(\sinh(z) := \frac{e^z-e^{-z}}{2}\)，\(\cosh(z) := \frac{e^z+e^{-z}}{2}\)。 Lebl, 2026, Exercises 11.4
