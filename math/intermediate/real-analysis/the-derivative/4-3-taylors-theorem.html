              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              4.3 Taylor’s theorem
              泰勒定理              		 (No definition) （无定义） Lebl, 2026, section 4.3
              First Derivative
              一阶导数              		 "When \(f \colon I \to \mathbb{R}\) is differentiable, we obtain a function \(f' \colon I \to \mathbb{R}\). The function \(f'\) is called the first derivative of \(f\)." 当 \(f \colon I \to \mathbb{R}\) 可微时，得到函数 \(f' \colon I \to \mathbb{R}\)。函数 \(f'\) 称为 \(f\) 的一阶导数。 Lebl, 2026, section 4.3
              Second Derivative
              二阶导数              		 "If \(f'\) is differentiable, we denote by \(f'' \colon I \to \mathbb{R}\) the derivative of \(f'\). The function \(f''\) is called the second derivative of \(f\)." 若 \(f'\) 可微，用 \(f'' \colon I \to \mathbb{R}\) 表示 \(f'\) 的导数。函数 \(f''\) 称为 \(f\) 的二阶导数。 Lebl, 2026, section 4.3
              nth Derivative
              n 阶导数              		 "With a larger number of derivatives the notation would get out of hand; we denote by \(f^{(n)}\) the $n$th derivative of \(f\)." 导数阶数多了，记号会失控；我们用 \(f^{(n)}\) 表示 \(f\) 的 \(n\) 阶导数。 Lebl, 2026, section 4.3
              n Times Differentiable
              n 次可微              		 "When \(f\) possesses \(n\) derivatives, we say \(f\) is \(n\) times differentiable." 当 \(f\) 有 \(n\) 阶导数时，称 \(f\) \(n\) 次可微。 Lebl, 2026, section 4.3
              Taylor Polynomial
              泰勒多项式              		 "For an \(n\) times differentiable function \(f\) defined near a point \(x_0 \in \mathbb{R}\), define the $n$th order Taylor polynomial for \(f\) at \(x_0\) as \(P_n^{x_0}(x) := \sum_{k=0}^n \frac{f^{(k)}(x_0)}{k!}{(x-x_0)}^k\)." 对在点 \(x_0 \in \mathbb{R}\) 附近有定义的 \(n\) 次可微函数 \(f\)，定义 \(f\) 在 \(x_0\) 处的 \(n\) 阶泰勒多项式为 \(P_n^{x_0}(x) := \sum_{k=0}^n \frac{f^{(k)}(x_0)}{k!}{(x-x_0)}^k\)。 Lebl, 2026, Definition 4.3.1
              Taylor’s Theorem
              泰勒定理              		 "Suppose \(f \colon [a,b] \to \mathbb{R}\) is a function with \(n\) continuous derivatives on \([a,b]\) and such that \(f^{(n+1)}\) exists on \((a,b)\). Given distinct points \(x_0\) and \(x\) in \([a,b]\), there is a point \(c\) between \(x_0\) and \(x\) such that \(f(x)=P_{n}^{x_0}(x)+\frac{f^{(n+1)}(c)}{(n+1)!}{(x-x_0)}^{n+1}\)." 设 \(f \colon [a,b] \to \mathbb{R}\) 在 \([a,b]\) 上有 \(n\) 阶连续导数，且 \(f^{(n+1)}\) 在 \((a,b)\) 上存在。给定 \([a,b]\) 中不同的点 \(x_0\) 与 \(x\)，存在介于 \(x_0\) 与 \(x\) 之间的点 \(c\)，使得 \(f(x)=P_{n}^{x_0}(x)+\frac{f^{(n+1)}(c)}{(n+1)!}{(x-x_0)}^{n+1}\)。 Lebl, 2026, Theorem 4.3.2
              Remainder Term
              余项              		 "The term \(R_n^{x_0}(x):= f(x)-P_n^{x_0}(x) = \frac{f^{(n+1)}(c)}{(n+1)!}{(x-x_0)}^{n+1}\) is called the remainder term." 项 \(R_n^{x_0}(x):= f(x)-P_n^{x_0}(x) = \frac{f^{(n+1)}(c)}{(n+1)!}{(x-x_0)}^{n+1}\) 称为余项。 Lebl, 2026, section 4.3
              Lagrange Form
              拉格朗日型              		 "This form is called the Lagrange form of the remainder." 这种形式称为余项的拉格朗日型。 Lebl, 2026, section 4.3
              Infinitely Differentiable
              无穷次可微              		 "If \(f\) is infinitely differentiable, that is, if \(f\) can be differentiated any number of times, then we define the Taylor series:" 若 \(f\) 无穷次可微，即 \(f\) 可以求任意多次导数，就定义泰勒级数： Lebl, 2026, section 4.3
              Taylor Series
              泰勒级数              		 "If \(f\) is infinitely differentiable, that is, if \(f\) can be differentiated any number of times, then we define the Taylor series: \(\sum_{k=0}^\infty \frac{f^{(k)}(x_0)}{k!}{(x-x_0)}^k\)." 若 \(f\) 无穷次可微，即 \(f\) 可以求任意多次导数，就定义泰勒级数：\(\sum_{k=0}^\infty \frac{f^{(k)}(x_0)}{k!}{(x-x_0)}^k\)。 Lebl, 2026, section 4.3
              Analytic Function
              解析函数              		 "Functions \(f\) whose Taylor series at every point \(x_0\) converges to \(f\) in some open interval containing \(x_0\) are called analytic functions." 在每一点 \(x_0\) 处的泰勒级数都在某个包含 \(x_0\) 的开区间内收敛到 \(f\) 的函数 \(f\)，称为解析函数。 Lebl, 2026, section 4.3
              Strict Relative Minimum
              严格相对极小值              		 "By a strict relative minimum of \(f\) at \(c\), we mean that there exists a \(\delta > 0\) such that \(f(x) > f(c)\) for all \(x \in (c-\delta,c+\delta)\) where \(x\neq c\)." \(f\) 在 \(c\) 处有严格相对极小值，是指存在 \(\delta > 0\)，使得对所有 \(x \neq c\) 的 \(x \in (c-\delta,c+\delta)\) 有 \(f(x) > f(c)\)。 Lebl, 2026, section 4.3
              Strict Relative Maximum
              严格相对极大值              		 "A strict relative maximum is defined similarly." 严格相对极大值的定义类似。 Lebl, 2026, section 4.3
              Second Derivative Test
              二阶导数判别法              		 "Suppose \(f \colon (a,b) \to \mathbb{R}\) is twice continuously differentiable, \(x_0 \in (a,b)\), \(f'(x_0) = 0\) and \(f''(x_0) > 0\). Then \(f\) has a strict relative minimum at \(x_0\)." 设 \(f \colon (a,b) \to \mathbb{R}\) 二次连续可微，\(x_0 \in (a,b)\)，\(f'(x_0) = 0\) 且 \(f''(x_0) > 0\)，则 \(f\) 在 \(x_0\) 处有严格相对极小值。 Lebl, 2026, Proposition 4.3.3
              nth Derivative Test
              n 阶导数判别法              		 "Suppose \(n \in \mathbb{N}\), \(x_0 \in (a,b)\), and \(f \colon (a,b) \to \mathbb{R}\) is \(n\) times continuously differentiable, with \(f^{(k)}(x_0) = 0\) for \(k=1,2,\ldots,n-1\), and \(f^{(n)}(x_0) \neq 0\). Prove: If \(n\) is odd, then \(f\) has neither a relative minimum, nor a maximum at \(x_0\). If \(n\) is even, then \(f\) has a strict relative minimum at \(x_0\) if \(f^{(n)}(x_0) > 0\) and a strict relative maximum at \(x_0\) if \(f^{(n)}(x_0) < 0\)." 设 \(n \in \mathbb{N}\)，\(x_0 \in (a,b)\)，\(f \colon (a,b) \to \mathbb{R}\) \(n\) 次连续可微，且 \(k=1,2,\ldots,n-1\) 时 \(f^{(k)}(x_0) = 0\)，\(f^{(n)}(x_0) \neq 0\)。证明：若 \(n\) 为奇数，则 \(f\) 在 \(x_0\) 处既无相对极小值也无极大值；若 \(n\) 为偶数，则 \(f^{(n)}(x_0) > 0\) 时 \(f\) 在 \(x_0\) 处有严格相对极小值，\(f^{(n)}(x_0) < 0\) 时有严格相对极大值。 Lebl, 2026, Exercise 4.3.10
