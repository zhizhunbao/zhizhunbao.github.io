              5.4 Cartesian Products
              笛卡尔积              		 (No definition) （无定义） Sundstrom, 2022, section 5.4
              Ordered Pair
              有序对              		 "An ordered pair (with first element from \(A\) and second element from \(B\)) is a single pair of objects, denoted by \((a, b)\), with \(a \in A\) and \(b \in B\) and an implied order." 有序对（第一个元素来自 \(A\)，第二个元素来自 \(B\)）是单独的一对对象，记作 \((a, b)\)，其中 \(a \in A\)，\(b \in B\)，并带有隐含的顺序。 Sundstrom, 2022, section 5.4
              Coordinates
              坐标              		 "The objects in the ordered pair are called the coordinates of the ordered pair." 有序对中的对象称为该有序对的坐标。 Sundstrom, 2022, section 5.4
              Cartesian Product
              笛卡尔积              		 "If \(A\) and \(B\) are sets, then the Cartesian product, \(A \times B\text{,}\) of \(A\) and \(B\) is the set of all ordered pairs \(\left( {x,y} \right)\) where \(x \in A\) and \(y \in B\text{.}\)" 如果 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 是集合，那么 \(A\) 与 \(B\) 的笛卡尔积 \(A \times B\) 是满足 \(x \in A\)、\(y \in B\) 的所有有序对 \(\left( {x,y} \right)\) 组成的集合。 Sundstrom, 2022, section 5.4
              Cartesian Plane
              笛卡尔平面              		 "This \(xy\)-plane, with which you are familiar, is a representation of the set \(\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}\) or \(\mathbb{R}^2\text{.}\) This plane is called the Cartesian plane." 你熟悉的这个 \(xy\) 平面表示的是集合 \(\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}\)（即 \(\mathbb{R}^2\)）。这个平面称为笛卡尔平面。 Sundstrom, 2022, section 5.4
              Ordered Triple
              有序三元组              		 "An ordered triple can be thought of as a single triple of objects, denoted by \(\left( {a,b,c} \right)\text{,}\) with an implied order." 有序三元组可以看作单独的三个对象，记作 \(\left( {a,b,c} \right)\)，并带有隐含的顺序。 Sundstrom, 2022, section 5.4
