              4.2 Other Forms of Mathematical Induction
              数学归纳法的其他形式              		 (No definition) （无定义） Sundstrom, 2022, section 4.2
              Factorial
              阶乘              		 "If \(n\) is a natural number, we define \(\boldsymbol{n}\) factorial, denoted by \(n!\), to be the product of the first \(n\) natural numbers. In addition, we define \(0!\) to be equal to 1." 如果 \(n\) 是自然数，定义 \(\boldsymbol{n}\) 的阶乘（记作 \(n!\)）为前 \(n\) 个自然数的乘积。另外，定义 \(0!\) 等于 1。 Sundstrom, 2022, section 4.2
              Domino Theory
              多米诺理论              		 "Now imagine that each statement in equation (29) is a domino in a chain of dominoes. When we prove the inductive step, we are proving that if one domino is knocked over, then it will knock over the next one in the chain." 现在想象式 (29) 中的每个陈述都是一串多米诺骨牌中的一块。证明归纳步骤，就是在证明如果一块骨牌被推倒，它就会推倒链中的下一块。 Sundstrom, 2022, section 4.2
              Extended Principle of Mathematical Induction
              扩展数学归纳法原理              		 "Let \(M\) be an integer. If \(T\) is a subset of \(\mathbb{Z}\) such that \(M \in T\text{,}\) and For every \(k \in \mathbb{Z}\) with \(k \geq M\text{,}\) if \(k \in T\text{,}\) then \(\left( {k + 1} \right) \in T\text{,}\) then \(T\) contains all integers greater than or equal to \(M\text{.}\)" 设 \(M\) 是整数。如果 \(T\) 是 \(\mathbb{Z}\) 的子集，满足 \(M \in T\)，并且对每个满足 \(k \geq M\) 的 \(k \in \mathbb{Z}\)，若 \(k \in T\) 则 \(\left( {k + 1} \right) \in T\)，那么 \(T\) 包含所有大于或等于 \(M\) 的整数。 Sundstrom, 2022, section 4.2
              Second Principle of Mathematical Induction
              第二数学归纳法原理              		 "Let \(M\) be an integer. If \(T\) is a subset of \(\mathbb{Z}\) such that \(M \in T\text{,}\) and For every \(k \in \mathbb{Z}\) with \(k \geq M\text{,}\) if \(\left\{ {M, M + 1, \ldots ,k} \right\} \subseteq T\text{,}\) then \(\left( {k + 1} \right) \in T\text{,}\) then \(T\) contains all integers greater than or equal to \(M\text{.}\)" 设 \(M\) 是整数。如果 \(T\) 是 \(\mathbb{Z}\) 的子集，满足 \(M \in T\)，并且对每个满足 \(k \geq M\) 的 \(k \in \mathbb{Z}\)，若 \(\left\{ {M, M + 1, \ldots ,k} \right\} \subseteq T\) 则 \(\left( {k + 1} \right) \in T\)，那么 \(T\) 包含所有大于或等于 \(M\) 的整数。 Sundstrom, 2022, section 4.2
              De Moivre's Theorem
              棣莫弗定理              		 "One version of the theorem can be stated as follows: If \(x\) is a real number, then for each nonnegative integer \(n\text{,}\) \(\left[ {\cos x + i(\sin x)} \right]^n = \cos (nx) + i( {\sin (nx)} )\!\text{.}\)" 该定理的一种形式可以表述为：如果 \(x\) 是实数，那么对每个非负整数 \(n\)，\(\left[ {\cos x + i(\sin x)} \right]^n = \cos (nx) + i( {\sin (nx)} )\)。 Sundstrom, 2022, section 4.2
