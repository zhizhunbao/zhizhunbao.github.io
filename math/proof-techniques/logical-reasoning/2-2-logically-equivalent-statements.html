              2.2 Logically Equivalent Statements
              逻辑等价的陈述              		 (No definition) （无定义） Sundstrom, 2022, section 2.2
              Logically Equivalent
              逻辑等价              		 "Two expressions are logically equivalent provided that they have the same truth value for all possible combinations of truth values for all variables appearing in the two expressions." 如果两个表达式对其中出现的所有变量的所有可能真值组合都有相同的真值，它们就是逻辑等价的。 Sundstrom, 2022, section 2.2
              Converse
              逆命题              		 "The converse of the conditional statement \(P \to Q\) is the conditional statement \(Q \to P\text{.}\)" 条件陈述 \(P \to Q\) 的逆命题是条件陈述 \(Q \to P\)。 Sundstrom, 2022, section 2.2
              Contrapositive
              逆否命题              		 "The contrapositive of the conditional statement \(P \to Q\) is the conditional statement \(\neg Q \to \neg P\text{.}\)" 条件陈述 \(P \to Q\) 的逆否命题是条件陈述 \(\neg Q \to \neg P\)。 Sundstrom, 2022, section 2.2
              De Morgan's Laws for Statements
              陈述的德摩根律              		 "For statements \(P\) and \(Q\text{,}\) The statement \(\neg \left( {P \wedge Q} \right)\) is logically equivalent to \(\neg P \vee \neg Q\text{.}\) This can be written as \(\neg \left( {P \wedge Q} \right) \equiv \;\neg P \vee \neg Q\text{.}\) The statement \(\neg \left( {P \vee Q} \right)\) is logically equivalent to \(\neg P \wedge \neg Q\text{.}\)" 对陈述 \(P\) 和 \(Q\)：陈述 \(\neg \left( {P \wedge Q} \right)\) 逻辑等价于 \(\neg P \vee \neg Q\)，可写成 \(\neg \left( {P \wedge Q} \right) \equiv \;\neg P \vee \neg Q\)。陈述 \(\neg \left( {P \vee Q} \right)\) 逻辑等价于 \(\neg P \wedge \neg Q\)。 Sundstrom, 2022, section 2.2
              Negation of a Conditional Statement
              条件陈述的否定              		 "The logical equivalency \(\neg \left( {P \to Q} \right) \equiv P \wedge \neg Q\) is interesting because it shows us that the negation of a conditional statement is not another conditional statement. The negation of a conditional statement can be written in the form of a conjunction." 逻辑等价式 \(\neg \left( {P \to Q} \right) \equiv P \wedge \neg Q\) 很有意思，因为它说明条件陈述的否定不是另一个条件陈述。条件陈述的否定可以写成合取的形式。 Sundstrom, 2022, section 2.2
              Important Logical Equivalencies
              重要逻辑等价式              		 "For statements \(P\text{,}\) \(Q\text{,}\) and \(R\text{,}\) De Morgan's Laws. \(\neg \left( {P \wedge Q} \right) \equiv \;\neg P \vee \neg Q\) \(\neg \left( {P \vee Q} \right) \equiv \;\neg P \wedge \neg Q\) Conditional Statements. \(P \to Q \equiv \;\neg Q \to \neg P \text{()}\) \(P \to Q \equiv \;\neg P \vee Q\) \(\neg \left( {P \to Q} \right) \equiv P \wedge \neg Q\) Biconditional Statement. \(\left( {P \leftrightarrow Q} \right) \equiv \left( {P \to Q} \right) \wedge \left( {Q \to P} \right)\) Double Negation. \(\neg \left( {\neg P} \right) \equiv P\)" 对陈述 \(P\)、\(Q\) 和 \(R\)：德摩根律：\(\neg \left( {P \wedge Q} \right) \equiv \;\neg P \vee \neg Q\)，\(\neg \left( {P \vee Q} \right) \equiv \;\neg P \wedge \neg Q\)；条件陈述：\(P \to Q \equiv \;\neg Q \to \neg P \text{()}\)，\(P \to Q \equiv \;\neg P \vee Q\)，\(\neg \left( {P \to Q} \right) \equiv P \wedge \neg Q\)；双条件陈述：\(\left( {P \leftrightarrow Q} \right) \equiv \left( {P \to Q} \right) \wedge \left( {Q \to P} \right)\)；双重否定：\(\neg \left( {\neg P} \right) \equiv P\) Sundstrom, 2022, section 2.2
              Distributive Laws for Statements
              陈述的分配律              		 "Distributive Laws. \(P \vee \left( {Q \wedge R} \right) \equiv \left( {P \vee Q} \right) \wedge \left( {P \vee R} \right)\) \(P \wedge \left( {Q \vee R} \right) \equiv \left( {P \wedge Q} \right) \vee \left( {P \wedge R} \right)\)" 分配律：\(P \vee \left( {Q \wedge R} \right) \equiv \left( {P \vee Q} \right) \wedge \left( {P \vee R} \right)\)，\(P \wedge \left( {Q \vee R} \right) \equiv \left( {P \wedge Q} \right) \vee \left( {P \wedge R} \right)\) Sundstrom, 2022, section 2.2
