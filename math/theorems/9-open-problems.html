              9 Open Problems
              未解决问题              		 (No definition) （无定义） Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-a
              9.1 Millennium Prize Problems
              千禧年大奖难题              		 "The seven Millennium Prize Problems were chosen by the founding Scientific Advisory Board of CMI, which conferred with leading experts worldwide. The focus of the board was on important classic questions that have resisted solution for many years." 七个千禧年大奖难题由 CMI（克雷数学研究所）创始科学顾问委员会与世界各地的顶尖专家商议后选定。委员会关注的是多年来未被解决的重要经典问题。 Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-a
              P vs NP
              P 与 NP 问题              		 "one of the outstanding problems in computer science is determining whether questions exist whose answer can be quickly checked, but which require an impossibly long time to solve by any direct procedure." 计算机科学中悬而未决的问题之一，是判定是否存在答案能被快速验证、却需要长得不可能的时间才能解出的问题（用任何直接的方法）。 Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-b
              Hodge Conjecture
              霍奇猜想              		 "The Hodge conjecture asserts that for particularly nice types of spaces called projective algebraic varieties, the pieces called Hodge cycles are actually (rational linear) combinations of geometric pieces called algebraic cycles." 霍奇猜想断言，对于称为射影代数簇的一类特别好的空间，称为霍奇闭链的部分实际上是称为代数闭链的几何部分的（有理线性）组合。 Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-c
              Poincaré Conjecture
              庞加莱猜想（已解决）              		 "Poincaré, almost a hundred years ago, knew that a two dimensional sphere is essentially characterized by this property of simple connectivity, and asked the corresponding question for the three dimensional sphere." 近一百年前，庞加莱就知道二维球面本质上由单连通这一性质刻画，并对三维球面提出了相应的问题。 Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-d
              Riemann Hypothesis
              黎曼猜想              		 "The Riemann hypothesis asserts that all interesting solutions of the equation ζ(s) = 0 lie on a certain vertical straight line." 黎曼猜想断言，方程 ζ(s) = 0 的所有有意义的解都位于某条竖直的直线上。 Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-e
              Yang-Mills and Mass Gap
              杨-米尔斯与质量间隙              		 "Progress in establishing the existence of the Yang-Mills theory and a mass gap will require the introduction of fundamental new ideas both in physics and in mathematics." 要在确立杨-米尔斯理论和质量间隙的存在性上取得进展，需要在物理和数学两方面引入全新的基本思想。 Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-f
              Navier-Stokes Equation
              纳维-斯托克斯方程              		 "The challenge is to make substantial progress toward a mathematical theory which will unlock the secrets hidden in the Navier-Stokes equations." 挑战在于取得实质性进展，建立一套能揭开纳维-斯托克斯方程中隐藏秘密的数学理论。 Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-g
              Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer Conjecture
              贝赫和斯维讷通-戴尔猜想              		 "the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture asserts that the size of the group of rational points is related to the behavior of an associated zeta function ζ(s) near the point s=1." 贝赫和斯维讷通-戴尔猜想断言，有理点群的大小与相应的 zeta 函数 ζ(s) 在 s=1 附近的性态有关。 Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-h
