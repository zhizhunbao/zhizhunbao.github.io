              2 Operations
              运算              		 (No definition) （无定义） Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(+\) "plus" 加 Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(-\) "minus" 减 Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(\pm\) "plus or minus" 加或减 Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(\mp\) "minus or plus" 减或加 Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(a\,b,\ ab,\ a \cdot b,\ a \times b\) "\(a\) multiplied by \(b\)"
              Note 1: "When multiplication is indicated by a dot, the dot shall be half high: \(a \cdot b\)."              		 \(a\) 乘以 \(b\)
              注 1：用点表示乘法时，点应位于半高处：\(a \cdot b\)。              		 Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(a/b,\ ab^{-1},\ \frac{a}{b}\) "\(a\) divided by \(b\)"
              Note 2: "\(a : b\) is as well used for “divided by”. However, this symbol is mainly used to express ratios such as length scales in maps."              		 \(a\) 除以 \(b\)
              注 2：\(a : b\) 也用来表示“除以”。不过这个符号主要用于表示比例，例如地图的比例尺。              		 Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(\lvert a \rvert\) "magnitude of \(a\)" \(a\) 的大小（绝对值） Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(a^n\) "\(a\) to the power \(n\)" \(a\) 的 \(n\) 次方 Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(\sqrt{a},\ a^{1/2},\ \sqrt{a^2+b^2},\ (a^2+b^2)^{1/2}\) "square root of \(a\), and of \(a^2 + b^2\)" \(a\) 的平方根，以及 \(a^2 + b^2\) 的平方根 Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(a^{1/n},\ \sqrt[n]{a}\) "$n$th root of \(a\)" \(a\) 的 \(n\) 次方根 Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(\langle a \rangle,\ \bar{a}\) "mean value of \(a\)" \(a\) 的平均值 Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(\operatorname{sgn} a\) "sign of \(a\) (equal to \(a/\lvert a \rvert\) if \(a \neq 0\), 0 if \(a = 0\))" \(a\) 的符号（\(a \neq 0\) 时等于 \(a/\lvert a \rvert\)，\(a = 0\) 时为 0） Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(n!\) "\(n\) factorial" \(n\) 的阶乘 Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(C^n_p,\ \binom{n}{p}\) "binominal coefficient, \(n!/p!(n-p)!\)" 二项式系数，\(n!/p!(n-p)!\) Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(\sum a_i,\ \sum_i a_i,\ \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i\) "sum of \(a_i\)" \(a_i\) 的和 Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
              \(\prod a_i,\ \prod_i a_i,\ \prod_{i=1}^{n} a_i\) "product of \(a_i\)" \(a_i\) 的积 Cohen et al., 2007, section 4.2, p. 105
