              4 Probability
              概率              		 "Probability axioms state that, given an event E in a sample space S which is either finite with N elements or countably infinite with N=∞ elements, we can write S=⋃_(i=1)^N E_i, and a quantity P(E_i), called the probability of event E_i, is defined such that" 概率公理说：给定样本空间 S（有限，有 N 个元素；或可数无限，N = ∞）中的事件 E，可以写成 S = ⋃ Eᵢ，并且定义一个量 P(Eᵢ)，称为事件 Eᵢ 的概率，使得 Weisstein, n.d., "Probability axioms" entry
              Probability axiom 1
              概率公理 1（非负且不超过 1）              		 "0≤P(E_i)≤1." 每个事件的概率在 0 和 1 之间。 Weisstein, n.d., "Probability axioms" entry
              Probability axiom 2
              概率公理 2（规范性）              		 "P(S)=1." 整个样本空间的概率是 1。 Weisstein, n.d., "Probability axioms" entry
              Additivity
              可加性              		 "P(E_1 ∪ E_2)=P(E_1)+P(E_2), where E_1 and E_2 are mutually exclusive." E₁、E₂ 互斥时，P(E₁ ∪ E₂) = P(E₁) + P(E₂)。 Weisstein, n.d., "Probability axioms" entry
              Countable additivity
              可数可加性              		 "P(⋃(i=1)^n E_i)=∑(i=1)^n P(E_i) for n=1, 2, ..., N where E_1, E_2, ... are mutually exclusive (i.e., E_1 ∩ E_2=∅)." E₁、E₂、…两两互斥时，并集的概率等于各自概率之和（n 可以到 N，包括无穷）。 Weisstein, n.d., "Probability axioms" entry
