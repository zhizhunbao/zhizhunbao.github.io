              Mathematical Sciences
              数学科学              		 "A strong mathematical foundation remains a bedrock of computer science education and infuses the practice of computing whether in developing algorithms, designing systems, modeling real-world phenomena, or computing with data." 扎实的数学基础依然是计算机科学教育的基石，并贯穿计算实践的方方面面，无论是开发算法、设计系统、为现实世界的现象建模，还是用数据进行计算。 Joint Task Force on Computing Curricula, 2024, MSF "Preamble" section, para. 1
              MSF
              数学与统计基础              		 "This Mathematical and Statistical Foundations (MSF) knowledge area – the successor to the ACM CS2013 curriculum's 'Discrete Structures' area – seeks to identify the mathematical and statistical material that undergirds modern computer science." 数学与统计基础（MSF）这一知识领域是 ACM CS2013 课程体系中"离散结构"领域的继任者，旨在找出数学和统计内容，它们支撑着现代计算机科学。 Joint Task Force on Computing Curricula, 2024, MSF "Preamble" section, para. 1
              MSC2020
              MSC2020              		 "Mathematical Reviews (MR) and zbMATH collaborate on maintaining the Mathematics Subject Classification, which is used by these reviewing services, publishers, funding agencies, and others to categorize items in the mathematical sciences literature. It is a taxonomy created by and for mathematical researchers." Mathematical Reviews（MR）和 zbMATH 共同维护数学学科分类，这些评论服务、出版社、资助机构等用它来给数学科学文献分类。它是由数学研究者创建、为数学研究者服务的分类体系。 Associate Editors of Mathematical Reviews and zbMATH, 2020, para. 1

              Axioms
              公理              		 "An axiom is a fundamental assumption that provides a starting point for reasoning. The axioms for (essentially) all of mathematics can be conveniently divided into three groups: propositional calculus, predicate calculus, and set theory." 公理是为推理提供起点的基本假设。（基本上）全部数学的公理可以方便地分为三组：命题演算、谓词演算和集合论。 Megill, 2026, "The Axioms" section
