# 9 Open Problems

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|

| **[9 Open Problems](9-open-problems.md)**<br>未解决问题 | (No definition) | （无定义） | [Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-a](https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/) |
| **9.1 Millennium Prize Problems**<br>千禧年大奖难题 | "The seven Millennium Prize Problems were chosen by the founding Scientific Advisory Board of CMI, which conferred with leading experts worldwide. The focus of the board was on **important classic questions that have resisted solution for many years**." | 七个千禧年大奖难题由 CMI（克雷数学研究所）创始科学顾问委员会与世界各地的顶尖专家商议后选定。委员会关注的是**多年来未被解决的重要经典问题**。 | [Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-a](https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/) |
| **P vs NP**<br>P 与 NP 问题 | "one of the outstanding problems in computer science is determining **whether questions exist whose answer can be quickly checked, but which require an impossibly long time to solve** by any direct procedure." | 计算机科学中悬而未决的问题之一，是判定**是否存在答案能被快速验证、却需要长得不可能的时间才能解出的问题**（用任何直接的方法）。 | [Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-b](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/p-vs-np/) |
| **Hodge Conjecture**<br>霍奇猜想 | "The Hodge conjecture asserts that for particularly nice types of spaces called projective algebraic varieties, **the pieces called Hodge cycles are actually (rational linear) combinations of geometric pieces called algebraic cycles**." | 霍奇猜想断言，对于称为射影代数簇的一类特别好的空间，**称为霍奇闭链的部分实际上是称为代数闭链的几何部分的（有理线性）组合**。 | [Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-c](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/hodge-conjecture/) |
| **Poincaré Conjecture**<br>庞加莱猜想（已解决） | "Poincaré, almost a hundred years ago, knew that **a two dimensional sphere is essentially characterized by this property of simple connectivity**, and **asked the corresponding question for the three dimensional sphere**." | 近一百年前，庞加莱就知道**二维球面本质上由单连通这一性质刻画**，并**对三维球面提出了相应的问题**。 | [Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-d](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/poincare-conjecture/) |
| **Riemann Hypothesis**<br>黎曼猜想 | "The Riemann hypothesis asserts that **all *interesting* solutions of the equation ζ(s) = 0 lie on a certain vertical straight line**." | 黎曼猜想断言，**方程 ζ(s) = 0 的所有*有意义的*解都位于某条竖直的直线上**。 | [Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-e](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/riemann-hypothesis/) |
| **Yang-Mills and Mass Gap**<br>杨-米尔斯与质量间隙 | "Progress in **establishing the existence of the Yang-Mills theory and a mass gap** will require the introduction of fundamental new ideas both in physics and in mathematics." | 要在**确立杨-米尔斯理论和质量间隙的存在性**上取得进展，需要在物理和数学两方面引入全新的基本思想。 | [Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-f](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/yang-mills-the-maths-gap/) |
| **Navier-Stokes Equation**<br>纳维-斯托克斯方程 | "The challenge is to make substantial progress toward **a mathematical theory which will unlock the secrets hidden in the Navier-Stokes equations**." | 挑战在于取得实质性进展，建立**一套能揭开纳维-斯托克斯方程中隐藏秘密的数学理论**。 | [Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-g](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/navier-stokes-equation/) |
| **Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer Conjecture**<br>贝赫和斯维讷通-戴尔猜想 | "the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture asserts that **the size of the group of rational points is related to the behavior of an associated zeta function ζ(s) near the point s=1**." | 贝赫和斯维讷通-戴尔猜想断言，**有理点群的大小与相应的 zeta 函数 ζ(s) 在 s=1 附近的性态有关**。 | [Clay Mathematics Institute, n.d.-h](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/birch-and-swinnerton-dyer-conjecture/) |
