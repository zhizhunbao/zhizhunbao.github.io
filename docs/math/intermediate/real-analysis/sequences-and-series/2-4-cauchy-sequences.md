| **Cauchy Sequence**<br>柯西序列 | "A sequence $\{ x_n \}_{n=1}^\infty$ is a **Cauchy sequence** if **for every $\epsilon > 0$ there exists an $M \in \mathbb{N}$ such that for all $n \geq M$ and all $k \geq M$, we have $\lvert x_n - x_k \rvert < \epsilon$**." | 若**对每个 $\epsilon > 0$，存在 $M \in \mathbb{N}$，使得对所有 $n \geq M$ 和所有 $k \geq M$ 有 $\lvert x_n - x_k \rvert < \epsilon$**，则序列 $\{ x_n \}_{n=1}^\infty$ 是**柯西序列**。 | [Lebl, 2026, Definition 2.4.1](https://www.jirka.org/ra/html/sec_cauchy.html#sec_cauchy-4) |
