| **Proof by Induction**<br>归纳法证明 | "Sometimes we need a way to **prove a statement for all integers $n$ after a certain point**, for instance integers greater than or equal to $n=1$. | **Divisibility**<br>整除 | "If an integer $n$ can be written as **a product $kd=n$ of two integers $k$ and $d$**, then we say that $d$ **divides** $n$, or that $n$ is **divisible** by $d$, or that $d$ is a divisor of $n$. We write $d\mid n$ to denote that $d$ divides $n$." | 若整数 $n$ 能写成**两个整数 $k$ 与 $d$ 的乘积 $kd=n$**，则称 $d$ **整除** $n$，或 $n$ **能被** $d$ **整除**，或 $d$ 是 $n$ 的因数。我们用 $d\mid n$ 表示 $d$ 整除 $n$。 | [Crisman, 2024, Definition 1.2.5](https://math.gordon.edu/ntic/ntic/first-review.html#defn-divisibility) |
| **Divisor**<br>因数 | "If an integer $n$ can be written as **a product $kd=n$ of two integers $k$ and $d$**, then we say that $d$ divides $n$, or that $n$ is divisible by $d$, or that $d$ is a **divisor** of $n$." | 若整数 $n$ 能写成**两个整数 $k$ 与 $d$ 的乘积 $kd=n$**，则称 $d$ 整除 $n$，或 $n$ 能被 $d$ 整除，或 $d$ 是 $n$ 的**因数**。 | [Crisman, 2024, Definition 1.2.5](https://math.gordon.edu/ntic/ntic/first-review.html#defn-divisibility) |
| **Proper Divisor**<br>真因数 | "We occasionally use the term **proper divisor** to denote **a positive divisor of $n$ which is not $n$**." | 我们有时用**真因数**这个词来表示 **$n$ 的不等于 $n$ 的正因数**。 | [Crisman, 2024, Example 1.2.6](https://math.gordon.edu/ntic/ntic/first-review.html#example-divisibility) |
| **Direct Proof**<br>直接证明 | "These are not hard to prove (see Exercise 1.4.1) using **direct proof**, where **no indirect or inductive steps are needed**." | 这些用**直接证明**不难证明（见习题 1.4.1），**不需要任何间接或归纳的步骤**。 | [Crisman, 2024, subsection 1.2.3](https://math.gordon.edu/ntic/ntic/first-review.html#subsection-divisibility-8) |
