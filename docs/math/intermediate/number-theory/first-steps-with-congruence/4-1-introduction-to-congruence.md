| **Congruence**<br>同余 | "We say that a number $a$ **is congruent to** $b$ (another number) **modulo $n$**, or $a \equiv b \text{ (mod }n)$, **precisely if $n\mid (a-b)$**. We call $n$, normally a positive integer greater than one, the modulus. The noun form of the relationship is called **congruence**." | 若**恰好 $n\mid (a-b)$**，就说数 $a$ **模 $n$ 同余于** $b$（另一个数），记作 $a \equiv b \text{ (mod }n)$。我们把 $n$（通常是大于一的正整数）称为模。这一关系的名词形式称为**同余**。 | [Crisman, 2024, Definition 4.1.1](https://math.gordon.edu/ntic/ntic/section-intro-congruence.html#definition-congruence) |
| **Modulus**<br>模 | "We say that a number $a$ is congruent to $b$ (another number) modulo $n$, or $a \equiv b \text{ (mod }n)$, precisely if $n\mid (a-b)$. We call **$n$, normally a positive integer greater than one**, the **modulus**." | 若 $n\mid (a-b)$，就说数 $a$ 模 $n$ 同余于 $b$（另一个数），记作 $a \equiv b \text{ (mod }n)$。我们把 **$n$**（**通常是大于一的正整数**）称为**模**。 | [Crisman, 2024, Definition 4.1.1](https://math.gordon.edu/ntic/ntic/section-intro-congruence.html#definition-congruence) |

| **Congruence-Remainder**<br>同余与余数 | "Saying $a \equiv b\text{ (mod }n)$ is **exactly the same thing as saying $a$ and $b$ leave the same remainder when divided by $n$**." | 说 $a \equiv b\text{ (mod }n)$，**与说 $a$ 和 $b$ 除以 $n$ 余数相同完全是一回事**。 | [Crisman, 2024, Lemma 4.1.2](https://math.gordon.edu/ntic/ntic/section-intro-congruence.html#lemma-congruence-remainder) |
