| **Coefficient**<br>系数 | "A linear equation in the unknowns $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ may be written in the form $a_1x_1 + a_2x_2 + \ldots + a_nx_n = b$, where $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n$ are **real numbers known as coefficients**." | 关于未知量 $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ 的线性方程可以写成形如 $a_1x_1 + a_2x_2 + \ldots + a_nx_n = b$ 的形式，其中 $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n$ 是**称为系数的实数**。 | [Austin, 2023, section 1.1](https://understandinglinearalgebra.org/sec-expect.html#sec-expect-4-5-1-1) |
