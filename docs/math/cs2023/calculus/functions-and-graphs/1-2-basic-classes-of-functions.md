| **Transcendental Function**<br>超越函数 | "a function that **cannot be expressed by a combination of basic arithmetic operations**" | **不能用基本算术运算的组合表示**的函数 | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 1 Key Terms](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/1-key-terms#fs-id1170573596940) |
