| **Instantaneous Velocity**<br>瞬时速度 | "The instantaneous velocity of an object with a position function that is given by $s(t)$ is **the value that the average velocities** on intervals of the form $[t,a]$ and $[a,t]$ approach as the values of t move closer to $a,$ provided such a value exists" | 位置函数为 $s(t)$ 的物体的瞬时速度，是当 t 的值越来越接近 $a,$ 时，在形如 $[t,a]$ 和 $[a,t]$ 的区间上**平均速度所趋近的值**，前提是这样的值存在 | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 2 Key Terms](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/2-key-terms#fs-id1170571100400) |

| **Limit**<br>极限 | "**the process of letting x or t approach a** in an expression; the limit of a function $f(x)$ as x approaches a is the value that $f(x)$ approaches as x approaches a" | 在表达式中**让 x 或 t 趋近 a 的过程**；当 x 趋近 a 时函数 $f(x)$ 的极限，是 x 趋近 a 时 $f(x)$ 所趋近的值 | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 2 Key Terms](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/2-key-terms#fs-id1170573410594) |

| **Secant**<br>割线 | "A secant line to a function $f(x)$ at a is **a line through the point** $(a,f(a))$ and another point on the function; the slope of the secant line is given by $m_{\text{sec}}=\frac{f(x)-f(a)}{x-a}$" | 函数 $f(x)$ 在 a 处的割线是**经过点** $(a,f(a))$ 和函数上另一点的直线；割线的斜率为 $m_{\text{sec}}=\frac{f(x)-f(a)}{x-a}$ | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 2 Key Terms](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/2-key-terms#fs-id1170573753167) |
| **Tangent**<br>切线 | "A tangent line to the graph of a function at a point $(a,f(a))$ is the line that **secant lines through** $(a,f(a))$ approach as they are taken through points on the function with x-values that approach a; the slope of the tangent line to a graph at a **measures the rate of change** of the function at a" | 函数图像在点 $(a,f(a))$ 处的切线，是当所取函数上的点的 x 值趋近 a 时，**经过 $(a,f(a))$ 的割线**所趋近的直线；图像在 a 处切线的斜率**度量函数在 a 处的变化率** | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 2 Key Terms](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/2-key-terms#fs-id1170573396339) |
