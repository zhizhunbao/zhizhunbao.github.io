| **Moment**<br>矩 | "if n masses are arranged on a number line, **the moment of the system with respect to the origin** is given by $M=\sum_{i=1}^{n}m_{i}x_{i};$ if, instead, we consider a region in the plane, bounded above by a function $f(x)$ over an interval $[a,b],$ then the moments of the region with respect to the x- and y-axes are given by $M_{x}=\rho {\int }_{a}^{b}\frac{{[f(x)]}^{2}}{2}dx$ and $M_{y}=\rho {\int }_{a}^{b}xf(x)dx,$ respectively" | 若 n 个质量排在数轴上，**系统关于原点的矩**为 $M=\sum_{i=1}^{n}m_{i}x_{i};$ 若改为考虑平面上的区域，上方由区间 $[a,b],$ 上的函数 $f(x)$ 围成，则区域关于 x 轴和 y 轴的矩分别为 $M_{x}=\rho {\int }_{a}^{b}\frac{{[f(x)]}^{2}}{2}dx$ 和 $M_{y}=\rho {\int }_{a}^{b}xf(x)dx,$ | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 6 Key Terms](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/6-key-terms#fs-id1167793280349) |
| **Symmetry Principle**<br>对称原理 | "the symmetry principle states that if a region R is symmetric about a line l, then **the centroid of R lies on l**" | 对称原理指出，若区域 R 关于直线 l 对称，则 **R 的形心在 l 上** | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 6 Key Terms](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/6-key-terms#fs-id1167793630122) |
| **Theorem of Pappus for Volume**<br>帕普斯体积定理 | "this theorem states that the volume of a solid of revolution formed by revolving a region around an external axis is equal to **the area of the region multiplied by the distance traveled by the centroid of the region**" | 这条定理指出，区域绕外部轴旋转所成旋转体的体积，等于**区域面积乘以区域形心经过的距离** | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 6 Key Terms](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/6-key-terms#fs-id1167793630152) |
