| **Density Function**<br>密度函数 | "a density function **describes how mass is distributed throughout an object**; it can be a linear density, expressed in terms of mass per unit length; an area density, expressed in terms of mass per unit area; or a volume density, expressed in terms of mass per unit volume; weight-density is also used to describe weight (rather than mass) per unit volume" | 密度函数**描述质量在物体中如何分布**；可以是线密度，用单位长度的质量表示；面密度，用单位面积的质量表示；或体密度，用单位体积的质量表示；重量密度也用来描述单位体积的重量（而不是质量） | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 6 Key Terms](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/6-key-terms#fs-id1167793976005) |
| **Hooke’s Law**<br>胡克定律 | "this law states that the force required to compress (or elongate) a spring **is proportional to the distance** the spring has been compressed (or stretched) from equilibrium; in other words, $F=kx,$ where $k$ is a constant" | 这条定律指出，压缩（或拉长）弹簧所需的力**与弹簧偏离平衡位置被压缩（或拉伸）的距离成正比**；换句话说，$F=kx,$ 其中 $k$ 为常数 | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 6 Key Terms](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/6-key-terms#fs-id1167793609795) |
| **Hydrostatic Pressure**<br>静水压力 | "**the pressure exerted by water** on a submerged object" | **水对浸没物体施加的压力** | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 6 Key Terms](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/6-key-terms#fs-id1167793609825) |
| **Work**<br>功 | "**the amount of energy it takes to move an object**; in physics, when a force is constant, work is expressed as the product of force and distance" | **移动物体所需的能量**；在物理中，力为常量时，功表示为力与距离之积 | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, chapter 6 Key Terms](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/6-key-terms#fs-id1167793609834) |
| **Area Density**<br>面密度 | "We assume the density is given in terms of mass per unit area (called **area density**), and further assume the density varies only along the disk’s radius (called radial density)." | 我们假定密度用单位面积的质量给出（称为**面密度**），并进一步假定密度只沿圆盘半径变化（称为径向密度）。 | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, section 6.5](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/6-5-physical-applications#term-00002) |
| **Radial Density**<br>径向密度 | "We assume the density is given in terms of mass per unit area (called area density), and further assume the density varies only along the disk’s radius (called **radial density**)." | 我们假定密度用单位面积的质量给出（称为面密度），并进一步假定密度只沿圆盘半径变化（称为**径向密度**）。 | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, section 6.5](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/6-5-physical-applications#term-00003) |
| **Joule**<br>焦耳 | "This same unit is also called **the joule**." | 这个单位也称为**焦耳**。 | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, section 6.5](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/6-5-physical-applications#term-00004) |
| **Spring Constant**<br>弹簧常数 | "The constant $k$ is called **the spring constant** and is always positive." | 常数 $k$ 称为**弹簧常数**，并且总是正的。 | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, section 6.5](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/6-5-physical-applications#term-00007) |
| **Pascals**<br>帕斯卡 | "In the metric system we have newtons per square meter, also called **pascals**." | 在公制中我们用牛顿每平方米，也称为**帕斯卡**。 | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, section 6.5](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/6-5-physical-applications#term-00008) |
| **Pascal’s Principle**<br>帕斯卡原理 | "By Pascal’s principle, **the pressure at a given depth is the same in all directions**, so it does not matter if the plate is submerged horizontally or vertically." | 根据帕斯卡原理，**给定深度处各个方向的压强相同**，所以板是水平还是竖直浸没都没有关系。 | [Strang & Herman, 2016a, section 6.5](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/6-5-physical-applications#term-00010) |
