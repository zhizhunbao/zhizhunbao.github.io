| **Conditional Probability**<br>条件概率 | "The probability that event $A$ occurs given that event $B$ has already occurred is called the **conditional probability** of $A$ given $B$. The probability it rains on Monday given that it rained on Sunday would be written as $P(\text{Rain on Monday} \vert \text{Rain on Sunday})$." | 在事件 $B$ 已经发生的条件下事件 $A$ 发生的概率称为给定 $B$ 时 $A$ 的**条件概率**。用符号表示为 $P(A \vert B)$。在周日下雨的条件下周一下雨的概率应写作 $P(\text{Rain on Monday} \vert \text{Rain on Sunday})$。 | [Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary](https://onlinestatbook.com/2/glossary/conditional_p.html) |
| **Favorable Outcome**<br>有利结果 | "A **favorable outcome** is the outcome of interest. For example one could define a favorable outcome in the flip of a coin as a head. | **Independent Events**<br>独立事件 | "Events $A$ and $B$ are **independent events** if the probability of Event $B$ occurring is the same whether or not Event $A$ occurs. For example, if you throw two dice, the probability that the second die comes up 1 is independent of whether the first die came up 1. Formally, this can be stated in terms of conditional probabilities : $P(A \vert B) = P(A)$ and $P(B \vert A) = P(B)$." | 如果事件 $B$ 发生的概率无论事件 $A$ 是否发生都相同，则事件 $A$ 与 $B$ 是**独立事件**。例如，如果掷两个骰子，第二个骰子出现 1 的概率与第一个骰子是否出现 1 无关。形式上，这可以用条件概率表述为：$P(A \vert B) = P(A)$ 且 $P(B \vert A) = P(B)$。 | [Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary](https://onlinestatbook.com/2/glossary/independent_events.html) |

| **False Positive**<br>假阳性 | "A **false positive** occurs when **a diagnostic procedure returns a positive result while the true state of the subject is negative**. For example, if a test for strep says the patient has strep when in fact he or she does not, then the error in diagnosis would be called a false positive. In some contexts, a false positive is called a false alarm. The concept is similar to a Type I error in significance testing." | 当**诊断程序给出阳性结果而受试者的真实状态为阴性**时，就出现**假阳性**。例如，如果链球菌检测显示患者感染了链球菌，而实际上并没有，那么这种诊断错误就称为假阳性。在某些情境中，假阳性也称为假警报。这一概念类似于显著性检验中的第一类错误。 | [Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary](https://onlinestatbook.com/2/glossary/false_positive.html) |
| **Misses**<br>漏报 | "**Misses** occur when a diagnostic test returns a negative result, but the true state of the subject is positive. For example, if a person has strep throat and the diagnostic test fails to indicate it, then a miss has occurred. The concept is similar to a Type II error in significance testing." | 当诊断测试返回阴性结果而受试者的真实状态为阳性时，就出现**漏报**。例如，如果一个人患有链球菌咽喉炎而诊断测试未能显示，就发生了一次漏报。这一概念类似于显著性检验中的第二类错误。 | [Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary](https://onlinestatbook.com/2/glossary/misses.html) |
| **Posterior Probability**<br>后验概率 | "The **posterior probability** of an event is the probability of the event computed following the collection of new data. One begins with a prior probability of an event and revises it in the light of new data. For example, if 0.01 of a population has schizophrenia then the probability that a person drawn at random would have schizophrenia is 0.01. This is the prior probability. If you then learn that their score on a personality test suggests the person is schizophrenic, you would adjust your probability accordingly. The adjusted probability is the posterior probability." | 事件的**后验概率**是在收集新数据之后计算得到的该事件的概率。人们从事件的先验概率出发，根据新数据对其进行修正。例如，如果总体中有 0.01 的人患精神分裂症，则随机抽取的一个人患精神分裂症的概率为 0.01。这就是先验概率。如果随后得知此人在人格测验中的得分表明其患有精神分裂症，就会相应地调整概率。调整后的概率就是后验概率。 | [Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary](https://onlinestatbook.com/2/glossary/posterior_probability.html) |
| **Prior Probability**<br>先验概率 | "The **prior probability** of an event is the probability of the event computed before the collection of new data. One begins with a prior probability of an event and revises it in the light of new data. For example, if 0.01 of a population has schizophrenia then the probability that a person drawn at random would have schizophrenia is 0.01. This is the prior probability. If you then learn that that there score on a personality test suggests the person is schizophrenic, you would adjust your probability accordingly. The adjusted probability is the posterior probability." | 事件的**先验概率**是在收集新数据之前计算得到的该事件的概率。人们从事件的先验概率出发，根据新数据对其进行修正。例如，如果总体中有 0.01 的人患精神分裂症，则随机抽取的一个人患精神分裂症的概率为 0.01。这就是先验概率。如果随后得知此人在人格测验中的得分表明其患有精神分裂症，就会相应地调整概率。调整后的概率就是后验概率。 | [Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary](https://onlinestatbook.com/2/glossary/prior_probability.html) |
