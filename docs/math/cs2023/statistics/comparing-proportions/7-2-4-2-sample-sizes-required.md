# 7.2.4.2 Sample Sizes Required

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **7.2.4.2 Sample Sizes Required**<br>所需样本量 | (No definition) | （无定义） | [NIST/SEMATECH, n.d., section 7.2.4.2](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc242.htm) |
| **Change in the Proportion Defective**<br>不合格比例的变化 | "Define $\delta$ as **the change in the proportion defective** that we are **interested in detecting** $\delta = \vert p_1 - p_0\vert$." | 把 $\delta$ 定义为我们**想要检测出**的**不合格比例的变化** $\delta = \vert p_1 - p_0\vert$。 | [NIST/SEMATECH, n.d., section 7.2.4.2, "Problem formulation"](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc242.htm) |
| **Minimum Sample Size**<br>最小样本量 | "Thus, the **minimum sample size** is For **a two-sided interval** $N \ge \left( \frac{z_{1-\alpha/2} \, \sqrt{p_0 (1-p_0)} + z_{1-\beta} \, \sqrt{p_1 (1-p_1)}}{\delta} \, \right)^2 \, .$" | 因此，对于**双侧区间**，**最小样本量**为 $N \ge \left( \frac{z_{1-\alpha/2} \, \sqrt{p_0 (1-p_0)} + z_{1-\beta} \, \sqrt{p_1 (1-p_1)}}{\delta} \, \right)^2$。 | [NIST/SEMATECH, n.d., section 7.2.4.2, "Minimum sample size"](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc242.htm) |
| **Continuity Correction**<br>连续性校正 | "Fleiss, Levin, and Paik also recommend the following **continuity correction**, $N = N' + 1/\delta \, ,$ with $N'$ denoting **the sample size computed using the above formula**." | Fleiss、Levin 和 Paik 还建议采用如下**连续性校正**：$N = N' + 1/\delta$，其中 $N'$ 表示**用上面公式算出的样本量**。 | [NIST/SEMATECH, n.d., section 7.2.4.2, "Continuity correction"](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc242.htm) |
