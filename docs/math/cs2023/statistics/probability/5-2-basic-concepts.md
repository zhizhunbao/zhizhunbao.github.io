| **Conditional Probability**<br>条件概率 | "The probability that event $A$ occurs given that event $B$ has already occurred is called the **conditional probability** of $A$ given $B$. Symbolically, this is written as $P(A \vert B)$. The probability it rains on Monday given that it rained on Sunday would be written as $P(\text{Rain on Monday} \vert \text{Rain on Sunday})$." | 在事件 $B$ 已经发生的条件下事件 $A$ 发生的概率称为给定 $B$ 时 $A$ 的**条件概率**。用符号表示为 $P(A \vert B)$。在周日下雨的条件下周一下雨的概率应写作 $P(\text{Rain on Monday} \vert \text{Rain on Sunday})$。 | [Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary](https://onlinestatbook.com/2/glossary/conditional_p.html) |
| **Favorable Outcome**<br>有利结果 | "A **favorable outcome** is the outcome of interest. For example one could define a favorable outcome in the flip of a coin as a head. The term "favorable outcome" does not necessarily mean that the outcome is desirable – in some experiments, the favorable outcome could be the failure of a test, or the occurrence of an undesirable event." | **有利结果**是所关注的结果。例如，可以把抛硬币时的有利结果定义为正面。“有利结果”一词不一定意味着该结果是令人满意的——在某些实验中，有利结果可能是测试失败，或不良事件的发生。 | [Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary](https://onlinestatbook.com/2/glossary/favorable_outcome.html) |
| **Independent Events**<br>独立事件 | "Events $A$ and $B$ are **independent events** if the probability of Event $B$ occurring is the same whether or not Event $A$ occurs. For example, if you throw two dice, the probability that the second die comes up 1 is independent of whether the first die came up 1. Formally, this can be stated in terms of conditional probabilities : $P(A \vert B) = P(A)$ and $P(B \vert A) = P(B)$." | 如果事件 $B$ 发生的概率无论事件 $A$ 是否发生都相同，则事件 $A$ 与 $B$ 是**独立事件**。例如，如果掷两个骰子，第二个骰子出现 1 的概率与第一个骰子是否出现 1 无关。形式上，这可以用条件概率表述为：$P(A \vert B) = P(A)$ 且 $P(B \vert A) = P(B)$。 | [Online Statistics Education, n.d., glossary](https://onlinestatbook.com/2/glossary/independent_events.html) |
