## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MSF**<br>数学与统计基础 | "This Mathematical and Statistical Foundations (MSF) knowledge area – the successor to the ACM CS2013 curriculum's 'Discrete Structures' area – seeks to identify the **mathematical and statistical material** that **undergirds modern computer science**." | 数学与统计基础（MSF）这一知识领域是 ACM CS2013 课程体系中"离散结构"领域的继任者，旨在找出**数学和统计内容**，它们**支撑着现代计算机科学**。 | [Joint Task Force on Computing Curricula, 2024, MSF "Preamble" section, para. 1](https://csed.acm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/3.1-Body-of-Knowledge-1.pdf#page=123) |
| **[Discrete Mathematics](discrete-mathematics/index.md)**<br>离散数学 | "Discrete mathematics is the branch of mathematics dealing with objects that can assume **only distinct, separated values**." | 离散数学是处理一类对象的数学分支，这些对象**只能取彼此分开的不同值**。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-b, "Discrete mathematics" entry, para. 1](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/DiscreteMathematics.html#:~:text=dealing%20with%20objects%20that%20can%20assume%20only%20distinct) |
| **[Probability](probability/index.md)**<br>概率 | "Probability is the branch of mathematics that studies the **possible outcomes of given events** together with the outcomes' **relative likelihoods and distributions**." | 概率是数学的一个分支，研究**给定事件的可能结果**，以及这些结果的**相对可能性和分布**。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-d, "Probability" entry, para. 1](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/Probability.html#:~:text=Probability%20is%20the%20branch%20of%20mathematics%20that%20studies%20the%20possible%20outcomes) |
| **[Statistics](statistics/index.md)**<br>统计 | "The mathematical study of the **likelihood and probability of events** occurring based on known information and inferred by **taking a limited number of samples**." | 对事件发生的**可能性和概率**所作的数学研究，依据已知信息，并通过**抽取有限数量的样本**来推断。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-e, "Statistics" entry, para. 1](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/Statistics.html#:~:text=The%20mathematical%20study%20of%20the%20likelihood%20and%20probability%20of%20events) |
| **[Linear Algebra](linear-algebra/index.md)**<br>线性代数 | "Linear algebra is the study of **sets of linear equations** and their **transformation properties**." | 线性代数研究**线性方程组**及其**变换性质**。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-c, "Linear algebra" entry, para. 1](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/LinearAlgebra.html#:~:text=Linear%20algebra%20is%20the%20study%20of%20sets%20of%20linear%20equations) |
| **[Calculus](calculus/index.md)**<br>微积分 | "'The' calculus, more properly called analysis (or real analysis or, in older literature, infinitesimal analysis), is the branch of mathematics studying the **rate of change of quantities** (which can be interpreted as slopes of curves) and the **length, area, and volume of objects**." | 这门微积分更恰当的名称是分析（或实分析，旧文献中称无穷小分析），是研究**量的变化率**（可解释为曲线的斜率）以及**物体的长度、面积和体积**的数学分支。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-a, "Calculus" entry, para. 2](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/Calculus.html#:~:text=is%20the%20branch%20of%20mathematics%20studying%20the%20rate%20of%20change) |
