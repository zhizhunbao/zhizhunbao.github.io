| **Properties of Power Series**<br>幂级数的性质 | "Let $[R; +, \cdot ]$ be a ring. Then: $R[[x]]$ is a ring under the operations of power series addition and multiplication, which depend on the operations in R. If R is a commutative ring, then $R[[x]]$ is a commutative ring. If R is a ring with unity, $1$, then $R[[x]]$ is a ring with unity (the unity in R[x] is $1 + 0x + 0 x^2 + \cdots$). If R is an integral domain, then $R[[x]]$ is an integral domain. If F is a field, then $F[[x]]$ is not a field. However, $F[[x]]$ is an integral domain." | 设 $[R; +, \cdot ]$ 是环。则：在幂级数加法和乘法运算（依赖于 R 中的运算）下，$R[[x]]$ 是环。若 R 是交换环，则 $R[[x]]$ 是交换环。若 R 是有单位元 $1$ 的环，则 $R[[x]]$ 是有单位元的环（R[x] 中的单位元是 $1 + 0x + 0 x^2 + \cdots$）。若 R 是整环，则 $R[[x]]$ 是整环。若 F 是域，则 $F[[x]]$ 不是域。但 $F[[x]]$ 是整环。 | [Doerr & Levasseur, n.d., Theorem 16.5.5](https://discretemath.org/ads/s-power-series.html#theorem-power-series-properties) |
