Tier: CS Core.

## Classification

![Permutations, combinations, counting, pigeonhole principle: its 4 terms in one column, in the order CS2023 names them: Permutation, Combination, Counting, Pigeonhole principle.](media/permutations-combinations-counting-pigeonhole-principle.png)

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Permutation**<br>排列 | "A permutation of a set S is a **one-to-one correspondence of S with itself**." | 集合 S 的排列是 **S 与它自身之间的一一对应**。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-d, "Permutation" entry, para. 1](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/Permutation.html#:~:text=is%20a%20one%2Dto%2Done%20correspondence%20of) |
| **Combination**<br>组合 | "The number of ways of **picking k unordered outcomes** from **n possibilities**." | **选出 k 个无序结果**的方式数，这些结果取自 **n 种可能**。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-a, "Combination" entry, para. 1](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/Combination.html#:~:text=The%20number%20of%20ways%20of%20picking) |
| **Counting**<br>计数 | (context) "Enumerative combinatorics is the part concerned specifically with **counting finite sets of objects** satisfying **prescribed conditions**." | （语境）枚举组合学是专门研究**对有限的对象集合计数**的部分，这些对象满足**规定的条件**。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-b, "Combinatorics" entry, para. 2](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/Combinatorics.html#:~:text=Enumerative%20combinatorics%20is%20the%20part%20concerned%20specifically%20with%20counting) |
| **Pigeonhole principle**<br>鸽巢原理 | "A.k.a. the pigeonhole principle. Given n boxes and m>n objects, **at least one box must contain more than one object**." | 又称鸽巢原理。给定 n 个盒子和 m>n 个物体，**至少有一个盒子必须装有多于一个物体**。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-c, "Dirichlet's box principle" entry, para. 1](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/DirichletsBoxPrinciple.html#:~:text=at%20least%20one%20box%20must%20contain%20more%20than%20one%20object) |
