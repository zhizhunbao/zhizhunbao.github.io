# 1.7 Graphs

## Classification

![Graphs: its 2 terms in one column, in the order CS2023 names them: Graph, Tree.](media/graphs.png)

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Graph**<br>图 | "In a mathematician's terminology, a graph is a **collection of points and lines** connecting **some (possibly empty) subset of them**." | 按数学家的术语，图是**点和线的集合**，这些线连接**其中某个子集的点**（子集可能为空）。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-a, "Graph" entry, para. 3](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/Graph.html#:~:text=a%20graph%20is%20a%20collection%20of%20points%20and%20lines) |
| **Tree**<br>树 | "A tree is a **finite simple graph** with at least one graph vertex that is **undirected, connected, and acyclic** (or, equivalently, a connected forest)." | 树是至少有一个顶点的**有限简单图**，它**无向、连通且无环**（等价地说，是一个连通的森林）。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-b, "Tree" entry, para. 1](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/Tree.html#:~:text=A%20tree%20is%20a%20finite%20simple%20graph) |
