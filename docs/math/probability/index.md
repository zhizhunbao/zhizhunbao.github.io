| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |

| **[Basic notions](basic-notions.md)**<br>基本概念 | (context) "Informally, the sample space for a given set of events is the **set of all possible values** the events may assume." | （语境）非正式地说，一组给定事件的样本空间是这些事件可能取的**所有可能值的集合**。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-f, "Sample space" entry, para. 1](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/SampleSpace.html#:~:text=the%20sample%20space%20for%20a%20given%20set%20of%20events) |
| **[Discrete random variables and distributions](discrete-random-variables-and-distributions.md)**<br>离散随机变量和分布 | (context) "A discrete distribution is a statistical distribution whose random variable takes values in a **countable set** almost surely." | （语境）离散分布是一种统计分布，它的随机变量几乎必然在一个**可数集合**中取值。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-c, "Discrete distribution" entry, para. 1](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/DiscreteDistribution.html#:~:text=A%20discrete%20distribution%20is%20a%20statistical%20distribution) |
| **[Continuous random variables and distributions](continuous-random-variables-and-distributions.md)**<br>连续随机变量和分布 | (context) "A continuous distribution is a statistical distribution for which the variables may take on a **continuous range of values**." | （语境）连续分布是一种统计分布，其中变量可以在**连续的取值范围**内取值。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-b, "Continuous distribution" entry, para. 1](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/ContinuousDistribution.html#:~:text=A%20continuous%20distribution%20is%20a%20statistical%20distribution) |
| **[Expectation, variance, law of large numbers, central limit theorem](expectation-variance-law-of-large-numbers-central-limit-theorem.md)**<br>期望、方差、大数定律、中心极限定理 | "The expectation value, also called the expected value, mathematical expectation, or mean, of a random variable is its **probability-weighted average**." | 随机变量的期望值（也称为期望、数学期望或均值）是它的**按概率加权的平均值**。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-d, "Expectation value" entry, para. 1](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/ExpectationValue.html#:~:text=is%20its%20probability%2Dweighted%20average) |
| **[Conditional distributions and expectation](conditional-distributions-and-expectation.md)**<br>条件分布和期望 | "A conditional distribution is the **statistical distribution of a random variable** given **specified information about another random variable or event**." | 条件分布是**一个随机变量的统计分布**，条件是已知**关于另一个随机变量或事件的特定信息**。 | [Weisstein, n.d.-a, "Conditional distribution" entry, para. 1](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/ConditionalDistribution.html#:~:text=A%20conditional%20distribution%20is%20the%20statistical%20distribution) |
