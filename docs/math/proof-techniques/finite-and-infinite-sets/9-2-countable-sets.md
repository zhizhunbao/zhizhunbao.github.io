| **Uncountable Set**<br>不可数集 | "**An infinite set that is not countably infinite** is called an uncountable set." | **不是可数无限的无限集**称为不可数集。 | [Sundstrom, 2022, section 9.2](https://gvsuoer.github.io/sundstrom-textbook/S_infinitesets.html#countinfinite) |
