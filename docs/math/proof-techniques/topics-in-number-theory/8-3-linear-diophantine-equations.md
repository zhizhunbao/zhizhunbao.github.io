This is called a linear congruence since **the variable** $x$ **occurs to the first power**." | 设 $n$ 是自然数，$a, b \in \mathbb{Z}$ 且 $a \ne 0$。形如 $ax \equiv b \pmod n$ **的同余式**称为一元线性同余式。之所以称为线性同余式，是因为**变量** $x$ **以一次幂出现**。 | [Sundstrom, 2022, section 8.3](https://gvsuoer.github.io/sundstrom-textbook/S_diophantine.html#A_lincongruence) |
