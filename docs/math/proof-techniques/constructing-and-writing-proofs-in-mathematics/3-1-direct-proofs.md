| **Lemma**<br>引理 | "A lemma is **a true mathematical statement** that was proven mainly **to help in the proof of some theorem**." | 引理是**一个真的数学陈述**，证明它主要是**为了帮助证明某个定理**。 | [Sundstrom, 2022, section 3.1](https://gvsuoer.github.io/sundstrom-textbook/S_directproof.html#p-1216) |

| **Congruence**<br>同余 | "What mathematicians call congruence is **a concept used to describe cycles in the world of the integers**." | 数学家所说的同余是**用来描述整数世界中的周期的概念**。 | [Sundstrom, 2022, section 3.1](https://gvsuoer.github.io/sundstrom-textbook/S_directproof.html#subsection-39) |
| **Congruent Modulo n**<br>模 n 同余 | "If $a$ and $b$ are integers, then we say that $\boldsymbol{a}$ is congruent to $\boldsymbol{b}$ modulo $\boldsymbol{n}$ provided that $n$ **divides** $a - b\text{.}$" | 如果 $a$ 和 $b$ 是整数，并且 $n$ **整除** $a - b$，就说 $\boldsymbol{a}$ 模 $\boldsymbol{n}$ 同余于 $\boldsymbol{b}$。 | [Sundstrom, 2022, section 3.1](https://gvsuoer.github.io/sundstrom-textbook/S_directproof.html#congruence) |
| **Reflexive Property of Congruence**<br>同余的自反性 | "**For every integer** $a\text{,}$ $a \equiv a \pmod n\text{.}$ This is called the reflexive property of congruence modulo $n\text{.}$" | **对每个整数** $a$，$a \equiv a \pmod n$。这称为模 $n$ 同余的自反性。 | [Sundstrom, 2022, section 3.1](https://gvsuoer.github.io/sundstrom-textbook/S_directproof.html#exer_cong-props) |
| **Symmetric Property of Congruence**<br>同余的对称性 | "For all integers $a$ and $b\text{,}$ **if** $a \equiv b \pmod n\text{,}$ **then** $b \equiv a \pmod n\text{.}$ This is called the symmetric property of congruence modulo $n\text{.}$" | 对所有整数 $a$ 和 $b$，**如果** $a \equiv b \pmod n$，**那么** $b \equiv a \pmod n$。这称为模 $n$ 同余的对称性。 | [Sundstrom, 2022, section 3.1](https://gvsuoer.github.io/sundstrom-textbook/S_directproof.html#exer_cong-props) |
| **Transitive Property of Congruence**<br>同余的传递性 | "For all integers $a\text{,}$ $b\text{,}$ and $c\text{,}$ **if** $a \equiv b \pmod n$ **and** $b \equiv c \pmod n\text{,}$ **then** $a \equiv c \pmod n\text{.}$ This is called the transitive property of congruence modulo $n\text{.}$" | 对所有整数 $a$、$b$ 和 $c$，**如果** $a \equiv b \pmod n$ **且** $b \equiv c \pmod n$，**那么** $a \equiv c \pmod n$。这称为模 $n$ 同余的传递性。 | [Sundstrom, 2022, section 3.1](https://gvsuoer.github.io/sundstrom-textbook/S_directproof.html#exer_cong-props) |
| **Consecutive Natural Numbers**<br>连续自然数 | "Three natural numbers are called consecutive natural numbers if they **can be written in the form** $m\text{,}$ $m + 1\text{,}$ and $m + 2\text{,}$ where $m$ is a natural number." | 如果三个自然数**能写成** $m$、$m + 1$ 和 $m + 2$ 的形式（其中 $m$ 是自然数），就称它们为连续自然数。 | [Sundstrom, 2022, section 3.1](https://gvsuoer.github.io/sundstrom-textbook/S_directproof.html#activity-12) |
