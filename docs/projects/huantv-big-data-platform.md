# Smart-TV Big Data Platform

A smart-TV data platform processing over a billion device records a day with sub-second queries, moved from self-managed CDH Hadoop on Tencent Cloud to Alibaba Cloud EMR, with Apache DolphinScheduler scheduling and ClickHouse queries.

- **Role:** Senior Big Data Architect: Architecture, Development & Operations
- **Organization:** Guangzhou Huan Technology (HuanTV), Guangzhou
- **Dates:** Apr 2015 – Sep 2022
- **Stack:** CDH Hadoop (Hive, Spark), Tencent Cloud (COS, EMR), Alibaba Cloud (EMR, DataWorks, Quick BI), ClickHouse, Kafka, Apache DolphinScheduler, Kubernetes, NGINX + Lua, GitLab, Jira, Confluence, JumpServer

## What it does

Collects, stores and analyzes smart-TV device data (viewing, advertising exposure and audience labels) for two companies: HuanTV, the employer, and Gozen Data (勾正数据), whose data business the same team and platform served. Over a billion device records a day, queried in under a second with ClickHouse and Spark.

In 2022 the self-managed CDH Hadoop clusters on Tencent Cloud virtual machines were migrated to Alibaba Cloud EMR (VPC and VPN, security groups, auto-scaling), with infrastructure cost down 35%.
