| **55 Management of Companies and Enterprises**<br>公司和企业管理 | "This sector comprises (1), comprises legal entities engaged only in **holding the securities of subsidiaries and affiliates** for the purpose of owning a controlling interest or influencing the management decisions of these businesses, or (2), establishments primarily engaged in **administering, overseeing, and managing other establishments** of the company or enterprise and which may also hold the securities of their subsidiaries and affiliates." | 本部门包括：（1）只从事**持有子公司和关联公司证券**、以取得控股权或影响这些企业管理决策的法律实体；或（2）主要从事**管理、监督和经营本公司或本企业其他机构**、并可能同时持有其子公司和关联公司证券的机构。 | [Statistics Canada, 2022, sector 55](https://www23.statcan.gc.ca/imdb/p3VD.pl?Function=getVD&TVD=1369825#:~:text=This%20sector%20comprises%20%281%29%2C%20comprises%20legal%20entities%20engaged) |
