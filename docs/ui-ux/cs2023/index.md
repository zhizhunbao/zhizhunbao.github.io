The pages under this one are the knowledge units of the CS2023 knowledge area Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), in CS2023's order, and their topics with CS2023's numbering: 6 units, 50 topics. It is the checklist of what this area covers: CS Core topics are the ones every computer science graduate needs, KA Core topics go deeper, and Non-core topics are electives.
