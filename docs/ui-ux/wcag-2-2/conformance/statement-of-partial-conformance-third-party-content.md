# 5.4 Statement of Partial Conformance - Third Party Content

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **5.4 Statement of Partial Conformance - Third Party Content**<br>部分一致性声明：第三方内容 | "Web pages that will later have additional content added **can use a 'statement of partial conformance'**. For example, an email program, a blog, an article that allows users to add comments, or applications supporting user-contributed content. Another example would be a page, such as a portal or news site, composed of content aggregated from multiple contributors, or sites that automatically insert content from other sources over time, such as when advertisements are inserted dynamically." | 之后会添加更多内容的网页**可以使用“部分一致性声明”**。例如电子邮件程序、博客、允许用户添加评论的文章，或支持用户贡献内容的应用。另一个例子是由多个贡献者聚合内容组成的页面（如门户或新闻站点），或自动插入其他来源内容的站点（如动态插入的广告）。 | [World Wide Web Consortium, 2024, section 5.4](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#conformance) |
