# 5.6 Privacy Considerations

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **5.6 Privacy Considerations**<br>隐私考虑 | "Success criteria within this specification which the Working Group has identified **possible implications for privacy**, either by providing protections for end users or which are important for website providers to take in to consideration when implementing features designed to protect user privacy, are listed below. This list reflects the current understanding of the Working Group but other Success criteria may have privacy implications that the Working Group is not aware of at the time of publishing." | 本规范中工作组认定**可能涉及隐私**的成功标准（为最终用户提供保护，或网站提供者在实现保护用户隐私的功能时需要考虑的）列在下面。这个清单反映工作组目前的理解，但其他成功标准也可能与隐私有关。 | [World Wide Web Consortium, 2024, section 5.6](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#conformance) |
