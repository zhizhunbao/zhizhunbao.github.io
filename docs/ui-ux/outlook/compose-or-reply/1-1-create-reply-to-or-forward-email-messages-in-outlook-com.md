# 1.1 Create, reply to, or forward email messages in Outlook.com

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1.1 Create, reply to, or forward email messages in Outlook.com**<br>在 Outlook.com 中新建、回复或转发邮件 | (Summary) In Outlook.com a message is **written and sent starting from New**, and a received message is **answered with Reply or Reply All, or passed on with Forward**. | （概述）在 Outlook.com 中，从“新建”开始**撰写并发送邮件**；收到的邮件可用“答复”或“全部答复”**回复，或用“转发”转给他人**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Create, reply to, or forward email messages in Outlook.com"](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/create-reply-to-or-forward-email-messages-in-outlook-com) |
| **Reply and Reply all**<br>答复与全部答复 | (Summary) A reply **puts RE: in front of the subject and keeps the original text below**, but **files attached to the original are left out**. | （概述）答复时**主题前加 RE:，原文保留在下方**，但**原邮件的附件不会带上**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Create, reply to, or forward email messages in Outlook.com", Tips about using reply, reply all, and forward](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/create-reply-to-or-forward-email-messages-in-outlook-com) |
| **Forward**<br>转发 | (Summary) A forward **puts FW: in front of the subject** and **carries the original message and its attachments**. | （概述）转发时**主题前加 FW:**，并**带上原邮件及其附件**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Create, reply to, or forward email messages in Outlook.com", Tips about using reply, reply all, and forward](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/create-reply-to-or-forward-email-messages-in-outlook-com) |
