# 6.26 Nav

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nav**<br>导航 | "Nav, or navigation, provides **a list of links** that lets people **move through the main sections** of an app or site. It’s **a high-level wayfinding component** that’s always easily accessible but can be minimized to free up space." | 导航提供**一组链接**，让用户**在应用或网站的各个主要部分之间移动**。它是**一个高层级的指路组件**，随时都能方便地打开，也可以收起来腾出空间。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Nav"](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/components/web/react/core/nav/usage#:~:text=Nav%2C%20or%20navigation%2C%20provides%20a%20list) |
| **Task oriented navigation**<br>面向任务导航 | "Depending on the product and the context, make your navigation **task-oriented (using verbs)**, or feature focused (using nouns)." | 根据产品和场景，把导航做成**面向任务的（用动词）**，或面向功能的（用名词）。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Nav", Behavior](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/components/web/react/core/nav/usage#:~:text=Depending%20on%20the%20product%20and%20the%20context) |
| **Feature focused navigation**<br>面向功能导航 | "Depending on the product and the context, make your navigation task-oriented (using verbs), or **feature focused (using nouns)**." | 根据产品和场景，把导航做成面向任务的（用动词），或**面向功能的（用名词）**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Nav", Behavior](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/components/web/react/core/nav/usage#:~:text=Depending%20on%20the%20product%20and%20the%20context) |
| **Simple nav**<br>简单导航 | "Simple navs list **the spaces in a site or app** as links **on the same level**." | 简单导航把**网站或应用里的各个空间**列成**同一层级**的链接。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Nav", Layout](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/components/web/react/core/nav/usage#:~:text=Simple%20navs%20list%20the%20spaces) |
| **Complex nav with categories**<br>带类别的复杂导航 | "For more complex navs, links can be **grouped into categories** for easier interaction." | 对更复杂的导航，链接可以**按类别分组**，方便操作。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Nav", Layout](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/components/web/react/core/nav/usage#:~:text=For%20more%20complex%20navs%2C%20links) |
| **Nav with icons (recommended)**<br>带图标的导航（推荐） | "Whenever possible, **use icons with nav category labels**. They create additional visual emphasis and **differentiate nav categories from the nav subitems** within them." | 只要可行，**就给导航类别的标签配上图标**。图标能增加视觉强调，并**把导航类别和其中的子项区分开**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Nav", Layout](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/components/web/react/core/nav/usage#:~:text=Whenever%20possible%2C%20use%20icons%20with) |
| **Nav without icons**<br>不带图标的导航 | "If icons aren’t technically possible or difficult to pick due to an overwhelming number of nav items, remove them. If nav categories don’t include an icon, **subitems are indented** to maintain **a clear hierarchy**." | 如果技术上做不到，或者导航项太多难以选图标，就去掉图标。导航类别不带图标时，**子项会缩进**，以保持**清楚的层级**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Nav", Layout](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/components/web/react/core/nav/usage#:~:text=If%20icons%20aren%E2%80%99t%20technically%20possible) |
| **Correct chevron location**<br>正确的箭头位置 | (context) "The nav category chevron **indicates sub-items are available**. The consistent placement of the chevron makes it predictable and easy to distinguish a nav category." | （语境）导航类别上的箭头**表示下面有子项**。箭头位置固定，用户就能预料，也容易认出这是一个导航类别。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Nav", Layout](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/components/web/react/core/nav/usage#:~:text=The%20nav%20category%20chevron%20indicates) |
| **Incorrect chevron location**<br>错误的箭头位置 | (context) "**Don’t move the chevron** to any other location on the nav category." | （语境）**不要把箭头挪到**导航类别上的其他位置。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Nav", Layout](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/components/web/react/core/nav/usage#:~:text=Don%E2%80%99t%20move%20the%20chevron) |
