# 2.5 Onboarding

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Onboarding**<br>引导（Onboarding） | "Onboarding experiences guide people through **how to get started** with **a product or feature**." | 引导体验带人了解**如何开始使用**某个**产品或功能**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding"](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/onboarding#:~:text=Onboarding%20experiences%20guide%20people%20through%20how) |
| **Relevant**<br>相关 | (context) "Make sure onboarding content is presented **within context** of **a closely related task**." | （语境）确保引导内容出现在**情境之中**，即与**一项密切相关的任务**放在一起。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Principles](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/onboarding#:~:text=Make%20sure%20onboarding%20content%20is%20presented) |
| **Non-distracting**<br>不干扰 | "Avoid being **a barrier** to **the user’s primary goal**." | 避免成为**障碍**，挡住**用户的主要目标**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Principles](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/onboarding#:~:text=Avoid%20being%20a%20barrier%20to%20the) |
| **Optional**<br>可选 | "Allow people to **exit and finish what they’re doing**, but give them **a way to come back to it later**." | 允许人们**退出并完成手头的事**，但给他们**以后回来的途径**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Principles](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/onboarding#:~:text=Allow%20people%20to%20exit%20and%20finish) |
| **Benefit-focused**<br>突出好处 | (context) "Let them know up front **how they’ll benefit**, so they’re **motivated to pay attention**." | （语境）一开始就告诉他们**能得到什么好处**，让他们**有动力去留意**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Principles](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/onboarding#:~:text=Let%20them%20know%20up%20front%20how) |
| **Coherent**<br>一致 | "Use **standard Fluent components** so that users **know what to expect** when interacting with your onboarding experience." | 使用**标准的 Fluent 组件**，让用户在与引导体验互动时**知道会发生什么**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Principles](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/onboarding#:~:text=Use%20standard%20Fluent%20components%20so%20that) |
| **Welcome**<br>欢迎 | "Welcome experiences **reassure people** they’re stating **in the right place**." | 欢迎体验**让人安心**，确认自己**来对了地方**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Scenarios and goals](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/onboarding#:~:text=Welcome%20experiences%20reassure%20people%20they%27re%20stating) |
| **Orient**<br>定向 | (context) "After welcoming the user to a new experience, it’s important to **show them helpful features** in context. To show where certain options appear, try **empty states or teaching popovers**." | （语境）欢迎用户进入新体验之后，重要的是在情境中**向他们展示有用的功能**。要指出某些选项在哪里，可以用**空状态或教学浮层**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Scenarios and goals](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/onboarding#:~:text=After%20welcoming%20the%20user%20to%20a) |
| **Notify**<br>通知 | (context) "When functionality in an app or experiences is **added or changed**, notify users about **what they can now gain** and how they might adapt their behaviors to the new UX." | （语境）当应用或体验里的功能**新增或改变**时，告诉用户**他们现在能得到什么**，以及该如何调整做法来适应新的体验。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Scenarios and goals](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/onboarding#:~:text=When%20functionality%20in%20an%20app%20or) |
| **Explain**<br>说明 | (context) "To tell people **what they can do** or provide details that might not be readily apparent, try putting **hints in the UI** that can tell people how they might move forward." | （语境）要告诉人们**能做什么**，或提供不太显眼的细节，可以在**界面里放提示**，告诉人们下一步怎么走。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Scenarios and goals](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/onboarding#:~:text=To%20tell%20people%20what%20they%20can) |
| **Take action**<br>采取行动 | (context) "Sometimes, before they dive in, users need to complete **some preliminary actions** needed to **use the app or feature**." | （语境）有时用户在深入之前，需要先完成**一些准备操作**，才能**使用应用或功能**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Scenarios and goals](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/onboarding#:~:text=Sometimes%2C%20before%20they%20dive%20in%2C%20users) |
