| **Investigative jobs**<br>研究型工作 | "Investigative jobs are all about **studying things** in **an organized or creative way**." | 研究型工作的核心是**研究事物**，方式是**有条理的或有创造性的**。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Interest"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/skills/16420/ca#:~:text=Investigative%20jobs%20are%20all%20about%20studying) |
| **Artistic jobs**<br>艺术型工作 | "Artistic jobs are all about **expressing yourself** in **a creative way** to make visual, performing, written, or applied art." | 艺术型工作的核心是**表达自我**，以**有创造性的方式**做出视觉、表演、文字或应用艺术。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Interest"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/skills/16420/ca#:~:text=Artistic%20jobs%20are%20all%20about%20expressing) |
| **Social jobs**<br>社会型工作 | "Social jobs are all about **working with people**. This often involves interacting with others to **help, care, teach, or inform them**." | 社会型工作的核心是**与人打交道**，常常要与他人互动，去**帮助、照顾、教导或告知他们**。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Interest"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/skills/16420/ca#:~:text=Social%20jobs%20are%20all%20about%20working) |
| **Enterprising jobs**<br>企业型工作 | "Enterprising jobs are all about **taking action** to **achieve goals** for a group or for yourself." | 企业型工作的核心是**采取行动**，为团队或自己**实现目标**。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Interest"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/skills/16420/ca#:~:text=Enterprising%20jobs%20are%20all%20about%20taking) |
| **Conventional jobs**<br>常规型工作 | "Conventional jobs are all about **following procedures** and **routines**." | 常规型工作的核心是**遵循程序**和**惯例**。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Interest"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/skills/16420/ca#:~:text=Conventional%20jobs%20are%20all%20about%20following) |
| **Knowledge**<br>知识 | "Knowledge refers to **the understanding** of **different theories and practices**." | 知识指**理解**，即对**各种理论与实践**的理解。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Knowledge"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/skills/16420/ca#:~:text=Knowledge%20refers%20to%20the%20understanding%20of) |
