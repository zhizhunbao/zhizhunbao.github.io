# 1.5 Resume Builder

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Resume Builder**<br>简历生成器 | (context) "Easily build **a professional-looking resume** that showcases **your goals and qualifications**." | （语境）轻松做出一份**专业样式的简历**，展示**你的目标和资历**。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Resume Builder", "Showcase your potential"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/resume-builder#:~:text=Easily%20build%20a%20professional-looking%20resume%20that) |
| **Standard account**<br>Standard 账户 | (context) "After you create **a Standard or Plus account** on Job Bank, just sign in to **access the Resume Builder**." | （语境）在 Job Bank 创建 **Standard 或 Plus 账户**后，登录即可**使用简历生成器**。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Resume Builder", "Step 1 Pick a template"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/resume-builder#:~:text=After%20you%20create%20a%20Standard%20or) |
| **Traditional template**<br>传统模板 | (context) "Use the traditional template if: you have **several years of experience** in your field; you’re looking for a job in your field; your career progression has been stable and your work history is fairly consistent (**no major gaps**)." | （语境）以下情况用传统模板：你在本领域有**多年经验**；你在本领域找工作；你的职业发展稳定，工作经历相当连贯（**没有大的空档**）。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Resume Builder", "Step 1 Pick a template"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/resume-builder#:~:text=Use%20the%20traditional%20template%20if%3A%20you) |
| **Youth and recent graduates’ template**<br>青年与应届毕业生模板 | (context) "Use the youth and recent graduates’ template if: you’re **a first-time job seeker** starting your professional career; you’ve just completed your education and you’re looking for an entry-level job in your field of study; you have **limited paid work experience**." | （语境）以下情况用青年与应届毕业生模板：你是**第一次求职**、刚开始职业生涯；你刚完成学业、在所学领域找入门级工作；你的**有薪工作经验有限**。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Resume Builder", "Step 1 Pick a template"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/resume-builder#:~:text=Use%20the%20youth%20and%20recent%20graduates%27) |
| **Pre-defined options**<br>预设选项 | (context) "you can search **thousands of pre-defined options** and **select the ones that suit you**." | （语境）你可以搜索**数千个预设选项**，**选出适合你的**。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Resume Builder", "Step 2 Fill in the blanks"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/resume-builder#:~:text=you%20can%20search%20thousands%20of%20pre-defined) |
| **Job duties**<br>工作职责 | (context) "you can **import job duties** for **any job title**." | （语境）你可以**导入工作职责**，适用于**任何职位名称**。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Resume Builder", "Step 2 Fill in the blanks"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/resume-builder#:~:text=you%20can%20import%20job%20duties%20for) |
