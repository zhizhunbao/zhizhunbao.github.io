# 1.4 Job Match

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Job Match**<br>职位匹配 | (context) "just **fill out your profile** on Job Bank, then sit back and **review job matches** as they come in." | （语境）只要在 Job Bank 上**填好你的个人档案**，然后坐等并**查看陆续到来的职位匹配**。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Job Match", "Simplify your job search"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/match#:~:text=just%20fill%20out%20your%20profile%20on) |
| **Plus account**<br>Plus 账户 | (context) "After you sign up for a **Plus account** on Job Bank, you will be able to **fill out a profile** detailing your education, experience and skills." | （语境）在 Job Bank 注册 **Plus 账户**后，你就可以**填写个人档案**，写明你的学历、经验和技能。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Job Match", "Step 1 Create your profile"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/match#:~:text=After%20you%20sign%20up%20for%20a) |
| **Profile**<br>个人档案 | (context) "No need to write anything! Just **pick from our pre-defined options**. Once you’ve filled out your profile, we will **email you** whenever it matches the job requirements for new positions." | （语境）什么都不用写！只要**从我们预设的选项中挑选**。填好档案后，每当它与新职位的要求匹配，我们都会**给你发邮件**。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Job Match", "Step 1 Create your profile"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/match#:~:text=No%20need%20to%20write%20anything%21%20Just) |
| **Matching algorithms**<br>匹配算法 | (context) "tailor them to your needs by choosing from **one of 3 different matching algorithms**. You can switch between them at any time in your profile settings. Pick whether you want to get matched based on **your work history, your education, or your experience and skills**." | （语境）从**三种不同的匹配算法中选一种**，让匹配符合你的需要。你可以随时在档案设置里切换。选择按**你的工作经历、你的学历，或你的经验和技能**来匹配。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Job Match", "Step 2 Manage your settings"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/match#:~:text=tailor%20them%20to%20your%20needs%20by) |
| **Default matching mode**<br>默认匹配模式 | (context) "Getting **too many matches**? In your settings, you can also **switch from the Default matching mode** to the Strict matching mode" | （语境）匹配**太多**？你还可以在设置里**从默认匹配模式切换**到严格匹配模式 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Job Match", "Step 2 Manage your settings"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/match#:~:text=Getting%20too%20many%20matches%3F%20In%20your) |
| **Strict matching mode**<br>严格匹配模式 | (context) "switch from the Default matching mode to the Strict matching mode to get results that are **more closely related** to **your profile details**." | （语境）从默认匹配模式切换到严格匹配模式，得到**更紧密相关**的结果，即与**你的档案信息**更贴近。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Job Match", "Step 2 Manage your settings"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/match#:~:text=switch%20from%20the%20Default%20matching%20mode) |
| **Invite to apply**<br>邀请投递 | (context) "When you’re matched with one of them, they can **preview your profile** and **invite you to apply** for their job." | （语境）当你与其中某位雇主匹配时，对方可以**预览你的档案**，并**邀请你投递**他们的职位。 | [Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Job Match", "Get noticed by employers"](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/match#:~:text=When%20you%27re%20matched%20with%20one%20of) |
