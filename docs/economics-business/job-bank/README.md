# Job Bank: allowed pages and term checklist

Not published (`docfx.json` excludes `**/README.md`). Source: Job Bank (https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/, Employment and Social Development Canada), its main menu as the site loads it (`/themes-sat/ajax/appmenu.xhtml`), cached 2026-11-06: 39 pages, list `design-refs/official-docs/urls_kb_jobbank_menu.txt`.

Status (2026-11-06): skeleton. Groups are the menu's groups that hold terms, in the menu's order (1. Job Search, 2. Training and Careers, 3. Labour Market Information, 4. Hiring); pages are their menu items, in order, named as the menu names them (title case). Each page has one row, its own subject, as a table-of-contents title; its terms are added when the page is read, one page at a time (repository README, "New terms from a new documentation set"). Each group's landing page (Find a job, Choose a career, Explore the market, Fill a position) is the source of its group row; About us is the source of the Job Bank row.

Left out (no terms): Help (Job Bank Support: help topics), About: Our network (partners) and What's new (release notes); the link hubs Resources for job seekers, Career planning advice and Resources for employers. "Job profiles" is in two menus (Training and careers, Labour market information) and has one page, 2.3. "Post a job" opens the employer sign-in; its page cites Fill a position.

Placement (Frank, 2026-11-06): the whole entry sits in Economics and Business, the time-bound reports (wages, outlooks, snapshots) included, rather than splitting them into Market.
