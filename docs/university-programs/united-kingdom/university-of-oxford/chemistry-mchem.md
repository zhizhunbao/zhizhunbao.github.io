These are broadly based, and include topics such as Biological Chemistry and Physics, which are presented in a chemical context.**<br>第一年课程涵盖无机化学、有机化学和物理化学的传统领域，以及化学数学。这些内容基础广泛，包括置于化学背景下讲授的生物化学与物理等主题。 | (Definition pending) | （定义待补） | [University of Oxford, n.d.](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/undergraduate/courses/course-listing/chemistry) |

| **The practical course aims to train students to solve problems practically, accurately, safely and efficiently so that by the end of the course they are ready to work as a professional chemist in a research laboratory. The practical course teaches the essential experimental skills, from the synthesis and characterisation of compounds to the operation of spectrometers and other instruments for physiochemical and analytical measurements. Computer applications and chemistry software packages, such as structure drawing, molecular modelling and computer coding, are covered in the practical course. It also makes tangible much of the material covered in the lectures, classes and tutorials.**<br>实践课程旨在训练学生切实、准确、安全、高效地解决问题，使其在课程结束时能够作为专业化学家在研究实验室工作。实践课程讲授基本实验技能，从化合物的合成与表征，到操作光谱仪及其他仪器进行物理化学和分析测量。实践课程还涵盖计算机应用和化学软件包，如结构绘制、分子建模和计算机编程。它也使讲座、课堂和导师辅导中的大量内容变得具体可感。 | (Definition pending) | （定义待补） | [University of Oxford, n.d.](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/undergraduate/courses/course-listing/chemistry) |
| **During the second year of the course, students build up their understanding of the subject and cover most of the core material in the degree.**<br>在课程的第二年，学生深化对该学科的理解，并学习学位中的大部分核心内容。 | (Definition pending) | （定义待补） | [University of Oxford, n.d.](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/undergraduate/courses/course-listing/chemistry) |
| **Examples of some of the topics included are: coordination chemistry, lanthanides and actinides, organometallic chemistry; organic synthesis, heteroatoms in organic chemistry, conformational analysis and ring chemistry; quantum and statistical mechanics, liquids and solutions, and spectroscopy.**<br>部分主题示例：配位化学、镧系与锕系元素、有机金属化学；有机合成、有机化学中的杂原子、构象分析与环化学；量子与统计力学、液体与溶液、光谱学。 | (Definition pending) | （定义待补） | [University of Oxford, n.d.](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/undergraduate/courses/course-listing/chemistry) |
| **The third year begins by completing the core material, followed by a wide variety of options courses, some of which relate to research interests in the department.**<br>第三年先完成核心内容，随后是多种选修课，其中一些与系内研究兴趣相关。 | (Definition pending) | （定义待补） | [University of Oxford, n.d.](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/undergraduate/courses/course-listing/chemistry) |
| **Core topics include: modern main group chemistry, spectroscopy and magnetism in inorganic chemistry, bioinorganic chemistry; pericyclic reactions, transition metal catalysis, radical reactions; soft condensed matter, photochemistry, magnetic resonance.**<br>核心主题包括：现代主族化学、无机化学中的光谱学与磁性、生物无机化学；周环反应、过渡金属催化、自由基反应；软凝聚态物质、光化学、磁共振。 | (Definition pending) | （定义待补） | [University of Oxford, n.d.](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/undergraduate/courses/course-listing/chemistry) |
| **Some examples of options courses that have been offered in recent years are: natural product chemistry, functional organic polymers and materials chemistry, solid state compounds in technology, organometallic catalysts, atmospheric chemistry and astrochemistry, and theoretical chemistry. The available options courses are subject to change each year.**<br>近年开设的部分选修课示例：天然产物化学、功能性有机聚合物与材料化学、技术中的固态化合物、有机金属催化剂、大气化学与天体化学、理论化学。可选修课程每年可能有所变化。 | (Definition pending) | （定义待补） | [University of Oxford, n.d.](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/undergraduate/courses/course-listing/chemistry) |
| **The fourth year is spent exclusively on research, providing students with the opportunity to immerse themselves in a significant project in one of the world’s premier research departments. A wide range of research themes are available, and there are also opportunities to work in related sciences in other departments. Students are supervised by a member of academic staff and have full access to the research facilities of their host laboratory.**<br>第四年完全用于研究，使学生有机会沉浸于世界一流研究系之一的重要项目中。可选研究主题广泛，也有机会在其他系的相关科学领域工作。学生由一名学术人员指导，并可充分使用所在实验室的研究设施。 | (Definition pending) | （定义待补） | [University of Oxford, n.d.](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/undergraduate/courses/course-listing/chemistry) |
| **Students generally enjoy the fourth year greatly, and it is very rare for a student to exercise the option of leaving after three years with an unclassified BA honours degree.**<br>学生通常十分享受第四年，极少有学生选择三年后离开并获得未分级文学学士荣誉学位。 | (Definition pending) | （定义待补） | [University of Oxford, n.d.](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/undergraduate/courses/course-listing/chemistry) |
