| **M.F.A. Thesis comprising of the production of a substantial artistic and/or curatorial body of work in a primary area of study and a research paper in a complementary area of study (or occasionally a creative work in a different field from the primary area). The length of the paper is determined by the committee, but generally the research paper accompanying the creative component of the thesis must be a substantial, scholarly work between 4,000 to 6,000 words. The artistic and/or curatorial body of work and the research paper should have substantial linkages in theoretical or topical context. An oral defence of both the body of work and the research paper is required. The oral defence will normally be held within six weeks of the research paper submission.**<br>美术硕士论文，包括在主要研究领域创作大量艺术和／或策展作品，以及在互补研究领域的一篇研究论文（偶尔为不同于主要领域的创意作品）：论文篇幅由委员会决定，但与论文创作部分配套的研究论文一般须为 4,000 至 6,000 字的实质性学术作品。艺术和／或策展作品与研究论文在理论或主题背景上应有实质联系。须对作品集与研究论文进行口头答辩。口头答辩通常在研究论文提交后六周内举行 | (Definition pending) | （定义待补） | [The University of British Columbia, n.d.](https://okanagan.calendar.ubc.ca/faculties-schools-and-colleges/college-graduate-studies/fine-arts/program-requirements) |
