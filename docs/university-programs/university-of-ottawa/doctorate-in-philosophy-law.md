The TAC is responsible for guiding the student throughout the program, including course selection, the comprehensive examination, thesis proposal, and thesis defense. |  | [University of Ottawa, n.d.](https://catalogue.uottawa.ca/en/graduate/doctorate-philosophy-law/) |
