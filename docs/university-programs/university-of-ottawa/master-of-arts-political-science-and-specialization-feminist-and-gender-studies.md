|  | Collaborative Program in Feminist and Gender Studies > Compulsory Courses: |  | Students must complete the two compulsory courses before their first enrollment for the major research paper or thesis. The thesis or major research paper must be on a topic related to women, gender, feminism or sexualities. The thesis or major research paper must demonstrate knowledge of feminist scholarship in the field or fields appropriate to the topic, and of feminist methodologies where applicable.
