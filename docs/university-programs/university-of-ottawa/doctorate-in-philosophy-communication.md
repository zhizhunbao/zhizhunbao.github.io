The committee is composed of the thesis supervisor and three other professors, at least two of whom must be members of the Department of Communication. By the end of the first year, the composition of the committee must be confirmed and the thesis topic must be enrolled officially. |  | [University of Ottawa, n.d.](https://catalogue.uottawa.ca/en/graduate/doctorate-philosophy-communication/) |
