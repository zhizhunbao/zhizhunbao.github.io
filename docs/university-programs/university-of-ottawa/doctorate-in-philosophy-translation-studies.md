|  | Thesis Advisory Committee (TAC) |  | During the first term of the program, a thesis advisory committee (TAC) is formed for the candidate. At least one member of the thesis committee, in addition to the supervisor, must be form the Faculty of Arts. The TAC is responsible for guiding the student throughout the program, including course selection, the comprehensive examination, thesis proposal, and thesis defense. |  | [University of Ottawa, n.d.](https://catalogue.uottawa.ca/en/graduate/doctorate-philosophy-translation-studies/) |
