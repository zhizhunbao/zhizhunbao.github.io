The dissertation in practice is the culminating component the Doctor of Education (EdD) degree in International Educational Leadership and Policy. The dissertation in practice includes the identification and investigation of a problem of practice, the application of theory, research, and policy analysis to the problem of practice, and a proposed plan for action to address the problem of practice. This professional dissertation aims to improve educational practice at local, regional, national, or international levels. It is expected to have meaningful generative impact on practice and policy.
