|  |  |  | The thesis (Dissertation in Practice) is the culminating component of the Doctor of Education degree in Child Study and Education. It includes the identification and investigation of a problem of practice or policy, the application of theory and research to this problem, and a design for implementing ideas that emerge from the findings. Specifically, the thesis (Dissertation in Practice) consists of original research in the form of a written proposal or plan for innovative and impactful educational policy, set of guidelines, advocacy, a development project, or activism within an education-related field, aimed at improving practice at local, regional, national, or international levels. |  | [University of Toronto, n.d.](https://sgs.calendar.utoronto.ca/degree/Applied-Psychology-and-Human-Development) |
