|  |  |  | The program requires a minimum of 4 graduate courses, including the 2 Quantum Information core courses, with an average of at least 70% (with unit weights equal to 0.50) for those entering the PhD program with a Master's degree. At least 3 of the 4 required courses must be PMATH graduate courses numbered in the 800's and 900's, and at least 1 of the 4 required courses must be a QIC graduate course (note that cross-listed courses, such as PMATH 871/QIC 710, are regarded as both PMATH and QIC courses).
