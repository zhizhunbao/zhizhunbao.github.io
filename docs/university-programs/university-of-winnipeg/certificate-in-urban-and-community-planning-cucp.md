The Certificate comprises 18 credit hours of core courses and 12 credit hours of electives intended to be fulfilled through the completion of elective courses already required for a degree. The Certificate is interdisciplinary – drawing on courses from Geography, Urban and Inner City Studies, Environmental Studies, History, Indigenous Studies, Political Science, and Economics.
