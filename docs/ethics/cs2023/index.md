Planned, no content yet. The pages under this one are the knowledge units of the CS2023 knowledge area Society, Ethics, and the Profession (SEP), in CS2023's order, and their topics with CS2023's numbering: 11 units, 100 topics. It is the checklist of what this area covers: CS Core topics are the ones every computer science graduate needs, KA Core topics go deeper, and Non-core topics are electives.
