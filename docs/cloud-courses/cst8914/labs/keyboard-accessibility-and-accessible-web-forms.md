# Keyboard accessibility and accessible web forms

Lab 7 of CST8914 Accessible by Design (week 7) asks me to find and fix keyboard problems on a page, make a registration form understandable to screen reader users, and make its success and error messages reach keyboard and screen reader users. The table lists the concepts it needs and the sections that explain them. My own answers come after the lab is submitted.
