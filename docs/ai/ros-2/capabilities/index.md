| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |

| **[4.2 Motion planning and manipulation](motion-planning-and-manipulation.md)**<br>运动规划与操作 | "Motion planning **enables you to control a robot’s movements to achieve an intended goal**. Manipulation capabilities **enable robots to analyze their environment and perform tasks like picking and placing objects**." | 运动规划**让你控制机器人的运动，以达到预期目标**。操作能力**让机器人分析其环境，并执行抓取和放置物体等任务**。 | [Open Robotics, n.d.-b, "Motion planning and manipulation", para. 1](https://docs.ros.org/en/lyrical/Capabilities/About-Motion-planning.html#motion-planning-and-manipulation) |

| **[4.4 Perception](perception.md)**<br>感知 | "Perception capabilities **help your robot to understand and interpret the world**." | 感知能力**帮助机器人理解和解释外部世界**。 | [Open Robotics, n.d.-d, "Perception", para. 1](https://docs.ros.org/en/lyrical/Capabilities/About-Perception.html#perception) |
