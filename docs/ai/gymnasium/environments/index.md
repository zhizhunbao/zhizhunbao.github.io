It is **a physics engine for facilitating research and development in robotics, biomechanics, graphics and animation, and other areas where fast and accurate simulation is needed**." | （语境）MuJoCo 是 Multi-Joint dynamics with Contact 的缩写。它是**一个物理引擎，用于推动机器人、生物力学、图形与动画以及其他需要快速准确仿真的领域的研究和开发**。 | [Farama Foundation, n.d.-c, "MuJoCo", para. 2](https://gymnasium.farama.org/environments/mujoco/) |
