## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **3.2 Box2D**<br>Box2D | "These environments all involve **toy games based around physics control, using box2d based physics and PyGame-based rendering**." | 这些环境都是**围绕物理控制的玩具游戏，使用基于 box2d 的物理引擎和基于 PyGame 的渲染**。 | [Farama Foundation, n.d.-b, "Box2D", para. 2](https://gymnasium.farama.org/environments/box2d/) |

| **Car Racing**<br>赛车 | "**The easiest control task to learn from pixels - a top-down racing environment.**" | **最容易从像素学习的控制任务：一个俯视视角的赛车环境。** | [Farama Foundation, n.d.-c, "Car Racing", para. 2](https://gymnasium.farama.org/environments/box2d/car_racing/) |
