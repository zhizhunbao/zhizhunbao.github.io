# 1.2.19 Visual and Interactive Content

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Visual and Interactive Content**<br>可视化与交互内容 | (Summary) Replies that **go beyond plain text**, such as data widgets and diagrams, along with **clickable questions** in place of typing. | （概述）**不只是纯文本**的回复，例如数据小组件和图表，还有用来代替打字的**可点选问题**。 | [Anthropic, n.d., "Visual and interactive content"](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13641943-visual-and-interactive-content) |
