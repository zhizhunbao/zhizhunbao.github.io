| **C17.800.480 Lupus Erythematosus, Cutaneous**<br>皮肤型红斑狼疮 | "A form of lupus erythematosus in which **the skin may be the only organ involved** or in which skin involvement precedes the spread into other body systems. It has been classified into three forms - acute (= LUPUS ERYTHEMATOSUS, SYSTEMIC with skin lesions), subacute, and chronic (= LUPUS ERYTHEMATOSUS, DISCOID)." | 红斑狼疮的一种类型，其中**皮肤可能是唯一受累的器官**，或皮肤受累先于向其他身体系统的扩散。分为三型：急性（即伴有皮肤病变的系统性红斑狼疮（LUPUS ERYTHEMATOSUS, SYSTEMIC））、亚急性和慢性（即盘状红斑狼疮（LUPUS ERYTHEMATOSUS, DISCOID））。 | [National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Lupus Erythematosus, Cutaneous" scope note](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D008178) |
| **C17.800.480.479 Lupus Erythematosus, Discoid**<br>盘状红斑狼疮 | "A **chronic form of cutaneous lupus erythematosus** (LUPUS ERYTHEMATOSUS, CUTANEOUS) in which the skin lesions mimic those of the systemic form but in which systemic signs are rare. It is characterized by the presence of **discoid skin plaques** showing varying degrees of edema, erythema, scaliness, follicular plugging, and skin atrophy. Lesions are surrounded by an elevated erythematous border. The condition typically involves the face and scalp, but widespread dissemination may occur." | 一种**皮肤型红斑狼疮的慢性类型**（LUPUS ERYTHEMATOSUS, CUTANEOUS），其皮损类似系统型，但全身征象少见。其特征为出现**盘状皮肤斑块**，表现不同程度的水肿、红斑、鳞屑、毛囊角栓和皮肤萎缩。皮损周围有隆起的红斑性边缘。本病通常累及面部和头皮，但也可广泛播散。 | [National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Lupus Erythematosus, Discoid" scope note](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D008179) |
| **C17.800.480.479.400 Panniculitis, Lupus Erythematosus**<br>狼疮性脂膜炎 | "A type of **lupus erythematosus** characterized by **deep dermal or subcutaneous nodules**, most often on the head, face, or upper arms. It is generally chronic and occurs most often in women between the ages of 20 and 45." | 一种**红斑狼疮**，特征为**真皮深层或皮下结节**，最常见于头部、面部或上臂。通常呈慢性，最常发生于 20 至 45 岁的女性。 | [National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Panniculitis, Lupus Erythematosus" scope note](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D015435) |
