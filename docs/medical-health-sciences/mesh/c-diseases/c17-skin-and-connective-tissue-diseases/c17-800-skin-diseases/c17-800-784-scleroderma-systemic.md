| **C17.800.784 Scleroderma, Systemic**<br>系统性硬皮病 | "A **chronic multi-system disorder of CONNECTIVE TISSUE**. It is characterized by SCLEROSIS in the SKIN, the LUNGS, the HEART, the GASTROINTESTINAL TRACT, the KIDNEYS, and the MUSCULOSKELETAL SYSTEM. Other important features include diseased small BLOOD VESSELS and AUTOANTIBODIES. The disorder is named for its most prominent feature (hard skin), and classified into subsets by the extent of skin thickening: LIMITED SCLERODERMA and DIFFUSE SCLERODERMA." | 一种**结缔组织**（CONNECTIVE TISSUE）**的慢性多系统疾病**。其特征为皮肤（SKIN）、肺（LUNGS）、心脏（HEART）、胃肠道（GASTROINTESTINAL TRACT）、肾（KIDNEYS）和肌肉骨骼系统（MUSCULOSKELETAL SYSTEM）的硬化（SCLEROSIS）。其他重要特征包括小血管（BLOOD VESSELS）病变和自身抗体（AUTOANTIBODIES）。该病以其最突出的特征（皮肤变硬）命名，并按皮肤增厚的范围分为亚型：局限性硬皮病（LIMITED SCLERODERMA）和弥漫性硬皮病（DIFFUSE SCLERODERMA）。 | [National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Scleroderma, Systemic" scope note](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D012595) |
| **C17.800.784.602 Scleroderma, Diffuse**<br>弥漫性硬皮病 | "A **rapid onset form of SYSTEMIC SCLERODERMA** with **progressive widespread SKIN thickening** over the arms, the legs and the trunk, resulting in stiffness and disability." | 一种**快速起病的系统性硬皮病**（SYSTEMIC SCLERODERMA）**类型**，伴有手臂、腿部和躯干**进行性广泛的皮肤**（SKIN）**增厚**，导致僵硬和残疾。 | [National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Scleroderma, Diffuse" scope note](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D045743) |

The CREST SYNDROME is a form of limited scleroderma." | **系统性硬皮病**（SYSTEMIC SCLERODERMA）**进展最慢的形式**，**皮肤增厚局限于面部、颈部以及肘和/或膝远端区域**，躯干不受累。CREST 综合征（CREST SYNDROME）是局限性硬皮病的一种形式。 | [National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Scleroderma, Limited" scope note](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D045745) |
| **C17.800.784.801.500 CREST Syndrome**<br>CREST 综合征 | "A **mild form of LIMITED SCLERODERMA**, a multi-system disorder. Its features include symptoms of CALCINOSIS; RAYNAUD DISEASE; ESOPHAGEAL MOTILITY DISORDERS; sclerodactyly, and TELANGIECTASIS. When the defect in esophageal function is not prominent, it is known as CRST syndrome." | **局限性硬皮病**（LIMITED SCLERODERMA）**的一种轻型**，是一种多系统疾病。其特征包括钙质沉着（CALCINOSIS）、雷诺病（RAYNAUD DISEASE）、食管动力障碍（ESOPHAGEAL MOTILITY DISORDERS）、指端硬化和毛细血管扩张（TELANGIECTASIS）等症状。当食管功能缺陷不明显时，称为 CRST 综合征。 | [National Library of Medicine, 2026, "CREST Syndrome" scope note](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D017675) |
