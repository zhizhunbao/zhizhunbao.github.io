| **C17.800.553 Nephrogenic Fibrosing Dermopathy**<br>肾源性纤维化皮病 | "A chronic, acquired, idiopathic, **progressive eruption of the skin that occurs in the context of RENAL FAILURE**. It is sometimes accompanied by systemic fibrosis. The pathogenesis seems to be multifactorial, with postulated involvement of circulating fibrocytes. There is a strong association between this disorder and the use of **gadolinium-based contrast agents**." | 一种慢性、获得性、特发性、**在肾衰竭**（RENAL FAILURE）**背景下发生的进行性皮疹**。有时伴有系统性纤维化。其发病机制似为多因素，推测与循环纤维细胞有关。本病与使用**钆基造影剂**之间存在密切关联。 | [National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Nephrogenic Fibrosing Dermopathy" scope note](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D054989) |
