| **C17.800.113 Calcinosis Cutis**<br>皮肤钙质沉着症 | "**Pathological deposition of calcium in the skin and subcutaneous tissue**. Excessive calcification of the skin may be associated with underlying diseases that cause tissue damage (e.g. EHLERS-DANLOS SYNDROME; PSEUDOXANTHOMA ELASTICUM; ROTHMUND-THOMSON SYNDROME; and WERNER SYNDROME) or that cause abnormal calcium and phosphate metabolism (e.g., CALCIPHYLAXIS; CHRONIC KIDNEY FAILURE; HYPERPARATHYROIDISM; and SARCOIDOSIS)." | **钙在皮肤和皮下组织中的病理性沉积**。皮肤过度钙化可能与引起组织损伤的基础疾病（如埃勒斯-当洛斯综合征（EHLERS-DANLOS SYNDROME）、弹性纤维假黄瘤（PSEUDOXANTHOMA ELASTICUM）、罗特蒙德-汤姆森综合征（ROTHMUND-THOMSON SYNDROME）和沃纳综合征（WERNER SYNDROME））有关，或与引起钙磷代谢异常的疾病（如钙化防御（CALCIPHYLAXIS）、慢性肾衰竭（CHRONIC KIDNEY FAILURE）、甲状旁腺功能亢进（HYPERPARATHYROIDISM）和结节病（SARCOIDOSIS））有关。 | [National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Calcinosis Cutis" scope note](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D000092182) |
