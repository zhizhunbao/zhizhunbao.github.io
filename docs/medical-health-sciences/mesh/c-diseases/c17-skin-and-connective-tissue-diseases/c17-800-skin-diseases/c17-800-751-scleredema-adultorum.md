| **C17.800.751 Scleredema Adultorum**<br>成人硬肿病 | "A **diffuse, non-pitting induration of the skin** of unknown etiology that occurs most commonly in association with diabetes mellitus, predominantly in females. It typically begins on the face or head and spreads to other areas of the body, sometimes involving noncutaneous tissues. Often it is preceded by any of various infections, notably staphylococcal infections. The condition resolves spontaneously, usually within two years of onset.
