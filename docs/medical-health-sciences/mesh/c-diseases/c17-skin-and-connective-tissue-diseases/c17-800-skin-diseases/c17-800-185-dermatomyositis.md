| **C17.800.185 Dermatomyositis**<br>皮肌炎 | "A subacute or chronic **inflammatory disease of muscle and skin**, marked by **proximal muscle weakness and a characteristic skin rash**. The illness occurs with approximately equal frequency in children and adults. The skin lesions usually take the form of a purplish rash (or less often an exfoliative dermatitis) involving the nose, cheeks, forehead, upper trunk, and arms. The disease is associated with a complement mediated intramuscular microangiopathy, leading to loss of capillaries, muscle ischemia, muscle-fiber necrosis, and perifascicular atrophy. The childhood form of this disease tends to evolve into a systemic vasculitis. Dermatomyositis may occur in association with malignant neoplasms. (From Adams et al., Principles of Neurology, 6th ed, pp1405-6)" | 一种亚急性或慢性的**肌肉和皮肤炎症性疾病**，以**近端肌无力和特征性皮疹**为特点。该病在儿童和成人中的发病率大致相等。皮肤病变通常表现为紫红色皮疹（较少为剥脱性皮炎），累及鼻、面颊、前额、上躯干和上肢。该病与补体介导的肌内微血管病有关，导致毛细血管丧失、肌肉缺血、肌纤维坏死和束周萎缩。儿童型往往发展为系统性血管炎。皮肌炎可伴发恶性肿瘤。（引自 Adams 等，《神经病学原理》，第 6 版，第 1405-1406 页） | [National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Dermatomyositis" scope note](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D003882) |
