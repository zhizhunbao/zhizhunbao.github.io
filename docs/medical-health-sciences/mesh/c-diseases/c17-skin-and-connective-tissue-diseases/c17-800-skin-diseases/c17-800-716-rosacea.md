| **C17.800.716 Rosacea**<br>酒渣鼻 | "A cutaneous disorder primarily of **convexities of the central part of the FACE**, such as FOREHEAD; CHEEK; NOSE; and CHIN. It is characterized by FLUSHING; ERYTHEMA; EDEMA; RHINOPHYMA; papules; and ocular symptoms. It may occur at any age but typically after age 30. There are various subtypes of rosacea: erythematotelangiectatic, papulopustular, phymatous, and ocular (National Rosacea Society's Expert Committee on the Classification and Staging of Rosacea, J Am Acad Dermatol 2002; 46:584-7)." | 一种主要累及**面部**（FACE）**中央凸出部位**的皮肤病，如前额（FOREHEAD）、面颊（CHEEK）、鼻（NOSE）和下颏（CHIN）。其特征为潮红（FLUSHING）、红斑（ERYTHEMA）、水肿（EDEMA）、鼻赘（RHINOPHYMA）、丘疹及眼部症状。可发生于任何年龄，但通常在 30 岁以后。酒渣鼻有多种亚型：红斑毛细血管扩张型、丘疹脓疱型、肥大增生型和眼型（美国国家酒渣鼻协会酒渣鼻分类与分期专家委员会，J Am Acad Dermatol 2002；46:584-7）。 | [National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Rosacea" scope note](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D012393) |
| **C17.800.716.500 Rhinophyma**<br>肥大性酒渣鼻 | "A **manifestation of severe ROSACEA** resulting in **significant enlargement of the NOSE** and occurring primarily in men. The nose is reddened and marked with TELANGIECTASIS." | 一种**重度酒渣鼻**（ROSACEA）**的表现**，导致**鼻**（NOSE）**显著增大**，主要发生于男性。由皮脂腺（SEBACEOUS GLANDS）及周围结缔组织（CONNECTIVE TISSUE）肥大所致。鼻部发红并伴有毛细血管扩张（TELANGIECTASIS）。 | [National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Rhinophyma" scope note](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D012224) |
