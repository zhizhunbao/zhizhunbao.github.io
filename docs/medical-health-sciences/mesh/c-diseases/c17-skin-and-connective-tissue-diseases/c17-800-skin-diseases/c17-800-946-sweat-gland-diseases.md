As the disease becomes chronic, ulcers appear, sinus tracts enlarge, fistulas develop, and fibrosis and scarring become evident." | 一种**大汗腺的慢性化脓性和瘢痕性疾病**，女性主要发生于腋窝，男性主要发生于腹股沟和肛周区域。其特征为毛孔闭塞继发细菌感染，发展为脓肿并最终破溃。随着疾病转为慢性，出现溃疡，窦道扩大，瘘管形成，纤维化和瘢痕变得明显。 | [National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Hidradenitis Suppurativa" scope note](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D017497) |

Its chief cause is thought to be emotional. Generalized hyperhidrosis may be induced by a hot, humid environment, by fever, or by vigorous exercise." | **过度出汗**。局限型最常见的部位是手掌、足底、腋窝、腹股沟皱襞和会阴区。其主要原因被认为是情绪性的。全身性多汗症可由炎热潮湿的环境、发热或剧烈运动诱发。 | [National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Hyperhidrosis" scope note](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D006945) |
