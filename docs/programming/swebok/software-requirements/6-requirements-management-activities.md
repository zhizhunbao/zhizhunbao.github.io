# 6. Requirements Management Activities

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Requirements Management Activities**<br>需求管理活动 | "In contrast, **requirements management** can be thought of as “**maintaining that agreement over time**.”" | 相对地，**需求管理**可以看作「**随时间维护这一共识**」。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 6, para. 1, p. 1-16](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Requirements Scrubbing**<br>需求清理 | "The goal of **requirements scrubbing** [22, c14, c32] is to find the **smallest set of simply stated requirements** that will meet stakeholder needs." | **需求清理** [22, c14, c32] 的目标是找出能满足干系人需要的**最小的一组表述简单的需求**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 6.1, para. 1, p. 1-16](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Requirements Change Control**<br>需求变更控制 | "**Change control** is **central to managing requirements**." | **变更控制**是**管理需求的核心**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 6.2, para. 1, p. 1-17](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Impact Analysis**<br>影响分析 | "an optional **impact analysis** stage to more thoroughly **examine benefits and costs of a requested change**;" | 一个可选的**影响分析**阶段，以更彻底地**审视所请求变更的收益和成本**； | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 6.2, para. 2, item 2, p. 1-17](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Scope Matching**<br>范围匹配 | "Scope matching [22, c14] involves **ensuring that the scope of requirements** to architect, design and construct **does not exceed any cost, schedule or staffing constraints** on the project." | 范围匹配 [22, c14] 指**确保需求范围**（要做架构、设计和构造的那部分）**不超出项目的成本、进度或人员约束**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 6.3, para. 1, p. 1-17](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Velocity-Based Sprint Planning**<br>基于速度的冲刺规划 | "In Agile life cycles, as long as some variant of **velocity-based sprint planning** is done, then the **only work allowed into a sprint/iteration** will be the work that can reasonably be expected to be completed during that sprint/iteration." | 在敏捷生命周期中，只要做了某种形式的**基于速度的冲刺规划**，那么**能进入一个冲刺或迭代的工作**就只有那些可以合理预期在该冲刺或迭代内完成的工作。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 6.3, para. 3, p. 1-17](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
