# 7. Practical Considerations

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Practical Considerations**<br>实践考量 | (No definition) | （无定义） | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7, p. 1-17](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Iterative Nature of the Requirements Process**<br>需求过程的迭代性 | "The tension created by **simultaneous breadth-wise and depth-wise requirements** in real-world projects often prompts teams to **perform requirements activities iteratively**." | 现实项目中**需求同时要求广度和深度**，由此产生的张力常促使团队**迭代地开展需求活动**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.1, para. 1, p. 1-17](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Requirements Prioritization**<br>需求优先级排序 | "Prioritizing requirements is useful throughout a software project because it helps **focus software engineers** on **delivering the most valuable functionality soonest**." | 给需求排优先级在整个软件项目中都有用，因为它帮助**软件工程师聚焦**于**尽快交付最有价值的功能**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.2, para. 1, p. 1-17](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Kano Model**<br>Kano 模型 | "The **Kano model**, which underlies [6, c17], shows that **considering only value, desirability or satisfaction can lead to erroneous priorities**." | **Kano 模型**（[6, c17] 以它为基础）表明，**只考虑价值、吸引力或满意度会导致错误的优先级**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.2, para. 3, p. 1-18](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Objective Function**<br>目标函数 | "The formula $\text{Priority} = \frac{\text{Value} * (1-\text{Risk})}{\text{Cost}}$ is just **one example** of an **objective function** to do so." | 公式 $\text{Priority} = \frac{\text{Value} * (1-\text{Risk})}{\text{Cost}}$ 只是**一个例子**，说明可以用怎样的**目标函数**来做这件事。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.2, para. 6, p. 1-18](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Requirements Tracing**<br>需求追踪 | "Requirements tracing can serve **two potentially useful purposes**. One is to serve as an **accounting exercise that documents consistency between pairs of related project work products**." | 需求追踪可以起到**两种可能有用的作用**。其一是作为一种**核账工作，记录成对的相关项目工作产品之间的一致性**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.3, para. 1, p. 1-18](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Requirements Stability and Volatility**<br>需求的稳定性与易变性 | "It is useful to **assess the likelihood that a requirement will change** in a given time. Identifying **potentially volatile requirements** helps the software engineer establish a design more tolerant of change, (e.g., [20])." | **评估一项需求在给定时间内变化的可能性**是有用的。识别出**可能易变的需求**，有助于软件工程师建立更能容忍变化的设计（例如 [20]）。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.4, para. 1, p. 1-19](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Measuring Requirements**<br>需求度量 | "As a practical matter, it may be useful to have some concept of the **volume of the requirements** for a particular **software product**." | 从实际出发，对**需求量**有个概念可能是有用的，针对的是某个特定的**软件产品**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.5, para. 1, p. 1-19](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Functional Size Measurement (FSM)**<br>功能规模度量（FSM） | "**Functional size measurement (FSM)** is a technique for **evaluating the size of a body of functional requirements**." | **功能规模度量（FSM）**是一种**评估一组功能需求规模**的技术。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.5, para. 1, p. 1-19](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Story Points**<br>故事点 | "**Story points** can also be considered a **measure of requirements size**." | **故事点**也可以看作一种**需求规模的度量**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.5, para. 1, p. 1-19](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Requirements Process Quality and Improvement**<br>需求过程质量与改进 | "This topic concerns **assessing the quality and improvement** of the **requirements process**." | 本主题关注**评估质量与改进**，对象是**需求过程**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.6, para. 1, p. 1-19](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
