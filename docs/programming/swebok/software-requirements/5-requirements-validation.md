# 5. Requirements Validation

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Requirements Validation**<br>需求确认 | "Requirements validation concerns **gaining confidence** that the requirements represent the **stakeholders’ true needs** as they are currently understood (and possibly documented)." | 需求确认关注**建立信心**，确信这些需求代表了按目前理解（并可能已记录）的**干系人的真实需要**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 5, para. 1, p. 1-15](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Requirements Reviews**<br>需求评审 | "The most common way to validate is by **reviewing or inspecting a requirements document**. One or more reviewers are asked to **look for errors, omissions, invalid assumptions, lack of clarity and deviation from accepted practice**." | 最常见的确认方式是**评审或检查需求文档**。请一位或多位评审者**查找错误、遗漏、无效假设、表述不清和偏离公认做法之处**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 5.1, para. 1, p. 1-15](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Definition of Done**<br>完成的定义 | "Providing checklists, quality criteria or a “**definition of done**” to the reviewers can **guide them to focus on specific aspects** of the requirements specification." | 给评审者提供检查单、质量标准或「**完成的定义**」，可以**引导他们关注需求规格说明的特定方面**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 5.1, para. 2, p. 1-16](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Simulation and Execution**<br>仿真与执行 | "Some specifications can be subjected to **simulation or actual execution** in place of or **in addition to human review**." | 有些规格说明可以通过**仿真或实际执行**来代替人工评审，或**作为人工评审的补充**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 5.2, para. 1, p. 1-16](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Prototyping**<br>原型法 | "If the requirements specification is not in a form that allows direct simulation or execution, an alternative is to have a software engineer **build a prototype** that **concretely demonstrates some important dimension of an implementation**." | 如果需求规格说明的形式不允许直接仿真或执行，另一个办法是让软件工程师**构建一个原型**，**具体地展示实现的某个重要维度**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 5.3, para. 1, p. 1-16](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
