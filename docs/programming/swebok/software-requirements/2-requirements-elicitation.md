# 2. Requirements Elicitation

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Requirements Elicitation**<br>需求获取 | "The goal of **requirements elicitation** is to **surface candidate requirements**. It is also called requirements capture, requirements discovery or requirements acquisition." | **需求获取**的目标是**让候选需求浮现出来**。它也叫需求捕获、需求发现或需求采集。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 2, para. 1, p. 1-6](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Requirements Sources**<br>需求来源 | "Requirements come — can be elicited — from **many different sources**. All potential requirements sources should be **identified and evaluated**." | 需求来自（也就是可以获取自）**许多不同的来源**。所有潜在的需求来源都应**被识别和评估**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 2.1, para. 1, p. 1-6](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Stakeholder**<br>干系人 | "A **stakeholder** can be defined as **any person, group or organization** that: is actively involved in the project; is affected by the project’s outcome; can influence the project’s outcome." | **干系人**可以定义为满足以下条件的**任何个人、群体或组织**：积极参与项目；受项目结果影响；能影响项目结果。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 2.1, para. 1, p. 1-6](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Clients**<br>委托方 | "**clients** — **those who pay** for the software to be constructed (e.g., organizational management);" | **委托方**：**为构建软件付钱的人**（例如组织的管理层）； | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 2.1, para. 2, item 1, p. 1-6](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Customers**<br>客户 | "**customers** — those who **decide whether a software product will be put into service**;" | **客户**：**决定软件产品是否投入使用**的人； | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 2.1, para. 2, item 2, p. 1-6](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Users**<br>用户 | "**users** — those who **interact directly or indirectly** with the software;" | **用户**：**直接或间接与软件交互**的人； | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 2.1, para. 2, item 3, p. 1-6](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |

| **Stakeholder Classes**<br>干系人类别 | "**Stakeholder classes** are **groups of stakeholders that have similar perspectives and needs**." | **干系人类别**是**视角和需要相近的干系人群组**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 2.1, para. 3, p. 1-7](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Stakeholder Analysis**<br>干系人分析 | "Many projects benefit from performing a **stakeholder analysis** to **identify as many important stakeholder classes as possible**." | 许多项目得益于做一次**干系人分析**，以**尽可能多地识别重要的干系人类别**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 2.1, para. 4, p. 1-7](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Common Requirements Elicitation Techniques**<br>常用需求获取技术 | "A wide variety of techniques can be used to **elicit requirements from stakeholders**. Some techniques work **better with certain stakeholder classes** than others." | 可以用多种技术**从干系人那里获取需求**。有些技术**对某些干系人类别更有效**。 | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 2.2, para. 1, p. 1-7](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
| **Interviews**<br>访谈 | (No definition) | （无定义） | [Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 2.2, para. 1, item 1, p. 1-7](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/v4) |
