## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1.2 Receipts and paid invoices**<br>收据与已付发票 | (Summary) Stripe **produces a receipt for each successful payment and refund**, which customers **read on the web** and may save as a PDF. | （概述）Stripe **为每笔成功的支付和退款生成收据**，客户可以**在网页上查看**，也可以保存为 PDF。 | [Stripe, n.d., "Receipts and paid invoices"](https://docs.stripe.com/receipts) |
| **Receipt URLs**<br>收据网址 | (Summary) **Each receipt has its own web address**, which an application can **take from the `receipt_url` field of the Charge object** to link customers to it. | （概述）**每张收据都有自己的网址**，应用可以**从 Charge 对象的 `receipt_url` 字段取得**，用来给客户提供链接。 | [Stripe, n.d., "Receipts and paid invoices", Receipt URLs](https://docs.stripe.com/receipts) |
