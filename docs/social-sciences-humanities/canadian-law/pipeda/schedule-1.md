# SCHEDULE 1 Principles Set Out in the National Standard of Canada Entitled Model Code for the Protection of Personal Information

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SCHEDULE 1 Principles Set Out in the National Standard of Canada Entitled Model Code for the Protection of Personal Information**<br>附表 1 加拿大国家标准《个人信息保护示范准则》所列原则 | (context) "Subject to sections 6 to 9, **every organization shall comply** with **the obligations set out in Schedule 1**." | （语境）在不违反第 6 至 9 条的前提下，**每个组织都应履行**其**附表 1 规定的义务**。 | [Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, s. 5(1)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/page-7.html#h-417659) |
| **4.1 Principle 1 — Accountability**<br>原则 1：责任 | "An organization is **responsible for personal information under its control** and shall **designate an individual or individuals** who are accountable for the organization’s compliance with the following principles." | 组织**对其控制下的个人信息负责**，并应**指定一名或多名个人**，负责组织对以下原则的遵守。 | [Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.1](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/page-7.html#h-417659) |
| **4.2 Principle 2 — Identifying Purposes**<br>原则 2：明确目的 | "The **purposes for which personal information is collected** shall be identified by the organization **at or before the time the information is collected**." | **收集个人信息的目的**应由组织**在收集信息时或之前**确定。 | [Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.2](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/page-7.html#h-417659) |
| **4.3 Principle 3 - Consent**<br>原则 3：同意 | "The **knowledge and consent of the individual** are required for **the collection, use, or disclosure of personal information**, except where inappropriate." | **个人的知情和同意**是**收集、使用或披露个人信息**的必要条件，不适宜的情况除外。 | [Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.3](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/page-7.html#h-417659) |
| **4.4 Principle 4 — Limiting Collection**<br>原则 4：限制收集 | "The collection of personal information shall be **limited to that which is necessary** for the purposes identified by the organization. Information shall be collected by **fair and lawful means**." | 个人信息的收集应**限于必要范围**，即组织所确定目的之所需。信息应通过**公平、合法的方式**收集。 | [Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.4](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/page-7.html#h-417659) |
| **4.5 Principle 5 —Limiting Use, Disclosure, and Retention**<br>原则 5：限制使用、披露和保留 | "Personal information shall **not be used or disclosed for purposes other than those for which it was collected**, except with the consent of the individual or as required by law. Personal information shall be **retained only as long as necessary** for the fulfilment of those purposes." | 个人信息**不得用于或披露给收集目的以外的用途**，经个人同意或法律要求的除外。个人信息**只应在必要期限内保留**，以实现上述目的。 | [Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.5](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/page-7.html#h-417659) |
| **4.6 Principle 6 — Accuracy**<br>原则 6：准确 | "Personal information shall be **as accurate, complete, and up-to-date** as is necessary for **the purposes for which it is to be used**." | 个人信息应**准确、完整并保持最新**，达到**其使用目的**所必需的程度。 | [Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.6](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/page-7.html#h-417659) |
| **4.7 Principle 7 — Safeguards**<br>原则 7：保障措施 | "Personal information shall be **protected by security safeguards** appropriate to **the sensitivity of the information**." | 个人信息应**受到安全保障措施的保护**，措施应与**信息的敏感程度**相称。 | [Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.7](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/page-7.html#h-417659) |
| **4.8 Principle 8 — Openness**<br>原则 8：公开 | "An organization shall make **readily available to individuals** specific information about **its policies and practices** relating to the management of personal information." | 组织应**让个人能够方便地获得**关于**其个人信息管理政策和做法**的具体信息。 | [Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.8](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/page-7.html#h-417659) |
| **4.9 Principle 9 — Individual Access**<br>原则 9：个人查阅 | "Upon request, an individual shall be **informed of the existence, use, and disclosure** of his or her personal information and shall be **given access to that information**. An individual shall be able to challenge the accuracy and completeness of the information and **have it amended as appropriate**." | 经请求，个人应**被告知其个人信息的存在、使用和披露情况**，并应**获准查阅这些信息**。个人应能够对信息的准确性和完整性提出质疑，并**酌情要求修改**。 | [Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.9](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/page-7.html#h-417659) |
| **4.10 Principle 10 — Challenging Compliance**<br>原则 10：对合规提出质疑 | "An individual shall be able to **address a challenge concerning compliance** with the above principles to **the designated individual or individuals** accountable for the organization’s compliance." | 个人应能够就上述原则的遵守情况**提出质疑**，并向**组织指定的负责人**提出。 | [Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.10](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/page-7.html#h-417659) |
