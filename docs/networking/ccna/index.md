## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CCNA**<br>CCNA | "CCNA validates a **broad range of fundamentals for all IT careers** - from **networking technologies, to security, to software development** - proving you have the skills businesses need to meet market demands." | CCNA 验证**适用于所有 IT 职业的广泛基础知识**，从**网络技术到安全，再到软件开发**，证明你具备企业满足市场需求所需的技能。 | [Cisco, n.d.-b, "Validate essential skills in enterprise networking" section, para. 1](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/learn/training-certifications/certifications/enterprise/ccna/index.html#:~:text=CCNA%20validates%20a%20broad%20range%20of%20fundamentals) |
| **[Network Infrastructure and Connectivity](network-infrastructure-and-connectivity/index.md)**<br>网络基础设施与连接 | (context) "Network infrastructure refers to the **hardware and software** that enable **network connectivity and communication** between users, devices, apps, the internet, and more." | （语境）网络基础设施是指**硬件和软件**，它们实现用户、设备、应用、互联网等之间的**网络连接和通信**。 | [Cisco, n.d.-e, para. 1](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/learn/topics/networking/what-is-network-infrastructure.html#:~:text=Network%20infrastructure%20refers%20to%20the%20hardware) |
| **[Switching and Network Access](switching-and-network-access/index.md)**<br>交换与网络接入 | (context) "A network switch is a vital component of a computer network that **connects multiple devices**, like computers, printers, and servers, **within a local area network** (LAN)." | （语境）网络交换机是计算机网络的重要组成部分，它**连接多台设备**（例如计算机、打印机和服务器），范围是**局域网之内**（LAN）。 | [Cisco, n.d.-d, "What is a network switch?" section, para. 1](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/learn/topics/small-business/what-are-the-different-types-of-network-switches.html#:~:text=A%20network%20switch%20is%20a%20vital%20component) |
| **[IP Routing](ip-routing/index.md)**<br>IP 路由 | "Routing is the process of **selecting and defining paths for IP-packet traffic** within or between networks as well as the process of **managing network traffic overall**." | 路由是**为 IP 数据包流量选择和定义路径**的过程（在网络内或网络之间），也是**整体管理网络流量**的过程。 | [Cisco, n.d.-h, para. 1](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/learn/topics/networking/what-is-routing.html#:~:text=Routing%20is%20the%20process%20of%20selecting) |
| **[Network Services and Security](network-services-and-security/index.md)**<br>网络服务与安全 | (context) "Network services are **applications at the network application layer** that connect users working in offices, branches, or remote locations to applications and data in a network."<br>"Network security **combines policies and technologies** to protect systems and digital assets from unauthorized access, misuse, and disruption." | （语境）网络服务是**网络应用层上的应用**，把在办公室、分支机构或远程位置工作的用户连接到网络中的应用和数据。<br>网络安全**结合策略和技术**，保护系统和数字资产免遭未授权访问、滥用和中断。 | [Cisco, n.d.-c, para. 1](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/learn/topics/networking/what-are-network-services.html#:~:text=Network%20services%20are%20applications)<br>[Cisco, n.d.-g, para. 1](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/learn/topics/security/what-is-network-security.html#:~:text=Network%20security%20combines%20policies) |
| **[AI, and Network Operations and Management](ai-and-network-operations-and-management/index.md)**<br>AI 与网络运维和管理 | (context) "Network management encompasses both the **essential processes and the specialized platforms** used by IT and NetOps teams to **configure, monitor, and optimize network performance**." | （语境）网络管理涵盖**基本流程和专用平台**，IT 和 NetOps 团队用它们来**配置、监控和优化网络性能**。 | [Cisco, n.d.-f, para. 1](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/learn/topics/networking/what-is-network-management.html#:~:text=Network%20management%20encompasses%20both) |
