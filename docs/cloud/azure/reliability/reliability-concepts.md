## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文解释 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shared responsibility model for reliability**<br>可靠性共同责任模型 | "The shared responsibility model for reliability has **three levels**:" | 可靠性共同责任模型有**三个层级**： | [Microsoft, 2026b, "Shared responsibility model for reliability" section, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-shared-responsibility#:~:text=The%20shared%20responsibility%20model%20for%20reliability%20has%20three%20levels) |
| **Core platform reliability**<br>核心平台可靠性 | "The Azure platform provides a **base level of reliability** for **all customers and all services** through the underlying infrastructure, services, and processes." | Azure 平台通过底层基础结构、服务和流程，提供**基本的可靠性**，面向**所有客户和所有服务**。 | [Microsoft, 2026b, "Shared responsibility model for reliability" section, list item 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-shared-responsibility#:~:text=The%20Azure%20platform%20provides%20a%20base%20level%20of%20reliability) |
| **Reliability-enhancing capabilities**<br>可靠性增强功能 | "Azure offers a suite of **built-in features and services that enhance reliability**, such as using availability zones, deploying across multiple regions, and implementing backup strategies. While Azure provides these capabilities, it's **your responsibility to evaluate and configure them** to align with your specific requirements." | Azure 提供一套**增强可靠性的内置功能和服务**，例如使用可用区、跨多个区域部署以及实施备份策略。虽然 Azure 提供这些功能，但**评估和配置它们是你的责任**，以符合你的具体要求。 | [Microsoft, 2026b, "Shared responsibility model for reliability" section, list item 2](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-shared-responsibility#:~:text=Azure%20offers%20a%20suite%20of%20built-in%20features%20and%20services%20that%20enhance%20reliability) |
| **Applications**<br>应用程序 | "To make effective use of the other levels, you must **design your application and workload for reliability**." | 要有效利用其他层级，你必须**为可靠性设计你的应用程序和工作负载**。 | [Microsoft, 2026b, "Shared responsibility model for reliability" section, list item 3](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-shared-responsibility#:~:text=To%20make%20effective%20use%20of%20the%20other%20levels) |
| **High availability**<br>高可用性 | "*High availability* is the state in which a specific workload can **maintain its necessary level of uptime on a day-to-day basis**, **even during transient faults and intermittent failures**." | 高可用性是指特定工作负载能够**日常保持所需正常运行时间水平**的状态，**即使在发生暂时性故障和间歇性失败时**也是如此。 | [Microsoft, 2025a, "High availability" section, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-business-continuity-high-availability-disaster-recovery#:~:text=is%20the%20state%20in%20which%20a%20specific%20workload%20can%20maintain) |
| **Fault tolerance**<br>容错 | "*Fault tolerance* is the ability of a system to **continue operating, in some defined capacity, in the event of a failure**." | 容错是系统**在发生故障时仍能以某种既定能力继续运行**的能力。 | [Microsoft, 2025a, "Fault tolerance" section, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-business-continuity-high-availability-disaster-recovery#:~:text=is%20the%20ability%20of%20a%20system%20to%20continue%20operating) |
| **Scalability and elasticity**<br>可伸缩性和弹性 | "Scalability and elasticity are the abilities of a system to **handle increased load by adding and removing resources** (scalability), and to **do so quickly as your requirements change** (elasticity)." | 可伸缩性和弹性是系统的能力：**通过添加和移除资源来处理增加的负载**（可伸缩性），以及**在需求变化时快速做到这一点**（弹性）。 | [Microsoft, 2025a, "Scalability and elasticity" section, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-business-continuity-high-availability-disaster-recovery#:~:text=are%20the%20abilities%20of%20a%20system%20to%20handle%20increased%20load) |
| **Chaos engineering**<br>混沌工程 | "Testing your solution's ability to **automatically tolerate or recover from a variety of fault types** is called **chaos engineering**." | 测试解决方案**自动承受各种故障类型或从中恢复**的能力，称为**混沌工程**。 | [Microsoft, 2025a, "Automated testing" section, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-business-continuity-high-availability-disaster-recovery#:~:text=Testing%20your%20solution) |
| **Azure Chaos Studio**<br>Azure 混沌工作室 | "Azure Chaos Studio is a **chaos engineering tool** that can **simulate some common fault types**." | Azure 混沌工作室是一种**混沌工程工具**，可以**模拟一些常见的故障类型**。 | [Microsoft, 2025a, "Automated testing" section, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-business-continuity-high-availability-disaster-recovery#:~:text=is%20a%20chaos%20engineering%20tool%20that%20can%20simulate%20some%20common%20fault%20types) |
| **Recovery Point Objective (RPO)**<br>恢复点目标 | "Recovery Point Objective (RPO) is the **maximum duration of acceptable data loss** **in the event of a disaster**." | 恢复点目标（RPO）是**可接受的最长数据丢失时长**，针对的是**发生灾难的情况**。 | [Microsoft, 2025a, "Disaster recovery requirements" section, list item 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-business-continuity-high-availability-disaster-recovery#:~:text=is%20the%20maximum%20duration%20of%20acceptable%20data%20loss) |
| **Recovery Time Objective (RTO)**<br>恢复时间目标 | "Recovery Time Objective (RTO) is the **maximum duration of acceptable downtime** **in the event of a disaster**, where 'downtime' is defined by your specification." | 恢复时间目标（RTO）是**可接受的最长停机时长**，针对的是**发生灾难的情况**，其中“停机”由你的规范定义。 | [Microsoft, 2025a, "Disaster recovery requirements" section, list item 2](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-business-continuity-high-availability-disaster-recovery#:~:text=is%20the%20maximum%20duration%20of%20acceptable%20downtime) |
| **Redundancy**<br>冗余 | "*Redundancy* is the ability to maintain **multiple identical copies of a service component**, and to use those copies in a way that prevents any one component from becoming a **single point of failure**." | 冗余是维护**服务组件的多个相同副本**的能力，并以某种方式使用这些副本，防止任何一个组件成为**单点故障**。 | [Microsoft, 2025b, para. 3](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-redundancy-replication-backup#:~:text=is%20the%20ability%20to%20maintain%20multiple%20identical%20copies%20of%20a%20service%20component) |
| **Replication**<br>复制 | "*Replication or data redundancy* is the ability to maintain **multiple copies of data**, called **replicas**." | 复制（或数据冗余）是维护**数据的多个副本**的能力，这些副本称为**副本（replica）**。 | [Microsoft, 2025b, para. 4](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-redundancy-replication-backup#:~:text=is%20the%20ability%20to%20maintain%20multiple%20copies%20of%20data%2C%20called%20replicas) |
| **Backup**<br>备份 | "*Backup* is the ability to maintain a **timestamped copy of data** that can be used to **restore data that has been lost**." | 备份是维护**带时间戳的数据副本**的能力，可用来**恢复已丢失的数据**。 | [Microsoft, 2025b, para. 5](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-redundancy-replication-backup#:~:text=is%20the%20ability%20to%20maintain%20a%20timestamped%20copy%20of%20data) |
| **Failover**<br>故障转移 | "With failover, you can **redirect traffic and requests** from **unhealthy instances to healthy ones**." | 通过故障转移，你可以**重定向流量和请求**，**从运行不正常的实例转到正常的实例**。 | [Microsoft, 2026c, para. 2](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-failover-failback#:~:text=With%20failover%2C%20you%20can%20redirect%20traffic%20and%20requests) |
| **Active-active**<br>主动-主动 | "Some solutions are built to use an *active-active* approach, which means **multiple instances all serve requests**." | 有些解决方案采用*主动-主动*方式，即**多个实例都处理请求**。 | [Microsoft, 2026c, "Active and passive instance roles" section, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-failover-failback#:~:text=which%20means%20multiple%20instances%20all%20serve%20requests) |
| **Planned failover**<br>计划内故障转移 | "In contrast, *planned failovers* are those that you **proactively trigger**." | 相比之下，*计划内故障转移*是你**主动触发**的故障转移。 | [Microsoft, 2026c, "Planned and unplanned failovers" section](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-failover-failback#:~:text=are%20those%20that%20you%20proactively%20trigger) |
| **Unplanned failover**<br>计划外故障转移 | "*Unplanned failovers* are those that are performed **during an outage of a component**, so that you can **restore service by using another instance**." | *计划外故障转移*是在**某个组件中断期间**执行的故障转移，以便**使用另一个实例恢复服务**。 | [Microsoft, 2026c, "Planned and unplanned failovers" section](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-failover-failback#:~:text=are%20those%20that%20are%20performed%20during%20an%20outage) |
| **Failback**<br>故障回复 | "*Failback* is the process of **reinstating and redirecting traffic back to the original primary instance**." | *故障回复*是**恢复原始主实例并把流量重新导回它**的过程。 | [Microsoft, 2026c, "Failback" section, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-failover-failback#:~:text=is%20the%20process%20of%20reinstating%20and%20redirecting%20traffic%20back) |
| **Service-level agreement (SLA)**<br>服务级别协议 (SLA) | "An SLA is a **set of conditional commitments**. It states that *if* a customer **meets certain requirements**, *then* the service provider **commits to a defined level of service**." | SLA 是一组**有条件的承诺**。它规定：*如果*客户**满足某些要求**，*那么*服务提供商**承诺提供规定的服务水平**。 | [Microsoft, 2026a, "What an SLA is and isn't" section, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-service-level-agreements#:~:text=An%20SLA%20is%20a%20set%20of%20conditional%20commitments) |
| **Service-level objective (SLO)**<br>服务级别目标 (SLO) | "A **service-level objective (SLO)** is an **internal reliability target** that you set for **your own workload**." | **服务级别目标 (SLO)** 是一个**内部可靠性目标**，由你为**自己的工作负载**设定。 | [Microsoft, 2026a, "How SLAs relate to SLOs and reliability targets" section, list item 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-service-level-agreements#:~:text=is%20an%20internal%20reliability%20target) |
| **Service credits**<br>服务抵扣额 | "Service credits are typically a **percentage of the monthly service fee** for the **affected service**." | 服务抵扣额通常是**月度服务费的一个百分比**，按**受影响的服务**计算。 | [Microsoft, 2026a, "Step 4: File the claim within the required timeframe" section, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/concept-service-level-agreements#:~:text=Service%20credits%20are%20typically%20a%20percentage) |
