|---|---|---|---|
| **Azure Well-Architected Framework**<br>Azure 架构良好的框架 | "The **Azure Well-Architected Framework** is a set of **quality-driven tenets, architectural decision points, and review tools** intended to help solution architects **build a technical foundation for their workloads**." | **Azure 架构良好的框架**是一套**以质量为导向的原则、架构决策点和评审工具**，帮助解决方案架构师**为工作负载打好技术基础**。 | [Microsoft, 2026, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/#:~:text=The%20Azure%20Well-Architected%20Framework%20is%20a%20set) |
| **[Reliability](reliability.md)**<br>可靠性 | "Apply reliability guidance to your architecture to make your workload **resilient to malfunction** and to ensure that it **returns to a fully functioning state after a failure** occurs." | 在架构中应用可靠性指导，让工作负载**能抵御故障**，并确保它**在故障发生后恢复到完全正常的状态**。 | [Microsoft, 2025d, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/reliability/#:~:text=Apply%20reliability%20guidance%20to%20your%20architecture) |
| **[Security](security.md)**<br>安全性 | "Apply security guidance to your architecture to help ensure the **confidentiality, integrity, and availability** of your data and systems." | 在架构中应用安全指导，帮助确保数据和系统的**机密性、完整性和可用性**。 | [Microsoft, 2025e, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/security/#:~:text=Apply%20security%20guidance%20to%20your%20architecture) |
| **[Cost Optimization](cost-optimization.md)**<br>成本优化 | "Apply cost optimization guidance in your architecture to **sustain and improve your return on investment (ROI)**." | 在架构中应用成本优化指导，以**保持并提高投资回报率（ROI）**。 | [Microsoft, 2025a, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/cost-optimization/#:~:text=Apply%20cost%20optimization%20guidance%20in%20your%20architecture) |
| **[Operational Excellence](operational-excellence.md)**<br>卓越运营 | "Apply operational excellence guidance to your workload to ensure **workload quality** through **standardized processes and team cohesion**." | 在工作负载中应用卓越运营指导，通过**标准化流程和团队协作**来确保**工作负载质量**。 | [Microsoft, 2025b, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/operational-excellence/#:~:text=Apply%20operational%20excellence%20guidance%20to%20your%20workload) |
| **[Performance Efficiency](performance-efficiency.md)**<br>性能效率 | "Apply performance efficiency guidance to your architecture to **efficiently meet workload demands**." | 在架构中应用性能效率指导，以**高效地满足工作负载的需求**。 | [Microsoft, 2025c, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/performance-efficiency/#:~:text=Apply%20performance%20efficiency%20guidance%20to%20your%20architecture) |
