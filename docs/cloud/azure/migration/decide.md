| Term | Definition | 中文解释 | Source |

| **Azure Migrate appliance**<br>Azure Migrate 设备 | "The Azure Migrate appliance is a **virtual appliance** that can be deployed on a **server in your datacenter**. | **Business case**<br>业务案例 | "A business case helps you **estimate the costs and savings** of **moving to Azure**." | 业务案例帮助你**估算成本和节省**，针对的是**迁移到 Azure**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Decide" section, para. 4](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/migrate/migrate-services-overview#:~:text=A%20business%20case%20helps%20you%20estimate%20the%20costs%20and%20savings) |
