## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Media**<br>媒体 | "**videos, music, and photographs** that are **stored as particular types of file on a computer**" | 以**特定文件类型存储在计算机上**的**视频、音乐和照片**。 | [Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "media" noun (VIDEO/MUSIC FILES)](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/media) |
| **Azure AI Video Indexer**<br>Azure AI 视频索引器 | "Azure AI Video Indexer is a **comprehensive AI solution** that enables organizations to **extract deep insights from video** (live and uploaded) **and audio content**." | Azure AI Video Indexer 是一个**全面的 AI 解决方案**，让组织可以**从视频**（直播和上传的）**和音频内容中提取深入见解**。 | [Microsoft, 2026a, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-video-indexer/video-indexer-overview#:~:text=is%20a%20comprehensive%20AI%20solution%20that%20enables%20organizations) |
| **Azure Front Door and Content Delivery Network (CDN)**<br>Azure Front Door和内容分发网络（CDN） | "Azure Front Door is an **advanced content delivery network (CDN)** for the cloud. It's designed to provide **fast, reliable, and secure access** to your applications' static and dynamic web content globally." | Azure Front Door 是面向云的**高级内容分发网络（CDN）**。它旨在为你的应用程序在全球范围内的静态和动态 Web 内容提供**快速、可靠和安全的访问**。 | [Microsoft, 2026b, para. 2](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/frontdoor/front-door-overview#:~:text=is%20an%20advanced%20content%20delivery%20network) |
| **Azure Media Player**<br>Azure 媒体播放器 | "Azure Media Player is a **web video player** that **plays media content from Microsoft Azure Media Services** on a wide variety of browsers and devices." | Azure Media Player 是一个 **Web 视频播放器**，它在各种浏览器和设备上**播放来自 Microsoft Azure 媒体服务的媒体内容**。 | [Microsoft, 2021, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/media-services/azure-media-player/azure-media-player-overview#:~:text=is%20a%20web%20video%20player%20that%20plays%20media%20content) |
| **Content Protection**<br>内容保护 | (context) "Use Azure Media Services to **secure your media** from the time it leaves your computer all the way through **storage, processing, and delivery**." | （语境）使用 Azure 媒体服务**保护你的媒体**，从它离开你的计算机开始，一直到**存储、处理和交付**。 | [Microsoft, 2023b, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/media-services/latest/drm-content-protection-concept#:~:text=Use%20Azure%20Media%20Services%20to%20secure%20your%20media) |
| **Encoding**<br>编码 | "The term encoding in Media Services applies to the process of **converting files containing digital video and/or audio** from **one standard format to another**, with the purpose of (a) reducing the size of the files, and/or (b) producing a format that's compatible with a broad range of devices and apps." | Media Services 中的编码一词，指的是把**包含数字视频和/或音频的文件**从**一种标准格式转换为另一种格式**的过程，目的是（a）减小文件大小，和/或（b）生成与各种设备和应用兼容的格式。 | [Microsoft, 2023c, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/media-services/latest/encode-concept#:~:text=The%20term%20encoding%20in%20Media%20Services%20applies%20to) |
| **Live and On-Demand Streaming**<br>实时和按需流式处理 | (context) "In Azure Media Services, **live events** are responsible for **processing live streaming content**." | （语境）在 Azure 媒体服务中，**直播活动**负责**处理直播流内容**。 | [Microsoft, 2023d, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/media-services/latest/stream-live-tutorial-with-api#:~:text=are%20responsible%20for%20processing%20live%20streaming%20content) |
| **Media Services**<br>媒体服务 | "Azure Media Services is a **cloud-based platform** that enables you to build solutions that achieve **broadcast-quality video streaming**, enhance accessibility and distribution, analyze content, and much more." | Azure 媒体服务是一个**基于云的平台**，让你可以构建实现**广播级视频流式处理**、增强可访问性和分发、分析内容等功能的解决方案。 | [Microsoft, 2023a, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/media-services/latest/media-services-overview#:~:text=is%20a%20cloud-based%20platform%20that%20enables%20you%20to%20build%20solutions) |
