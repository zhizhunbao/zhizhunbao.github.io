# 2.1 Physical infrastructure

Sources: [AZ-900 unit: Describe Azure physical infrastructure](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/describe-core-architectural-components-of-azure/5-describe-azure-physical-infrastructure).

## Classification

![Physical infrastructure sorted into five groups: datacenters (servers arranged in racks, power, cooling, networking infrastructure), availability zones (zonal, zone-redundant and non-regional services), regions (low-latency network), region pairs (paired in two directions, paired in only one direction, data-residency) and sovereign regions (US DoD Central, US Gov Virginia, US Gov Arizona, China East, China North).](media/physical-infrastructure.png)

| **Availability zones**<br>可用区 | "Availability zones are **physically separate datacenters** within an **Azure region**." | 可用区是**物理上分离的数据中心**，位于 **Azure 区域**内。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Availability Zones" section, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/describe-core-architectural-components-of-azure/5-describe-azure-physical-infrastructure#:~:text=Availability%20zones%20are%20physically%20separate%20datacenters%20within%20an%20Azure%20region) |
| **Zonal services**<br>可用区服务 | "**Zonal services**: You **pin the resource to a specific zone** (for example, VMs, managed disks, IP addresses)." | **可用区服务**：你把**资源固定到特定的可用区**（例如虚拟机、托管磁盘、IP 地址）。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Use availability zones for your workloads" section, list item 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/describe-core-architectural-components-of-azure/5-describe-azure-physical-infrastructure#:~:text=You%20pin%20the%20resource%20to%20a%20specific%20zone) |
| **Zone-redundant services**<br>区域冗余服务 | "**Zone-redundant services**: The platform **replicates automatically across zones** (for example, zone-redundant storage, SQL Database)." | **区域冗余服务**：平台**自动跨可用区复制**（例如区域冗余存储、SQL 数据库）。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Use availability zones for your workloads" section, list item 2](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/describe-core-architectural-components-of-azure/5-describe-azure-physical-infrastructure#:~:text=The%20platform%20replicates%20automatically%20across%20zones) |
| **Non-regional services**<br>非区域服务 | "**Non-regional services**: Services are **always available from Azure geographies** and are **resilient to zone-wide outages as well as region-wide outages**." | **非区域服务**：服务**始终可从 Azure 地理位置获得**，并且**能承受整个可用区乃至整个区域的中断**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Use availability zones for your workloads" section, list item 3](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/describe-core-architectural-components-of-azure/5-describe-azure-physical-infrastructure#:~:text=Services%20are%20always%20available%20from%20Azure%20geographies) |
| **Regions**<br>区域 | "A region is a **geographical area on the planet** that contains **at least one, but potentially multiple datacenters** that are nearby and networked together with a **low-latency network**." | 区域是**地球上的一个地理区域**，包含**至少一个、也可能多个数据中心**，它们彼此相邻，并通过**低延迟网络**互联。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Regions" section, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/describe-core-architectural-components-of-azure/5-describe-azure-physical-infrastructure#:~:text=A%20region%20is%20a%20geographical%20area%20on%20the%20planet) |
| **Low-latency network**<br>低延迟网络 | (context) "A region is a geographical area on the planet that contains at least one, but potentially multiple datacenters that are nearby and networked together with a **low-latency network**." | （语境）区域是地球上的一个地理区域，包含至少一个、也可能多个数据中心，它们彼此相邻，并通过**低延迟网络**互联。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Regions" section, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/describe-core-architectural-components-of-azure/5-describe-azure-physical-infrastructure#:~:text=networked%20together%20with%20a%20low%2Dlatency%20network) |
| **Region pairs**<br>区域对 | "Most Azure regions are **paired with another region** within the **same geography** (such as US, Europe, or Asia) **at least 300 miles away**." | 大多数 Azure 区域都**与另一个区域配对**，后者位于**同一地理位置**（如美国、欧洲或亚洲），且**相距至少 300 英里**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Region pairs" section, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/describe-core-architectural-components-of-azure/5-describe-azure-physical-infrastructure#:~:text=Most%20Azure%20regions%20are%20paired%20with%20another%20region) |
| **Paired in two directions**<br>双向配对 | "Most regions are **paired in two directions**, meaning they are **the backup for the region that provides a backup for them** (West US and East US back each other up)." | 大多数区域是**双向配对**的，也就是说，它们**为给自己提供备份的那个区域提供备份**（美国西部和美国东部互为备份）。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Additional advantages of region pairs" section, "Important" note](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/describe-core-architectural-components-of-azure/5-describe-azure-physical-infrastructure#:~:text=Most%20regions%20are%20paired%20in%20two%20directions) |
| **Paired in only one direction**<br>单向配对 | "However, some regions, such as Brazil South, are **paired in only one direction**. In a one-direction pairing, the **Primary region does not provide backup for its secondary region**." | 不过，有些区域（如巴西南部）**只单向配对**。在单向配对中，**主区域不为其次要区域提供备份**。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Additional advantages of region pairs" section, "Important" note](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/describe-core-architectural-components-of-azure/5-describe-azure-physical-infrastructure#:~:text=are%20paired%20in%20only%20one%20direction) |
| **Data-residency**<br>数据驻留 | (context) "Data continues to **reside within the same geography as its pair** (except for Brazil South) for **data-residency** and compliance purposes." | （语境）数据继续**存放在与其配对区域相同的地理位置内**（巴西南部除外），以满足**数据驻留**和合规要求。 | [Microsoft, n.d., "Additional advantages of region pairs" section, list item 3](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/describe-core-architectural-components-of-azure/5-describe-azure-physical-infrastructure#:~:text=for%20data%2Dresidency%20and%20compliance%20purposes) |
