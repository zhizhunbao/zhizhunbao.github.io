## Glossary

| Term | Definition | 中文翻译 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mixed Reality**<br>混合现实 | "**Mixed reality** is a **blend of physical and digital worlds**, unlocking **natural and intuitive 3D human, computer, and environmental interactions**." | **混合现实**是**物理世界与数字世界的融合**，开启了人、计算机与环境之间**自然、直观的 3D 交互**。 | [Microsoft, 2021, para. 2](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/mixed-reality/discover/mixed-reality#:~:text=Mixed%20reality%20is%20a%20blend%20of%20physical%20and%20digital%20worlds) |
| **Azure Digital Twins**<br>Azure 数字孪生 | "Azure Digital Twins is a **platform as a service (PaaS) offering** that enables the **creation of twin graphs based on digital models of entire environments**, which could be buildings, factories, farms, energy networks, railways, stadiums, and more—even entire cities." | Azure 数字孪生是一种**平台即服务（PaaS）产品**，它支持**基于整个环境的数字模型创建孪生图**，这些环境可以是建筑、工厂、农场、能源网络、铁路、体育场等，甚至是整座城市。 | [Microsoft, 2025, para. 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/digital-twins/overview#:~:text=is%20a%20platform%20as%20a%20service%20%28PaaS%29%20offering) |
