## International Students

IRCC open data, Temporary Residents: Study Permit Holders, Canada - Post-Secondary by Province/Territory of Designated Learning Institution, DLI name, Institution Type and Year in which permit(s) became effective (January 2016 - September 2026, source line "IRCC, September 30, 2026"). A count is the number of study permits that took effect in the year, not the students enrolled. Each row is one DLI as IRCC names it, so a partner campus run under another college's name is a row of its own. IRCC rounds values to the nearest 5 and shows 0 to 5 as "--". Canada total: 525,770 (2023), 369,790 (2024), 245,575 (2025).
