# Interview: The Full Stack Midterm Project

The full story is in [The Full Stack Midterm Project](../../learning/2026/full-stack-midterm-project.md).

## Interview Version

| Step | Description | 中文翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| **Problem** | In a cloud-native course, the teacher put us into **random groups of three** for a midterm project: an order analytics service on RabbitMQ, deployed to Azure. One teammate insisted on a long, rigid plan and **rejected my proposal** to split the work and finish in three or four days. At his pace, I saw a real **risk of missing the deadline**. | 在一门云原生课上，老师把我们**随机分成三人一组**做期中项目：一个基于 RabbitMQ、部署到 Azure 的订单分析服务。一位组员坚持一个又长又死板的计划，**拒绝了我的提议**：分头做、三四天做完。按他的节奏，我看到了**赶不上截止时间的实际风险**。 |
| **What I did** | From how he behaved in class, often late or absent, I already had a first impression, and his reaction to my proposal confirmed it. I didn't argue. I **built a complete backup on my own**, code, deployment, video and documentation, and **told the teacher about the risk early**, without blaming anyone. When our tasks depended on each other too much, I **changed the split so nobody had to wait**, and persuaded him to agree. | 从他上课的表现，比如经常迟到、缺课，我已经有了第一印象；他对我提议的反应证实了这个印象。我没有争论。我**自己做了一个完整的备份**：代码、部署、视频和文档，并且**提前告诉老师有风险**，没有责怪任何人。当我们的任务互相依赖太多时，我**调整了分工，让谁也不用等谁**，并说服他同意。 |
| **Result** | On the deadline night he still hadn't submitted by 11:30, so **I submitted at 11:45** and told him his final version could replace mine. He submitted at 11:55. **The group submitted on time.** | 截止那晚，他到 11:30 还没提交，所以**我 11:45 先提交了**，告诉他可以用他的最终版本覆盖。他 11:55 提交。**全组按时提交。** |
| **Answers** | Questions about **working with a difficult teammate**, **managing risk**, conflict and communication. | 用来回答关于**和难合作的队友共事**、**风险管理**、冲突和沟通的提问。 |
