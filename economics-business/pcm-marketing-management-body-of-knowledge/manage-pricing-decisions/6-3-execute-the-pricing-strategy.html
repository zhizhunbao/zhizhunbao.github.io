              6.3 Execute the Pricing Strategy
              执行定价策略              		 (No definition) （无定义） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 3, p. 14
              Just-noticeable difference
              最小可觉差              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 3, Key Terms, p. 14
              Price-fixing
              价格操纵              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 3, Key Terms, p. 14
              Price discrimination
              价格歧视              		 "Price tailoring (or price discrimination) is the practice of charging different buyers different prices for the same quantity and quality of product." 差别定价（或价格歧视）是向不同买家收取不同价格的做法，针对的是同样数量和质量的产品。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Price tailoring", Definition
              Deceptive pricing
              欺骗性定价              		 "Deceptive pricing means savings claims, price comparisons, “special” sales, “two-for-one” sales, “factory” prices, or “wholesale” prices that are unlawful if false or deceptive." 欺骗性定价指节省宣称、价格比较、「特价」销售、「买一送一」、「出厂价」或「批发价」等，若虚假或具欺骗性即属违法。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Unfair competition", Definition
              Bait and switch
              诱饵调包              		 "Bait (or bait-and-switch) advertising is an alluring but deceptive and insincere offer whereby the advertiser does not intend to sell the advertised product or service at the unusually low advertised price." 诱饵（或诱饵调包）广告是一种诱人但具欺骗性、并无诚意的报价：广告主并不打算按广告上那个异常低的价格出售所宣传的产品或服务。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Bait advertising", Definition
              Predatory pricing
              掠夺性定价              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 3, Key Terms, p. 14
              Fair trade laws
              公平贸易法              		 "Fair trade laws permitted suppliers of branded goods to impose resale price maintenance contracts fixing minimum retail prices." 公平贸易法曾允许品牌商品的供应商强制推行转售价格维持合同，规定最低零售价。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Fair trade laws", Definition
              Minimum markup laws
              最低加成法              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 3, Key Terms, p. 14
              Loss leader products
              亏本引流品              		 "A loss leader is an item that is sold at a “loss” of markup that would normally be obtained on the particular item, for the express purpose of increasing store traffic." 亏本引流品是放弃该商品通常应得的加成出售的商品，明确目的是增加门店客流。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Loss leader", Definition
