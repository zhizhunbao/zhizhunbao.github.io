              Odd pricing
              奇数定价              		 (context) "Odd price refers to a price ending in an odd number (e.g., 1,3,5,7,9) or a price just under a round number (e.g., $0.89, $3.99, $44.98)." （语境）奇数价格指以奇数结尾的价格（如 1、3、5、7、9）或略低于整数的价格（如 $0.89、$3.99、$44.98）。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Pricing strategy", Definition
              Even pricing
              偶数定价              		 (context) "Even price refers to a price ending in a whole number or in tenths (e.g., $0.50, $5.00, $8.10, $75.00)." （语境）偶数价格指以整数结尾或以十分位结尾的价格（如 $0.50、$5.00、$8.10、$75.00）。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Pricing strategy", Definition
              Psychological pricing
              心理定价              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 2, Key Terms, p. 14
              One-price strategy
              单一价格策略              		 (context) "A one-price policy dictates that, at a given time, all customers pay the same price for any given item of merchandise." （语境）单一价格政策规定，在同一时间，任何一件商品所有客户都付同样的价格。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Pricing strategy", Definition
              Variable pricing
              可变定价              		 (context) "A variable price policy adjusts prices to different customers depending on their relative purchasing power or bargaining ability." （语境）可变价格政策对不同客户调整价格，依据是客户的相对购买力或议价能力。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Pricing strategy", Definition
              Everyday low pricing (EDLP)
              天天低价（EDLP）              		 "Everyday low price is a retailing policy or strategy whereby presumably low prices are set initially on items and maintained from period to period, as opposed to the occasional offering of items at special or reduced sales prices." 天天低价是一种零售政策或策略：商品一开始就定在应属较低的价格，并逐期保持不变，而不是偶尔以特价或降价出售。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Pricing strategy", Definition
              High/low pricing
              高低价策略              		 "Hi-Lo (or high-low) pricing is the opposite of everyday low pricing." 高低价策略是反过来的做法，与天天低价相反。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Pricing strategy", Definition
              Auction pricing
              拍卖定价              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 2, Key Terms, p. 14
              Reverse auctions
              反向拍卖              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 2, Key Terms, p. 14
              Cost-plus pricing
              成本加成定价              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 2, Key Terms, p. 14
              Markup on cost
              按成本加成              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 2, Key Terms, p. 14
              Markup on sales price
              按售价加成              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 2, Key Terms, p. 14
              Average cost pricing
              平均成本定价              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 2, Key Terms, p. 14
              Target return pricing
              目标回报定价              		 "Target return pricing is a method of pricing that attempts to cover all costs and achieve a target return." 目标回报定价是一种力求覆盖全部成本并达到目标回报的定价方法。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Price", Definition
              Discounts
              折扣              		 "Discount refers to a price reduction offered by a seller to a buyer." 折扣指价格减让，由卖方给买方。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Discount", Definition
              Allowances
              折让              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 2, Key Terms, p. 14
              Cash discounts
              现金折扣              		 "A cash discount is a discount offered to customers for prompt payment of invoices." 现金折扣是为客户及时支付其发票款项而给予的折扣。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Discount", Definition
              Trade discounts
              交易折扣              		 "A trade discount (or functional discount) is a discount offered to a retailer or other channel member for the functions performed in the movement of goods from the manufacturer to the consumer." 交易折扣（或功能折扣）是因零售商或其他渠道成员在商品从制造商流向消费者的过程中所承担的职能而给予的折扣。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Discount", Definition
              Quantity discounts
              数量折扣              		 "A quantity discount is a reduction in price for volume purchases." 数量折扣是一种价格减让，针对大批量购买。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Discount", Definition
              Seasonal discounts
              季节折扣              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 2, Key Terms, p. 14
              Promotional allowances
              促销折让              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 2, Key Terms, p. 14
              FOB Pricing
              离岸价定价（FOB）              		 (context) "FOB origin is a form of geographical pricing in which the seller quotes prices from the point of shipment." （语境）起运地离岸价是一种地理定价方式，卖方以发货地点为准报价。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Free on board", Definition
              Uniform delivered pricing
              统一交货定价              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） American Marketing Association, n.d., Domain 6, Topic 2, Key Terms, p. 14
              Zone pricing
              分区定价              		 "Zone pricing is the delivered cost based on factory price plus averaged freight rate for the section or territory to which goods are shipped (same delivered cost to all in the zone)." 分区定价是按出厂价加上货物运往的区段或区域的平均运费算出的交货成本（同一区内交货成本相同）。 Marketing Accountability Standards Board, n.d., "Price", Definition
