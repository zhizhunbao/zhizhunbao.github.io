              Artificial Time Constraints
              人为时间限制              		 "pricing strategy that creates a sense of urgency in buyers’ minds" 制造紧迫感的定价策略，作用于买家心中 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Fixed Costs
              固定成本              		 "costs of doing business that do not change based on number of units produced" 不会变化的经营成本，不受生产数量影响 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Price
              价格              		 "the exchange of something of value between a buyer and seller" 有价值之物的交换，发生在买卖双方之间 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Price Anchoring
              价格锚定              		 "a frame of reference for a buyer to set an expectation of a price" 一种参照框架，让买家对价格形成预期 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Price Appearance
              价格外观              		 "the way in which a customer perceives a price based on how it is visually represented" 客户对价格的感知方式，取决于价格在视觉上如何呈现 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Price Gouging
              价格欺诈              		 "when companies take advantage of a situation, typically an emergency or natural disaster, and charge exceptionally high prices for products or services" 企业利用某种局面（通常是紧急情况或自然灾害），对产品或服务收取异常高的价格 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Profit
              利润              		 "the financial gain of a company" 财务收益，即一家企业获得的收益 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Total Costs
              总成本              		 "total expenses of doing business" 全部开支，即经营业务的开支 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Total Revenue
              总收入              		 "the money generated from normal business operations" 所产生的资金，来自正常经营活动 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Variable Costs
              变动成本              		 "costs that vary based on the number of units produced" 会变化的成本，随生产数量而定 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
