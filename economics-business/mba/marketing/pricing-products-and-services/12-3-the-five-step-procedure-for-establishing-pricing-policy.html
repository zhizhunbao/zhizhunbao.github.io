              12.3 The Five-Step Procedure for Establishing Pricing Policy

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              12 Pricing Products and Services
              产品与服务定价              		 (No definition) （无定义） Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12
              12.3 The Five-Step Procedure for Establishing Pricing Policy
              制定定价政策的五步程序              		 (No definition) （无定义） Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, section 12.3
              Competition-Based Objective
              竞争导向目标              		 "product pricing based on the prices of a company’s competitors" 依据价格为产品定价，参照的是企业竞争对手的价格 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Consumer Confidence
              消费者信心              		 "an economic indicator that measures the degree of optimism that consumers have regarding the overall state of the country’s economy and their own financial situations" 一种经济指标，衡量消费者的乐观程度，即对国家整体经济状况及自身财务状况的看法 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Cost-Based Objective
              成本导向目标              		 "product pricing based on the costs of doing business" 依据成本为产品定价，即经营业务的成本 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Cross-Elasticity of Demand
              需求交叉弹性              		 "the change in price of one good or service as a similar good or service’s price changes" 某一商品或服务价格的变化，随同类商品或服务价格的变动而发生 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Customer Value–based Objective
              顾客价值导向目标              		 "product pricing based on a company’s understanding of the value-added benefits of a product" 依据企业的理解为产品定价，即企业对产品附加价值利益的理解 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Customer-Driven Objective
              顾客驱动目标              		 "product pricing based on what a customer is willing to pay for a product or service" 依据客户愿意支付的价格来定价，针对的是产品或服务 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Demand
              需求              		 "a buyer’s ability and willingness to purchase a specific product or service" 买家的能力和意愿，即购买某一特定产品或服务的能力和意愿 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Demand Curve
              需求曲线              		 "a graph that illustrates the relationship between demand and price" 表示关系的图，即需求与价格之间的关系 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Demand Elasticity
              需求弹性              		 "measure of the change in the quantity demanded in relation to the change in its price" 衡量需求量的变化相对于其价格变化的程度 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Discretionary Income
              可自由支配收入              		 "a household’s money that is left over after all taxes and necessities are paid" 家庭剩余的钱，即在缴清全部税款和必需开支之后 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Income Effect
              收入效应              		 "the perception buyers have of how price changes will affect their income" 买家的感知，即他们认为价格变动会如何影响自己的收入 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Inflation
              通货膨胀              		 "an economic measure of the rate of rising prices of goods and services in an economy" 衡量价格上涨速度的经济指标，对象是经济中的商品和服务 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Market Share–oriented Objective
              以市场份额为目标的目标              		 "setting prices at, below, or above competitors in an effort to increase market share" 将价格定在与竞争对手持平、低于或高于其水平，以求提高市场份额 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Prestige Pricing
              声望定价              		 "a strategy marketers use to set high prices knowing that demand will increase with higher prices because the higher price increases the perceived value of the product" 营销者定高价的策略：他们知道价格越高需求反而越大，因为高价提高了产品的感知价值 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Sales-Oriented Objective
              以销售为目标的目标              		 "setting prices based on the goal of increasing the volume of sales" 以提高为目标来定价，提高的是销售量 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Substitutes
              替代品              		 "products and services that are similar to the one being offered" 相似的产品和服务，与所提供的那一种相似 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Target Return Objective
              目标收益目标              		 "setting prices so they return a specific profit during a given period of time" 定价使其带来特定利润，期限为一定时期 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms
              Unemployment Rate
              失业率              		 "measure of the number of people not employed in an economy during a given period of time" 衡量经济中未就业人数的指标，按一定时期计 Gomez Albrecht et al., 2023, chapter 12 Key Terms

              References
