              2.3 Job Profiles

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              Job Profiles
              职业概况              		 (context) "Learn about an occupation: available jobs, wages, career prospects, skills, job requirements and more." （语境）了解一个职业：在招职位、工资、职业前景、技能、入职要求等。 Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Job Profiles", para. 1
              Summary
              概要              		 (No definition) （无定义） Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", tab "Summary"
              Description
              描述              		 (No definition) （无定义） Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", tab "Description"
              Wages
              工资              		 (No definition) （无定义） Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", tab "Wages"
              Prospects
              前景              		 (No definition) （无定义） Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", tab "Prospects"
              Jobs
              职位              		 (No definition) （无定义） Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", tab "Jobs"
              Requirements
              要求              		 (No definition) （无定义） Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", tab "Requirements"
              Competencies
              能力素质              		 (No definition) （无定义） Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", tab "Competencies"
              Employment requirements
              入职要求              		 "This is what you typically need for the job." 这是通常需要具备的条件，针对这份工作。 Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Employment requirements"
              Professional certification and licensing
              职业认证与执照              		 "You might need to get a certification and/or license from a regulatory authority (or become registered as an apprentice) before you start working." 你可能需要向监管机构取得认证和／或执照（或注册为学徒），然后才能开始工作。 Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Registered nurse - extended class (RN-EC) in Canada", "Professional certification and licensing"
              Foreign credential recognition
              外国资历认证              		 "you have to request an assessment of the training, education and/or work experience that you obtained in another country to verify if your qualifications are recognized in Canada. This process is called foreign credential recognition." 你必须申请对你在其他国家获得的培训、教育和／或工作经验进行评估，以核实你的资历在加拿大是否被认可。这个过程叫外国资历认证。 Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Registered nurse - extended class (RN-EC) in Canada", "Professional certification and licensing"
              Skills
              技能              		 "Skills are capabilities that you develop over time." 技能是能力，是你随时间培养起来的。 Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Skills"
              Personal attributes
              个人特质              		 "Personal attributes are the traits that you are born with." 个人特质是特质，是你与生俱来的。 Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Personal Attributes"
              Interests
              兴趣              		 "Interests reflect your preferences for work environments and outcomes." 兴趣反映你的偏好，即对工作环境和工作成果的偏好。 Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Interest"
              Realistic jobs
              现实型工作              		 "Realistic jobs are all about handling objects, tools, machines, or animals in a standard way." 现实型工作的核心是操作物件、工具、机器或动物，而且是以标准的方式。 Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Interest"
              Investigative jobs
              研究型工作              		 "Investigative jobs are all about studying things in an organized or creative way." 研究型工作的核心是研究事物，方式是有条理的或有创造性的。 Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Interest"
              Artistic jobs
              艺术型工作              		 "Artistic jobs are all about expressing yourself in a creative way to make visual, performing, written, or applied art." 艺术型工作的核心是表达自我，以有创造性的方式做出视觉、表演、文字或应用艺术。 Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Interest"
              Social jobs
              社会型工作              		 "Social jobs are all about working with people. This often involves interacting with others to help, care, teach, or inform them." 社会型工作的核心是与人打交道，常常要与他人互动，去帮助、照顾、教导或告知他们。 Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Interest"
              Enterprising jobs
              企业型工作              		 "Enterprising jobs are all about taking action to achieve goals for a group or for yourself." 企业型工作的核心是采取行动，为团队或自己实现目标。 Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Interest"
              Conventional jobs
              常规型工作              		 "Conventional jobs are all about following procedures and routines." 常规型工作的核心是遵循程序和惯例。 Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Interest"
              Knowledge
              知识              		 "Knowledge refers to the understanding of different theories and practices." 知识指理解，即对各种理论与实践的理解。 Employment and Social Development Canada, n.d., "Chef in Canada", "Knowledge"

              References
