              8.1 Education Quality and Accountability Office

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              Education Quality and Accountability Office
              安大略省教育质量与问责办公室（EQAO）              		 "As an evidence-based research-informed organization, EQAO is focused on empowering educators, parents, guardians, stakeholders and the public at large with the insights and information needed to support student learning and improve student outcomes." 作为一个以证据为本、以研究为依据的机构，EQAO 致力于为教育工作者、家长、监护人、利益相关方和广大公众赋能，提供支持学生学习所需的洞察和信息，并改善学生的学业成果。 Education Quality and Accountability Office, n.d., para. 1
              Provincial standard
              省标准              		 "(context) Percentage of fully participating students at or above the provincial standard (Levels 3 and 4) for the 2025-2026 school year." （语境）2025-2026 学年全程参加测评的学生中，达到或超过省标准（第 3、4 级）的比例。 Education Quality and Accountability Office, 2026, "Percentage of fully participating students at or above the provincial standard" heading
              Ontario Secondary School Literacy Test (OSSLT)
              安大略省中学读写能力测试              		 "(context) Percentage of First-Time Eligible Students Who Were Successful" （语境）首次具备资格并通过测试的学生所占的比例 Education Quality and Accountability Office, 2026, "Percentage of First-Time Eligible Students Who Were Successful" heading
