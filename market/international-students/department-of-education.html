              （澳大利亚）教育部              		 "We provide strategic direction and national leadership of Australia’s education system – through early years, school, higher education and research." 我们为澳大利亚教育体系提供战略方向和国家层面的领导，涵盖幼儿、中小学、高等教育和科研。 Department of Education, n.d., "Our role - what we do", para. 1
