              Canada's Top 50 Research Colleges
              加拿大 50 强研究型学院              		 "(context) The combined sponsored research income of the colleges and cégeps on Canada's Top 50 Research Colleges reached $314.8 million in Fiscal 2024, a strong gain of 19.8% over Fiscal 2023." （语境）加拿大 50 强研究型学院榜上各学院和 cégep 的赞助研究收入合计在 2024 财年达到 3.148 亿加元，比 2023 财年大幅增长 19.8%。 Research Infosource, 2025b, para. 1
