              6.5 U.S. News

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              U.S. News
              美国新闻与世界报道              		 "U.S. News & World Report is a multifaceted digital media company dedicated to helping consumers, business leaders and policy officials make important decisions in their lives, businesses and communities." 《美国新闻与世界报道》是一家多元化的数字媒体公司，致力于帮助消费者、企业领导者和政策制定者做出重要决定，涉及他们的生活、企业和社区。 U.S. News & World Report, n.d., para. 1
              Best Global Universities
              全球最佳大学排名              		 "The 12th annual U.S. News & World Report Best Global Universities rankings compare institutions worldwide based on academic research performance and international reputation." 第 12 届年度《美国新闻与世界报道》全球最佳大学排名比较全球各地的院校，依据是学术研究表现和国际声誉。 Wellington & Brooks, 2026, para. 1

              Indicators

              Weights of the 2026-2027 edition: "Global rankings are calculated using 13 indicators", "unchanged from the prior edition"; the groups are the article's section headings.

              Group Indicator Weight
              Qualitative Indicators Global research reputation 12.5%
              Regional research reputation 12.5%
              Bibliometric Indicators Publications 10%
              Books 2.5%
              Conferences 2.5%
              Normalized citation impact (NCI) 10%
              Total citations 7.5%
              Number of publications that are among the 10% most cited 12.5%
              Percentage of total publications that are among the 10% most cited 10%
              International collaboration – relative to country 5%
              International collaboration 5%
              Scientific Excellence Indicators Number of highly cited papers that are among the top 1% most cited in their respective field 5%
              Percentage of total publications that are among the top 1% most highly cited papers 5%

              Source: Wellington & Brooks, 2026, "Qualitative Indicators", "Bibliometric Indicators" and "Scientific Excellence Indicators" sections
