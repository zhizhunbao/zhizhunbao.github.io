              6. University Rankings

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              University Rankings
              大学排名              		 "(context) the position or level something or someone has in a list that compares their importance, quality, success, etc." （语境）某事物或某人所处的位置或级别，处在一份比较其重要性、质量、成功程度等的列表中 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "ranking" noun, Business English
              Maclean's
              麦克林              		 "In 1991, Maclean’s launched its inaugural University Rankings and quickly became the definitive voice on post-secondary education in Canada." 1991 年，Maclean's 推出了首届大学排名，并很快成为加拿大高等教育领域的权威声音。 Maclean's, n.d., "About us", para. 1
              QS
              QS              		 "QS Quacquarelli Symonds connects and supports the global higher education sector – students, institutions, governments and industry partners – through data-driven insights, expert guidance, global networks and services that drive better decisions and outcomes." QS Quacquarelli Symonds 连接并支持全球高等教育界（学生、院校、政府和行业伙伴），所凭借的是数据驱动的洞察、专家指导、全球网络和服务，以促成更好的决策和结果。 QS Quacquarelli Symonds, n.d., "About QS" section, para. 1
              ShanghaiRanking
              软科              		 "ShanghaiRanking Consultancy is a higher education data and analytics company dedicated to measuring and enhancing the global competitiveness of universities." 软科是一家高等教育数据与分析公司，致力于衡量并提升大学的全球竞争力。 ShanghaiRanking Consultancy, n.d., "About ShanghaiRanking" section, para. 1
              Times Higher Education
              泰晤士高等教育              		 "Times Higher Education’s mission is to be the definitive source of data, insight and expertise on higher education worldwide." 泰晤士高等教育的使命是成为数据、洞察和专业知识的权威来源，服务于全球高等教育。 Times Higher Education, n.d., "About us" section, para. 1
              U.S. News
              美国新闻与世界报道              		 "U.S. News & World Report is a multifaceted digital media company dedicated to helping consumers, business leaders and policy officials make important decisions in their lives, businesses and communities." 《美国新闻与世界报道》是一家多元化的数字媒体公司，致力于帮助消费者、企业领导者和政策制定者做出重要决定，涉及他们的生活、企业和社区。 U.S. News & World Report, n.d., para. 1
