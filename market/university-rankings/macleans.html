              6.1 Maclean's

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              Maclean's
              麦克林              		 "In 1991, Maclean’s launched its inaugural University Rankings and quickly became the definitive voice on post-secondary education in Canada." 1991 年，Maclean's 推出了首届大学排名，并很快成为加拿大高等教育领域的权威声音。 Maclean's, n.d.-a, "About us", para. 1
              Maclean's University Rankings
              麦克林大学排名              		 "Maclean’s annual university rankings continue a tradition established in 1991: to provide essential information in a comprehensive package to help students choose the university that best suits their needs." Maclean's 年度大学排名延续了 1991 年确立的传统：以完整的一揽子形式提供必要信息，帮助学生选择最适合自己需要的大学。 Maclean's, 2026d, para. 2

              University Rankings

              Large Universities

              Rank University Definition 中文翻译 Source
              1 McGill University
              麦吉尔大学              		 "McGill University attracts bright students from across Canada and around the world." 麦吉尔大学吸引着优秀的学生，他们来自加拿大各地和世界各国。 Maclean's, n.d.-b, school profile
              2 University of Toronto
              多伦多大学              		 "With more than 16,500 faculty, over 700 undergraduate programs and more than 102,000 students on three campuses, the University of Toronto is in a class of its own." 多伦多大学拥有 16,500 多名教职员、700 多个本科专业和 102,000 多名学生，分布在三个校区，自成一格。 Maclean's, n.d.-b, school profile
              3 University of British Columbia
              不列颠哥伦比亚大学              		 "An undergraduate could feel overwhelmed at a school as big as UBC, with its sprawling Point Grey campus and an international reputation for research and graduate programs." 在像 UBC 这样大的学校里，本科生可能会感到不知所措：它有广阔的 Point Grey 校区，并在研究和研究生项目上享有国际声誉。 Maclean's, n.d.-b, school profile
              4 University of Waterloo
              滑铁卢大学              		 "The University of Waterloo might be known for its science and engineering programs, but it offers more than 100 undergraduate programs across six faculties: arts, engineering, environment, health, math and science." 滑铁卢大学或许以理工科专业闻名，但它在文、工、环境、健康、数学和理学六个学院开设了 100 多个本科专业。 Maclean's, n.d.-b, school profile
              5 Queen’s University
              女王大学              		 "Queen’s University is a hub of academic research on the shore of Lake Ontario near the mouth of the St. Lawrence River." 女王大学是学术研究的中心，位于圣劳伦斯河口附近的安大略湖畔。 Maclean's, n.d.-b, school profile
              6 University of Alberta
              阿尔伯塔大学              		 "The research-intensive University of Alberta offers more than 200 undergraduate programs and over 500 graduate programs." 研究密集型的阿尔伯塔大学开设 200 多个本科专业和 500 多个研究生专业。 Maclean's, n.d.-b, school profile
              6* McMaster University
              麦克马斯特大学              		 "McMaster University sits on a green campus with ivy-covered buildings that blend in with modern buildings made of glass and steel." 麦克马斯特大学坐落在绿意盎然、楼宇爬满常春藤的校园里，这些老楼与玻璃和钢材建成的现代建筑相映成趣。 Maclean's, n.d.-b, school profile
              8 University of Ottawa
              渥太华大学              		 "The University of Ottawa is a diverse, bilingual university with more than 550 programs in 10 faculties." 渥太华大学是一所多元的双语大学，在 10 个学院开设 550 多个专业。 Maclean's, n.d.-b, school profile
              9 University of Calgary
              卡尔加里大学              		 "The University of Calgary, set on more than 200 hectares in the city’s northwest quadrant, has long been a leader in energy and engineering." 卡尔加里大学占地 200 多公顷，位于城市西北区，长期以来是能源和工程领域的领导者。 Maclean's, n.d.-b, school profile
              10 Western University
              西安大略大学              		 "Built along the banks of the Thames River in the “forest city” of London, Ontario, Western is one of the largest universities in the province." 西安大略大学建在安大略省“森林之城”伦敦的泰晤士河畔，是该省规模最大的大学之一。 Maclean's, n.d.-b, school profile
              11 Université Laval
              拉瓦尔大学              		 "Université Laval is a large, research-intensive institution in Quebec City." 拉瓦尔大学是一所大型研究密集型院校，位于魁北克城。 Maclean's, n.d.-b, school profile
              12 Université de Montréal
              蒙特利尔大学              		 "Université de Montréal looms large on the mountain, and its inﬂuence on Quebec society can be seen in its impressive list of graduates." 蒙特利尔大学高踞山上，它对魁北克社会的影响从一长串杰出校友中可见一斑。 Maclean's, n.d.-b, school profile
              13 York University
              约克大学              		 "With a student body numbering almost 55,000, it’s one of North America’s largest schools." 约克大学学生人数近 55,000，是北美规模最大的学校之一。 Maclean's, n.d.-b, school profile
              14 Toronto Metropolitan University
              多伦多都会大学              		 "More than 44,000 undergraduate and graduate students attend Toronto Metropolitan University, which offers programs in more than 125 disciplines." 44,000 多名本科生和研究生就读于多伦多都会大学，该校开设 125 个以上学科的专业。 Maclean's, n.d.-b, school profile
              15 Concordia University
              康考迪亚大学              		 "Concordia is one of Canada’s largest universities, offering more than 500 programs at the undergraduate and graduate levels." 康考迪亚是加拿大规模最大的大学之一，开设本科和研究生层次的 500 多个专业。 Maclean's, n.d.-b, school profile
