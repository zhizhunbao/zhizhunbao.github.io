              6.2 QS

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              QS
              QS              		 "QS Quacquarelli Symonds connects and supports the global higher education sector – students, institutions, governments and industry partners – through data-driven insights, expert guidance, global networks and services that drive better decisions and outcomes." QS Quacquarelli Symonds 连接并支持全球高等教育界（学生、院校、政府和行业伙伴），所凭借的是数据驱动的洞察、专家指导、全球网络和服务，以促成更好的决策和结果。 QS Quacquarelli Symonds, n.d.-a, "About QS" section, para. 1
              QS World University Rankings
              QS 世界大学排名              		 "The QS World University Rankings, the most comprehensive ranking of its kind, shines a light on the best institutions from across the world" QS 世界大学排名是同类排名中最全面的排名，它照亮的是来自世界各地的最佳院校 QS Quacquarelli Symonds, n.d.-b, para. 1
