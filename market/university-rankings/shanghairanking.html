              6.3 ShanghaiRanking

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              ShanghaiRanking
              软科              		 "ShanghaiRanking Consultancy is a higher education data and analytics company dedicated to measuring and enhancing the global competitiveness of universities." 软科是一家高等教育数据与分析公司，致力于衡量并提升大学的全球竞争力。 ShanghaiRanking Consultancy, n.d., "About ShanghaiRanking" section, para. 1
              Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU)
              世界大学学术排名              		 "ARWU presents the world's top 1000 research universities annually based on transparent methodology and objective third-party data." ARWU 每年发布全球前 1000 所研究型大学，依据是透明的方法和客观的第三方数据。 ShanghaiRanking Consultancy, n.d., "Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU)" section

              Indicators

              Weights of the 2026 edition: six indicators in four criteria.

              Criteria Indicator Code Weight
              Quality of Education Alumni of an institution winning Nobel Prizes and Fields Medals Alumni 10%
              Quality of Faculty Staff of an institution winning Nobel Prizes and Fields Medals Award 20%
              Highly Cited Researchers HiCi 20%
              Research Output Papers published in Nature and Science N&S 20%
              Papers indexed in Science Citation Index-Expanded and Social Science Citation Index (Web of Science) PUB 20%
              Per Capita Performance Per capita academic performance of an institution PCP 10%

              "For institutions specialized in humanities and social sciences such as London School of Economics, N&S is not considered, and the weight of N&S is relocated to other indicators."

              Source: ShanghaiRanking Consultancy, 2026c, "Indicators and Weights for ARWU" section
