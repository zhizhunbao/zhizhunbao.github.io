              7. Certification Bodies

              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              Certification Bodies
              认证机构              		 (context) "the process of giving official or legal approval to a person, company, product, etc. that has reached a particular standard" （语境）对达到特定标准的个人、公司、产品等给予正式或法定认可的过程 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "certification" noun
              Cisco
              Cisco 认证              		 (context) "CCNA validates a broad range of fundamentals for all IT careers ..." （语境）CCNA 验证适用于所有 IT 职业的广泛基础知识…… Cisco Systems, Inc., n.d., 'Validate essential skills in enterprise networking' section, para. 1
              CompTIA
              CompTIA（美国计算机行业协会）              		 "CompTIA plays a critical role in the global technology ecosystem. We are the largest vendor-neutral credentialing organization for technology workers ..." CompTIA 在全球技术生态系统中发挥着关键作用。我们是面向技术从业者的最大的厂商中立认证机构…… Computing Technology Industry Association, n.d., 'What is CompTIA’s mission?' section, para. 1
              ISC2
              ISC2（国际信息系统安全认证联盟）              		 "ISC2 is the world’s leading member association for cybersecurity professionals." ISC2 是全球领先的会员协会，服务于网络安全专业人员。 ISC2, n.d., 'Our Vision' section, para. 2
              ISTQB
              国际软件测试资质认证委员会（ISTQB）              		 "ISTQB® is the leading global certification scheme in the field of software testing." ISTQB® 是软件测试领域领先的全球认证体系。 International Software Testing Qualifications Board, n.d., heading
              Microsoft
              Microsoft 凭据（Microsoft Credentials）              		 "From role-based Certifications to scenario-based Applied Skills, now available for technical and business professionals, Microsoft-verified credentials help you grow your AI career ..." 从基于角色的认证到基于场景的 Applied Skills，现已面向技术和业务专业人员提供，经 Microsoft 验证的凭据帮助你发展 AI 职业…… Microsoft, n.d., para. 2
