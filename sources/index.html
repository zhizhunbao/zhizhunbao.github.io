              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              Sources
              来源              		 "someone or something that supplies information" 提供信息的人或事物 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "source" noun
              Standards Developing Organizations
              标准制定组织              		 "any organization that develops and approves standards using various methods to establish consensus among its participants." 任何以各种方法在参与者之间达成共识、以此制定并批准标准的组织。 National Institute of Standards and Technology, n.d., "standards developing organization" entry, definition 1
              Government Agencies
              政府机构              		 "a government organization" 政府组织 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "agency" noun (ORGANIZATION)
              Learned Societies
              学会              		 (context) "an organization to which people who share similar interests can belong" （语境）一个有共同兴趣的人可以加入的组织 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "society" noun (ORGANIZATION)
              Classification Systems
              分类体系              		 (context) "a system for naming and organizing things, especially plants and animals, into groups that share similar qualities" （语境）一种给事物命名并加以组织的体系，尤其用于植物和动物，把它们分成具有相似特性的组 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "taxonomy" noun
              Curricula
              课程标准              		 "the subjects studied in a school, college, etc. and what each subject includes" 学校、学院等所学的科目以及每个科目包括的内容 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "curriculum" noun
              Reference Works
              参考工具              		 (context) "a book of facts, such as a dictionary or an encyclopedia, that you look at to discover particular information" （语境）一种汇集事实的书，如词典或百科全书，供人查阅以找到特定信息 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "reference book" noun
              Certification Bodies
              认证机构              		 (context) "the process of giving official or legal approval to a person, company, product, etc. that has reached a particular standard" （语境）对达到特定标准的个人、公司、产品等给予正式或法定认可的过程 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "certification" noun
              Companies
              公司              		 "an organization that sells goods or services in order to make money" 一个销售商品或服务以赚钱的组织 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "company" noun (BUSINESS)
              Open-Source Projects
              开源项目              		 (context) "Open source software or information can be obtained legally and for free from the internet, and can be used, shared or changed without paying or asking for special permission" （语境）开源软件或信息可以从互联网上合法、免费地获取，并且无需付费或特别许可就能使用、分享或修改 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "open source" adjective
              Research and Advisory Firms
              研究咨询公司              		 (context) "detailed study of a subject, especially in order to discover new information about something or reach a new understanding of it" （语境）对某一主题的详细研究，尤其是为了发现新信息或获得新的理解 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "research" noun, definition 1
              News Media
              新闻媒体              		 "forms of communication, including the internet, newspapers, magazines, television, etc., that collect and give information about current events, considered as a group" 互联网、报纸、杂志、电视等收集并提供时事信息的传播形式的总称 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "news media" noun
