              Dielectrics and electrical insulation
              电介质与电气绝缘              		 (context) "a substance or device that is able to contain an electric field without conducting (= passing along) electricity" （语境）一种物质或器件，能够容纳电场而不导电（即不让电流通过） Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "dielectric" noun
              Education

              Power electronics

              电力工程与能源              		 (context) "the power from something such as electricity or oil that can do work, such as providing light and heat" （语境）来自电或石油等事物的能量，可以做功，例如提供光和热 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "energy" noun (POWER)

              科学：总论              		 "(knowledge from) the careful study of the structure and behaviour of the physical world, especially by watching, measuring, and doing experiments, and the development of theories to describe the results of these activities" （来自）对物质世界的结构和行为的仔细研究（尤其是通过观察、测量和实验）以及为描述这些活动的结果而发展理论（所得的知识） Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "science" noun

              Solid state circuits
              固态电路              		 (context) "used to refer to an electronic device in which the flow of electrical current is through solid material and not through a vacuum (= space without air)" （语境）指一种电子器件，其中电流通过固体材料流动，而不是通过真空（即没有空气的空间） Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "solid-state" adjective

              超导              		 "the property of allowing an electrical current to flow through at a very low temperature" 在极低温度下让电流通过的性质 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "superconductivity" noun

              Ultrasonics, ferroelectrics, and frequency control
