              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              3.1 Readable
              可读              		 "Make text content readable and understandable." 让文本内容可读、可理解。 World Wide Web Consortium, 2024, Guideline 3.1
              3.1.1 Language of Page
              页面语言（A 级）              		 "The default human language of each web page can be programmatically determined." 每个网页的默认人类语言可以由程序确定。 World Wide Web Consortium, 2024, Success Criterion 3.1.1
              3.1.2 Language of Parts
              局部语言（AA 级）              		 "The human language of each passage or phrase in the content can be programmatically determined except for proper names, technical terms, words of indeterminate language, and words or phrases that have become part of the vernacular of the immediately surrounding text." 内容中每个段落或短语的人类语言可以由程序确定，专有名词、技术术语、语言不确定的词，以及已成为周围文本所用语言一部分的词或短语除外。 World Wide Web Consortium, 2024, Success Criterion 3.1.2
              3.1.3 Unusual Words
              不常见的词（AAA 级）              		 "A mechanism is available for identifying specific definitions of words or phrases used in an unusual or restricted way, including idioms and jargon." 提供一种机制，用于识别以不常见或受限方式使用的词或短语的具体定义，包括习语和行话。 World Wide Web Consortium, 2024, Success Criterion 3.1.3
              3.1.4 Abbreviations
              缩写（AAA 级）              		 "A mechanism for identifying the expanded form or meaning of abbreviations is available." 提供一种识别缩写的展开形式或含义的机制。 World Wide Web Consortium, 2024, Success Criterion 3.1.4
              3.1.5 Reading Level
              阅读水平（AAA 级）              		 "When text requires reading ability more advanced than the lower secondary education level after removal of proper names and titles, supplemental content, or a version that does not require reading ability more advanced than the lower secondary education level, is available." 当文本在去掉专有名词和标题后仍要求高于初中教育水平的阅读能力时，提供补充内容或一个不要求高于初中教育水平阅读能力的版本。 World Wide Web Consortium, 2024, Success Criterion 3.1.5
              3.1.6 Pronunciation
              发音（AAA 级）              		 "A mechanism is available for identifying specific pronunciation of words where meaning of the words, in context, is ambiguous without knowing the pronunciation." 提供一种机制，用于在不知道发音就无法确定词义的上下文中识别词的具体发音。 World Wide Web Consortium, 2024, Success Criterion 3.1.6

              References

              World Wide Web Consortium. (2024, December 12). Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 (W3C Recommendation). https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/
