              2.5 Onboarding

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              Onboarding
              引导（Onboarding）              		 "Onboarding experiences guide people through how to get started with a product or feature." 引导体验带人了解如何开始使用某个产品或功能。 Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding"
              Relevant
              相关              		 (context) "Make sure onboarding content is presented within context of a closely related task." （语境）确保引导内容出现在情境之中，即与一项密切相关的任务放在一起。 Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Principles
              Non-distracting
              不干扰              		 "Avoid being a barrier to the user’s primary goal." 避免成为障碍，挡住用户的主要目标。 Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Principles
              Optional
              可选              		 "Allow people to exit and finish what they’re doing, but give them a way to come back to it later." 允许人们退出并完成手头的事，但给他们以后回来的途径。 Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Principles
              Benefit-focused
              突出好处              		 (context) "Let them know up front how they’ll benefit, so they’re motivated to pay attention." （语境）一开始就告诉他们能得到什么好处，让他们有动力去留意。 Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Principles
              Coherent
              一致              		 "Use standard Fluent components so that users know what to expect when interacting with your onboarding experience." 使用标准的 Fluent 组件，让用户在与引导体验互动时知道会发生什么。 Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Principles
              Welcome
              欢迎              		 "Welcome experiences reassure people they’re stating in the right place." 欢迎体验让人安心，确认自己来对了地方。 Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Scenarios and goals
              Orient
              定向              		 (context) "After welcoming the user to a new experience, it’s important to show them helpful features in context. To show where certain options appear, try empty states or teaching popovers." （语境）欢迎用户进入新体验之后，重要的是在情境中向他们展示有用的功能。要指出某些选项在哪里，可以用空状态或教学浮层。 Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Scenarios and goals
              Notify
              通知              		 (context) "When functionality in an app or experiences is added or changed, notify users about what they can now gain and how they might adapt their behaviors to the new UX." （语境）当应用或体验里的功能新增或改变时，告诉用户他们现在能得到什么，以及该如何调整做法来适应新的体验。 Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Scenarios and goals
              Explain
              说明              		 (context) "To tell people what they can do or provide details that might not be readily apparent, try putting hints in the UI that can tell people how they might move forward." （语境）要告诉人们能做什么，或提供不太显眼的细节，可以在界面里放提示，告诉人们下一步怎么走。 Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Scenarios and goals
              Take action
              采取行动              		 (context) "Sometimes, before they dive in, users need to complete some preliminary actions needed to use the app or feature." （语境）有时用户在深入之前，需要先完成一些准备操作，才能使用应用或功能。 Microsoft, n.d., "Onboarding", Scenarios and goals

              References
