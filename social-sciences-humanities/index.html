              Social Sciences and Humanities
              UNESCO 叙词表              		 (context) "the study of society and the way people live; the subjects connected with this, for example history, economics, etc." （语境）对社会和人们生活方式的研究；也指与此相关的学科，例如历史、经济学等 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "social science" noun
              ELSST
              ELSST（欧洲语言社会科学叙词表）              		 "The European Language Social Science Thesaurus (ELSST) is a broad-based, multilingual thesaurus for the social sciences." 欧洲语言社会科学叙词表（ELSST）是一套面向社会科学的覆盖面广的多语种叙词表。 Consortium of European Social Science Data Archives, n.d., para. 1
              UNESCO Thesaurus
              UNESCO 叙词表              		 "The UNESCO Thesaurus is a controlled and structured list of concepts used in subject analysis and retrieval of documents and publications in the fields of education, culture, natural sciences, social and human sciences, communication and information." UNESCO 叙词表是一份受控的、结构化的概念列表，用于教育、文化、自然科学、社会与人文科学、传播与信息等领域文献和出版物的主题分析与检索。 UNESCO, n.d., para. 1
