              Requirements and Prohibitions
              要求与禁止事项              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） Justice Laws Website, n.d., An Act to promote the efficiency and adaptability of the Canadian economy, Requirements and Prohibitions
              6 Unsolicited electronic messages
              未经请求的电子信息              		 "It is prohibited to send or cause or permit to be sent to an electronic address a commercial electronic message unless (a) the person to whom the message is sent has consented to receiving it, whether the consent is express or implied; and (b) the message complies with subsection (2)." 禁止发送或致使、允许他人发送至电子地址的商业电子信息，除非（a）收件人已同意接收，无论是明示还是默示同意；并且（b）该信息符合第（2）款。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., An Act to promote the efficiency and adaptability of the Canadian economy, s. 6(1)
              7 Altering transmission data
              更改传输数据              		 "It is prohibited, in the course of a commercial activity, to alter or cause to be altered the transmission data in an electronic message so that the message is delivered to a destination other than or in addition to that specified by the sender" 禁止在商业活动过程中更改或致使他人更改电子信息的传输数据，使该信息被投递到发件人指定之外或额外的目的地 Justice Laws Website, n.d., An Act to promote the efficiency and adaptability of the Canadian economy, s. 7(1)
              8 Installation of computer program
              安装计算机程序              		 "A person must not, in the course of a commercial activity, install or cause to be installed a computer program on any other person’s computer system" 任何人不得在商业活动过程中，安装或致使他人安装计算机程序到他人的计算机系统上 Justice Laws Website, n.d., An Act to promote the efficiency and adaptability of the Canadian economy, s. 8(1)
              9 Contravention of sections 6 to 8
              违反第 6 至 8 条              		 "It is prohibited to aid, induce, procure or cause to be procured the doing of any act contrary to any of sections 6 to 8." 禁止帮助、诱使、促成或致使他人促成任何违反第 6 至 8 条的行为。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., An Act to promote the efficiency and adaptability of the Canadian economy, s. 9
              10 Express consent — sections 6 to 8
              明示同意（第 6 至 8 条）              		 "A person who seeks express consent for the doing of an act described in any of sections 6 to 8 must, when requesting consent, set out clearly and simply the following information: (a) the purpose or purposes for which the consent is being sought; (b) prescribed information that identifies the person seeking consent and, if the person is seeking consent on behalf of another person, prescribed information that identifies that other person; and (c) any other prescribed information." 为第 6 至 8 条所述行为寻求明示同意的人，在请求同意时必须清楚、简明地列出以下信息：（a）寻求同意的一项或多项目的；（b）用于识别寻求同意者的规定信息，如代表他人寻求同意，还须有用于识别该他人的规定信息；（c）其他规定信息。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., An Act to promote the efficiency and adaptability of the Canadian economy, s. 10(1)
              11 Unsubscribe mechanism — section 6
              退订机制（第 6 条）              		 "The unsubscribe mechanism referred to in paragraph 6(2)(c) must (a) enable the person to whom the commercial electronic message is sent to indicate, at no cost to them, the wish to no longer receive any commercial electronic messages, or any specified class of such messages, from the person who sent the message or the person — if different — on whose behalf the message is sent, using (i) the same electronic means by which the message was sent, or (ii) if using those means is not practicable, any other electronic means that will enable the person to indicate the wish; and (b) specify an electronic address, or link to a page on the World Wide Web that can be accessed through a web browser, to which the indication may be sent." 第 6（2）（c）款所述的退订机制必须：（a）使商业电子信息的收件人能够免费表示不再接收发件人或（如不同）被代表者发送的任何商业电子信息或其中某一类信息，所用方式为（i）发送该信息时所用的同一电子手段，或（ii）该手段不可行时，能让收件人表示此意愿的其他电子手段；（b）指明可接收该意愿的电子地址或可通过浏览器访问的万维网页面链接。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., An Act to promote the efficiency and adaptability of the Canadian economy, s. 11(1)
              12 Contravention of section 6
              违反第 6 条              		 "A person contravenes section 6 only if a computer system located in Canada is used to send or access the electronic message." 违反第 6 条，仅限于使用位于加拿大的计算机系统发送或访问电子信息的情形。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., An Act to promote the efficiency and adaptability of the Canadian economy, s. 12(1)
              13 Burden of proof
              举证责任              		 "A person who alleges that they have consent to do an act that would otherwise be prohibited under any of sections 6 to 8 has the onus of proving it." 声称已获同意的人（其行为若无同意即受第 6 至 8 条禁止）负有举证责任。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., An Act to promote the efficiency and adaptability of the Canadian economy, s. 13
