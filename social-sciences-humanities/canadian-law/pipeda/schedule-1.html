              SCHEDULE 1 Principles Set Out in the National Standard of Canada Entitled Model Code for the Protection of Personal Information
              附表 1 加拿大国家标准《个人信息保护示范准则》所列原则              		 (context) "Subject to sections 6 to 9, every organization shall comply with the obligations set out in Schedule 1." （语境）在不违反第 6 至 9 条的前提下，每个组织都应履行其附表 1 规定的义务。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, s. 5(1)
              4.1 Principle 1 — Accountability
              原则 1：责任              		 "An organization is responsible for personal information under its control and shall designate an individual or individuals who are accountable for the organization’s compliance with the following principles." 组织对其控制下的个人信息负责，并应指定一名或多名个人，负责组织对以下原则的遵守。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.1
              4.2 Principle 2 — Identifying Purposes
              原则 2：明确目的              		 "The purposes for which personal information is collected shall be identified by the organization at or before the time the information is collected." 收集个人信息的目的应由组织在收集信息时或之前确定。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.2
              4.3 Principle 3 - Consent
              原则 3：同意              		 "The knowledge and consent of the individual are required for the collection, use, or disclosure of personal information, except where inappropriate." 个人的知情和同意是收集、使用或披露个人信息的必要条件，不适宜的情况除外。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.3
              4.4 Principle 4 — Limiting Collection
              原则 4：限制收集              		 "The collection of personal information shall be limited to that which is necessary for the purposes identified by the organization. Information shall be collected by fair and lawful means." 个人信息的收集应限于必要范围，即组织所确定目的之所需。信息应通过公平、合法的方式收集。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.4
              4.5 Principle 5 —Limiting Use, Disclosure, and Retention
              原则 5：限制使用、披露和保留              		 "Personal information shall not be used or disclosed for purposes other than those for which it was collected, except with the consent of the individual or as required by law. Personal information shall be retained only as long as necessary for the fulfilment of those purposes." 个人信息不得用于或披露给收集目的以外的用途，经个人同意或法律要求的除外。个人信息只应在必要期限内保留，以实现上述目的。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.5
              4.6 Principle 6 — Accuracy
              原则 6：准确              		 "Personal information shall be as accurate, complete, and up-to-date as is necessary for the purposes for which it is to be used." 个人信息应准确、完整并保持最新，达到其使用目的所必需的程度。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.6
              4.7 Principle 7 — Safeguards
              原则 7：保障措施              		 "Personal information shall be protected by security safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information." 个人信息应受到安全保障措施的保护，措施应与信息的敏感程度相称。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.7
              4.8 Principle 8 — Openness
              原则 8：公开              		 "An organization shall make readily available to individuals specific information about its policies and practices relating to the management of personal information." 组织应让个人能够方便地获得关于其个人信息管理政策和做法的具体信息。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.8
              4.9 Principle 9 — Individual Access
              原则 9：个人查阅              		 "Upon request, an individual shall be informed of the existence, use, and disclosure of his or her personal information and shall be given access to that information. An individual shall be able to challenge the accuracy and completeness of the information and have it amended as appropriate." 经请求，个人应被告知其个人信息的存在、使用和披露情况，并应获准查阅这些信息。个人应能够对信息的准确性和完整性提出质疑，并酌情要求修改。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.9
              4.10 Principle 10 — Challenging Compliance
              原则 10：对合规提出质疑              		 "An individual shall be able to address a challenge concerning compliance with the above principles to the designated individual or individuals accountable for the organization’s compliance." 个人应能够就上述原则的遵守情况提出质疑，并向组织指定的负责人提出。 Justice Laws Website, n.d., Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Schedule 1, clause 4.10
