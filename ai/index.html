              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              AI
              人工智能              		 "A machine-based system that can, for a given set of human-defined objectives, make predictions, recommendations, or decisions influencing real or virtual environments." 一种基于机器的系统，能够针对一组人类定义的目标，做出影响真实或虚拟环境的预测、建议或决策。 National Institute of Standards and Technology, n.d.-b, "artificial intelligence" entry, definition 2
              CS2023 AI
              CS2023 AI              		 "Artificial intelligence (AI) studies problems that are difficult or impractical to solve with traditional algorithmic approaches." 人工智能（AI）研究一类问题，这些问题难以或不便解决，若只用传统算法方法。 Joint Task Force on Computing Curricula, 2024, AI "Preamble" section, para. 1
              Machine Learning
              机器学习              		 (context) "Explain how machine learning works as an optimization/search process." （语境）解释机器学习如何作为一个优化/搜索过程来运作。 Joint Task Force on Computing Curricula, 2024, AI-ML "Illustrative Learning Outcomes", 6
              Deep Learning
              深度学习              		 (context) "In particular, there has been a shift from engineered representations to representations learned automatically through optimization over large datasets." （语境）特别是，已经出现了一种转变：从人工设计的表示转向通过在大型数据集上进行优化而自动学习到的表示。 Joint Task Force on Computing Curricula, 2024, AI "Preamble" section, para. 2
              Reinforcement Learning
              强化学习              		 (context) "Describe the differences among the three main styles of learning (supervised, reinforcement, and unsupervised) and determine which is appropriate to a particular problem domain." （语境）描述三种主要学习方式（监督学习、强化学习和无监督学习）之间的区别，并判断哪一种适合特定的问题领域。 Joint Task Force on Computing Curricula, 2024, AI-ML "Illustrative Learning Outcomes", 1
              Computer Vision
              计算机视觉              		 (context) "Summarize the importance of image and object recognition in AI and indicate several significant applications of this technology." （语境）概述图像和物体识别在 AI 中的重要性，并指出这项技术的几个重要应用。 Joint Task Force on Computing Curricula, 2024, AI-Vision "Illustrative Learning Outcomes", 1
              Natural Language Processing
              自然语言处理              		 (context) "Define and contrast deterministic and stochastic grammars, providing examples to show the adequacy of each." （语境）定义并对比确定性文法和随机文法，并举例说明各自的适用性。 Joint Task Force on Computing Curricula, 2024, AI-NLP "Illustrative Learning Outcomes", 1
              Agents and Cognitive Systems
              智能体与认知系统              		 (context) "Characterize and contrast the standard agent architectures." （语境）刻画并对比标准的智能体架构。 Joint Task Force on Computing Curricula, 2024, AI-Agents "Illustrative Learning Outcomes", 1
              AI Platforms
              AI 平台              		 (context) "The platform provides streamlined management through unified role-based access control (RBAC), networking, and policies under one Azure resource provider namespace." （语境）该平台提供简化的管理，通过统一的基于角色的访问控制（RBAC）、网络和策略，位于一个 Azure 资源提供程序命名空间之下。 Microsoft, n.d., "What is Microsoft Foundry?", para. 1
              Model Evaluation
              模型评测              		 (context) "We present Holistic Evaluation of Language Models (HELM) to improve the transparency of language models." （语境）我们提出语言模型整体评估（HELM），以提高语言模型的透明度。 Liang et al., 2023, Abstract
              Robotics
              机器人              		 (context) "List capabilities and limitations of today's state-of-the-art robot systems, including their sensors and the crucial sensor processing that informs those systems." （语境）列出当今最先进机器人系统的能力和局限，包括它们的传感器以及为这些系统提供信息的关键传感器处理。 Joint Task Force on Computing Curricula, 2024, AI-Robotics "Illustrative Learning Outcomes", 1
              Applications and Societal Impact
              应用与社会影响              		 (context) "Given a real-world application domain and problem, formulate an AI solution to it, identifying proper data/input, preprocessing, representations, AI techniques, and evaluation metrics/methodology." （语境）给定一个现实应用领域和问题，为它构建 AI 解决方案，确定合适的数据/输入、预处理、表示、AI 技术以及评估指标/方法。 Joint Task Force on Computing Curricula, 2024, AI-SEP "Illustrative Learning Outcomes", 1
