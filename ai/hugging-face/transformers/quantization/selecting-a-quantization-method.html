              6.2 Selecting a Quantization Method

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              Selecting a Quantization Method
              选择量化方法              		 (context) "This guide helps you choose the most common and production-ready quantization techniques depending on your use case, and presents the advantages and disadvantages of each technique." （语境）本指南帮助你根据用例选择最常见、可用于生产的量化技术，并介绍每种技术的优点与缺点。 Hugging Face, n.d., "Selecting a quantization method", para. 1
              Quantization Picker
              量化方法选择器              		 (context) "For a comprehensive overview of all supported methods and their features, refer to the quantization picker or the full compatibility table below it." （语境）要全面了解所有受支持的方法及其特性，请参考量化方法选择器或其下方的完整兼容性表。 Hugging Face, n.d., "Selecting a quantization method", para. 2
              On-the-Fly Quantization
              即时量化              		 (context) "These methods are generally easier to use as they don’t need a separate calibration dataset or step." （语境）这些方法通常更易使用，因为它们不需要单独的校准数据集或校准步骤。 Hugging Face, n.d., "No Calibration Required (On-the-fly Quantization)", para. 1
              bitsandbytes
              bitsandbytes              		 (context) "ease of use and QLoRA fine-tuning on NVIDIA and Intel GPUs" （语境）易用，并可在 NVIDIA 与 Intel GPU 上做 QLoRA 微调。 Hugging Face, n.d., "Inference", table, bitsandbytes
              HQQ (Half-Quadratic Quantization)
              HQQ（半二次量化）              		 (context) "fast on the fly quantization without calibration" （语境）快速的即时量化，无需校准。 Hugging Face, n.d., "Inference", table, HQQ
              SINQ
              SINQ              		 (context) "super-fast but high-quality on the fly quantization without calibration" （语境）超快且高质量的即时量化，无需校准。 Hugging Face, n.d., "Inference", table, SINQ
              torchao
              torchao              		 (context) "flexibility and fast inference with torch.compile" （语境）灵活，并能借助 torch.compile 快速推理。 Hugging Face, n.d., "Inference", table, torchao
              Calibration-Based Quantization
              基于校准的量化              		 (context) "These methods require an upfront calibration step using a dataset to potentially achieve higher accuracy." （语境）这些方法需要用数据集做一个预先的校准步骤，以期达到更高的准确度。 Hugging Face, n.d., "Calibration-based Quantization", para. 1
              GPTQ
              GPTQ              		 (context) "good 4-bit accuracy with upfront calibration" （语境）4 位下准确度好，需预先校准。 Hugging Face, n.d., "Inference", table, GPTQModel or AWQ
              GPTQ Calibration Time
              GPTQ 校准时间              		 (context) "Calibration for 8B model takes ~20 minutes on one A100 gpu." （语境）8B 模型的校准在一块 A100 GPU 上约需 20 分钟。 Hugging Face, n.d., "GPTQ/GPTQModel", para. 1
              AWQ (Activation-Aware Weight Quantization)
              AWQ（激活感知权重量化）              		 (context) "Often achieves high accuracy at 4-bit. (Sometimes surpasses GPTQ on specific tasks.)" （语境）常常能在 4 位下达到高准确度。（在特定任务上有时超过 GPTQ。） Hugging Face, n.d., "AWQ (Activation-aware Weight Quantization)", table, Pros
              AWQ Calibration Time
              AWQ 校准时间              		 (context) "Calibration for 8B model takes ~10 minutes on one A100 gpu." （语境）8B 模型的校准在一块 A100 GPU 上约需 10 分钟。 Hugging Face, n.d., "AWQ (Activation-aware Weight Quantization)", para. 1
              compressed-tensors
              compressed-tensors              		 (context) "loading specific quantized formats (FP8)" （语境）加载特定的量化格式（FP8）。 Hugging Face, n.d., "Inference", table, compressed-tensors
              QLoRA
              QLoRA              		 (context) "The standard method for QLoRA fine-tuning via PEFT." （语境）标准方法，用于通过 PEFT 进行 QLoRA 微调。 Hugging Face, n.d., "Fine-tuning", "bitsandbytes", Description
              Research Quantization Methods
              研究性量化方法              		 (context) "Methods like AQLM, SpQR, VPTQ, HIGGS, etc., push the boundaries of compression (< 2-bit) or explore novel techniques." （语境）AQLM、SpQR、VPTQ、HIGGS 等方法推进压缩的极限（低于 2 位），或探索新技术。 Hugging Face, n.d., "Research", para. 1
              8-bit Quantization
              8 位量化              		 (context) "Very close to baseline bf16 model" （语境）非常接近于基线 bf16 模型。 Hugging Face, n.d., "Benchmark Comparison", key takeaways table, 8-bit
              4-bit Quantization
              4 位量化              		 (context) "AWQ/GPTQ often lead in accuracy but need calibration. HQQ/bnb-nf4/SINQ are easy on-the-fly." （语境）AWQ/GPTQ 准确度常常领先，但需要校准。HQQ/bnb-nf4/SINQ 即时量化很方便。 Hugging Face, n.d., "Benchmark Comparison", key takeaways table, 4-bit
              Sub-4-bit Quantization
              低于 4 位的量化              		 (context) "Noticeable drop, especially at 2-bit" （语境）明显下降，尤其是在 2 位时。 Hugging Face, n.d., "Benchmark Comparison", key takeaways table, Sub-4-bit
              Task-Specific Benchmarking
              针对任务的基准测试              		 (context) "Always benchmark the performance (accuracy and speed) of the quantized model on your specific task and hardware to ensure it meets your requirements." （语境）务必对量化模型的性能（准确度与速度）做基准测试，在你的具体任务和硬件上，确认它满足你的要求。 Hugging Face, n.d., "Benchmark Comparison", note
