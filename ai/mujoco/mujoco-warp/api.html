              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              9.5 API
              API              		 (No definition) （无定义） Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API"
              Model
              Model 模型              		 "Model definition and parameters." 模型的定义和参数。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              Data
              Data 数据              		 "Dynamic state that updates each step." 每一步都会更新的动态状态。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              BiasType
              BiasType 偏置类型              		 "Type of actuator bias." 执行器偏置的类型。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              BroadphaseFilter
              BroadphaseFilter 粗测过滤              		 "Bitmask specifying which collision functions to run during broadphase." 指定粗测阶段要运行哪些碰撞函数的位掩码。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              BroadphaseType
              BroadphaseType 粗测类型              		 "Type of broadphase algorithm." 粗测算法的类型。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              Callback
              Callback 回调              		 "Callbacks for custom physics behavior." 用于自定义物理行为的回调。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              ConeType
              ConeType 摩擦锥类型              		 "Type of friction cone." 摩擦锥的类型。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              Constraint
              Constraint 约束              		 "Constraint data." 约束数据。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              Contact
              Contact 接触              		 "Contact data." 接触数据。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              CtrlChart
              CtrlChart 控制坐标卡              		 "Orientation chart type for SO(3) actuators." SO(3) 执行器的朝向坐标卡类型。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              CtrlInput
              CtrlInput 控制输入              		 "Control input signature bitflags for actuators." 执行器的控制输入签名位标志。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              DisableBit
              DisableBit 禁用位              		 "Disable default feature bitflags." 禁用默认功能的位标志。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              DynType
              DynType 动力学类型              		 "Type of actuator dynamics." 执行器动力学的类型。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              EnableBit
              EnableBit 启用位              		 "Enable optional feature bitflags." 启用可选功能的位标志。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              GainType
              GainType 增益类型              		 "Type of actuator gain." 执行器增益的类型。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              GeomType
              GeomType 几何类型              		 "Type of geometry." 几何体的类型。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              IntegratorType
              IntegratorType 积分器类型              		 "Integrator mode." 积分器模式。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              JointType
              JointType 关节类型              		 "Type of degree of freedom." 自由度的类型。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              ObjType
              ObjType 对象类型              		 "Type of object." 对象的类型。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              Option
              Option 选项              		 "Physics options." 物理选项。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              OverflowType
              OverflowType 溢出类型              		 "Bitmask for physics and collision overflows." 物理与碰撞溢出的位掩码。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              RenderContext
              RenderContext 渲染上下文              		 "Context for rendering." 用于渲染的上下文。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              SolverType
              SolverType 求解器类型              		 "Constraint solver algorithm." 约束求解器算法。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              State
              State 状态              		 "State component elements as integer bitflags." 以整数位标志表示的状态分量元素。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1
              TrnType
              TrnType 传动类型              		 "Type of actuator transmission." 执行器传动的类型。 Google DeepMind, n.d., "MuJoCo Warp API", para. 1

              References

              Google DeepMind. (n.d.). MuJoCo Warp API. MuJoCo Documentation (Version 3.17.0). Retrieved October 31, 2026, from https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/stable/mjwarp/api.html
