              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              3.1 Atari Wrappers
              Atari 包装器              		 (context) "Atari 2600 preprocessings" （语境）Atari 2600 预处理 Raffin et al., n.d., "AtariWrapper", para. 1
              AtariWrapper
              AtariWrapper Atari 包装器              		 "Atari 2600 preprocessings" Atari 2600 预处理 Raffin et al., n.d., "Atari Wrappers", para. 1
              ClipRewardEnv
              ClipRewardEnv 奖励裁剪包装器              		 "Clip the reward to {+1, 0, -1} by its sign." 按符号把奖励裁剪为 {+1, 0, -1}。 Raffin et al., n.d., "Atari Wrappers", para. 1
              ClipRewardEnv.reward
              reward 方法              		 "Bin reward to {+1, 0, -1} by its sign." 按符号把奖励归入 {+1, 0, -1}。 Raffin et al., n.d., "Atari Wrappers", para. 1
              EpisodicLifeEnv
              EpisodicLifeEnv 生命即回合包装器              		 "Make end-of-life == end-of-episode, but only reset on true game over." 让生命结束等同于回合结束，但只在真正游戏结束时重置。 Raffin et al., n.d., "Atari Wrappers", para. 1
              EpisodicLifeEnv.reset
              reset 方法              		 "Calls the Gym environment reset, only when lives are exhausted." 仅在生命耗尽时调用 Gym 环境的 reset。 Raffin et al., n.d., "Atari Wrappers", para. 1
              EpisodicLifeEnv.step
              step 方法              		 "Uses the step() of the env that can be overwritten to change the returned data." 使用 env 的 step()，可以重写它来改变返回的数据。 Raffin et al., n.d., "Atari Wrappers", para. 1
              FireResetEnv
              FireResetEnv 开火重置包装器              		 "Take action on reset for environments that are fixed until firing." 对开火前保持静止的环境，在重置时执行动作。 Raffin et al., n.d., "Atari Wrappers", para. 1
              FireResetEnv.reset
              reset 方法              		 "Uses the reset() of the env that can be overwritten to change the returned data." 使用 env 的 reset()，可以重写它来改变返回的数据。 Raffin et al., n.d., "Atari Wrappers", para. 1
              MaxAndSkipEnv
              MaxAndSkipEnv 跳帧取最大包装器              		 "Return only every skip-th frame (frameskipping) and return the max between the two last frames." 只返回每第 skip 帧（跳帧），并返回最后两帧之间的最大值。 Raffin et al., n.d., "Atari Wrappers", para. 1
              MaxAndSkipEnv.step
              step 方法              		 "Step the environment with the given action Repeat action, sum reward, and max over last observations." 用给定的动作让环境前进一步 重复动作、累加奖励，并对最后几个观测取最大值。 Raffin et al., n.d., "Atari Wrappers", para. 1
              NoopResetEnv
              NoopResetEnv 空操作重置包装器              		 "Sample initial states by taking random number of no-ops on reset." 在重置时执行随机次数的空操作来采样初始状态。 Raffin et al., n.d., "Atari Wrappers", para. 1
              StickyActionEnv
              StickyActionEnv 粘滞动作包装器              		 "Sticky action." 粘滞动作。 Raffin et al., n.d., "Atari Wrappers", para. 1
              WarpFrame
              WarpFrame 帧变换包装器              		 "Convert to grayscale and warp frames to 84x84 (default) as done in the Nature paper and later work." 转换为灰度并把帧变换为 84x84（默认），与 Nature 论文及后续工作的做法相同。 Raffin et al., n.d., "Atari Wrappers", para. 1
              WarpFrame.observation
              observation 方法              		 "returns the current observation from a frame" 从一帧返回当前的观测 Raffin et al., n.d., "Atari Wrappers", para. 1

              References

              Raffin, A., Hill, A., Gleave, A., Kanervisto, A., Ernestus, M., & Dormann, N.. (n.d.). Atari Wrappers. Stable-Baselines3 docs (Version 2.9.2a0). Retrieved October 31, 2026, from https://stable-baselines3.readthedocs.io/en/master/common/atari_wrappers.html
