              3 Common
              通用模块              		 (No definition) （无定义） Raffin et al., n.d.-j, "Common", documentation menu
              3.1 Atari Wrappers
              Atari 包装器              		 (context) "Atari 2600 preprocessings" （语境）Atari 2600 预处理 Raffin et al., n.d.-b, "AtariWrapper", para. 1
              3.2 Environments Utils
              环境工具函数              		 (No definition) （无定义） Raffin et al., n.d.-d, "Environments Utils"
              3.3 Custom Environments
              自定义环境              		 "Those environments were created for testing purposes." 这些环境是为测试而创建的。 Raffin et al., n.d.-c, "Custom Environments", para. 1
              3.4 Probability Distributions
              概率分布              		 "Probability distributions used for the different action spaces:" 用于不同动作空间的概率分布： Raffin et al., n.d.-i, "Probability Distributions", para. 1
              3.5 Evaluation Helper
              评估辅助函数              		 (context) "Runs the policy for n_eval_episodes episodes and outputs the average return per episode (sum of undiscounted rewards)." （语境）将策略运行 n_eval_episodes 个回合，并输出每回合的平均回报（未折扣奖励之和）。 Raffin et al., n.d.-e, "evaluate_policy", para. 1
              3.6 Gym Environment Checker
              Gym 环境检查器              		 "Check that an environment follows Gym API." 检查一个环境是否遵循 Gym API。 Raffin et al., n.d.-f, "check_env", para. 1
              3.7 Monitor Wrapper
              监视器包装器              		 "A monitor wrapper for Gym environments, it is used to know the episode reward, length, time and other data." 一个用于 Gym 环境的监视器包装器，用来了解回合奖励、长度、时间和其他数据。 Raffin et al., n.d.-h, "Monitor", para. 1
              3.8 Logger
              日志记录器              		 (No definition) （无定义） Raffin et al., n.d.-g, "Logger"
              3.9 Action Noise
              动作噪声              		 (context) "The action noise base class" （语境）动作噪声的基类 Raffin et al., n.d.-a, "ActionNoise", para. 1
              3.10 Utils
              工具函数              		 (No definition) （无定义） Raffin et al., n.d.-k, "Utils"
