              2.5 HER
              HER（事后经验回放）              		 "HER is an algorithm that works with off-policy methods (DQN, SAC, TD3 and DDPG for example)." HER 是一种与离策略方法配合使用的算法（例如 DQN、SAC、TD3 和 DDPG）。 Raffin et al., n.d., "HER", para. 2
              HER Replay Buffer
              HER 回放缓冲区              		 (No definition) （无定义） Raffin et al., n.d., "HER Replay Buffer"
              HerReplayBuffer
              HerReplayBuffer 后见经验回放缓冲区              		 "Hindsight Experience Replay (HER) buffer." 后见经验回放（HER）缓冲区。 Raffin et al., n.d., "HER Replay Buffer", para. 1
              HerReplayBuffer.add
              add 方法              		 "Add elements to the buffer." 向缓冲区添加元素。 Raffin et al., n.d., "HER Replay Buffer", para. 1
              HerReplayBuffer.extend
              extend 方法              		 "Add a new batch of transitions to the buffer" 向缓冲区添加一批新的转移 Raffin et al., n.d., "HER Replay Buffer", para. 1
              HerReplayBuffer.reset
              reset 方法              		 "Reset the buffer." 重置缓冲区。 Raffin et al., n.d., "HER Replay Buffer", para. 1
              HerReplayBuffer.sample
              sample 方法              		 "Sample elements from the replay buffer." 从回放缓冲区中采样元素。 Raffin et al., n.d., "HER Replay Buffer", para. 1
              HerReplayBuffer.set_env
              set_env 方法              		 "Sets the environment." 设置环境。 Raffin et al., n.d., "HER Replay Buffer", para. 1
              HerReplayBuffer.size
              size 方法              		 (No definition) （无定义） Raffin et al., n.d., "HER Replay Buffer"
              HerReplayBuffer.swap_and_flatten
              swap_and_flatten 方法              		 "Swap and then flatten axes 0 (buffer_size) and 1 (n_envs) to convert shape from [n_steps, n_envs, …] (when … is the shape of the features) to [n_steps * n_envs, …] (which maintain the order)" 交换然后展平第 0 轴（buffer_size）和第 1 轴（n_envs），把形状从 [n_steps, n_envs, …]（… 为特征的形状）转换为 [n_steps * n_envs, …]（保持顺序） Raffin et al., n.d., "HER Replay Buffer", para. 1
              HerReplayBuffer.to_torch
              to_torch 方法              		 "Convert a numpy array to a PyTorch tensor." 把 numpy 数组转换为 PyTorch 张量。 Raffin et al., n.d., "HER Replay Buffer", para. 1
              HerReplayBuffer.truncate_last_trajectory
              truncate_last_trajectory 方法              		 "If called, we assume that the last trajectory in the replay buffer was finished (and truncate it)." 如果调用它，我们就认为回放缓冲区中的最后一条轨迹已经结束（并将其截断）。 Raffin et al., n.d., "HER Replay Buffer", para. 1
              Goal Selection Strategies
              目标选择策略              		 (No definition) （无定义） Raffin et al., n.d., "Goal Selection Strategies"
              GoalSelectionStrategy
              GoalSelectionStrategy 目标选择策略              		 "The strategies for selecting new goals when creating artificial transitions." 创建人工转移时选择新目标的策略。 Raffin et al., n.d., "Goal Selection Strategies", para. 1

              References

              Raffin, A., Hill, A., Gleave, A., Kanervisto, A., Ernestus, M., & Dormann, N.. (n.d.). HER. Stable-Baselines3 docs (Version 2.9.2a0). Retrieved October 31, 2026, from https://stable-baselines3.readthedocs.io/en/master/modules/her.html
