              1. Modes

              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              1 Modes
              模式              		 (context) "Ultralytics YOLO26 is not just another object detection model; it's a versatile framework designed to cover the entire lifecycle of machine learning models—from data ingestion and model training to validation, deployment, and real-world tracking." （语境）Ultralytics YOLO26 不只是又一个目标检测模型；它是一个多用途框架，旨在覆盖机器学习模型的整个生命周期：从数据摄取、模型训练，到验证、部署和真实场景中的跟踪。 Ultralytics, n.d.-a, "Introduction", para. 1
              1.1 Train
              训练              		 "Training a deep learning model involves feeding it data and adjusting its parameters so that it can make accurate predictions." 训练深度学习模型就是给它输入数据并调整其参数，使它能做出准确的预测。 Ultralytics, n.d.-e, "Model Training with Ultralytics YOLO", para. 1
              1.2 Val
              验证              		 "Validation is a critical step in the machine learning pipeline, allowing you to assess the quality of your trained models." 验证是机器学习流水线中的关键一步，让你评估训练好的模型的质量。 Ultralytics, n.d.-f, "Model Validation with Ultralytics YOLO", para. 1
              1.3 Predict
              预测              		 (context) "In the world of machine learning and computer vision, the process of making sense of visual data is often called inference or prediction." （语境）在机器学习和计算机视觉领域，理解视觉数据的过程通常称为推理或预测。 Ultralytics, n.d.-d, "Model Prediction with Ultralytics YOLO", para. 1
              1.4 Export
              导出              		 "Export mode in Ultralytics YOLO26 offers a versatile range of options for exporting your trained model to different formats, making it deployable across various platforms and devices." Ultralytics YOLO26 的导出模式提供多种选项，把训练好的模型导出为不同格式，使其能部署到各种平台和设备上。 Ultralytics, n.d.-c, "Model Export with Ultralytics YOLO", para. 1
              1.5 Track
              跟踪              		 "Object tracking in the realm of video analytics is a critical task that not only identifies the location and class of objects within the frame but also maintains a unique ID for each detected object as the video progresses." 视频分析中的目标跟踪是一项关键任务，它不仅识别画面中物体的位置和类别，还随着视频推进为每个检测到的物体保持唯一的 ID。 Ultralytics, n.d.-g, "Multi-Object Tracking with Ultralytics YOLO", para. 1
              1.6 Benchmark
              基准测试              		 "Benchmark mode in Ultralytics YOLO26 serves this purpose by providing a robust framework for assessing the speed and accuracy of your model across a range of export formats." Ultralytics YOLO26 的基准测试模式提供一个稳健的框架，用于评估模型在一系列导出格式下的速度和精度。 Ultralytics, n.d.-b, "Model Benchmarking with Ultralytics YOLO", para. 1
