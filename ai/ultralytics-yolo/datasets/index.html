              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              4 Datasets
              数据集              		 (context) "Ultralytics provides support for various datasets to facilitate computer vision tasks such as detection, instance segmentation, semantic segmentation, depth estimation, classification, pose estimation, and oriented bounding boxes (OBB)." （语境）Ultralytics 支持多种数据集，以便开展计算机视觉任务，如检测、实例分割、语义分割、深度估计、分类、姿态估计和旋转边界框（OBB）。 Ultralytics, n.d.-a, "Datasets Overview", para. 1
              4.1 Detection
              检测数据集              		 (context) "This guide introduces various formats of datasets that are compatible with the Ultralytics YOLO model and provides insights into their structure, usage, and how to convert between different formats." （语境）本指南介绍与 Ultralytics YOLO 模型兼容的各种数据集格式，并说明它们的结构、用法以及如何在不同格式之间转换。 Ultralytics, n.d.-f, "Object Detection Datasets Overview", para. 1
              4.2 Segmentation
              实例分割数据集              		 (context) "Instance segmentation is a computer vision task that involves identifying and delineating individual objects within an image." （语境）实例分割是一项识别并勾勒图像中各个物体的计算机视觉任务。 Ultralytics, n.d.-d, "Instance Segmentation Datasets Overview", para. 1
              4.3 Semantic Segmentation
              语义分割数据集              		 (context) "Semantic segmentation assigns one class label to every pixel in an image." （语境）语义分割给图像中的每个像素分配一个类别标签。 Ultralytics, n.d.-i, "Semantic Segmentation Datasets Overview", para. 1
              4.4 Depth Estimation
              深度估计数据集              		 (context) "Monocular depth estimation assigns a depth value in meters to every pixel in an image." （语境）单目深度估计给图像中的每个像素分配一个以米为单位的深度值。 Ultralytics, n.d.-b, "Depth Estimation Datasets Overview", para. 1
              4.5 Pose
              姿态数据集              		 (No definition) （无定义） Ultralytics, n.d.-h, "Pose Estimation Datasets Overview"
              4.6 Classification
              分类数据集              		 (No definition) （无定义） Ultralytics, n.d.-c, "Image Classification Datasets Overview"
              4.7 Oriented Bounding Boxes (OBB)
              旋转边界框数据集              		 (context) "This guide explains the various OBB dataset formats compatible with Ultralytics YOLO models, offering insights into their structure, application, and methods for format conversions." （语境）本指南说明与 Ultralytics YOLO 模型兼容的各种 OBB 数据集格式，介绍它们的结构、用途和格式转换方法。 Ultralytics, n.d.-g, "Oriented Bounding Box (OBB) Datasets Overview", para. 1
              4.8 Multi-Object Tracking
              多目标跟踪数据集              		 "Multi-object tracking is a critical component in video analytics that identifies objects and maintains unique IDs for each detected object across video frames." 多目标跟踪是视频分析中的一个关键组成部分，它识别物体，并在各视频帧中为每个检测到的物体保持唯一的 ID。 Ultralytics, n.d.-e, "Multi-object Tracking Datasets Overview", para. 1
              4.9 Explorer
              Explorer              		 (context) "As of ultralytics>=8.3.12, Ultralytics Explorer has been removed." （语境）从 ultralytics>=8.3.12 起，Ultralytics Explorer 已被移除。 Ultralytics, n.d.-j, "Ultralytics Explorer", para. 1
