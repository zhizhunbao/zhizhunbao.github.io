              3.8 YOLOv6
              YOLOv6              		 "Meituan YOLOv6, released in 2022, offers a strong balance between speed and accuracy, making it a popular choice for real-time applications." 美团 YOLOv6 于 2022 年发布，在速度和精度之间取得了很好的平衡，因此常用于实时应用。 Ultralytics, n.d., "Meituan YOLOv6", para. 1
              Key Features
              主要特性              		 (No definition) （无定义） Ultralytics, n.d., "Key Features"
              Bidirectional Concatenation (BiC) Module
              双向拼接（BiC）模块              		 "Bidirectional Concatenation (BiC) Module: YOLOv6 introduces a BiC module in the neck of the detector, enhancing localization signals and delivering performance gains with negligible speed degradation." 双向拼接（BiC）模块：YOLOv6 在检测器的颈部引入 BiC 模块，以此增强定位信号，并以几乎可以忽略的速度损失带来性能提升。 Ultralytics, n.d., "Key Features", item 1
              Anchor-Aided Training (AAT) Strategy
              锚框辅助训练（AAT）策略              		 "Anchor-Aided Training (AAT) Strategy: This model proposes AAT to enjoy the benefits of both anchor-based and anchor-free paradigms without compromising inference efficiency." 锚框辅助训练（AAT）策略：该模型提出 AAT，以便在不影响推理效率的前提下兼得基于锚框和无锚框两种范式的好处。 Ultralytics, n.d., "Key Features", item 2
              Enhanced Backbone and Neck Design
              增强的骨干与颈部设计              		 "Enhanced Backbone and Neck Design: By deepening YOLOv6 to include another stage in the backbone and neck, this model achieved strong performance on the COCO dataset at high-resolution input when released." 增强的骨干与颈部设计：通过加深 YOLOv6，在骨干网络和颈部各增加一个阶段，该模型发布时在高分辨率输入下于 COCO 数据集上取得了强劲的性能。 Ultralytics, n.d., "Key Features", item 3
              Self-Distillation Strategy
              自蒸馏策略              		 "Self-Distillation Strategy: A new self-distillation strategy is implemented to boost the performance of smaller models of YOLOv6, enhancing the auxiliary regression branch during training and removing it at inference to avoid a marked speed decline." 自蒸馏策略：实现了一种新的自蒸馏策略，它用来提升 YOLOv6 较小模型的性能，训练时增强辅助回归分支，推理时去掉它以避免明显的速度下降。 Ultralytics, n.d., "Key Features", item 4
