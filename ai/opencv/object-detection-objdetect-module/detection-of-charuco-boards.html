              7.3 Detection of ChArUco Boards
              ChArUco 标定板检测              		 (context) "A ChArUco board tries to combine the benefits of these two approaches:" （语境）ChArUco 标定板试图结合这两种方法的优点： OpenCV team, n.d., "Detection of ChArUco Boards", para. 3
              ChArUco Board Creation
              ChArUco 标定板生成              		 (No definition) （无定义） OpenCV team, n.d., "ChArUco Board Creation"
              CharucoBoard
              CharucoBoard 类              		 "The aruco module provides the cv::aruco::CharucoBoard class that represents a Charuco Board and which inherits from the cv::aruco::Board class." aruco 模块提供 cv::aruco::CharucoBoard 类，它表示一个 ChArUco 标定板，并继承自 cv::aruco::Board 类。 OpenCV team, n.d., "ChArUco Board Creation", para. 1
              CharucoBoard::generateImage()
              generateImage 方法              		 (context) "The function cv::aruco::CharucoBoard::generateImage() is provided in cv::aruco::CharucoBoard class" （语境）函数 cv::aruco::CharucoBoard::generateImage() 由 cv::aruco::CharucoBoard 类提供 OpenCV team, n.d., "ChArUco Board Creation", para. 1
              ChArUco Board Detection
              ChArUco 标定板检测              		 "When you detect a ChArUco board, what you are actually detecting is each of the chessboard corners of the board." 检测 ChArUco 标定板时，实际检测的是标定板上的每个棋盘角点。 OpenCV team, n.d., "ChArUco Board Detection", para. 1
              Taking input Image
              获取输入图像              		 "The original image where the markers are to be detected." 原始图像，要在其中检测标记。 OpenCV team, n.d., "ChArUco Board Detection", item 1
              Reading the camera calibration Parameters(only for detection with camera calibration)
              读取相机标定参数（仅用于带相机标定的检测）              		 (context) "For detection of charuco corners without calibration, this step is not required." （语境）对于不带标定的 ChArUco 角点检测，这一步不是必需的。 OpenCV team, n.d., "ChArUco Board Detection", item 2
              Detecting the markers and interpolation of charuco corners from markers
              检测标记并由标记插值出 ChArUco 角点              		 "So that, first markers are detected, and then ChArUco corners are interpolated from markers." 因此，先检测标记，再由标记插值出 ChArUco 角点。 OpenCV team, n.d., "ChArUco Board Detection", item 3
              CharucoDetector::detectBoard()
              detectBoard 方法              		 "The method that detect the ChArUco corners is cv::aruco::CharucoDetector::detectBoard()." 检测 ChArUco 角点的方法是 cv::aruco::CharucoDetector::detectBoard()。 OpenCV team, n.d., "ChArUco Board Detection", item 3
              ChArUco Pose Estimation
              ChArUco 位姿估计              		 (context) "The final goal of the ChArUco boards is finding corners very accurately for a high precision calibration or pose estimation." （语境）ChArUco 标定板的最终目标是非常精确地找到角点，用于高精度的标定或位姿估计。 OpenCV team, n.d., "ChArUco Pose Estimation", para. 1
              CharucoBoard::matchImagePoints()
              matchImagePoints 方法              		 (context) "To perform pose estimation for charuco boards, you should use cv::aruco::CharucoBoard::matchImagePoints() and cv::solvePnP():" （语境）要对 ChArUco 标定板做位姿估计，应使用 cv::aruco::CharucoBoard::matchImagePoints() 和 cv::solvePnP()： OpenCV team, n.d., "ChArUco Pose Estimation", para. 3
