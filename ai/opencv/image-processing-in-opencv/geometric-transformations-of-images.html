              2.2 Geometric Transformations of Images
              图像的几何变换              		 (context) "Learn to apply different geometric transformations to images like rotation, translation etc." （语境）学习对图像施加各种几何变换，如旋转、平移等。 OpenCV team, n.d.-b, "Image Processing in OpenCV", item 2
              Transformations
              变换              		 (context) "OpenCV provides two transformation functions, cv.warpAffine and cv.warpPerspective, with which you can perform all kinds of transformations." （语境）OpenCV 提供两个变换函数 cv.warpAffine 和 cv.warpPerspective，用它们可以完成各种变换。 OpenCV team, n.d.-a, "Transformations", para. 1
              Scaling
              缩放              		 "Scaling is just resizing of the image." 缩放就是改变图像的大小。 OpenCV team, n.d.-a, "Scaling", para. 1
              Translation
              平移              		 "Translation is the shifting of an object's location." 平移是物体位置的移动。 OpenCV team, n.d.-a, "Translation", para. 1
              Rotation
              旋转              		 (context) "Rotation of an image for an angle θ is achieved by the transformation matrix of the form" （语境）把图像旋转角度 θ 是通过如下形式的变换矩阵实现的 OpenCV team, n.d.-a, "Rotation", para. 1
              Affine Transformation
              仿射变换              		 "In affine transformation, all parallel lines in the original image will still be parallel in the output image." 在仿射变换中，原图像中所有平行的直线在输出图像中仍然平行。 OpenCV team, n.d.-a, "Affine Transformation", para. 1
              Perspective Transformation
              透视变换              		 "For perspective transformation, you need a 3x3 transformation matrix. Straight lines will remain straight even after the transformation." 透视变换需要一个 3×3 的变换矩阵。变换之后直线仍然保持为直线。 OpenCV team, n.d.-a, "Perspective Transformation", para. 1
