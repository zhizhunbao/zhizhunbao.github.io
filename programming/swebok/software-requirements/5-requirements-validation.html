              5. Requirements Validation

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              Requirements Validation
              需求确认              		 "Requirements validation concerns gaining confidence that the requirements represent the stakeholders’ true needs as they are currently understood (and possibly documented)." 需求确认关注建立信心，确信这些需求代表了按目前理解（并可能已记录）的干系人的真实需要。 Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 5, para. 1, p. 1-15
              Requirements Reviews
              需求评审              		 "The most common way to validate is by reviewing or inspecting a requirements document. One or more reviewers are asked to look for errors, omissions, invalid assumptions, lack of clarity and deviation from accepted practice." 最常见的确认方式是评审或检查需求文档。请一位或多位评审者查找错误、遗漏、无效假设、表述不清和偏离公认做法之处。 Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 5.1, para. 1, p. 1-15
              Definition of Done
              完成的定义              		 "Providing checklists, quality criteria or a “definition of done” to the reviewers can guide them to focus on specific aspects of the requirements specification." 给评审者提供检查单、质量标准或「完成的定义」，可以引导他们关注需求规格说明的特定方面。 Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 5.1, para. 2, p. 1-16
              Simulation and Execution
              仿真与执行              		 "Some specifications can be subjected to simulation or actual execution in place of or in addition to human review." 有些规格说明可以通过仿真或实际执行来代替人工评审，或作为人工评审的补充。 Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 5.2, para. 1, p. 1-16
              Prototyping
              原型法              		 "If the requirements specification is not in a form that allows direct simulation or execution, an alternative is to have a software engineer build a prototype that concretely demonstrates some important dimension of an implementation." 如果需求规格说明的形式不允许直接仿真或执行，另一个办法是让软件工程师构建一个原型，具体地展示实现的某个重要维度。 Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 5.3, para. 1, p. 1-16
