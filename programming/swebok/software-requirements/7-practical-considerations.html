              7. Practical Considerations

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              Practical Considerations
              实践考量              		 (No definition) （无定义） Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7, p. 1-17
              Iterative Nature of the Requirements Process
              需求过程的迭代性              		 "The tension created by simultaneous breadth-wise and depth-wise requirements in real-world projects often prompts teams to perform requirements activities iteratively." 现实项目中需求同时要求广度和深度，由此产生的张力常促使团队迭代地开展需求活动。 Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.1, para. 1, p. 1-17
              Requirements Prioritization
              需求优先级排序              		 "Prioritizing requirements is useful throughout a software project because it helps focus software engineers on delivering the most valuable functionality soonest." 给需求排优先级在整个软件项目中都有用，因为它帮助软件工程师聚焦于尽快交付最有价值的功能。 Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.2, para. 1, p. 1-17
              Kano Model
              Kano 模型              		 "The Kano model, which underlies [6, c17], shows that considering only value, desirability or satisfaction can lead to erroneous priorities." Kano 模型（[6, c17] 以它为基础）表明，只考虑价值、吸引力或满意度会导致错误的优先级。 Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.2, para. 3, p. 1-18
              Objective Function
              目标函数              		 "The formula \(\text{Priority} = \frac{\text{Value} * (1-\text{Risk})}{\text{Cost}}\) is just one example of an objective function to do so." 公式 \(\text{Priority} = \frac{\text{Value} * (1-\text{Risk})}{\text{Cost}}\) 只是一个例子，说明可以用怎样的目标函数来做这件事。 Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.2, para. 6, p. 1-18
              Requirements Tracing
              需求追踪              		 "Requirements tracing can serve two potentially useful purposes. One is to serve as an accounting exercise that documents consistency between pairs of related project work products." 需求追踪可以起到两种可能有用的作用。其一是作为一种核账工作，记录成对的相关项目工作产品之间的一致性。 Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.3, para. 1, p. 1-18
              Requirements Stability and Volatility
              需求的稳定性与易变性              		 "It is useful to assess the likelihood that a requirement will change in a given time. Identifying potentially volatile requirements helps the software engineer establish a design more tolerant of change, (e.g., [20])." 评估一项需求在给定时间内变化的可能性是有用的。识别出可能易变的需求，有助于软件工程师建立更能容忍变化的设计（例如 [20]）。 Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.4, para. 1, p. 1-19
              Measuring Requirements
              需求度量              		 "As a practical matter, it may be useful to have some concept of the volume of the requirements for a particular software product." 从实际出发，对需求量有个概念可能是有用的，针对的是某个特定的软件产品。 Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.5, para. 1, p. 1-19
              Functional Size Measurement (FSM)
              功能规模度量（FSM）              		 "Functional size measurement (FSM) is a technique for evaluating the size of a body of functional requirements." 功能规模度量（FSM）是一种评估一组功能需求规模的技术。 Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.5, para. 1, p. 1-19
              Story Points
              故事点              		 "Story points can also be considered a measure of requirements size." 故事点也可以看作一种需求规模的度量。 Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.5, para. 1, p. 1-19
              Requirements Process Quality and Improvement
              需求过程质量与改进              		 "This topic concerns assessing the quality and improvement of the requirements process." 本主题关注评估质量与改进，对象是需求过程。 Washizaki, 2026, Chapter 1, section 7.6, para. 1, p. 1-19
