              CA Sections
              CA 类目（《化学文摘》分类）              		 "You will find that CAS information is divided into 80 different sections. Each CA section covers only one broad area of scientific inquiry." 你会发现，CAS 信息被划分为 80 个不同的类目。每个 CA 类目只涵盖一个宽泛的科学研究领域。 Chemical Abstracts Service, n.d., 1 and list item 1
              BIOCHEMISTRY (BIO/SC)
              生物化学              		 "the scientific study of the chemistry of living things" 对生物化学过程的科学研究 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "biochemistry" noun
              ORGANIC (ORG/SC)
              有机化学              		 "the scientific study of chemical substances that contain carbon, including artificial substances such as plastics" 对含碳化学物质的科学研究，包括塑料等人造物质 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "organic chemistry" noun
              MACROMOLECULAR (MAC/SC)
              高分子化学              		 (context) "a large molecule, for example a protein" （语境）一种大分子，例如蛋白质 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "macromolecule" noun
              APPLIED (APP/SC)
              应用化学              		 (context) "a science, or science in general, that has a practical use" （语境）一门科学或泛指科学，它具有实际用途 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "applied science" noun
              PHYSICAL, INORGANIC, AND ANALYTICAL (PIA/SC)
              物理、无机与分析化学              		 (context) "the scientific study of chemical substances that do not contain carbon" （语境）对不含碳的化学物质的科学研究 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "inorganic chemistry" noun
