              23. DevOps

              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              DevOps
              DevOps              		 "DevOps combines development (Dev) and operations (Ops) to unite people, process, and technology in application planning, development, delivery, and operations." DevOps 把开发（Dev）和运维（Ops）结合起来，在应用的规划、开发、交付和运维中把人员、流程和技术统一起来。 Microsoft, 2023, para. 1
              Plan
              规划              		 "In the planning phase, DevOps teams ideate, define, and describe the features and capabilities of the applications and systems they plan to build." 在规划阶段，DevOps 团队构思、定义并描述他们打算构建的应用和系统的功能和能力。 Microsoft, 2023, "Planning" section, para. 1
              Develop
              开发              		 "The development phase includes all aspects of developing software code." 开发阶段包括开发软件代码的所有方面。 Microsoft, 2023, "Development" section, para. 1
              Deliver
              交付              		 "Delivery is the process of consistently and reliably deploying applications into production environments, ideally via continuous delivery (CD)." 交付是持续、可靠地把应用部署到生产环境的过程，理想情况下通过持续交付（CD）完成。 Microsoft, 2023, "Deliver" section, para. 1
              Operate
              运维              		 "The operations phase involves maintaining, monitoring, and troubleshooting applications in production environments, including hybrid or public clouds like Azure." 运维阶段包括在生产环境（包括混合云或 Azure 这样的公有云）中维护、监视应用并排除故障。 Microsoft, 2023, "Operations" section, para. 1
