              1.2 Service Models

              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文解释 Source
              Service models
              服务模型              		 "Service models describe how cloud resources are delivered to users. They determine how much control an organization has over infrastructure, platforms, or applications." 服务模型描述云资源如何交付给用户。它们决定组织对基础结构、平台或应用程序拥有多少控制权。 Microsoft, n.d.-e, "Types of cloud service models" section, para. 1
              Infrastructure as a service (IaaS)
              基础结构即服务 (IaaS)              		 "Infrastructure as a service (IaaS) is the most flexible category of cloud services. It provides the maximum amount of control for your cloud resources." 基础结构即服务 (IaaS) 是云服务中最灵活的类别。它为你的云资源提供最大程度的控制。 Microsoft, n.d.-a, "Describe Infrastructure as a Service" unit, para. 1
              Platform as a service (PaaS)
              平台即服务 (PaaS)              		 "Platform as a service (PaaS) is a middle ground between renting space in a datacenter (infrastructure as a service) and paying for a complete and deployed solution (software as a service)." 平台即服务 (PaaS) 是一个中间地带，介于租用数据中心中的空间（基础结构即服务）和购买完整且已部署的解决方案（软件即服务）之间。 Microsoft, n.d.-b, "Describe Platform as a Service" unit, para. 1
              Software as a service (SaaS)
              软件即服务 (SaaS)              		 "Software as a service (SaaS) is the most complete cloud service model from a product perspective. With SaaS, you're essentially renting or using a fully developed application." 从产品角度看，软件即服务 (SaaS) 是最完整的云服务模型。使用 SaaS 时，你实际上是在租用或使用一个完全开发好的应用程序。 Microsoft, n.d.-c, "Describe Software as a Service" unit, para. 1
              Shared responsibility model
              共同责任模型              		 "With the shared responsibility model, these responsibilities get shared between the cloud provider and the consumer." 在共同责任模型下，这些责任由云提供商和使用者共同承担。 Microsoft, n.d.-d, "How responsibilities shift in cloud" section, para. 2
