              26.1 Strategy

              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文解释 Source
              Strategy
              战略              		 "A cloud adoption strategy directs how your business adopts cloud technology." 云采用战略指导你的企业如何采用云技术。 Microsoft, 2026, para. 1
              Motivations, mission and objectives
              动机、使命和目标              		 "Define motivations, mission, and objectives. Establish the business reasons for adopting cloud and the outcomes by which success is measured." 定义动机、使命和目标。确立采用云的业务原因，以及衡量成功的结果。 Microsoft, 2026, "How to build a cloud adoption strategy" section, item 1
              Motivations
              动机              		 (context) "Motivations for adopting and expanding your cloud footprint can vary." （语境）采用和扩展云规模的动机各不相同。 Microsoft, 2025, "Define your motivations" section, para. 1
              Strategy team
              战略团队              		 "Define your strategy team. Identify the leaders and stakeholders accountable for setting direction and ensuring execution across the organization." 定义战略团队。确定负责设定方向的领导者和利益相关者，并确保在整个组织中执行。 Microsoft, 2026, "How to build a cloud adoption strategy" section, item 2
              Prepare your organization
              准备组织              		 "Prepare the organization. Secure executive sponsorship, align organizational strategies, evaluate your operating model's fit for cloud, and decide whether to shift toward a product-oriented model." 准备组织。争取高管支持，协调组织战略，评估运营模式是否适合云，并决定是否转向面向产品的模式。 Microsoft, 2026, "How to build a cloud adoption strategy" section, item 3
              Inform your strategy
              为战略提供依据              		 "Inform your strategy. Apply strategic considerations across the dimensions that shape cloud outcomes: cost efficiency, resiliency, security, and sustainability." 为战略提供依据。在影响云成果的各个维度上运用战略考量：成本效率、复原能力、安全性和可持续性。 Microsoft, 2026, "How to build a cloud adoption strategy" section, item 4

              References

              • Microsoft. (2025, February 3). Determine your motivations, mission, and objectives. Cloud Adoption Framework. Microsoft Learn. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/strategy/motivations
              • Microsoft. (2026, May 14). Develop a cloud adoption strategy. Cloud Adoption Framework. Microsoft Learn. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/strategy/
