              8.2 Storage Redundancy

              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              Locally redundant storage (LRS)
              本地冗余存储（LRS）              		 "Locally redundant storage (LRS) replicates the data within your storage accounts to a single physical datacenter located in the primary region of your choice." 本地冗余存储 (LRS) 把存储帐户中的数据复制到单个物理数据中心，该数据中心位于你选择的主要区域。 Microsoft, 2026, "Redundancy in the primary region" section, list item 1
              Zone-redundant storage (ZRS)
              区域冗余存储（ZRS）              		 "Zone-redundant storage (ZRS) copies your data synchronously across three or more Azure availability zones in the primary region." 区域冗余存储 (ZRS) 把数据同步复制到三个或更多 Azure 可用区，这些可用区位于主要区域。 Microsoft, 2026, "Redundancy in the primary region" section, list item 2
              Geo-redundant storage (GRS)
              异地冗余存储（GRS）              		 "Geo-redundant storage (GRS) copies your data synchronously within one or more Azure availability zones in the primary region by using LRS. It then copies your data asynchronously to the secondary region." 异地冗余存储 (GRS) 在主要区域的一个或多个 Azure 可用区内使用 LRS 同步复制数据，然后把数据异步复制到次要区域。 Microsoft, 2026, "Redundancy in a secondary region" section, list item 1
              Geo-zone-redundant storage (GZRS)
              异地区域冗余存储（GZRS）              		 "Geo-zone-redundant storage (GZRS) copies your data synchronously across three or more Azure availability zones in the primary region by using ZRS. It then copies your data asynchronously to the secondary region." 异地区域冗余存储 (GZRS) 在主要区域的三个或更多 Azure 可用区之间使用 ZRS 同步复制数据，然后把数据异步复制到次要区域。 Microsoft, 2026, "Redundancy in a secondary region" section, list item 2
              Read-access GRS (RA-GRS)
              读取访问异地冗余存储（RA-GRS）              		 "Read-access geo-redundant storage (RA-GRS) or read-access geo-zone-redundant storage (RA-GZRS) configurations permit read access to the secondary region." 读取访问异地冗余存储 (RA-GRS) 或读取访问异地区域冗余存储（RA-GZRS）配置允许对次要区域进行读取访问。 Microsoft, 2026, "Read access to data in the secondary region" section, para. 2
              Read-access GZRS (RA-GZRS)
              读取访问异地区域冗余存储（RA-GZRS）              		 "Read-access geo-redundant storage (RA-GRS) or read-access geo-zone-redundant storage (RA-GZRS) configurations permit read access to the secondary region." 读取访问异地冗余存储（RA-GRS）或读取访问异地区域冗余存储 (RA-GZRS) 配置允许对次要区域进行读取访问。 Microsoft, 2026, "Read access to data in the secondary region" section, para. 2
