              3. Reliability

              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              Reliability
              可靠性              		 "Reliability is the ability of a workload to keep meeting business expectations, even during failures or disruptions." 可靠性是工作负载持续满足业务期望的能力，即使在发生故障或中断时也是如此。 Microsoft, 2026a, para. 1
              Azure regions
              Azure 区域              		 "Regions: Azure operates in over 70 regions worldwide. This geographic distribution enables workloads to withstand region-wide disruptions and outages through planned failover capabilities, while following your data residency requirements." 区域：Azure 在全球 70 多个区域运营。这种地理分布使工作负载能够通过计划内的故障转移能力承受区域范围的中断和停运，同时遵循你的数据驻留要求。 Microsoft, 2026a, "Resilient foundations in Azure" section, item 2
              Azure availability zones
              Azure 可用区              		 "Availability zones: Many Azure regions include physically separate datacenters within the same region. These availability zones are connected by high-speed, low-latency networks. Each zone has independent power, cooling, and networking to protect against datacenter-level failures while maintaining synchronous replication capabilities for many Azure services." 可用区：许多 Azure 区域包含同一区域内物理上分开的数据中心。这些可用区由高速、低延迟的网络连接。每个可用区都有独立的电源、冷却和网络，以防范数据中心级别的故障，同时为许多 Azure 服务保持同步复制能力。 Microsoft, 2026a, "Resilient foundations in Azure" section, item 3
              Reliability concepts
              可靠性概念              		 (context) "Azure provides platform capabilities that translate reliability concepts into actionable building blocks for your architecture." （语境）Azure 提供平台能力，把可靠性概念转化为你的架构中可落地的构建基块。 Microsoft, 2026a, "Azure services that support your reliability" section, para. 1
