              3.2 Azure Availability Zones

              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文解释 Source
              Availability zone
              可用区              		 "An availability zone is a logical grouping of one or more physically separate datacenters within a region." 可用区是区域内一个或多个物理上独立的数据中心的逻辑分组。 Microsoft, 2026, "Datacenters and availability zones" section, para. 1
              Zone-redundant resources
              可用区冗余资源              		 "Zone-redundant resources are replicated or distributed across multiple availability zones by the service." 可用区冗余资源由服务复制或分布到多个可用区。 Microsoft, 2026, "Types of availability zone support" section, item 1
              Zonal resources
              单可用区资源              		 "A zonal resource is deployed to a single availability zone that you select yourself." 单可用区资源部署到你自己选择的单个可用区。 Microsoft, 2026, "Types of availability zone support" section, item 2
              Zone-resilient
              可用区复原              		 "your resource is considered to be zone-resilient: that is, it's resilient to the outage of a single availability zone." 资源被视为具有可用区复原能力：也就是说，它能承受单个可用区的中断。 Microsoft, 2026, "Types of availability zone support" section, para. 2
              Nonzonal deployment
              非可用区部署              		 "If a resource isn't configured to use availability zones, either due to the region you use not supporting zones or because of your configuration choices, it's called a nonzonal or regional deployment." 如果资源未配置为使用可用区（原因可能是所用区域不支持可用区，也可能是你的配置选择），就称为非可用区部署或区域部署。 Microsoft, 2026, "Types of availability zone support" section, para. 4
              Physical zone
              物理可用区              		 "Each datacenter is assigned to a physical zone." 每个数据中心都分配到一个物理可用区。 Microsoft, 2026, "Physical and logical availability zones" section, para. 1
              Logical zone
              逻辑可用区              		 "Physical zones are mapped to logical zones in your Azure subscription, and different subscriptions might have a different mapping order." 物理可用区映射到你的 Azure 订阅中的逻辑可用区，不同订阅的映射顺序可能不同。 Microsoft, 2026, "Physical and logical availability zones" section, para. 1

              References
