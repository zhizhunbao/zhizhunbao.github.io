              3.3 Reliability Concepts

              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文解释 Source
              Shared responsibility model for reliability
              可靠性共同责任模型              		 "The shared responsibility model for reliability has three levels:" 可靠性共同责任模型有三个层级： Microsoft, 2026b, "Shared responsibility model for reliability" section, para. 1
              Core platform reliability
              核心平台可靠性              		 "The Azure platform provides a base level of reliability for all customers and all services through the underlying infrastructure, services, and processes." Azure 平台通过底层基础结构、服务和流程，提供基本的可靠性，面向所有客户和所有服务。 Microsoft, 2026b, "Shared responsibility model for reliability" section, list item 1
              Reliability-enhancing capabilities
              可靠性增强功能              		 "Azure offers a suite of built-in features and services that enhance reliability, such as using availability zones, deploying across multiple regions, and implementing backup strategies. While Azure provides these capabilities, it's your responsibility to evaluate and configure them to align with your specific requirements." Azure 提供一套增强可靠性的内置功能和服务，例如使用可用区、跨多个区域部署以及实施备份策略。虽然 Azure 提供这些功能，但评估和配置它们是你的责任，以符合你的具体要求。 Microsoft, 2026b, "Shared responsibility model for reliability" section, list item 2
              Applications
              应用程序              		 "To make effective use of the other levels, you must design your application and workload for reliability." 要有效利用其他层级，你必须为可靠性设计你的应用程序和工作负载。 Microsoft, 2026b, "Shared responsibility model for reliability" section, list item 3
              High availability
              高可用性              		 "High availability is the state in which a specific workload can maintain its necessary level of uptime on a day-to-day basis, even during transient faults and intermittent failures." 高可用性是指特定工作负载能够日常保持所需正常运行时间水平的状态，即使在发生暂时性故障和间歇性失败时也是如此。 Microsoft, 2025a, "High availability" section, para. 1
              Fault tolerance
              容错              		 "Fault tolerance is the ability of a system to continue operating, in some defined capacity, in the event of a failure." 容错是系统在发生故障时仍能以某种既定能力继续运行的能力。 Microsoft, 2025a, "Fault tolerance" section, para. 1
              Scalability and elasticity
              可伸缩性和弹性              		 "Scalability and elasticity are the abilities of a system to handle increased load by adding and removing resources (scalability), and to do so quickly as your requirements change (elasticity)." 可伸缩性和弹性是系统的能力：通过添加和移除资源来处理增加的负载（可伸缩性），以及在需求变化时快速做到这一点（弹性）。 Microsoft, 2025a, "Scalability and elasticity" section, para. 1
              Chaos engineering
              混沌工程              		 "Testing your solution's ability to automatically tolerate or recover from a variety of fault types is called chaos engineering." 测试解决方案自动承受各种故障类型或从中恢复的能力，称为混沌工程。 Microsoft, 2025a, "Automated testing" section, para. 1
              Azure Chaos Studio
              Azure 混沌工作室              		 "Azure Chaos Studio is a chaos engineering tool that can simulate some common fault types." Azure 混沌工作室是一种混沌工程工具，可以模拟一些常见的故障类型。 Microsoft, 2025a, "Automated testing" section, para. 1
              Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
              恢复点目标              		 "Recovery Point Objective (RPO) is the maximum duration of acceptable data loss in the event of a disaster." 恢复点目标（RPO）是可接受的最长数据丢失时长，针对的是发生灾难的情况。 Microsoft, 2025a, "Disaster recovery requirements" section, list item 1
              Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
              恢复时间目标              		 "Recovery Time Objective (RTO) is the maximum duration of acceptable downtime in the event of a disaster, where 'downtime' is defined by your specification." 恢复时间目标（RTO）是可接受的最长停机时长，针对的是发生灾难的情况，其中“停机”由你的规范定义。 Microsoft, 2025a, "Disaster recovery requirements" section, list item 2
              Redundancy
              冗余              		 "Redundancy is the ability to maintain multiple identical copies of a service component, and to use those copies in a way that prevents any one component from becoming a single point of failure." 冗余是维护服务组件的多个相同副本的能力，并以某种方式使用这些副本，防止任何一个组件成为单点故障。 Microsoft, 2025b, para. 3
              Replication
              复制              		 "Replication or data redundancy is the ability to maintain multiple copies of data, called replicas." 复制（或数据冗余）是维护数据的多个副本的能力，这些副本称为副本（replica）。 Microsoft, 2025b, para. 4
              Backup
              备份              		 "Backup is the ability to maintain a timestamped copy of data that can be used to restore data that has been lost." 备份是维护带时间戳的数据副本的能力，可用来恢复已丢失的数据。 Microsoft, 2025b, para. 5
              Failover
              故障转移              		 "With failover, you can redirect traffic and requests from unhealthy instances to healthy ones." 通过故障转移，你可以重定向流量和请求，从运行不正常的实例转到正常的实例。 Microsoft, 2026c, para. 2
              Active-active
              主动-主动              		 "Some solutions are built to use an active-active approach, which means multiple instances all serve requests." 有些解决方案采用主动-主动方式，即多个实例都处理请求。 Microsoft, 2026c, "Active and passive instance roles" section, para. 1
              Planned failover
              计划内故障转移              		 "In contrast, planned failovers are those that you proactively trigger." 相比之下，计划内故障转移是你主动触发的故障转移。 Microsoft, 2026c, "Planned and unplanned failovers" section
              Unplanned failover
              计划外故障转移              		 "Unplanned failovers are those that are performed during an outage of a component, so that you can restore service by using another instance." 计划外故障转移是在某个组件中断期间执行的故障转移，以便使用另一个实例恢复服务。 Microsoft, 2026c, "Planned and unplanned failovers" section
              Failback
              故障回复              		 "Failback is the process of reinstating and redirecting traffic back to the original primary instance." 故障回复是恢复原始主实例并把流量重新导回它的过程。 Microsoft, 2026c, "Failback" section, para. 1
              Service-level agreement (SLA)
              服务级别协议 (SLA)              		 "An SLA is a set of conditional commitments. It states that if a customer meets certain requirements, then the service provider commits to a defined level of service." SLA 是一组有条件的承诺。它规定：如果客户满足某些要求，那么服务提供商承诺提供规定的服务水平。 Microsoft, 2026a, "What an SLA is and isn't" section, para. 1
              Service-level objective (SLO)
              服务级别目标 (SLO)              		 "A service-level objective (SLO) is an internal reliability target that you set for your own workload." 服务级别目标 (SLO) 是一个内部可靠性目标，由你为自己的工作负载设定。 Microsoft, 2026a, "How SLAs relate to SLOs and reliability targets" section, list item 1
              Service credits
              服务抵扣额              		 "Service credits are typically a percentage of the monthly service fee for the affected service." 服务抵扣额通常是月度服务费的一个百分比，按受影响的服务计算。 Microsoft, 2026a, "Step 4: File the claim within the required timeframe" section, para. 1
