              25.2 Security

              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文解释 Source
              Security
              安全性              		 "A secure workload is resilient to attacks and incorporates the interrelated security principles of confidentiality, integrity, and availability (also known as the CIA triad) in addition to meeting business goals." 安全的工作负载除了满足业务目标，还能抵御攻击，并纳入相互关联的机密性、完整性和可用性安全原则（也称为 CIA 三元组）。 Microsoft, 2023, para. 1
              Plan your security readiness
              规划安全就绪情况              		 "Strive to adopt and implement security practices in architectural design decisions and operations with minimal friction." 力求在架构设计决策和运营中采用并实施安全做法，同时把摩擦降到最低。 Microsoft, 2023, "Plan your security readiness" section, para. 1
              Design to protect confidentiality
              保护机密性的设计              		 "Prevent exposure to privacy, regulatory, application, and proprietary information through access restrictions and obfuscation techniques." 防止暴露隐私、法规、应用程序和专有信息，手段是访问限制和混淆技术。 Microsoft, 2023, "Design to protect confidentiality" section, para. 1
              Design to protect integrity
              保护完整性的设计              		 "Prevent corruption of design, implementation, operations, and data to avoid disruptions that can stop the system from delivering its intended utility or cause it to operate outside the prescribed limits. The system should provide information assurance throughout the workload lifecycle." 防止破坏设计、实现、运营和数据，以免中断让系统无法提供预期效用，或让它在规定的限制之外运行。系统应在整个工作负载生命周期中提供信息保障。 Microsoft, 2023, "Design to protect integrity" section, para. 1
              Design to protect availability
              保护可用性的设计              		 "Prevent or minimize system and workload downtime and degradation in the event of a security incident by using strong security controls. You must maintain data integrity during the incident and after the system recovers." 防止或尽量减少系统和工作负载的停机与降级，在发生安全事件时依靠强有力的安全控制做到这一点。在事件期间和系统恢复之后，都必须保持数据完整性。 Microsoft, 2023, "Design to protect availability" section, para. 1
              Sustain and evolve your security posture
              维持和改善安全状况              		 "Incorporate continuous improvement and apply vigilance to stay ahead of attackers who are continuously evolving their attack strategies." 引入持续改进并保持警惕，以领先于攻击者，他们的攻击策略在不断演变。 Microsoft, 2023, "Sustain and evolve your security posture" section, para. 1

              References

              • Microsoft. (2023, November 15). Security design principles. Microsoft Azure Well-Architected Framework. Microsoft Learn. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/security/principles
