              25.5 Performance Efficiency

              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文解释 Source
              Performance Efficiency
              性能效率              		 "Performance efficiency is the ability of a workload to adapt to changing demands by scaling up to meet increased load without impacting user experience, and scaling down to conserve resources during low demand." 性能效率是工作负载适应不断变化的需求的能力：向上扩展以满足增加的负载而不影响用户体验，在需求低时向下缩减以节省资源。 Microsoft, 2025, para. 2
              Negotiate realistic performance targets
              协商现实性能目标              		 "The intended user experience is defined, and there's a strategy to develop a benchmark and measure targets against the pre-established business requirements." 预期的用户体验已经定义，并且有策略来制定基准，并对照预先确定的业务要求衡量目标。 Microsoft, 2025, "Negotiate realistic performance targets" section, para. 1
              Design to meet capacity requirements
              设计以满足产能要求              		 "Provide enough supply to address anticipated demand." 提供足够的供给来应对预期的需求。 Microsoft, 2025, "Design to meet capacity requirements" section, para. 1
              Achieve and sustain performance
              实现和保持性能              		 "Protect against performance degradation while the system is in use and as it evolves." 在系统使用和演进的过程中防止性能下降。 Microsoft, 2025, "Achieve and sustain performance" section, para. 1
              Optimize for long-term improvement
              针对长期改进进行优化              		 "Improve system efficiency within the defined performance targets to increase workload value." 提高系统效率，并保持在既定的性能目标之内，以增加工作负载的价值。 Microsoft, 2025, "Optimize for long-term improvement" section, para. 1

              References

              • Microsoft. (2025, August 1). Performance Efficiency design principles. Microsoft Azure Well-Architected Framework. Microsoft Learn. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/performance-efficiency/principles
