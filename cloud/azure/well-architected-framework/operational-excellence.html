              25.4 Operational Excellence

              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文解释 Source
              Operational Excellence
              卓越运营              		 "At the core of the Operational Excellence pillar are DevOps practices that ensure workload quality through standardized workflows and team cohesion." 卓越运营支柱的核心是 DevOps 做法，它们通过标准化工作流和团队凝聚力确保工作负载质量。 Microsoft, 2025, para. 1
              Embrace DevOps culture
              拥抱 DevOps 文化              		 "Empower development and operations teams to continuously improve their system design and processes by working together with a mindset of collaboration, shared responsibility, and ownership." 让开发和运营团队持续改进其系统设计和流程，方法是带着协作、共担责任和主人翁的思维方式一起工作。 Microsoft, 2025, "Embrace DevOps culture" section, para. 1
              Establish development standards
              建立开发标准              		 "Optimize productivity by standardizing development practices, enforcing quality gates, and tracking progress and success through systematic change management." 优化生产力，方法是标准化开发做法、强制执行质量门禁，并通过系统化的变更管理跟踪进度和成效。 Microsoft, 2025, "Establish development standards" section, para. 1
              Evolve operations with observability
              通过可观测性改进操作              		 "Gain visibility into the system, derive insight, and make data-driven decisions." 获得对系统的可见性、得出见解，并做出数据驱动的决策。 Microsoft, 2025, "Evolve operations with observability" section, para. 1
              Automate for efficiency
              自动执行以提高效率              		 "Replace repetitive manual tasks with software automation that completes them quicker, with greater consistency and accuracy, and reduces risks." 用软件自动化取代重复的手动任务，让任务完成得更快、更一致、更准确，并降低风险。 Microsoft, 2025, "Automate for efficiency" section, para. 1
              Adopt safe deployment practices
              采用安全部署做法              		 "Achieve consistency in deployments by using guardrails that reduce the effect of errors and unexpected conditions." 实现部署的一致性，方法是使用防护措施来减少错误和意外情况的影响。 Microsoft, 2025, "Adopt safe deployment practices" section, para. 1

              References

              • Microsoft. (2025, May 6). Operational Excellence design principles. Microsoft Azure Well-Architected Framework. Microsoft Learn. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/operational-excellence/principles
