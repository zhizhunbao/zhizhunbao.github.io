              2.7 Kubernetes Self-Healing
              Kubernetes 自我修复              		 "Kubernetes is designed with self-healing capabilities that help maintain the health and availability of workloads." Kubernetes 设计有自我修复能力，帮助维持工作负载的健康和可用性。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Kubernetes Self-Healing", para. 1
              2.7.1 Self-Healing capabilities
              自我修复能力              		 (No definition) （无定义） The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Self-Healing capabilities"
              Container-level restarts
              容器级重启              		 "If a container inside a Pod fails, Kubernetes restarts it based on the restartPolicy." 如果 Pod 内的容器失败，Kubernetes 会重启它，依据是 restartPolicy。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Self-Healing capabilities", para. 1
              Replica replacement
              副本替换              		 "If a Pod in a Deployment or StatefulSet fails, Kubernetes creates a replacement Pod to maintain the specified number of replicas." 如果 Deployment 或 StatefulSet 中的 Pod 失败，Kubernetes 会创建一个替代 Pod 以维持指定的副本数。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Self-Healing capabilities", para. 2
              Persistent storage recovery
              持久存储恢复              		 "If a node is running a Pod with a PersistentVolume (PV) attached, and the node fails, Kubernetes can reattach the volume to a new Pod on a different node." 如果某节点上运行着挂接了 PersistentVolume（PV）的 Pod，而该节点失败，Kubernetes 可以把卷重新挂接到另一个节点上的新 Pod。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Self-Healing capabilities", para. 3

              References

              The Kubernetes Authors. (n.d.). Kubernetes Self-Healing. Kubernetes Documentation. Retrieved October 31, 2026, from https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/architecture/self-healing/
