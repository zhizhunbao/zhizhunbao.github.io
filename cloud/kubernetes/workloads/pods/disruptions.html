              4.1.7 Disruptions
              干扰              		 (context) "This guide is for application owners who want to build highly available applications, and thus need to understand what types of disruptions can happen to Pods." （语境）本指南面向想构建高可用应用的应用所有者，他们因此需要了解 Pod 可能遇到哪些类型的干扰。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Disruptions", para. 1
              4.1.7.1 Voluntary and involuntary disruptions
              自愿与非自愿干扰              		 (No definition) （无定义） The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Voluntary and involuntary disruptions"
              involuntary disruptions
              非自愿干扰              		 "We call these unavoidable cases involuntary disruptions to an application." 我们把这些无法避免的情况称为对应用的非自愿干扰。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Voluntary and involuntary disruptions", para. 2
              voluntary disruptions
              自愿干扰              		 (context) "We call other cases voluntary disruptions." （语境）我们把其他情况称为自愿干扰。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Voluntary and involuntary disruptions", para. 10
              4.1.7.2 Pod disruption budgets
              Pod 干扰预算              		 "Kubernetes offers features to help you run highly available applications even when you introduce frequent voluntary disruptions." Kubernetes 提供的功能帮助你运行高可用的应用，即使你引入频繁的自愿干扰。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Pod disruption budgets", para. 1
              PodDisruptionBudget (PDB)
              Pod 干扰预算（PDB）              		 "A PDB limits the number of Pods of a replicated application that are down simultaneously from voluntary disruptions." PDB 限制一个多副本应用中的 Pod 数量，即因自愿干扰而同时宕掉的那些。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Pod disruption budgets", para. 2

              References

              The Kubernetes Authors. (n.d.). Disruptions. Kubernetes Documentation. Retrieved October 31, 2026, from https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/workloads/pods/disruptions/
