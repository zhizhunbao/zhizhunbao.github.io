              6.8 Volume Snapshots
              卷快照              		 "In Kubernetes, a VolumeSnapshot represents a snapshot of a volume on a storage system." 在 Kubernetes 中，VolumeSnapshot（卷快照）代表存储系统上一个卷的快照。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Volume Snapshots", para. 1
              6.8.1 Introduction
              介绍              		 (No definition) （无定义） The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Introduction"
              VolumeSnapshotContent
              VolumeSnapshotContent              		 (context) "VolumeSnapshotContents are resources in the cluster." （语境）VolumeSnapshotContent 是集群中的资源。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Lifecycle of a volume snapshot and volume snapshot content", para. 1
              6.8.2 Lifecycle of a volume snapshot and volume snapshot content
              卷快照与卷快照内容的生命周期              		 "There are two ways snapshots may be provisioned: pre-provisioned or dynamically provisioned." 快照有两种供应方式，即预先供应或动态供应。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Provisioning Volume Snapshot", para. 1
              Pre-provisioned
              预先供应              		 (context) "A cluster administrator creates a number of VolumeSnapshotContents." （语境）集群管理员会创建若干 VolumeSnapshotContent。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Pre-provisioned", para. 1
              Dynamic
              动态              		 "Instead of using a pre-existing snapshot, you can request that a snapshot to be dynamically taken from a PersistentVolumeClaim." 你可以不使用已有的快照，而是请求从某个 PersistentVolumeClaim 动态拍摄快照。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Dynamic", para. 1
              Persistent Volume Claim as Snapshot Source Protection
              作为快照源的 PVC 保护              		 "The purpose of this protection is to ensure that in-use PersistentVolumeClaim API objects are not removed from the system while a snapshot is being taken from it" 这项保护的目的是确保使用中的 PersistentVolumeClaim API 对象不会被从系统中删除，当正在从它拍摄快照时 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Persistent Volume Claim as Snapshot Source Protection", para. 1
