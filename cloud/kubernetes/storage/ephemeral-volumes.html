              6.4 Ephemeral Volumes
              临时卷              		 (context) "Some applications need additional storage but don't care whether that data is stored persistently across restarts." （语境）有些应用需要额外的存储，但不在乎这些数据在重启后是否持久保存。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Ephemeral Volumes", para. 2
              6.4.0 Ephemeral Volumes
              临时卷              		 (No definition) （无定义） The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Ephemeral Volumes"
              Ephemeral volumes
              临时卷              		 (context) "Ephemeral volumes are designed for these use cases." （语境）临时卷就是为这些用例设计的。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Ephemeral Volumes", para. 4
              inline
              内联              		 "Ephemeral volumes are specified inline in the Pod spec, which simplifies application deployment and management." 临时卷在 Pod 规约中内联指定，从而简化应用的部署和管理。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Ephemeral Volumes", para. 5
              6.4.1 Types of ephemeral volumes
              临时卷的类型              		 "Kubernetes supports several different kinds of ephemeral volumes for different purposes:" Kubernetes 支持多种不同的临时卷，用于不同的目的： The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Types of ephemeral volumes", para. 1
              CSI ephemeral volumes
              CSI 临时卷              		 "CSI ephemeral volumes must be provided by third-party CSI storage drivers." CSI 临时卷必须由第三方 CSI 存储驱动提供。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Types of ephemeral volumes", para. 8
              Generic ephemeral volumes
              通用临时卷              		 "Generic ephemeral volumes are similar to emptyDir volumes in the sense that they provide a per-pod directory for scratch data that is usually empty after provisioning." 通用临时卷与 emptyDir 卷类似，都为每个 Pod 提供一个临时数据目录，供应后通常为空。 The Kubernetes Authors, n.d., "Generic ephemeral volumes", para. 1
