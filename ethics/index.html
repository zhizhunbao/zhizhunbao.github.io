              Ethics
              伦理              		 "Computing professionals' actions change the world. To act responsibly, they should reflect upon the wider impacts of their work, consistently supporting the public good." 计算专业人员的行为改变着世界。为了负责任地行事，他们应当反思其工作的更广泛影响，始终支持公共利益。 Association for Computing Machinery, 2018, "Preamble" section, para. 1
              CS2023 SEP
              CS2023 SEP              		 "It is more evident today than ever that students must also be exposed to the larger societal context of computing to develop an understanding of the critical and relevant social, ethical, legal, and professional issues and responsibilities at hand." 如今比以往任何时候都更明显：学生还必须接触计算的更大社会背景，从而理解当前关键且相关的社会、伦理、法律和职业问题与责任。 Joint Task Force on Computing Curricula, 2024, SEP "Preamble" section, para. 2
              ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct
              ACM 职业道德与职业行为准则              		 "The Code is designed to inspire and guide the ethical conduct of all computing professionals, including current and aspiring practitioners, instructors, students, influencers, and anyone who uses computing technology in an impactful way." 本准则旨在激励和指导道德行为，对象是所有计算专业人员，包括现任和有志的从业者、教师、学生、有影响力的人，以及任何以有影响的方式使用计算技术的人。 Association for Computing Machinery, 2018, "Preamble" section, para. 2
