              PhySH
              PhySH（物理学主题词表）              		 "PhySH (Physics Subject Headings) is a physics classification scheme developed by the American Physical Society (APS) to organize journal, meeting, and other content by topic." PhySH（物理学主题词表）是美国物理学会（APS）开发的一种物理学分类方案，用于按主题组织期刊、会议及其他内容。 American Physical Society, n.d., para. 1

              加速器与束流              		 (context) "in physics, a machine that makes extremely small pieces of matter travel at very high speeds, so that scientists can study the way they behave" （语境）在物理学中，指一种使极小的物质粒子以极高速度运动的机器，以便科学家研究它们的行为方式 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "particle accelerator" noun

              流体动力学              		 (context) "the science that studies the movement of gases and the way solid bodies, such as aircraft, move through them" （语境）研究气体的运动以及固体（如飞机）如何在气体中运动的科学 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "aerodynamics" noun
              Gravitation, Cosmology & Astrophysics
              引力、宇宙学与天体物理              		 (context) "in science, the force that attracts all objects towards one another" （语境）在科学中，指使所有物体相互吸引的力 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "gravitation" noun (FORCE)
              Interdisciplinary Physics
              交叉物理              		 (context) "involving two or more different subjects or areas of knowledge" （语境）涉及两个或更多不同学科或知识领域的 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "interdisciplinary" adjective
              Networks
              网络              		 (context) "a large system consisting of many similar parts that are connected together to allow movement or communication between or along the parts, or between the parts and a control centre" （语境）由许多相互连接的相似部分组成的大型系统，使各部分之间、沿各部分或各部分与控制中心之间能够移动或通信 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "network" noun
              Nonlinear Dynamics
              非线性动力学              		 (context) "a scientific theory about situations that obey particular laws but appear to have little or no order" （语境）一种科学理论，研究那些遵循特定规律却看起来几乎没有秩序的情形 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "chaos theory" noun
              Nuclear Physics
              核物理              		 (context) "relating to the nucleus of an atom" （语境）与原子核有关的 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "nuclear" adjective (PHYSICS)
              Particles & Fields
              粒子与场              		 (context) "the part of physics that studies particles (= extremely small pieces of matter)" （语境）物理学中研究粒子（即极小物质单元）的分支 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "particle physics" noun

              Physics of Living Systems
              生命系统物理              		 (context) "the science that uses the laws and methods of physics to explain biology" （语境）运用物理学的定律和方法来解释生物学的科学 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "biophysics" noun
              Plasma Physics
              等离子体物理              		 (context) "a type of gas with almost no electrical charge, found in the sun and other stars" （语境）一种几乎不带电荷的气体，存在于太阳和其他恒星中 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "plasma" noun (GAS)
              Polymers & Soft Matter
              聚合物与软物质              		 (context) "a chemical substance consisting of large molecules made from many smaller and simpler molecules" （语境）由大分子构成的化学物质，这些大分子由许多更小、更简单的分子组成 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "polymer" noun
              Quantum Information, Science & Technology
              量子信息科学与技术              		 (context) "computing that does certain types of complicated calculations much faster than traditional computing, using qubits ..." （语境）一种计算方式，在某些复杂计算上比传统计算快得多，使用量子比特…… Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "quantum computing" noun
              Statistical Physics & Thermodynamics
              统计物理与热力学              		 (context) "the area of physics connected with the action of heat and other types of energy, and the relationship between them" （语境）物理学中与热和其他形式能量的作用以及它们之间关系有关的领域 Cambridge University Press & Assessment, n.d., "thermodynamics" noun
