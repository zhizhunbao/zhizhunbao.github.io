              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              A Anatomy
              解剖学              		 (No definition) （无定义） National Library of Medicine, 2026
              A01 Body Regions
              身体部位              		 "Anatomical areas of the body." 身体的解剖区域。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Body Regions" scope note
              A02 Musculoskeletal System
              肌肉骨骼系统              		 "The MUSCLES, bones (BONE AND BONES), and CARTILAGE of the body." 身体的肌肉（MUSCLES）、骨骼（BONE AND BONES）和软骨（CARTILAGE）。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Musculoskeletal System" scope note
              A03 Digestive System
              消化系统              		 "A group of organs stretching from the MOUTH to the ANUS, serving to breakdown foods, assimilate nutrients, and eliminate waste. In humans, the digestive system includes the GASTROINTESTINAL TRACT and the accessory glands (LIVER; BILIARY TRACT; PANCREAS)." 一组从口（MOUTH）延伸到肛门（ANUS）的器官，用于分解食物、吸收营养和排出废物。在人体中，消化系统包括胃肠道（GASTROINTESTINAL TRACT）和附属腺体（肝脏 LIVER；胆道 BILIARY TRACT；胰腺 PANCREAS）。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Digestive System" scope note
              A04 Respiratory System
              呼吸系统              		 "The tubular and cavernous organs and structures, by means of which pulmonary ventilation and gas exchange between ambient air and the blood are brought about." 管状和腔状的器官与结构，借助它们实现肺通气以及环境空气与血液之间的气体交换。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Respiratory System" scope note
              A05 Urogenital System
              泌尿生殖系统              		 "All the organs involved in reproduction and the formation and release of URINE." 参与生殖以及尿液（URINE）生成和排出的全部器官。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Urogenital System" scope note
              A06 Endocrine System
              内分泌系统              		 "The system of glands that release their secretions (hormones) directly into the circulatory system. In addition to the ENDOCRINE GLANDS, included are the CHROMAFFIN SYSTEM and the NEUROSECRETORY SYSTEMS." 由腺体组成的系统，这些腺体把分泌物（激素）直接释放进循环系统。除内分泌腺（ENDOCRINE GLANDS）外，还包括嗜铬系统（CHROMAFFIN SYSTEM）和神经分泌系统（NEUROSECRETORY SYSTEMS）。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Endocrine System" scope note
              A07 Cardiovascular System
              心血管系统              		 "The HEART and the BLOOD VESSELS by which BLOOD is pumped and circulated through the body." 心脏（HEART）和血管（BLOOD VESSELS），血液（BLOOD）借助它们被泵出并在全身循环。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Cardiovascular System" scope note
              A08 Nervous System
              神经系统              		 "The entire nerve apparatus, composed of a central part, the brain and spinal cord, and a peripheral part, the cranial and spinal nerves, autonomic ganglia, and plexuses. (Stedman, 26th ed)" 整个神经装置，由中枢部分（脑和脊髓）和周围部分（脑神经和脊神经、自主神经节和神经丛）组成。（Stedman，第 26 版） National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Nervous System" scope note
              A09 Sense Organs
              感觉器官              		 "Specialized organs adapted for the reception of stimuli by the NERVOUS SYSTEM." 为神经系统（NERVOUS SYSTEM）接收刺激而特化的器官。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Sense Organs" scope note
              A10 Tissues
              组织              		 "Collections of differentiated CELLS, such as EPITHELIUM; CONNECTIVE TISSUE; MUSCLES; and NERVE TISSUE. Tissues are cooperatively arranged to form organs with specialized functions such as RESPIRATION; DIGESTION; REPRODUCTION; MOVEMENT; and others." 分化细胞（CELLS）的集合，如上皮（EPITHELIUM）、结缔组织（CONNECTIVE TISSUE）、肌肉（MUSCLES）和神经组织（NERVE TISSUE）。组织协同排列，构成具有特定功能的器官，如呼吸（RESPIRATION）、消化（DIGESTION）、生殖（REPRODUCTION）、运动（MOVEMENT）等。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Tissues" scope note
              A11 Cells
              细胞              		 "The fundamental, structural, and functional units or subunits of living organisms. They are composed of CYTOPLASM containing various ORGANELLES and a CELL MEMBRANE boundary." 生物体的基本结构和功能单位或亚单位。它们由含有各种细胞器（ORGANELLES）的细胞质（CYTOPLASM）和作为边界的细胞膜（CELL MEMBRANE）组成。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Cells" scope note
              A12 Fluids and Secretions
              体液和分泌物              		 "Liquid substances produced by living organisms to fulfill specific functions or excreted as waste." 由生物体产生的液态物质，用于完成特定功能或作为废物排出。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Fluids and Secretions" scope note
              A13 Animal Structures
              动物结构              		 "Organs and other anatomical structures of non-human vertebrate and invertebrate animals." 非人类脊椎动物和无脊椎动物的器官及其他解剖结构。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Animal Structures" scope note
              A14 Stomatognathic System
              口颌系统              		 "The mouth, teeth, jaws, pharynx, and related structures as they relate to mastication, deglutition, and speech." 口、牙、颌、咽及相关结构，就其与咀嚼、吞咽和言语的关系而言。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Stomatognathic System" scope note
              A15 Hemic and Immune Systems
              血液和免疫系统              		 "Organs involved in the production of BLOOD, including the cellular and the molecular components essential in providing defense against foreign organisms or substances." 参与血液（BLOOD）生成的器官，包括在抵御外来生物或物质中必不可少的细胞成分和分子成分。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Hemic and Immune Systems" scope note
              A16 Embryonic Structures
              胚胎结构              		 "The anatomical parts that make up an organism in the early stages of development." 生物体在发育早期阶段的各个解剖部分。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Embryonic Structures" scope note
              A17 Integumentary System
              皮肤系统              		 "The outer covering of the body composed of the SKIN and the skin appendages, which are the HAIR, the NAILS; and the SEBACEOUS GLANDS and the SWEAT GLANDS and their ducts." 身体的外层覆盖物，由皮肤（SKIN）及皮肤附属器组成，附属器即毛发（HAIR）、指（趾）甲（NAILS），以及皮脂腺（SEBACEOUS GLANDS）、汗腺（SWEAT GLANDS）和它们的导管。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Integumentary System" scope note
              A18 Plant Structures
              植物结构              		 "The parts of plants, including SEEDS." 植物的各部分，包括种子（SEEDS）。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Plant Structures" scope note
              A19 Fungal Structures
              真菌结构              		 "The parts of fungi." 真菌的各部分。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Fungal Structures" scope note
              A20 Bacterial Structures
              细菌结构              		 "The parts of bacteria." 细菌的各部分。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Bacterial Structures" scope note
              A21 Viral Structures
              病毒结构              		 "The structural parts of the VIRION." 病毒体（VIRION）的结构部分。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Viral Structures" scope note
