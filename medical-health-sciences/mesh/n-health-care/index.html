              N Health Care
              卫生保健              		 (No definition) （无定义） National Library of Medicine, 2026
              N01 Population Characteristics
              人群特征              		 "Qualities and characterization of various types of populations within a social or geographic group, with emphasis on demography, health status, and socioeconomic factors." 社会或地理群体中各类人群的特质和特征描述，重点在人口统计、健康状况和社会经济因素。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Population Characteristics" scope note
              N02 Health Care Facilities Workforce and Services
              卫生保健设施、人力和服务              		 "The services provided in the delivery of health care, associated facilities in health care, and attendant manpower required or available." 提供卫生保健所提供的服务、卫生保健中的相关设施，以及所需或可用的配套人力。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Health Care Facilities Workforce and Services" scope note
              N03 Health Care Economics and Organizations
              卫生保健经济和组织              		 "The economic aspects of health care, its planning, and delivery. It includes government agencies and organizations in the private sector." 卫生保健的经济方面，及其规划和提供。包括政府机构和私营部门组织。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Health Care Economics and Organizations" scope note
              N04 Health Services Administration
              卫生服务管理              		 "The organization and administration of health services dedicated to the delivery of health care." 致力于提供卫生保健的卫生服务的组织和管理。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Health Services Administration" scope note
              N05 Health Care Quality, Access, and Evaluation
              卫生保健质量、可及性和评价              		 "The concept concerned with all aspects of the quality, accessibility, and appraisal of health care and health care delivery." 涉及卫生保健及其提供的质量、可及性和评价各个方面的概念。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Health Care Quality, Access, and Evaluation" scope note
              N06 Environment and Public Health
              环境和公共卫生              		 "Natural and man-made environments and their impact on public health." 自然环境和人造环境及其对公共卫生的影响。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Environment and Public Health" scope note
