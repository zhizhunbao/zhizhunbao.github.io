              E Analytical, Diagnostic and Therapeutic Techniques, and Equipment

              Classification

              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              E Analytical, Diagnostic and Therapeutic Techniques, and Equipment
              分析、诊断和治疗技术及设备              		 (No definition) （无定义） National Library of Medicine, 2026
              E01 Diagnosis
              诊断              		 "The determination of the nature of a disease or condition, or the distinguishing of one disease or condition from another. Assessment may be made through physical examination, laboratory tests, or the likes. Computerized programs may be used to enhance the decision-making process." 确定疾病或状况的性质，或把一种疾病或状况与另一种区分开。评估可通过体格检查、实验室检查等进行。可借助计算机程序改进决策过程。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Diagnosis" scope note
              E02 Therapeutics
              治疗学              		 "Procedures concerned with the remedial treatment or prevention of diseases." 涉及疾病的治疗或预防的程序。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Therapeutics" scope note
              E03 Anesthesia and Analgesia
              麻醉和镇痛              		 "Medical methods of either relieving pain caused by a particular condition or removing the sensation of pain during a surgery or other medical procedure." 医学方法，或缓解特定状况引起的疼痛，或在手术或其他医疗程序中消除痛觉。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Anesthesia and Analgesia" scope note
              E04 Surgical Procedures, Operative
              外科手术              		 "Operations carried out for the correction of deformities and defects, repair of injuries, and diagnosis and cure of certain diseases. (Taber, 18th ed.)." 为矫正畸形和缺陷、修复损伤以及诊断和治愈某些疾病而进行的手术。（Taber，第 18 版） National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Surgical Procedures, Operative" scope note
              E05 Investigative Techniques
              研究技术              		 "Investigative techniques used in pre-clinical and clinical research, epidemiology, chemistry, immunology, genetics, etc. They do not include techniques specifically applied to DIAGNOSIS; THERAPEUTICS; anesthesia and analgesia; SURGICAL PROCEDURES, OPERATIVE; and DENTISTRY." 用于临床前和临床研究、流行病学、化学、免疫学、遗传学等的研究技术。不包括专门用于诊断（DIAGNOSIS）、治疗（THERAPEUTICS）、麻醉和镇痛、外科手术（SURGICAL PROCEDURES, OPERATIVE）和牙科（DENTISTRY）的技术。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Investigative Techniques" scope note
              E06 Dentistry
              牙科学              		 "The profession concerned with the teeth, oral cavity, and associated structures, and the diagnosis and treatment of their diseases including prevention and the restoration of defective and missing tissue." 涉及牙齿、口腔及相关结构的职业，以及其疾病的诊断和治疗，包括预防以及缺损和缺失组织的修复。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Dentistry" scope note
              E07 Equipment and Supplies
              设备和用品              		 "Expendable and nonexpendable equipment, supplies, apparatus, and instruments that are used in diagnostic, surgical, therapeutic, scientific, and experimental procedures." 用于诊断、外科、治疗、科研和实验程序的消耗性和非消耗性设备、用品、器械和仪器。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Equipment and Supplies" scope note
