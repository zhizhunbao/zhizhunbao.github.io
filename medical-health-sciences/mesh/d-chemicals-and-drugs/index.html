              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              D Chemicals and Drugs
              化学品和药物              		 (No definition) （无定义） National Library of Medicine, 2026
              D01 Inorganic Chemicals
              无机化学品              		 "A broad class of substances encompassing all those that do not include carbon and its derivatives as their principal elements. However, carbides, carbonates, cyanides, cyanates, and carbon disulfide are included in this class." 一大类物质，涵盖所有不以碳及其衍生物为主要元素的物质。不过，碳化物、碳酸盐、氰化物、氰酸盐和二硫化碳也归入此类。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Inorganic Chemicals" scope note
              D02 Organic Chemicals
              有机化学品              		 "A broad class of substances containing carbon and its derivatives. Many of these chemicals will frequently contain hydrogen with or without oxygen, nitrogen, sulfur, phosphorus, and other elements. They exist in either carbon chain or carbon ring form." 一大类含有碳及其衍生物的物质。这些化学品常含氢，可含或不含氧、氮、硫、磷和其他元素。它们以碳链或碳环的形式存在。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Organic Chemicals" scope note
              D03 Heterocyclic Compounds
              杂环化合物              		 "Cyclic compounds that include atoms other than carbon in their ring structure." 环结构中含有碳以外原子的环状化合物。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Heterocyclic Compounds" scope note
              D04 Polycyclic Compounds
              多环化合物              		 "Compounds which contain two or more rings in their structure." 结构中含有两个或更多环的化合物。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Polycyclic Compounds" scope note
              D05 Macromolecular Substances
              大分子物质              		 "Compounds and molecular complexes that consist of very large numbers of atoms and are generally over 500 kDa in size. In biological systems macromolecular substances usually can be visualized using ELECTRON MICROSCOPY and are distinguished from ORGANELLES by the lack of a membrane structure." 由极多原子组成的化合物和分子复合物，大小一般超过 500 kDa。在生物系统中，大分子物质通常可用电子显微镜（ELECTRON MICROSCOPY）观察，并因没有膜结构而与细胞器（ORGANELLES）相区别。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Macromolecular Substances" scope note
              D06 Hormones, Hormone Substitutes, and Hormone Antagonists
              激素、激素代用品和激素拮抗剂              		 "A collective grouping for both naturally occurring and synthetic hormones, substitutes, and antagonists." 天然和合成的激素、代用品和拮抗剂的统称。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Hormones, Hormone Substitutes, and Hormone Antagonists" scope note
              D08 Enzymes and Coenzymes
              酶和辅酶              		 "Biological catalysts and their cofactors." 生物催化剂及其辅因子。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Enzymes and Coenzymes" scope note
              D09 Carbohydrates
              碳水化合物              		 "A class of organic compounds composed of carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen in a ratio of Cn(H2O)n. The largest class of organic compounds, including STARCH; GLYCOGEN; CELLULOSE; POLYSACCHARIDES; and simple MONOSACCHARIDES." 一类由碳、氢和氧按 Cn(H2O)n 比例组成的有机化合物。是最大的一类有机化合物，包括淀粉（STARCH）、糖原（GLYCOGEN）、纤维素（CELLULOSE）、多糖（POLYSACCHARIDES）和简单的单糖（MONOSACCHARIDES）。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Carbohydrates" scope note
              D10 Lipids
              脂质              		 "A generic term for fats and lipoids, the alcohol-ether-soluble constituents of protoplasm, which are insoluble in water." 脂肪和类脂的总称，是原生质中可溶于醇和醚的成分，不溶于水。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Lipids" scope note
              D12 Amino Acids, Peptides, and Proteins
              氨基酸、肽和蛋白质              		 "Amino acids and chains of amino acids connected by peptide linkages." 氨基酸，以及由肽键连接的氨基酸链。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Amino Acids, Peptides, and Proteins" scope note
              D13 Nucleic Acids, Nucleotides, and Nucleosides
              核酸、核苷酸和核苷              		 "Complex compounds of high molecular weight occurring in living cells. These are basically of two types, ribonucleic (RNA) and deoxyribonucleic (DNA) acids, both of which consist of nucleotides (nucleoside phosphates linked together by phosphate bridges)." 存在于活细胞中的高分子量复杂化合物。基本上分为两类，核糖核酸（RNA）和脱氧核糖核酸（DNA），两者都由核苷酸组成（核苷磷酸通过磷酸桥连接在一起）。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Nucleic Acids, Nucleotides, and Nucleosides" scope note
              D20 Complex Mixtures
              复杂混合物              		 "Mixtures of many components in inexact proportions, usually natural, such as PLANT EXTRACTS; VENOMS; and MANURE. These are distinguished from DRUG COMBINATIONS which have only a few components in definite proportions." 由多种成分按不确定比例组成的混合物，通常来自天然，如植物提取物（PLANT EXTRACTS）、毒液（VENOMS）和粪肥（MANURE）。它们有别于药物组合（DRUG COMBINATIONS），后者只有少数成分且比例确定。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Complex Mixtures" scope note
              D23 Biological Factors
              生物因子              		 "Endogenously synthesized compounds that influence biological processes not otherwise classified under ENZYMES; HORMONES or HORMONE ANTAGONISTS." 内源合成的化合物，影响生物过程，且未另归入酶（ENZYMES）、激素（HORMONES）或激素拮抗剂（HORMONE ANTAGONISTS）。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Biological Factors" scope note
              D25 Biomedical and Dental Materials
              生物医学和牙科材料              		 "Substances used in biomedicine or dentistry predominantly for their physical, as opposed to chemical, properties." 在生物医学或牙科中使用的物质，主要利用其物理性质而非化学性质。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Biomedical and Dental Materials" scope note
              D26 Pharmaceutical Preparations
              药物制剂              		 "Drugs intended for human or veterinary use, presented in their finished dosage form. Included here are materials used in the preparation and/or formulation of the finished dosage form." 供人用或兽用的药物，以最终剂型呈现。也包括制备和/或配制最终剂型所用的材料。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Pharmaceutical Preparations" scope note
              D27 Chemical Actions and Uses
              化学作用和用途              		 "A group of pharmacologic activities, effects on living systems and the environment, and modes of employment of drugs and chemicals. They are broken into actions, which describe their effects, and uses, which describe how they are employed." 药物和化学品的一组药理活性、对生物系统和环境的影响以及使用方式。它们分为作用，描述其效果，和用途，描述其使用方式。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Chemical Actions and Uses" scope note
