              Glossary

              Term Definition 中文翻译 Source
              C Diseases
              疾病              		 (No definition) （无定义） National Library of Medicine, 2026
              C01 Infections
              感染              		 "Invasion of the host organism by microorganisms or their toxins or by parasites that can cause pathological conditions or diseases." 微生物或其毒素、或寄生虫对宿主生物的侵入，可引起病理状态或疾病。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Infections" scope note
              C04 Neoplasms
              肿瘤              		 "New abnormal growth of tissue. Malignant neoplasms show a greater degree of anaplasia and have the properties of invasion and metastasis, compared to benign neoplasms." 组织新的异常生长。与良性肿瘤相比，恶性肿瘤表现出更高程度的间变，并具有浸润和转移的特性。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Neoplasms" scope note
              C05 Musculoskeletal Diseases
              肌肉骨骼疾病              		 "Diseases of the muscles and their associated ligaments and other connective tissue and of the bones and cartilage viewed collectively." 肌肉疾病及其相关韧带和其他结缔组织的疾病，以及骨和软骨的疾病的统称。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Musculoskeletal Diseases" scope note
              C06 Digestive System Diseases
              消化系统疾病              		 "Diseases in any part of the GASTROINTESTINAL TRACT or the accessory organs (LIVER; BILIARY TRACT; PANCREAS)." 发生在胃肠道（GASTROINTESTINAL TRACT）任何部位或附属器官（肝脏 LIVER；胆道 BILIARY TRACT；胰腺 PANCREAS）的疾病。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Digestive System Diseases" scope note
              C07 Stomatognathic Diseases
              口颌疾病              		 "General or unspecified diseases of the stomatognathic system, comprising the mouth, teeth, jaws, and pharynx." 口颌系统的一般性或未特指的疾病，该系统包括口、牙、颌和咽。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Stomatognathic Diseases" scope note
              C08 Respiratory Tract Diseases
              呼吸道疾病              		 "Diseases involving the RESPIRATORY SYSTEM." 涉及呼吸系统（RESPIRATORY SYSTEM）的疾病。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Respiratory Tract Diseases" scope note
              C09 Otorhinolaryngologic Diseases
              耳鼻喉疾病              		 "Pathological processes of the ear, the nose, and the throat, also known as the ENT diseases." 耳、鼻和喉的病理过程，也称 ENT 疾病。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Otorhinolaryngologic Diseases" scope note
              C10 Nervous System Diseases
              神经系统疾病              		 "Diseases of the central and peripheral nervous system. This includes disorders of the brain, spinal cord, cranial nerves, peripheral nerves, nerve roots, autonomic nervous system, neuromuscular junction, and muscle." 中枢和周围神经系统的疾病。包括脑、脊髓、脑神经、周围神经、神经根、自主神经系统、神经肌肉接头和肌肉的疾患。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Nervous System Diseases" scope note
              C11 Eye Diseases
              眼病              		 "Diseases affecting the eye." 累及眼睛的疾病。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Eye Diseases" scope note
              C12 Urogenital Diseases
              泌尿生殖疾病              		 "Pathological processes of the URINARY TRACT and the reproductive system (GENITALIA)." 泌尿道（URINARY TRACT）和生殖系统（生殖器 GENITALIA）的病理过程。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Urogenital Diseases" scope note
              C14 Cardiovascular Diseases
              心血管疾病              		 "Pathological conditions involving the CARDIOVASCULAR SYSTEM including the HEART; the BLOOD VESSELS; or the PERICARDIUM." 涉及心血管系统（CARDIOVASCULAR SYSTEM）的病理状态，包括心脏（HEART）、血管（BLOOD VESSELS）或心包（PERICARDIUM）。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Cardiovascular Diseases" scope note
              C15 Hemic and Lymphatic Diseases
              血液和淋巴疾病              		 "Hematologic diseases and diseases of the lymphatic system collectively." 血液系统疾病和淋巴系统疾病的统称。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Hemic and Lymphatic Diseases" scope note
              C16 Congenital, Hereditary, and Neonatal Diseases and Abnormalities
              先天性、遗传性和新生儿疾病及畸形              		 "Diseases existing at birth and often before birth, or that develop during the first month of life (INFANT, NEWBORN, DISEASES), regardless of causation. Of these diseases, those characterized by structural deformities are termed CONGENITAL ABNORMALITIES." 出生时即存在、且常在出生前就已存在的疾病，或在出生后第一个月内发生的疾病（新生儿疾病 INFANT, NEWBORN, DISEASES），不论病因。其中以结构畸形为特征的称为先天畸形（CONGENITAL ABNORMALITIES）。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Congenital, Hereditary, and Neonatal Diseases and Abnormalities" scope note
              C17 Skin and Connective Tissue Diseases
              皮肤和结缔组织疾病              		 "A collective term for diseases of the skin and its appendages and of connective tissue." 皮肤及其附属器疾病和结缔组织疾病的统称。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Skin and Connective Tissue Diseases" scope note
              C18 Nutritional and Metabolic Diseases
              营养和代谢疾病              		 "A collective term for nutritional disorders resulting from poor absorption or nutritional imbalance, and metabolic disorders resulting from defects in biosynthesis (ANABOLISM) or breakdown (CATABOLISM) of endogenous substances." 营养障碍和代谢障碍的统称：前者由吸收不良或营养失衡引起，后者由内源性物质的合成（ANABOLISM）或分解（CATABOLISM）缺陷引起。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Nutritional and Metabolic Diseases" scope note
              C19 Endocrine System Diseases
              内分泌系统疾病              		 "Pathological processes of the ENDOCRINE GLANDS, and diseases resulting from abnormal level of available HORMONES." 内分泌腺（ENDOCRINE GLANDS）的病理过程，以及由可用激素（HORMONES）水平异常引起的疾病。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Endocrine System Diseases" scope note
              C20 Immune System Diseases
              免疫系统疾病              		 "Disorders caused by abnormal or absent immunologic mechanisms, whether humoral, cell-mediated, or both." 由免疫机制异常或缺失引起的疾患，无论是体液免疫、细胞介导免疫，还是两者兼有。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Immune System Diseases" scope note
              C21 Disorders of Environmental Origin
              环境源性疾患              		 "Disorders caused by external forces rather than by physiologic dysfunction or by pathogens." 由外部力量而非生理功能障碍或病原体引起的疾患。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Disorders of Environmental Origin" scope note
              C22 Animal Diseases
              动物疾病              		 "Diseases that occur in VERTEBRATE animals." 发生在脊椎动物（VERTEBRATE）身上的疾病。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Animal Diseases" scope note
              C23 Pathological Conditions, Signs and Symptoms
              病理状态、体征和症状              		 "Abnormal anatomical or physiological conditions and objective or subjective manifestations of disease, not classified as disease or syndrome." 异常的解剖或生理状态，以及疾病的客观或主观表现，不归为疾病或综合征。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Pathological Conditions, Signs and Symptoms" scope note
              C24 Occupational Diseases
              职业病              		 "Diseases caused by factors involved in one's employment." 由与本人职业有关的因素引起的疾病。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Occupational Diseases" scope note
              C25 Chemically-Induced Disorders
              化学物质所致疾患              		 "Disorders caused by the intentional or unintentional ingestion or exposure to chemical substances such as PHARMACEUTICAL PREPARATIONS; NOXAE; and PESTICIDES." 由有意或无意摄入或接触化学物质引起的疾患，如药物制剂（PHARMACEUTICAL PREPARATIONS）、有害物质（NOXAE）和农药（PESTICIDES）。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Chemically-Induced Disorders" scope note
              C26 Wounds and Injuries
              创伤和损伤              		 "Damage inflicted on the body as the direct or indirect result of an external force, with or without disruption of structural continuity." 身体所受的损害，是外力直接或间接作用的结果，可伴有或不伴有结构连续性的破坏。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Wounds and Injuries" scope note
