              C17.800.113 Calcinosis Cutis
              皮肤钙质沉着症              		 "Pathological deposition of calcium in the skin and subcutaneous tissue.               C17.800.185 Dermatomyositis
              皮肌炎              		 "A subacute or chronic inflammatory disease of muscle and skin, marked by proximal muscle weakness and a characteristic skin rash. The illness occurs with approximately equal frequency in children and adults. The skin lesions usually take the form of a purplish rash (or less often an exfoliative dermatitis) involving the nose, cheeks, forehead, upper trunk, and arms. The disease is associated with a complement mediated intramuscular microangiopathy, leading to loss of capillaries, muscle ischemia, muscle-fiber necrosis, and perifascicular atrophy. The childhood form of this disease tends to evolve into a systemic vasculitis. Dermatomyositis may occur in association with malignant neoplasms. (From Adams et al., Principles of Neurology, 6th ed, pp1405-6)" 一种亚急性或慢性的肌肉和皮肤炎症性疾病，以近端肌无力和特征性皮疹为特点。该病在儿童和成人中的发病率大致相等。皮肤病变通常表现为紫红色皮疹（较少为剥脱性皮炎），累及鼻、面颊、前额、上躯干和上肢。该病与补体介导的肌内微血管病有关，导致毛细血管丧失、肌肉缺血、肌纤维坏死和束周萎缩。儿童型往往发展为系统性血管炎。皮肌炎可伴发恶性肿瘤。（引自 Adams 等，《神经病学原理》，第 6 版，第 1405-1406 页） National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Dermatomyositis" scope note

              C17.800.257 Exanthema
              疹病              		 "Diseases in which skin eruptions or rashes are a prominent manifestation. Classically, six such diseases were described with similar rashes; they were numbered in the order in which they were reported. Only the fourth (Duke's disease), fifth (ERYTHEMA INFECTIOSUM), and sixth (EXANTHEMA SUBITUM) numeric designations survive as occasional synonyms in current terminology." 以皮疹或出疹为突出表现的疾病。传统上曾描述过六种皮疹相似的此类疾病，按报道顺序编号。在当前术语中，只有第四病（杜克病）、第五病（传染性红斑（ERYTHEMA INFECTIOSUM））和第六病（幼儿急疹（EXANTHEMA SUBITUM））的数字名称仍偶尔作为同义词使用。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Exanthema" scope note

              C17.800.417 Keratoacanthoma

The eruptive form usually involves both sexes and appears as a generalized papular eruption." 一种良性、非肿瘤性、通常具有自限性的上皮病变，在临床和组织病理学上与鳞状细胞癌极为相似。它有单发型、多发型和发疹型。单发型和多发型发生于日光暴露部位，组织学上相同，主要累及白人男性。发疹型通常两性均可累及，表现为泛发性丘疹样皮疹。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Keratoacanthoma" scope note

              C17.800.553 Nephrogenic Fibrosing Dermopathy

              C17.800.600 Photosensitivity Disorders
              光敏性疾病              		 "Abnormal responses to sunlight or artificial light due to extreme reactivity of light-absorbing molecules in tissues. It refers almost exclusively to skin photosensitivity, including sunburn, reactions due to repeated prolonged exposure in the absence of photosensitizing factors, and reactions requiring photosensitizing factors such as photosensitizing agents and certain diseases. With restricted reference to skin tissue, it does not include photosensitivity of the eye to light, as in photophobia or photosensitive epilepsy." 对日光或人工光的异常反应，由组织中吸光分子的极端反应性所致。它几乎专指皮肤光敏性，包括晒伤、无光敏因素时反复长时间暴露引起的反应，以及需要光敏因素（如光敏剂和某些疾病）的反应。由于仅限于皮肤组织，它不包括眼对光的光敏性，如畏光或光敏性癫痫。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Photosensitivity Disorders" scope note

              C17.800.716 Rosacea
              酒渣鼻              		 "A cutaneous disorder primarily of convexities of the central part of the FACE, such as FOREHEAD; CHEEK; NOSE; and CHIN. It is characterized by FLUSHING; ERYTHEMA; EDEMA; RHINOPHYMA; papules; and ocular symptoms. It may occur at any age but typically after age 30. There are various subtypes of rosacea: erythematotelangiectatic, papulopustular, phymatous, and ocular (National Rosacea Society's Expert Committee on the Classification and Staging of Rosacea, J Am Acad Dermatol 2002; 46:584-7)." 一种主要累及面部（FACE）中央凸出部位的皮肤病，如前额（FOREHEAD）、面颊（CHEEK）、鼻（NOSE）和下颏（CHIN）。其特征为潮红（FLUSHING）、红斑（ERYTHEMA）、水肿（EDEMA）、鼻赘（RHINOPHYMA）、丘疹及眼部症状。可发生于任何年龄，但通常在 30 岁以后。酒渣鼻有多种亚型：红斑毛细血管扩张型、丘疹脓疱型、肥大增生型和眼型（美国国家酒渣鼻协会酒渣鼻分类与分期专家委员会，J Am Acad Dermatol 2002；46:584-7）。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Rosacea" scope note

              C17.800.751 Scleredema Adultorum
              成人硬肿病              		 "A diffuse, non-pitting induration of the skin of unknown etiology that occurs most commonly in association with diabetes mellitus, predominantly in females. It typically begins on the face or head and spreads to other areas of the body, sometimes involving noncutaneous tissues. Often it is preceded by any of various infections, notably staphylococcal infections. The condition resolves spontaneously, usually within two years of onset. (From Dorland, 27th ed)" 一种病因不明的弥漫性、非凹陷性皮肤硬肿，最常与糖尿病相伴发生，以女性为主。它通常始于面部或头部，并扩展到身体其他部位，有时累及皮肤以外的组织。发病前常有各种感染，尤其是葡萄球菌感染。该病可自发消退，通常在发病后两年内。（引自 Dorland，第 27 版） National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Scleredema Adultorum" scope note
              C17.800.767 Scleroderma, Localized
              局限性硬皮病              		 "A term used to describe a variety of localized asymmetrical SKIN thickening that is similar to those of SYSTEMIC SCLERODERMA but without the disease features in the multiple internal organs and BLOOD VESSELS. Lesions may be characterized as patches or plaques (morphea), bands (linear), or nodules." 用于描述多种局限性不对称皮肤（SKIN）增厚的术语，类似于系统性硬皮病（SYSTEMIC SCLERODERMA）的皮肤增厚，但没有多个内脏器官和血管（BLOOD VESSELS）的病变特征。皮损可表现为斑片或斑块（硬斑病）、带状（线状）或结节。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Scleroderma, Localized" scope note
              C17.800.784 Scleroderma, Systemic
              系统性硬皮病              		 "A chronic multi-system disorder of CONNECTIVE TISSUE. It is characterized by SCLEROSIS in the SKIN, the LUNGS, the HEART, the GASTROINTESTINAL TRACT, the KIDNEYS, and the MUSCULOSKELETAL SYSTEM. Other important features include diseased small BLOOD VESSELS and AUTOANTIBODIES. The disorder is named for its most prominent feature (hard skin), and classified into subsets by the extent of skin thickening: LIMITED SCLERODERMA and DIFFUSE SCLERODERMA." 一种结缔组织（CONNECTIVE TISSUE）的慢性多系统疾病。其特征为皮肤（SKIN）、肺（LUNGS）、心脏（HEART）、胃肠道（GASTROINTESTINAL TRACT）、肾（KIDNEYS）和肌肉骨骼系统（MUSCULOSKELETAL SYSTEM）的硬化（SCLEROSIS）。其他重要特征包括小血管（BLOOD VESSELS）病变和自身抗体（AUTOANTIBODIES）。该病以其最突出的特征（皮肤变硬）命名，并按皮肤增厚的范围分为亚型：局限性硬皮病（LIMITED SCLERODERMA）和弥漫性硬皮病（DIFFUSE SCLERODERMA）。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Scleroderma, Systemic" scope note

              C17.800.973 Xanthogranuloma, Juvenile
              幼年黄色肉芽肿              		 "Benign disorder of infants and children caused by proliferation of HISTIOCYTES, macrophages found in tissues. These histiocytes, usually lipid-laden non-Langerhans cells, form multiple yellow-red nodules most often in the skin, the eye, and sometimes in the viscera. Patients appear to have normal lipid metabolism and are classified as a normolipemic non-Langerhans cell histiocytosis." 婴儿和儿童的良性疾病，由组织细胞（HISTIOCYTES）增生所致，组织细胞即存在于组织中的巨噬细胞。这些组织细胞通常为富含脂质的非朗格汉斯细胞，形成多发性黄红色结节，最常见于皮肤、眼，有时见于内脏。患者脂质代谢似乎正常，被归类为血脂正常的非朗格汉斯细胞组织细胞增生症。 National Library of Medicine, 2026, "Xanthogranuloma, Juvenile" scope note
