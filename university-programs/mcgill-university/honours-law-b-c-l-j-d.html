              Minimum Writing Requirement All students are required to submit at least one research paper. This requirement may be satisfied by: McGill University, n.d.
              Minimum Writing Requirement writing and essay in a course in which the essay constitutes no less than 75% of the final grade; writing a term essay under independent supervision, for credit, within the Faculty of Law; writing an article, note, or comment of equivalent substance that is published or accepted for publication in the McGill Law Journal and approved by the Faculty Adviser to that publication. McGill University, n.d.
              Minimum Writing Requirement Papers written jointly do not satisfy this requirement. McGill University, n.d.
