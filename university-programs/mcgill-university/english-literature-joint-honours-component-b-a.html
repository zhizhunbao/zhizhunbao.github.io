              Complementary Courses (24 credits) In addition to the 6-credit requirement for Advanced Study described below, all Joint Honours students' programs of study shall include 6 credits of study at the 400 level or above. Students are encouraged to take courses at the 300 level and above. At least 3 of the 24 credits must be devoted to a course on a Major Author as indicated under the rubrics dedicated to these offerings in each year’s list of Complementary Courses on the Department of English website ( http://www.mcgill.ca/english ). In addition to the Advanced Study requirement, 3 of the remaining 18 Complementary Courses credits must be completed at the 500 level. A maximum of 9 of the 24 credits are allowed at the 200 level, none in the final year of the program. McGill University, n.d.
              Complementary Courses (24 credits) Note on Topics Courses: The Department of English offers courses which change topic from academic year to academic year. Depending on the topic in a specific year, these courses may count toward different program requirements. At the time they register for a topics course, students should confirm with their program adviser the program requirement it fulfils for that academic year. McGill University, n.d.
              Advanced Study 6 credits of advanced study, in one of the following two forms A or B, in order of preference: McGill University, n.d.

              Advanced Study B) Two 3-credit 500-level courses selected in consultation with the student's adviser(s). McGill University, n.d.
              Advanced Study (In very rare cases, a third alternative may be approved at the discretion of the Joint Honours Adviser, but only when it is formally recommended for the joint subject according to the description of that Joint Honours program found in the Arts section of the Course Catalogue. For example, Joint Honours with Anthropology allows the option of combining 3 credits of essay work with 3 credits in the joint subject to create a joint essay.) McGill University, n.d.
