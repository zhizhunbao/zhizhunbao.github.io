              Language Requirement Speech Sciences majors are required to complete 6 credits of coursework in a single language other than English.Courses may be taken in any non-English language and at any level. The University of British Columbia, n.d.
              Language Requirement Hence, these credits must be completed by all Speech Sciences students as part of their B.A. and cannot be waived based on existing language competency. The University of British Columbia, n.d.
