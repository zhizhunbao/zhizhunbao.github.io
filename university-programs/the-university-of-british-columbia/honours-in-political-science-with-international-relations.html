              Language requirementHonours in Political Science with International Relations (PSIR) students are required to complete 6 credits of post-secondary coursework in one language other than English. The University of British Columbia, n.d.
              These credits must be completed by all PSIR major students and cannot be waived based on prior language study or an existing language competency. The University of British Columbia, n.d.
