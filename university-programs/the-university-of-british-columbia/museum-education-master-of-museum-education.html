              Program Requirements The Master of Museum Education (M.M.Ed.) program consists of a minimum of 30 credits, of which 24 must be courses numbered at the 500-level. The University of British Columbia, n.d.
              Program Requirements A maximum of 6 credits may be taken at the 300- or 400-level. The University of British Columbia, n.d.
              Program Requirements Students select either a program consisting entirely of courses (for example, ten 3-credit courses), or 27 credits of coursework, plus a 3-credit graduating project. The University of British Columbia, n.d.
