              Program Requirements To obtain both the BEd degree and the BSc degree, students must successfully complete 150 units as follows: 72 units in science, 51 units in education, 24 units in French, and 3 units in free option. Major (Science) – 36 units Minor in Science – 24 units Minor in French – 24 units Technology ( EDU M 341 ) – 3 units History or Philosophy of Science – 3 units Options (Science) – 6 units Education courses – 51 units Free option – 3 units University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements Normally, a maximum of 48 units at the junior level is permitted in the combined program. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements Students must successfully complete a minimum of 12 units at the 300-level in the major and, in addition, a minimum of 6 units at the 300-level in the science minor. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements The following courses must be included in the French minor: FRANC 238 , 3 units in Francophonies (see Classification of Courses ) and 3 units in literature ( LITT 135 ). University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements Normally at least 30 units at the junior-level must be successfully completed before students may register in senior-level courses. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements In order to be admitted with advanced credits in the program, students must normally possess a Grade Point Average of at least 2.0. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements To continue in the combined BEd/BSc program requires a Grade Point Average of at least 2.0. Students who do not meet this requirement must withdraw from the combined program and may apply for admission to either a BEd program or a BSc program if eligible. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements To obtain both the BEd and BSc degrees, students must maintain a GPA of 2.0 in all courses for which a mark is granted and in all the major courses in science. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements To be recommended for the Interim Professional Certificate, students must obtain a Grade Point Average of at least 2.0 in all compulsory professional courses. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements Students who receive a failing grade in “Stages I or II” will not be allowed to register in any further Education Field Experiences courses at Faculté Saint‑Jean and will be required to withdraw from the program. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements Students who withdraw from “Stages I or II” must have the approval of the Vice-Dean to reregister for these courses. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements Students in schools during the field experience are expected to conduct themselves according to the Alberta Teachers’ Association Code of Professional Conduct and the University of Alberta Code of Student Behaviour: Conduct and Discipline. Student teachers shall University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements recognize and accept that the welfare of the students is of ultimate concern and that the cooperating teacher has the final responsibility for what occurs in the classroom; and University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements maintain an ethical and professional attitude toward all members of the school community and become familiar with the Alberta Teachers’ Association Code of Professional Conduct. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements Students are held accountable to these standards and answerable to the Vice-Dean. University of Alberta, n.d.
