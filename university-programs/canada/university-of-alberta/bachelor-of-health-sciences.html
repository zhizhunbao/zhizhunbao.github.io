              Program Requirements The BHSc program comprises 120 credit units over four years. In summary the degree consists of: University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements Degree Core: A group of required Health Sciences courses totalling 69 credit units. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements Degree Concentration: Students choose and complete one 18-credit degree concentration. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements Health Electives: Students select 24-credits from a broad list of approved health electives. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements Pre-professional option: Students select and complete health professional program prerequisites as health electives. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements Open Electives: Students select 9 credits of electives from any course across the University. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements Language of Instruction University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements The program is delivered in English. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements | Course Sequence Students must complete one 18 unit concentration selected from Population and Public Health, Health Systems Innovation, Mental Health and Wellness. University of Alberta, n.d.

              Program Requirements | Course Sequence The pre-professional option provides students with guidance and preparation to support a future application to a health professional program. Courses in the pre-professional option count towards health electives. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Program Requirements | Course Sequence The sequencing of when students take courses, particularly outside of the core requirement, is flexible. Students must be aware of and consider course prerequisites when planning course enrolments. University of Alberta, n.d.
