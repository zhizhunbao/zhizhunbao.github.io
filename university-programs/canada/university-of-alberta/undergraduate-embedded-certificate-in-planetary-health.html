              Courses

              Level Type Course Code Course Name Credits Source
              Certificate eligibility and completion requirements Students from any faculty can complete this certificate. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Certificate eligibility and completion requirements Students wanting to complete this certificate will be expected to complete 15 credits of courses. Nine of these credits derive from the three core courses ( SPH 200 , SPH 414 and SPH 416 ). The remaining six credits can be selected from the approved course list. At least one elective course must be from a faculty other than the School of Public Health. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Certificate eligibility and completion requirements Students interested in pursuing this Undergraduate Embedded Certificate should declare their interest to their home faculty as well as the School of Public Health ( sphprograms@ualberta.ca ) as early as possible. University of Alberta, n.d.
