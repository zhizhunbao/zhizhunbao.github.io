              (1) Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) The Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) for students entering the program is a four year program consisting of coursework (99 units) and practice experience (40 units). The courses are required for all students. The elective courses permit exploration of the field of pharmacy. The fourth-year focuses on practice experiences. University of Alberta, n.d.
              (1) Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) | Orientation It is mandatory that each student, after acceptance into the program, attend the Faculty’s Orientation. This is scheduled immediately before the beginning of the first term. University of Alberta, n.d.
