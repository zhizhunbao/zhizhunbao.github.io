              Courses

              Level Type Course Code Course Name Credits Source
              Common Requirements The BA at Faculté Saint-Jean is a four-year program, with 120 units. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Common Requirements The requirements concerning French courses are those of Faculté Saint‑Jean. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Common Requirements Students must complete 6 units in a language course other than French. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Common Requirements Students must complete LITT 135 , normally during the second year of their program. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Common Requirements Students registered in a program with concentration must complete 6 units in Artistic Expression and Literature 6 units in Science. Students registered in an Honors program must complete either 6 units in Artistic Expression and Literature or 6 units in Science. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Common Requirements Options may be Arts or Science courses. However, up to 12 units may be taken in areas other than arts and science. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Common Requirements Exceptionally, the Associate Dean (Academic) may authorize a student to spend less than or more than four years to meet the requirements of a degree. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Common Requirements The student in a program in Social Studies or Philosophical and Social Thought must complete one course in Research methods: SCSOC 225 . University of Alberta, n.d.
