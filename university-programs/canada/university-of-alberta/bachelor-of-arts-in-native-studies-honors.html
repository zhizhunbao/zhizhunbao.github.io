              Courses

              Level Type Course Code Course Name Credits Source
              General Program Requirements Residence Requirement: the Honors degree requires 120 units of which University of Alberta, n.d.
              General Program Requirements 66 units must be courses in the Faculty of Native Studies or Faculty of Arts, University of Alberta, n.d.
              General Program Requirements 60 units must be courses taken at the University of Alberta, and University of Alberta, n.d.
              General Program Requirements 30 units must be completed after being admitted to the honors program. University of Alberta, n.d.
              General Program Requirements Course Load Requirements Students usually complete the Honors program in four consecutive Fall/Winter periods, with 30 units in each Fall/Winter. With the prior approval of the Native Studies Honors Advisor, a reduced course load may be taken in extenuating circumstances provided that 9 units is taken in each term. Requests to take less than 9 units per term may be considered by the Faculty’s Honors Committee in exceptional circumstances; however, a course load of less than 9 units in each term will not normally be permitted in the Honors program. All degree requirements should be completed within 5 academic years of commencing the second year of the program. University of Alberta, n.d.
              General Program Requirements Advising: Honors students are advised to have their programs and registration checked by the Academic Advisor annually and are responsible for the accuracy of their programs. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Course Requirements The Honors degree requires 120 units comprised of the following groups of courses: University of Alberta, n.d.
              Course Requirements | Faculty Common Core (30 units) BA NS Honors and BA NS students complete the same basic requirements. See Faculty Common Requirements . University of Alberta, n.d.
              Course Requirements | Major subject (48-60 units) Language course in NS (6 units) University of Alberta, n.d.

              Course Requirements | Major subject (48-60 units) 12 units in NS courses at the 200 or 300 level (excluding NS 200 ) University of Alberta, n.d.
              Course Requirements | Major subject (48-60 units) NS 490 Community-Based Research or other 400-level methods course (Faculty approval required) University of Alberta, n.d.
              Course Requirements | Major subject (48-60 units) 9 units in NS courses at the 400-level University of Alberta, n.d.
              Course Requirements | Major subject (48-60 units) NS 498 Honors Paper or Project University of Alberta, n.d.
              Course Requirements | Minor subject (12-24 units) Students may choose to do a minor but minors are not required in the Honors program. A minimum of senior 12 units with at least 6 units at the 300- and 400-levels is normally required. Students should consult the relevant Faculty or Department for minor requirements. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Course Requirements | Options (0-42 units) Depending on the number of courses taken in the major and on there being a minor, students may take up to 42 units in options. Acceptable option courses may be taken from the credit course offerings open to Native Studies students from any U of A Faculty but must meet their prerequisite requirements. University of Alberta, n.d.
              Course Requirements | Junior courses No more than 48 units at the 100-level are permitted for credit in the program. University of Alberta, n.d.
