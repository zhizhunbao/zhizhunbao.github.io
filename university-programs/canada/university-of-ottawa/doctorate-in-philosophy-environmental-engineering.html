              Minimum Requirements Students who fail 6 units, or whose research progress is deemed unsatisfactory are required to withdraw from the program. University of Ottawa, n.d.
              Transfer from Master’s to PhD Master's students with outstanding performance in the master’s courses may request transfer into the PhD program without completing the master’s degree. Students who are permitted to do so must successfully complete a total of 24 course units (15 at the Master's and 9 at the PhD) for a PhD. University of Ottawa, n.d.
