              Minimum Standards The passing grade in all graduate courses is C+ (65%). Students who receive failing grades in more than two courses (or in the same course twice) must withdraw from the program. University of Ottawa, n.d.
              Duration of Program The diploma may be completed in 6 terms. The maximum duration is three years from the initial enrollment in the program. University of Ottawa, n.d.
              Retained Units Students enrolled in the Leadership and Management diploma or the Organizational Performance Management diploma can transfer all course units to the MBA program, subject to the following conditions: University of Ottawa, n.d.
              Retained Units courses must have been completed within the past five years; courses must have been completed with a minimum grade of B (70%). University of Ottawa, n.d.
