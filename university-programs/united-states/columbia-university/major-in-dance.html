The following course, which culminates in a 25-30-page written thesis and an oral presentation to the Department at the end of the semester, is required of all seniors: Columbia University, n.d.
              Senior Work DNCE BC3591 SENIOR SEMINAR Columbia University, n.d.
              Senior Work In addition, all majors must take one of the following two courses, depending on whether the senior requirement is completed with a creative project or a two-semester written thesis: 3-4 Columbia University, n.d.
              Senior Work DNCE BC3592 Senior Project: Research for Dance Columbia University, n.d.
              Senior Work DNCE BC3593 SR PROJECT:REPERTORY FOR DANCE Columbia University, n.d.
              Senior Work Students who are double majors may request permission to write a two-semester combined thesis. Columbia University, n.d.
              Electives Five additional 3- or 4-point courses, chosen in consultation with the major advisor, are required. Electives may be chosen from among the departmental offerings listed above or below, including additional coursework in Composition, Movement Science, and/or Senior Work beyond the major requirement. 15-20 Columbia University, n.d.
