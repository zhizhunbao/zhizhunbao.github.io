              Fourth Year 2. Students must take at least 2.0 credits, but no more than 2.5 credits, of FAH at the 200 level. Of these 2.0-2.5 credits, at least 1.5 credits must be completed at UTM (see Note 3 below for the required area distribution and Note 4 below for the St. George exceptions allowed). VCC 200 level courses do not satisfy FAH 200 level requirements. University of Toronto, n.d.
              Fourth Year 3. At least 0.5 credit of FAH at the 200-level must be completed in each of the following three areas: Ancient & Medieval; 15th-18th Centuries; and 19th-21st Centuries. See the departmental website (www.utm.utoronto.ca/dvs) for the distribution of courses by area. University of Toronto, n.d.
              Fourth Year credit of FAH at the 200 level may be taken at St. George in an area not covered by current UTM offerings (i.e. one of the following courses – FAH248H1 Canadian Painting 1665-1960 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              Fourth Year credit of FAH at the 200 level may be taken at St. George in an area not covered by current UTM offerings (i.e. one of the following courses – (or) FAH260H1 The Artistic Landscape of East Asia 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              Fourth Year credit of FAH at the 200 level may be taken at St. George in an area not covered by current UTM offerings (i.e. one of the following courses – (or) FAH262H1 Art and Visual Experience in Modern and Contemporary East Asia 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              Fourth Year credit of FAH at the 200 level may be taken at St. George in an area not covered by current UTM offerings (i.e. one of the following courses – (or) FAH270H1 Architecture: Rituals and Monuments 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              Fourth Year credit of FAH at the 200 level may be taken at St. George in an area not covered by current UTM offerings (i.e. one of the following courses – (or) FAH272H1 Modern Architecture from 1750 to the Present 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              Fourth Year 5. For the complete list of VCC courses that satisfy Art & Art History program requirements, please see the departmental website (www.utm.utoronto.ca/dvs). University of Toronto, n.d.
              Fourth Year 6. As studio space is limited in the 100-level and 200-level FAS courses, priority will be given during the first registration period to students enrolled in the Art & Art History Major or Specialist, Art History Major or Specialist, CCIT Major, VCC Specialist, and to newly admitted students who indicated the Art & Art History code on their application. Students committed to the program should make sure that they are officially registered in the program as soon as possible. University of Toronto, n.d.
              Fourth Year 7. All 300 and 400 level FAS courses are to be enrolled in on ACORN. Students are required to have completed 1.5 credits in FAH or VCC before beginning their 300/400 level FAS courses. University of Toronto, n.d.
              Fourth Year It is recommended that students take at least one of the following ‘practicum’ courses FAH451H5 Curating Now: Turning Concepts into Curatorial Projects 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              Fourth Year It is recommended that students take at least one of the following ‘practicum’ courses (or) FAS453H5 Art Education Practice 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              Fourth Year It is recommended that students take at least one of the following ‘practicum’ courses (or) FAS454H5 Professional Practice 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              Fourth Year It is recommended that students take at least one of the following ‘practicum’ courses (or) FAS455H5 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              Fourth Year 9. No more than 17.0 credits of FAH and FAS may be completed. University of Toronto, n.d.
              Fourth Year 10. Students enrolling in any FAS course will be required to pay a fee of $101-$187 per half-credit course ($202-$374 per full-credit course) in ancillary fees to cover consumable materials used in studio as well as take-away materials. These charges will be automatically added to UofT student account upon enrolment in the course on ACORN. For details on ancillary fees, please see the Student Accounts website (www.fees.utoronto.ca). University of Toronto, n.d.
