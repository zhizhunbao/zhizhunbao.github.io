              The Major Program in Physical and Human Geography requires the completion of a total of 8.0 credits of which 4.0 credits are to be EES courses, and 4.0 credits are to be GGR or CIT courses. Among these 8.0 credits, the student must include: University of Toronto, n.d.
              1. 2.0 credits as follows: University of Toronto, n.d.
              EESA01H3 Introduction to Environmental Science 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              EESA06H3 Introduction to Planet Earth 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              GGRA02H3 Introduction to Human Geography: Imagining Better Worlds 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              GGRA03H3 Cities and Environments: Urban Life, Livelihoods & Landscapes 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              2. 1.5 credits from the following: University of Toronto, n.d.
              EESB02H3 Principles of Geomorphology 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              EESB03H3 Principles of Climatology 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              EESB04H3 Principles of Hydrology 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              EESB05H3 Principles of Soil Science 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              EESB15H3 Earth History 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              3. At least 1.5 credits from the following: University of Toronto, n.d.
              CITB01H3 Canadian Cities and Planning 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              GGRB02H3 Geographic Thought: Critical Perspectives on Key Concepts in Human Geography 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              GGRB05H3 Urban Geography 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              GGRB13H3 Social Geography: Life and Death, Place and Power 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              GGRB18H3 Whose Land? Indigenous-Canada-Land Relations 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              ESTB02H3 Whose Land? Indigenous-Canada-Land Relations 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              GGRB21H3 Political Ecology: Nature, Society and Environmental Change 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              GGRB28H3 Geographies of Disease: Beyond Epidemiology 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              GGRB55H3 Cultural Geography 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              4. At least 1.0 credit at the C- or D-level from EES courses University of Toronto, n.d.
              5. At least 1.0 credit at the C- or D-level from GGR or CIT courses University of Toronto, n.d.
              6. At least one additional 0.5 credit from GGR or CIT courses University of Toronto, n.d.
