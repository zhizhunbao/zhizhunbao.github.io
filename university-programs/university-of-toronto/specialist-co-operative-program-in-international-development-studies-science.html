              Co-op Work Term Requirements; To be eligible for placement, students must have completed 14.5 credits, including 10.5 credits from Requirements 1. through 4. as noted above IDSC01H3 Research Design for Development Fieldwork 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              Co-op Work Term Requirements The Co-op work terms are a minimum of 8-months and a maximum of 12-months in country. University of Toronto, n.d.
              Co-op Work Term Requirements During placement, students are required to attend a monthly check-in meeting with the IDS Co-op Program Manager and submit 4 bi-monthly placement progress reports (one every 2 months). Students also begin working on a research project under the supervision of a faculty member. University of Toronto, n.d.
              Co-op Work Term Requirements; In addition to the academic course requirements, students are required to complete two additional non-credit courses COPB30H3 Passport to Placement I 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
