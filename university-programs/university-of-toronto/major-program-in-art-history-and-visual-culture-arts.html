              Courses

              Level Type Course Code Course Name Credits Source
              This program requires the completion of 7.0 credits in Art History and Visual Culture (VPH) as follows: University of Toronto, n.d.
              1. Courses at the A-level (0.5 credit): University of Toronto, n.d.
              VPHA46H3 Ways of Seeing: Introduction to Art Histories 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              2. Courses at the B-level (0.5 credit): University of Toronto, n.d.
              VPHB39H3 Ten Key Words in Art History: Unpacking Methodology 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              3. Courses at the C-level (1.5 credits): University of Toronto, n.d.
              VPHC49H3 Advanced Studies in Art Theory 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              VPHC54H3 Art Writing 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              VPHC72H3 Art and Visual Culture in Spaces, Places, and Institutions 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              4. Courses at the D-level (0.5 credit): University of Toronto, n.d.
              VPHD48H3 Advanced Seminar in Art History and Visual Culture 0.5 University of Toronto, n.d.
              5. 4.0 additional credits in VPH courses, including: University of Toronto, n.d.
              (i) At least 1.5 credits must be in courses at the C- or D-level; University of Toronto, n.d.
              (ii) Must include diversity in the time-period and cultural geography; University of Toronto, n.d.
              (iii) Must include at least 1.0 credit dealing with periods prior to 1800; University of Toronto, n.d.
              (iv) Must include at least 1.0 credit dealing with periods after 1800; and University of Toronto, n.d.
              (v) Must include 0.5 credit dealing with the arts of Asia, Africa, or the Diaspora University of Toronto, n.d.
              [Note that the courses in Req#5 can count towards more than one criteria, as long as 4.0 unique credits are taken. For example, a C-level post-1800 course may fulfill both (i) and (iv)] University of Toronto, n.d.
