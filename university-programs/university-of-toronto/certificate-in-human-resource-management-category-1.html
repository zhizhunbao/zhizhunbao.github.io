              Level Type Course Code Course Name Credits Source
              A Certificate in Human Resource Management will be awarded upon successful completion of five (5.0) full-course equivalents (FCEs) with a minimum cumulative Grade Point Average of 1.85. University of Toronto, n.d.
              Students must complete 5.0 FCEs, of which no more than 1.0 credit may be taken from Group B. University of Toronto, n.d.
              Most students will complete the 5.0 credits from Group A. University of Toronto, n.d.
