              Level Type Course Code Course Name Credits Source
              Within this 13-month program, students must successfully complete 22 courses, including the set of 14 core courses, with an accumulated credit weighting of 11.5 full-course equivalents (FCEs). University of Toronto, n.d.
              Students must complete a structured sequence of courses. No advanced standing will be granted for previous academic work completed or professional designations earned. Students who are unable to follow courses in their prescribed order must attain special approval from the Academic Director in order to continue in the program. University of Toronto, n.d.
              With the permission of the Academic Director, students in good standing may apply to participate and take up to one course in an international exchange program approved by the University of Toronto. Courses selected are subject to the approval of the Academic Director. University of Toronto, n.d.
