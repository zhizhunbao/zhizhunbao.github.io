              Co-op Work Term Requirements Students must successfully complete three Co-op work terms, totaling 12 months in duration, which can be fulfilled through the following options: University of Toronto, n.d.
              Co-op Work Term Requirements Three 4-month work terms, or University of Toronto, n.d.
              Co-op Work Term Requirements One 4-month work term and one 8-month work term, or University of Toronto, n.d.
              Co-op Work Term Requirements One 12-month work term. University of Toronto, n.d.
              Co-op Work Term Requirements Students must be available for work terms during the Fall, Winter, and Summer semesters and are required to complete at least one of their work terms in either the Fall or Winter semester. As a result, students must also take courses during the Summer semesters. University of Toronto, n.d.
