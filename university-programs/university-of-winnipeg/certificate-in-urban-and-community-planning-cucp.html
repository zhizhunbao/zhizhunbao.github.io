              Students must fill out a Certificate Declaration Form and consult with the Departmental Advisor for the Certificate. University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              GRADUATION REQUIREMENT 30 Credit Hours University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              GENERAL REQUIREMENTS 18 Credit Hours of Core Courses (see curriculum below) University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              12 Credit Hours of Electives (see curriculum below) University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              The Certificate in Urban and Community Planning (CUCP) is a 30-credit-hour program designed for students interested in urban issues or a career in city planning or administration, offered in partnership with Urban and Inner City Studies. The Certificate is best competed while pursuing four-year programs – either Honours or 4-year degree programs. The Certificate comprises 18 credit hours of core courses and 12 credit hours of electives intended to be fulfilled through the completion of elective courses already required for a degree. The Certificate is interdisciplinary – drawing on courses from Geography, Urban and Inner City Studies, Environmental Studies, History, Indigenous Studies, Political Science, and Economics. Potential students pursuing degrees outside of Geography or Urban and Inner City Studies are encouraged to consult with the Departmental Advisor for the Certificate on potential pathways to complete the Certificate. University of Winnipeg, n.d.
