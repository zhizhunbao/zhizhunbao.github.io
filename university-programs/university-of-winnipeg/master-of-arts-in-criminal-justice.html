              In the Courses and Knowledge Synthesis Project-based stream, students will complete 27 credit hours: 4 required courses (12 credit hours), 4 elective courses (12 credit hours), and the Knowledge Synthesis Project (3 credit hours). University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              In the Courses and Thesis-based stream, students will complete 27 credit hours: 4 required courses (12 credit hours), 2 elective courses (6 credit hours), and the Thesis (9 credit hours). University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              Electives for either stream may be taken from CJ electives, approved courses from the MPA or Indigenous Governance graduate programs, and/or through the Western Deans agreement. University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              Differences between the two streams pertain to their breadth and scope. Thesis stream students are required to propose and execute an original and elaborate research study and provide an extensive written manuscript. KSP students will complete a project that synthesizes an existing body of knowledge based on specific methodologies (e.g., scoping review, systematic review, meta- analysis). More details can be found on the Criminal Justice website under the Graduate Program section: https://www.uwinnipeg.ca/criminal-justice/graduate-program-and-courses/index.html University of Winnipeg, n.d.

For either the Thesis or KSP, the faculty supervisor must be a member of the Criminal Justice Department. The supervisor will provide direction to the student on the program of study, direct research, and supervise the KSP or Thesis. University of Winnipeg, n.d.
