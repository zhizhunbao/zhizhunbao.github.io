              The Post-Baccalaureate Diploma in Education at the University of Winnipeg is subject to the following requirements: General Stream University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              The Post-Baccalaureate Diploma in Education at the University of Winnipeg is subject to the following requirements: Students must complete 30 credit hours of course work to earn their PBDE. University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              The Post-Baccalaureate Diploma in Education at the University of Winnipeg is subject to the following requirements: A minimum of 18 credit hours must be University of Winnipeg Education courses at the 5,000 level (which also fulfills the Residency Requirement). University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              The Post-Baccalaureate Diploma in Education at the University of Winnipeg is subject to the following requirements: Although the entire Program might be comprised of Education courses, a maximum of 12 credit hours may be below the 5,000 level and taken from any Faculty. (Students, however, may not count courses at the 1,000 level or lower). University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              The Post-Baccalaureate Diploma in Education at the University of Winnipeg is subject to the following requirements: A maximum of 12 credit hours may be taken from other post-secondary institutions and, upon approval from the Faculty of Education, transferred to the University of Winnipeg’s PBDE Program. University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              The Post-Baccalaureate Diploma in Education at the University of Winnipeg is subject to the following requirements: There will be no credit given for professional development sessions (i.e. only formal university courses will be accepted for the PBDE). University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              The Post-Baccalaureate Diploma in Education at the University of Winnipeg is subject to the following requirements: Students must achieve an overall GPA of 2.5 (or C+ Average) on the 30 credit hours needed for the PBDE. University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              The Post-Baccalaureate Diploma in Education at the University of Winnipeg is subject to the following requirements: PBDE Indigenous Knowledges Stream University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              The Post-Baccalaureate Diploma in Education at the University of Winnipeg is subject to the following requirements: The University of Winnipeg’s Post Baccalaureate Diploma in Indigenous Knowledges provides professional growth opportunities for in-service teachers. Students explore Indigenous Knowledges, perspectives, experiences, and culture in ways that enable and empower teachers to grow their professional practice such that their own students have improved cross-curricular learning outcomes. The program retains much of the individualization and flexibility of the PBDE General Stream yet ensures appropriate breadth by organizing students’ studies around four content areas: 1) Indigenous Approaches to Education; 2) Indigenous Governance, Leadership, Policy, and Treaty in the Context of Education; 3) Education and its History as a Colonial Institution; 4) Traditional Ways of Knowing and Being. Deviations from the normal requirements below must be approved by the program director or designate. University of Winnipeg, n.d.
              The Post-Baccalaureate Diploma in Education at the University of Winnipeg is subject to the following requirements: Required Course – 3 credit hours University of Winnipeg, n.d.
