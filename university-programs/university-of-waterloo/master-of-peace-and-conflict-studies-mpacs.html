              Peace Research: an agreement between the student and the supervising faculty member about research expectations, length of paper, format, topic, types of sources that can be used, and anticipated outcomes is required. Students will be required to prepare a detailed proposal prior to registration in this course that will fully explain the proposed research as well as provide a short bibliography to ensure that adequate sources exist to successfully complete the research. Students will meet periodically with their instructor throughout the term to ensure that milestones are reached. Written work will be evaluated per normal academic criteria.
              和平研究：需要学生与指导教师就研究预期、论文篇幅、格式、主题、可使用的资料类型和预期成果达成协议。学生在注册本课程前须准备一份详细的研究计划书，充分说明拟开展的研究，并提供一份简短的参考书目，以确保有足够的资料来顺利完成研究。学生将在整个学期中定期与指导教师会面，以确保达到各项阶段目标。书面作业按常规学术标准评分              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） University of Waterloo, n.d.
              Internship: students will be required to submit a petition outlining the details of the proposed internship such as place, position, cost, academic work expectations, security concerns, etc. Students will be expected to engage in substantial research on issues related to the host agency as part of the internship. While PACS has the agreement of over ten organizations who are interested in hosting interns, it is anticipated that internships will be negotiated to fit the unique long-term goals of each student. Host agencies will be expected to submit a reference evaluating the student intern at the end of the internship. Written work submitted by the student (evidence of research and reflective report) will be evaluated per normal academic criteria.
              实习：学生须提交申请书，说明拟议实习的细节，如地点、职位、费用、学术工作要求、安全问题等。学生须对东道机构相关议题开展大量研究，作为实习的一部分。PACS已与十多个有意接收实习生的组织达成协议，预计实习将根据每位学生独特的长期目标协商安排。东道机构须在实习结束时提交评价实习生的推荐信。学生提交的书面成果（研究证据和反思报告）将按常规学术标准评分              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） University of Waterloo, n.d.
