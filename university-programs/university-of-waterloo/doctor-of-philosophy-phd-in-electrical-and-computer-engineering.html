              Courses

              Level Type Course Code Course Name Credits Source
              The coursework associated with the program is intended to provide a foundation for advanced learning in the chosen field of research. University of Waterloo, n.d.
              Students holding a Master of Applied Science (MASc) degree or equivalent must obtain at least 4 courses (0.50 unit weight per course) of graduate credit (7 0.50 unit weight courses from a Bachelor program). University of Waterloo, n.d.
              At least 2 of the courses must be from the list of approved core courses (updated by the Department annually) in one of the approved research fields as specified at the time of the student's admission, unless this requirement has already been achieved during a University of Waterloo Electrical and Computer Engineering MASc program. University of Waterloo, n.d.
              A minimum of 2 courses must be taken from within the Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering. Core courses may count towards this 2 course minimum. University of Waterloo, n.d.
              The remaining 2 courses may be taken from outside of the Department but must be from the faculties of Engineering, Mathematics, and/or Science (unless otherwise approved). University of Waterloo, n.d.
              The choice of courses must meet with the approval of the supervisor. University of Waterloo, n.d.
              To obtain credit, an individual course must be passed with at least a 75% average. University of Waterloo, n.d.
              Students may be required to withdraw from the program at any time if they fail to maintain a minimum cumulative average of 78% in their coursework or if they fail to receive satisfactory progress reports regarding their research activities. University of Waterloo, n.d.
              Courses taken with a grade below 75% cannot be retaken for graduate course credit. University of Waterloo, n.d.
