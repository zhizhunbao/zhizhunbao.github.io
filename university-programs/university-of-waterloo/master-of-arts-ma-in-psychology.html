The acceptance of transfer credits from prior registration at another university will be determined in individual cases by the Associate Dean (Graduate Studies) of the Faculty at the time of admission to the program. Transfer credits must be "unused" credits (i.e., they must not have been credited towards an earlier acquired degree or other academic credential). Transfer credits must be specified in writing at the time of school recommendation for admission. A minimum of 70% (University of Waterloo converted grade) is required for transfer credit. University of Waterloo, n.d.
