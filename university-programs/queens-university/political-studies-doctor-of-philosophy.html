              Political Studies - Doctor of Philosophy
              政治研究 - 哲学博士              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） Queen’s University, n.d.
              /992/993/994 Graduate Research and Development Seminar (GRADS): These non-credit courses are essentially a weekly research and professional development forum for graduate students. The goal is to provide professional development instruction on topics such as writing grant applications, using reference management software in research, and journal publishing, combined with opportunities to present ongoing thesis research and mock job talks. GRADS fosters consistent, high-quality research exchange and helps students build essential academic and transferable skills, including clear communication, peer feedback, public speaking, and translating research beyond one’s field. To fulfill this requirement, PhD students are required to attend 75% of sessions in GRADS in years 1-4 (POLS 991-994) and present research at two stages in their program: the dissertation proposal in the winter of the second year (POLS 992) and a dissertation capstone (in preparation for the defense) or mock job talk (in preparation for interviews) in the fall of the year in which a student either defends or applies for external positions (whichever comes first – usually year 4/POLS 994).
              /992/993/994 研究生研究与发展研讨会（GRADS）：这些不计学分的课程本质上是面向研究生的每周研究与专业发展论坛。目标是就撰写资助申请、在研究中使用文献管理软件、期刊发表等主题提供专业发展指导，并提供展示在研论文研究和模拟求职报告的机会。GRADS 促进持续、高质量的研究交流，帮助学生培养重要的学术与可迁移技能，包括清晰表达、同行反馈、公开演讲，以及将研究成果推广到本领域之外。为满足此项要求，博士生须在第1至4年（POLS 991-994）出席 75% 的 GRADS 场次，并在项目的两个阶段展示研究：第二年冬季的论文开题（POLS 992），以及学生答辩或申请外部职位当年秋季（以先到者为准，通常为第4年/POLS 994）的论文总结（为答辩做准备）或模拟求职报告（为面试做准备）              		 (Definition pending) （定义待补） Queen’s University, n.d.
