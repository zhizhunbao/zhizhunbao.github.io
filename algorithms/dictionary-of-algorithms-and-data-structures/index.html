              Dictionary of Algorithms and Data Structures

              Planned, no content yet. The pages under this one are the 15 areas of the NIST Dictionary of Algorithms and Data Structures (DADS), in the order of its index by area. Each area page lists all its entries (algorithms, data structures, classic problems, algorithmic techniques and definitions) with their type and a link to their definition. The dictionary is the reference for definitions, quoted with an APA reference when a topic of CS2023 AL is written; the 15 entries with no area are not listed. The source leaves out algorithms specific to business data processing, communications, operating systems or distributed algorithms, programming languages, AI, graphics, and numerical analysis.
